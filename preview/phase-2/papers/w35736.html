The Welfare Impacts of Urban Renewal and Forced Relocation: Evidence from Chicago’s HOPE VI Demolitions

城市更新与强制搬迁的福利影响：来自芝加哥HOPE VI拆除项目的证据

Milena Almagro, Eric Chyn, Bryan A. Stuart

其他主题 美国

Abstract

This paper studies one of the largest spatial redevelopment efforts in the United States: HOPE VI public housing demolitions. Focusing on Chicago, we estimate a neighborhood choice model combining administrative records tracking displaced public-housing residents with Census Bureau data capturing citywide sorting and prices. Demolitions generate large welfare losses driven by displacement costs, partially offset by housing vouchers. Demolitions also raise housing prices and reshape neighborhood composition, with gains concentrated among higher-income homeowners. Counterfactuals show that more generous vouchers and counseling programs mitigate but do not eliminate displaced residents' losses, while expanded redevelopment yields larger citywide gains through general-equilibrium effects.

中文摘要

本文研究了美国最大的空间再开发行动之一：HOPE VI公共住房拆除项目。我们以芝加哥为研究对象，估计了一个邻里选择模型，该模型结合了追踪被搬迁公共住房居民的行政记录，以及捕捉全市分选和价格的美国人口普查局数据。拆除项目产生了巨大的福利损失，其驱动因素是搬迁成本，而住房券部分抵消了这些损失。拆除项目还提高了住房价格并重塑了邻里构成，其收益集中在收入较高的房主群体。反事实分析表明，更慷慨的住房券和咨询项目可以缓解但不能消除被搬迁居民的损失，而扩大再开发则通过一般均衡效应带来更大的全市收益。
