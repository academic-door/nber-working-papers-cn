Diversification as a Process: Geographic Expansion and the Formation of National Insurers

作为过程的多元化：地理扩张与全国性保险公司的形成

Pawel Janas

金融与资产定价 美国

Abstract

Large financial intermediaries are more stable than small ones, and the largest are also the most geographically diversified. Whether that breadth is a source of stability or a byproduct of scale is difficult to establish, since intermediaries diversify by growing large. I examine how the diversified intermediary is assembled, using a newly digitized firm-by-state panel of the near-universe of U.S. life insurers from 1888 to 1940, the largest nonbank institutional investors of the period. Over these decades, the number of insurers per state more than quadrupled and within-state concentration declined by half, yet a small number of geographically extensive insurers retained a stable share of national premiums. I decompose firm-level outcomes into the insurer’s underwriting level and the composition of its book. Insurers entered new states before attaining scale, each entry constituting a small and favorably selected position with the convex payoff of a real option. The most successful decile of entries generated half of a cohort’s premium. Expansion was costly on two margins: a broad insurer earned less per dollar of coverage and paid 15.1 percent more in claims than an equally large insurer writing in the same state and year. The pricing cost is attributable to the composition of a broad insurer’s book, the claims cost to the insurer itself. A comparison across firms understates the claims cost, since the markets a broad insurer adds exhibit favorable claims experience of their own. Stability, by contrast, is attributable to scale, since breadth’s advantages in volatility and survival vanish conditional on size. Risk pooling was bounded: approximately nine tenths of an insurer’s loss-ratio variance was common to its markets. Diversification was the product of costly expansion, and the stability attributed to it is the scale that expansion produced.

中文摘要

大型金融中介比小型金融中介更稳定，而最大的金融中介在地理上也最多元化。这种广度是稳定的来源，还是规模的副产品，难以确定，因为中介通过成长为大型来实现多元化。我考察多元化中介如何被组装起来，使用新数字化的1888年至1940年几乎涵盖美国寿险公司全体的公司-州面板数据，这些公司是当时最大的非银行机构投资者。在这些十年间，每州保险公司数量增长超过四倍，州内集中度下降一半，但少数地理范围广泛的保险公司在全国保费中保持了稳定份额。我将公司层面结果分解为保险公司的承保水平和其账簿构成。保险公司在达到规模之前进入新州，每次进入都构成一个小且经过有利选择的头寸，具有实物期权的凸性收益。最成功的十分之一进入贡献了一个 cohort 保费的一半。扩张在两个边际上代价高昂：广泛经营的保险公司每美元保额赚得更少，并且比在同州和同年度承保的同等规模保险公司多支付15.1%的赔付。定价成本可归因于广泛经营保险公司的账簿构成，赔付成本则归因于保险公司本身。跨公司比较低估了赔付成本，因为广泛保险公司新增的市场本身表现出有利的赔付经验。相比之下，稳定性可归因于规模，因为广度在波动性和生存方面的优势在控制规模后消失。风险池化是有限的：保险公司损失率方差的约十分之九是其各市场共有的。多元化是代价高昂扩张的产物，而归因于它的稳定性是扩张所产生的规模。
