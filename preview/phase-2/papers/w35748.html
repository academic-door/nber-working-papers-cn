Does Information Influence the Choice between Social Security Disability and Early Retirement Benefits?

信息是否影响社会保障残疾福利与提前退休福利之间的选择？

Priyanka Anand, Tran Thi Phu-Duyen, Sita Slavov

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine how information affects the choice between Social Security Disability Insurance (DI) and Old-Age Insurance (OAI) benefits for people between 62 and full retirement age. We find that receiving an initial Social Security statement, which provides recipients with personalized estimated benefit amounts available from DI and OAI, increases the probability of applying for both programs among those with a work-limiting health condition or a low work ability index, and this response is concentrated among people who are further from full retirement age. The increase in DI applications is split between applications for DI only and applications for both programs.

中文摘要

我们考察信息如何影响62岁至完全退休年龄之间人群在社会保障残疾保险（DI）和老年保险（OAI）福利之间的选择。我们发现，收到初始社会保障声明——该声明向接收者提供DI和OAI可获得的个性化估计福利金额——会增加那些有工作限制健康状况或低工作能力指数的人申请这两个项目的概率，且这种反应集中在距离完全退休年龄较远的人群中。DI申请的增加在仅申请DI和同时申请两个项目之间平分。
