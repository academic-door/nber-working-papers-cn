NBER Working Paper w35732所在周报

Social Capital and Innovation: Evidence from Facebook Friendship Networks

社会资本与创新：来自Facebook友谊网络的证据

Brad Cannon, David Hirshleifer, Joshua Thornton

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

Using Facebook friendship data, we study how three aspects of social capital shape innovative activity. We find that the most important aspect of social capital in explaining innovation is Economic Connectedness (EC)--the share of high-income friendships. One standard deviation greater EC is associated with 97% more patents per capita among patenting ZIP Codes and 45% more breakthrough patents per capita among ZIP Codes with breakthrough patents. Reverse-causality tests, a within-inventor relocation design, and a quasi-experiment using fracking-driven economic shocks to non-local friends support a causal interpretation. Mechanism tests provide evidence consistent with a financing channel.

中文摘要

利用Facebook友谊数据，我们研究社会资本的三个方面如何塑造创新活动。我们发现，在解释创新方面最重要的社会资本方面是经济连通性（EC）——高收入友谊所占比例。EC每高出1个标准差，在拥有专利的ZIP Code中与人均专利数多97%相关，在拥有突破性专利的ZIP Code中与人均突破性专利数多45%相关。反向因果检验、发明人内部迁移设计，以及利用水力压裂驱动的经济冲击对非本地朋友影响的准实验，支持因果解释。机制检验提供的证据与融资渠道一致。
