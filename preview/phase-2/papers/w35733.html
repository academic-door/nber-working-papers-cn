(Mis)measuring Uncertainty: Bunching in Probabilistic Expectations

（错误）测量不确定性：概率预期中的堆积

Carola Binder, Laura Crespo, Carlos Gento, Luis M. Guirola, Ernesto Villanueva

其他主题 西班牙

Abstract

Probabilistic expectation questions are often used to measure subjective uncertainty, but respondents frequently assign all probability to one outcome. We show that this bunching is partly a survey artifact rather than genuine certainty. In the (online) ECB Consumer Expectations Survey and the (in person) Spanish Survey of Household Finances, bunching is higher among less financially literate respondents and increases with panel tenure. Two survey-design changes provide stronger evidence: enforcing neutral interviewer protocols reduces bunching by about 15 percentage points, and a later questionnaire redesign reduces it by a further 18 percentage points. Interviewer-level variation also falls after standardization. These findings imply that degenerate responses in probabilistic expectations can reflect task burden and survey administration, causing standard measures to overstate certainty and understate uncertainty.

中文摘要

概率预期问题常被用来测量主观不确定性，但受访者经常将所有概率赋予某一结果。我们表明，这种堆积在一定程度上是调查假象，而非真正确定。在（在线）欧洲央行消费者预期调查和（面对面）西班牙家庭金融调查中，堆积在金融素养较低的受访者中更高，并随面板参与时间增加而上升。两项调查设计变更提供了更强证据：执行中立的访员协议使堆积减少约15个百分点，随后的一次问卷重新设计又使其减少18个百分点。访员层面的差异在标准化后也有所下降。这些发现意味着，概率预期中的退化回答可能反映任务负担和调查实施，导致标准测量高估确定性并低估不确定性。
