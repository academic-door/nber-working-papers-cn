Who Matters in College? Familiarity, Ability Signals, and Student Achievement

大学中谁重要？熟悉度、能力信号与学生成绩

Isaac Ahimbisibwe, Scott E. Carrell, Travis Lybbert

教育与劳动 乌干达

Abstract

This study examines how social influences on academic performance evolve during transition to university when students encounter new social environments and publicly observable signals of academic ability. Using administrative data from a large, elite, and linguistically and culturally heterogeneous public university in Uganda and exploiting random assignment of students to dormitories, we examine how exposure to coethnic peers and peers with scholarship status affects academic performance. We find that greater exposure to peers from similar linguistic and cultural backgrounds improve academic achievement early in university, but these effects fade over time. In contrast, exposure to scholarship peers generates persistent gains in academic performance that increase in magnitude over time. Our setting allows us to distinguish between peers’ underlying academic ability and publicly observable signals of ability since scholarship status is visible, while underlying academic preparation is not. We find that exposure to scholarship peers improves academic performance regardless of those peers’ underlying ability, while exposure to high-ability peers without a scholarship signal has no detectable effect. These results suggest that students respond more strongly to observable signals of academic standing than to peers’ underlying academic preparation. Taken together, our findings indicate that the sources of academic influence evolve over time.

中文摘要

本研究考察，当学生进入新的社会环境并接触到公开可观察的学术能力信号时，学术表现的社会影响在向大学过渡期间如何演变。利用乌干达一所大型、精英且语言和文化高度异质的公立大学的行政数据，并利用学生被随机分配到宿舍这一机制，我们考察接触同族同龄人和具有奖学金身份的同龄人如何影响学术表现。我们发现，更多接触来自相似语言和文化背景的同龄人，会改善大学早期的学业成绩，但这些效应随时间消退。相比之下，接触奖学金同龄人会产生持续的学业表现收益，且其幅度随时间增加。我们的场景使我们能够区分同龄人的潜在学术能力与公开可观察的能力信号，因为奖学金身份可见，而潜在学术准备不可见。我们发现，接触奖学金同龄人会改善学业表现，无论这些同龄人的潜在能力如何；而接触没有奖学金信号的高能力同龄人则没有可检测的影响。这些结果表明，学生对可观察的学术地位信号的反应，强于对同龄人潜在学术准备的反应。综合来看，我们的发现表明，学术影响的来源随时间演变。
