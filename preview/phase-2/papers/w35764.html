Incentivizing Self-Protection From Wildfires

激励野火自我防护

Patrick Baylis, Judson Boomhower, Bob Horton, Chris Mulverhill

环境与气候 美国

Abstract

Losses from natural disasters can be mitigated through investments in property protection, yet adoption of low-cost risk-reducing measures remains low. In wildfire-exposed areas, managing vegetation and other fuels next to homes decreases structure vulnerability, but few properties comply with best practices despite widespread outreach efforts. We report on two randomized field experiments that incentivized thousands of Oregon homeowners to participate in pre-existing fire department programs to reduce wildfire risk. Across both experiments, financial incentives of $100 to $500 resulted in up to 12% of homeowners scheduling free defensible space assessments, while information-only approaches achieved assessment takeup rates of 3% at most. Responses to subsidies are largest among owners of more valuable homes, while baseline wildfire risk does not predict responsiveness. On-the-ground and remotely-sensed measures of vegetation provide noisy estimates of treatment effects on endline vegetation. Finally, we consider the extent to which the estimated behavioral elasticities from these experiments might shed light on potential homeowner responses to pricing of wildfire risk reductions in home insurance premiums, a practice that is becoming more common due to technological and regulatory changes.

中文摘要

自然灾害造成的损失可以通过财产保护投资来减轻，但低成本风险降低措施的采用率仍然很低。在野火暴露地区，管理房屋旁边的植被和其他燃料可降低结构脆弱性，但尽管有广泛的宣传努力，很少有房产遵守最佳实践。我们报告了两项随机实地实验，这些实验激励了数千名俄勒冈州房主参与现有的消防部门项目以降低野火风险。在两项实验中，100美元至500美元的财务激励使多达12%的房主安排了免费的可防御空间评估，而仅提供信息的方法最多实现了3%的评估参与率。补贴的反应在更有价值房屋的业主中最大，而基线野火风险并不能预测反应性。地面和遥感植被测量对期末植被处理效应的估计提供了噪声较大的估计。最后，我们考虑这些实验估计的行为弹性在多大程度上可能揭示房主对房屋保险费中野火风险降低定价的潜在反应，由于技术和监管变化，这种做法正变得越来越普遍。
