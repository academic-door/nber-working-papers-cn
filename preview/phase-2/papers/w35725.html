NBER Working Paper w35725所在周报

How Disability Benefits in Early Life Affect Adult Outcomes

生命早期残疾福利如何影响成年结果

Manasi Deshpande, Alessandra Voena, Jason B. Weitze

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

We estimate the effect of receiving Supplemental Security Income (SSI) in childhood on adult outcomes using multiple sources of variation. Using variation across and within two quasi-experiments, we find that the program has heterogeneous effects that vary with the parental earnings response to SSI benefits: the program has positive effects on children when parents do not adjust their labor supply in response to SSI income (meaning household income increases), but zero or negative effects on children when parents offset the transfer by reducing earnings (meaning household income remains constant or falls). These results suggest that, relative to parent non-work time, income is crucial in the human capital production of low-income children with disabilities. We estimate a model of maternal labor supply and child human capital formation to quantify the relative importance of these channels. Our findings indicate that 1) the income effects of SSI on children's human capital are substantial, with a limited role for perverse incentive effects from conditioning benefits on disability status, and 2) parental work on net improves children's outcomes by increasing household resources, despite the potential decrease in parental time.

中文摘要

我们利用多种变异来源，估计儿童期领取补充保障收入（SSI）对成年结果的影响。利用两个准实验之间和内部的变异，我们发现该项目的效果具有异质性，并随父母对SSI福利的 earnings 反应而变化：当父母不因SSI收入调整劳动供给时（意味着家庭收入增加），该项目对儿童产生正向影响；但当父母通过减少 earnings 来抵消转移支付时（意味着家庭收入保持不变或下降），该项目对儿童的影响为零或为负。这些结果表明，相对于父母的非工作时间，收入对于低收入残疾儿童的人力资本生产至关重要。我们估计了一个母亲劳动供给和儿童人力资本形成模型，以量化这些渠道的相对重要性。我们的发现表明：1）SSI对儿童人力资本的收入效应相当大，而将福利与残疾身份挂钩所带来的逆向激励效应作用有限；2）父母工作总体上通过增加家庭资源改善了儿童结果，尽管父母时间可能减少。
