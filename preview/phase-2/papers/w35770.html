Advertising Claims, Pricing, and Customer Evidence

广告声明、定价与客户证据

Joshua S. Gans

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

Why might firms make different claims in advertising both with respect to themselves and to rivals? I study how claims direct consumers to different customer experiences, and how prices change the available evidence. Attributing successes to the advertiser requires a low price when its product is superior; attributing failures to the rival supports a higher price. Focussing on a single strategic advertiser, the model shows that the claim that attracts the most customers to a superior product never maximises profit. The optimal campaign raises advertiser profit but lowers consumer surplus relative to fully informed choice, and reduces total welfare whenever product reliabilities differ. When both firms choose claims and prices, competition can sustain a mix of positive and negative claims with price dispersion, restoring claims that a lone advertiser would reject. A rival's pricing response alone can make failure attribution optimal by weakening incentives to undercut. Allowing the rival to advertise can lower both prices and shift customers towards it if the incumbent retains its previous claim; allowing the incumbent to change its claim can eliminate this additional effect. Advertising, therefore, changes not only persuasion but also pricing incentives, making its market consequences different from its effects at fixed prices.

中文摘要

为什么企业会在广告中对自身和竞争对手作出不同声明？我研究声明如何将消费者引向不同的客户体验，以及价格如何改变可用证据。将成功归因于广告主，在其产品更优时需要低价格；将失败归因于竞争对手则支持更高价格。聚焦于单一策略性广告主，模型表明，能吸引最多客户选择更优产品的声明从不会最大化利润。相对于完全信息选择，最优广告活动提高广告主利润但降低消费者剩余，并在产品可靠性不同时降低总福利。当两家企业都选择声明和价格时，竞争可以维持正负声明并存并伴随价格离散，从而恢复单一广告主会拒绝的声明。仅竞争对手的定价反应就能通过削弱降价激励，使失败归因成为最优。若允许竞争对手做广告，在 incumbent 保留此前声明时，可以降低双方价格并将客户转向竞争对手；若允许 incumbent 改变声明，则可消除这一额外效应。因此，广告不仅改变说服，也改变定价激励，使其市场后果不同于固定价格下的效应。
