Using Policy Functions to Estimate Merger Impacts: An Application to JetBlue-Spirit

使用政策函数估计合并影响：以捷蓝航空-精神航空为例

Gautam Gowrisankaran, Christopher D. Bruegge, Alex Gross

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Analyses of impacts of potential mergers in differentiated product markets often rely on non-transparent identification and strong conduct assumptions. We propose a simpler alternative: using quasi-exogenous variation in market structure to estimate equilibrium pricing policy functions. When price can appropriately capture consumer welfare, this methodology may require fewer assumptions than a structural equilibrium approach, including allowing variation in conduct. We apply this methodology to the JetBlue–Spirit merger, estimating policy functions using entry events. Our counterfactual price simulations account for changes in JetBlue’s capacity and Sprit’s elimination. The merger would cause $2.6 billion in annual harm under our preferred specification.

中文摘要

对差异化产品市场中潜在合并影响的分析，往往依赖于不透明的识别和强行为假设。我们提出一个更简单的替代方案：利用市场结构的准外生变化来估计均衡定价政策函数。当价格能够适当捕捉消费者福利时，该方法可能比结构性均衡方法需要更少假设，包括允许行为变化。我们将该方法应用于捷蓝航空-精神航空合并，使用进入事件估计政策函数。我们的反事实价格模拟考虑了捷蓝航空运力的变化和精神航空的消除。在我们偏好的设定下，该合并将造成每年26亿美元的损害。

The Macroeconomic Effect of Stimulus Checks: Evidence from Postwar Veterans’ Payments

刺激支票的宏观经济效应：来自二战后退伍军人支付的证据

Joao Guerreiro, Jonathon Hazell, Diego R. Känzig, Ed Manuel

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

Do stimulus checks—one-off, deficit-financed lump-sum payments to households—boost the economy? We study a natural experiment: payments to U.S. veterans after World War II that mimic stimulus checks but were plausibly exogenous to the economy. These payments led to a temporary increase in transfers and a persistent, hump-shaped increase in consumption. The transfer multiplier—the cumulative response of consumption to transfers—reaches 1 after six months and exceeds 2 after a year. The standard heterogeneous-agent New Keynesian model cannot match the hump-shaped consumption response. A version with imperfect expectations—with households underreacting on impact but overreacting at longer horizons—can match our estimates.

中文摘要

刺激支票——向家庭提供的一次性、赤字融资的总额支付——会提振经济吗？我们研究一个自然实验：二战后向美国退伍军人支付的款项，其类似于刺激支票，但可能与经济状况外生。这些支付导致转移支付暂时增加，并带来持续、驼峰形的消费增加。转移支付乘数——消费对转移支付的累积响应——在六个月后达到1，并在一年后超过2。标准异质性代理人新凯恩斯模型无法匹配驼峰形消费响应。一个具有不完美预期的版本——家庭在冲击当期反应不足，但在更长时期反应过度——可以匹配我们的估计。

The Remains of the Trade: The U.S.–China Trade War and its Aftermath

贸易的余波：美中贸易战及其后果

Pol Antràs, Adrian Kulesza, Andrea F. Presbitero

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 美国

Abstract

We study how the U.S.-China trade war has reshaped global trade patterns, creating significant spillovers to third countries. Using recent tariff policy changes and monthly international trade flow data, we document a redirection of Chinese exports away from the U.S. market and toward third countries. To interpret the impact of these reallocations on third countries, we introduce a taxonomy that separates four exposure margins - export competition, import competition, consumer gains, and sourcing gains - and construct theoretically-derived indices that map countries into these channels. Recent work often proxies such exposure with trade-basket similarity. We show why this can be problematic: similarity measures are symmetric and omit the size of product-specific shocks, Chinese market penetration, substitution elasticities, and incidence weights. Our model-based indices therefore generate country rankings that differ substantially from conventional similarity measures.

中文摘要

我们研究美中贸易战如何重塑全球贸易模式，并对第三国产生显著溢出效应。利用近期关税政策变化和月度国际贸易流量数据，我们记录了出口从美国市场转向第三国的重新定向。为解释这些再分配对第三国的影响，我们引入一个分类法，将四个暴露边际分开——出口竞争、进口竞争、消费者收益和采购收益——并构建理论推导的指数，将国家映射到这些渠道。近期研究常用贸易篮子相似性来代理这种暴露。我们说明为何这可能有问题：相似性指标是对称的，并忽略了产品特定冲击的规模、中国市场渗透、替代弹性和归宿权重。因此，我们基于模型的指数产生的国家排名与传统相似性指标存在显著差异。

The Protective Effects of a Healthy Spouse: Medicare as the Family Member of Last Resort

健康配偶的保护效应：作为家庭成员最后依靠的医疗保险

Itzik Fadlon, Tal Gross, Alex Hoagland, Timothy Layton

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the value of informal care from a spouse and how access to such care shapes optimal insurance coverage for long-term care. Using Medicare claims and a novel spousal crosswalk, we examine how the sudden incapacitation of a spouse affects their partner's use of formal care. A spouse's health shock increases their partner's probability of a nursing-home stay by 30%. We document significant negative health effects for the partner, but provide evidence that their increase in nursing-home stays is largely driven by substitution from informal care to formal care. When their partners are incapacitated, Medicare recipients become less sensitive to the price of formal care, consistent with a higher valuation of that care and reduced moral hazard. We develop a framework to interpret these findings. The framework translates differences in the level and price elasticity of demand for formal care into a lower-bound estimate of the value of informal care provided by a spouse. It also suggests that optimal insurance for long-term care should be designed at the household level with more generous coverage for those who do not have access to informal care.

中文摘要

我们研究配偶提供的非正式护理的价值，以及获得此类护理如何影响长期护理的最优保险覆盖。利用医疗保险索赔数据和一种新的配偶交叉匹配方法，我们考察配偶突然失能如何影响其伴侣对正式护理的使用。配偶的健康冲击使其伴侣入住养老院的概率增加30%。我们记录了伴侣显著的负面健康效应，但提供证据表明，其养老院入住增加在很大程度上是由非正式护理向正式护理的替代所驱动。当伴侣失能时，医疗保险受益人对正式护理价格的敏感度降低，这与对该护理更高估值和道德风险降低相一致。我们开发一个框架来解释这些发现。该框架将正式护理需求水平和价格弹性的差异转化为对配偶提供的非正式护理价值的下界估计。它还表明，长期护理的最优保险应在家庭层面设计，对无法获得非正式护理的人提供更慷慨的覆盖。

Unbundling Labor

劳动的拆分

Chris Edmond, Simon Mongey

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Why is there so much wage inequality within occupations? Why has the overall level of within-occupation wage inequality risen? Why is the rise concentrated in cognitive-intensive occupations, while inequality has fallen in manual-intensive ones? To answer these questions we develop a general equilibrium model with heterogeneous workers who supply multiple skills jointly, as an indivisible bundle. This bundling friction allows a given skill to be priced differently in different occupations. We find in US data that differences in skill prices, not worker composition, explain why within-occupation inequality is higher in more cognitively intensive occupations and account for about a third of the rise in within-occupation inequality from 1980 to 2010. We show that a uniform increase in the productivity of cognitive skill can drive inequality up in cognitive-intensive occupations and down in manual ones, just as in the data.

中文摘要

为什么职业内部存在如此多的工资不平等？为什么职业内部工资不平等的总体水平上升？为什么这种上升集中在认知密集型职业，而体力密集型职业的不平等却下降？为回答这些问题，我们开发一个一般均衡模型，其中异质性工人作为不可分割的捆绑共同提供多种技能。这种捆绑摩擦使得给定技能在不同职业中定价不同。我们在美国数据中发现，技能价格差异而非工人构成，解释了为什么认知密集型职业内部不平等更高，并解释了1980年至2010年职业内部不平等上升的约三分之一。我们表明，认知技能生产率的统一提高可以推动认知密集型职业的不平等上升，并推动体力密集型职业的不平等下降，正如数据所示。

Risk Premia, Firm Insurance, and Endogenous Labor Income Risk

风险溢价、企业保险与内生劳动收入风险

Maarten Meeuwis, Dimitris Papanikolaou, Lawrence D.W. Schmidt

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

We study how aggregate financial conditions shape firm insurance and, through it, labor income risk. In a directed search model with dynamic wage contracts and two-sided limited commitment, firm insurance against idiosyncratic shocks erodes when risk premia rise. Using U.S. administrative data, we document new evidence supporting the model: pass-through of firm shocks to earnings rises in bad times, especially for lower-paid workers near the separation margin. The model reproduces many untargeted time-series and cross-sectional features of earnings risk and implies substantial welfare costs of idiosyncratic risk, high private discount rates on human capital, and large gains from recession-contingent transfers.

中文摘要

我们研究总体金融状况如何塑造企业保险，并通过其影响劳动收入风险。在一个具有动态工资合同和双边有限承诺的定向搜索模型中，当风险溢价上升时，企业对特质冲击的保险会削弱。利用美国行政数据，我们记录了支持该模型的新证据：企业冲击向收入的传递在坏时期上升，尤其是对接近离职边际的低薪工人。该模型再现了收入风险的许多未目标化时间序列和横截面特征，并意味着特质风险带来显著福利成本、人力资本的高私人贴现率，以及衰退条件转移支付带来的巨大收益。

Regulating Entrepreneurship: The Case of Capital Requirements

监管创业：资本要求的案例

Annika Bacher, Andreas Fagereng, Marius A. K. Ring, Ella Getz Wold

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 挪威

Abstract

Governments have long imposed minimum equity requirements on new corporations. Opponents view them as barriers to entry while proponents argue that they protect stakeholders from financially unviable businesses. We study this quantity-quality trade-off using a Norwegian reform and comprehensive data linking entrepreneurs to their firms. A 70% reduction in required capital nearly doubled entrepreneurial entry, with no deterioration in survival, profitability, productivity, or interest-bearing leverage and no differences in founders’ ex-ante income, liquidity, or ability. These results suggest capital requirements restrict entrepreneurship without screening on quality or liquidity. Rather, we highlight returns-to-scale heterogeneity as an important determinant of the observed entry response.

中文摘要

政府长期以来对新公司施加最低股权要求。反对者将其视为进入壁垒，而支持者则认为它们保护利益相关者免受财务上不可行的企业之害。我们利用挪威的一项改革以及将创业者与其公司联系起来的全面数据，研究这种数量与质量之间的权衡。所需资本减少70%几乎使创业进入翻倍，而存活率、盈利能力、生产率或有息杠杆没有恶化，创始人的事前收入、流动性或能力也没有差异。这些结果表明，资本要求限制了创业，却没有在质量或流动性上进行筛选。相反，我们强调规模收益异质性是所观察到的进入反应的重要决定因素。

Measuring and Projecting U.S. Investment in Semiconductor Manufacturing Capacity

衡量和预测美国在半导体制造能力方面的投资

David M. Byrne, Robert J. Kurtzman, Heidi L. Williams

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

We consider official and alternative data sources for measuring and projecting the role of U.S. manufacturing in the global semiconductor manufacturing industry. We find that the government sources we consider face significant limitations for tracking semiconductor fab investment in real time: they use industry and product categories that are too broad and come with substantial publication lags. We then analyze project-level alternative data from Semiconductor Equipment and Materials International (SEMI), which provides quarterly tracking of individual fab projects with greater timeliness and specificity. Comparing SEMI data with official statistics through 2023, we find broad consistency in investment levels, validating SEMI as a credible supplement to official statistics. This work provides both a practical guide for tracking real-time trends in semiconductor manufacturing and a case study in synthesizing government and alternative data sources.

中文摘要

我们考察用于衡量和预测美国制造业在全球半导体制造产业中作用的官方和替代数据来源。我们发现，我们所考虑的政府来源在实时跟踪半导体晶圆厂投资方面面临重大局限：它们使用的行业和产品类别过于宽泛，并且存在相当大的发布滞后。随后，我们分析来自国际半导体设备与材料组织（SEMI）的项目层面替代数据，该数据以更高的及时性和具体性提供对单个晶圆厂项目的季度跟踪。将SEMI数据与截至2023年的官方统计进行比较，我们发现投资水平总体一致，这验证了SEMI作为官方统计可信补充的价值。这项工作既为实时跟踪半导体制造趋势提供了实用指南，也提供了综合政府与替代数据来源的案例研究。

Governing Global Externalities: Investor Voice Amid Fragmented Policy

治理全球外部性：政策碎片化下的投资者声音

Robin Döttling, Doron Levit, Nadya Malenko, Magdalena Rola-Janicka

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We study whether investor voice can help address global externalities under fragmented regulation. In our setting, a multinational firm generates externalities across jurisdictions, national regulators face political frictions that can impede corrective regulation, and policy differences create scope for regulatory arbitrage and production leakage. We compare two channels of investor influence: corporate stewardship, which affects firms’ global operations, and policy advocacy, which affects local regulation. Both channels can help address global externalities, but their relative effectiveness depends on political frictions abroad. When these frictions are severe enough to prevent foreign regulation, leakage weakens policy advocacy but strengthens stewardship. When foreign regulation is politically feasible, however, stewardship can crowd out foreign regulation and reinforce policy miscoordination, while local policy advocacy can crowd in foreign regulation and reduce externalities abroad.

In the context of the literature of experienced learning, this novel finding sheds light on the persistence of past populist policies—central banks operating under the shadow of past populist regimes that relied on inflation-prone deficit monetization continue today needing to send stronger signals of their independence and commitment to price stability to effectively anchor inflation expectations.

中文摘要

我们探讨民粹主义政权、财政货币化与通胀之间的历史联系，以及这些联系如何影响21世纪的货币政策。使用自1960年以来大量发达经济体和新兴市场的数据，我们表明，从历史上看，左倾民粹主义政权与中央银行对中央政府的贷款增加相关，而这是赤字货币化的一个衡量指标。反过来，中央银行贷款又与通胀显著上升相关。我们表明，过去对依赖赤字货币化的民粹主义的暴露会影响今天的货币政策执行。拥有赤字货币化和左翼民粹主义政权历史的国家，会系统性地对通胀预期偏离目标作出更强反应。即使在控制过去通胀对货币政策规则的直接影响后，这一效应仍然存在。在经验学习文献的背景下，这一新发现揭示了过去民粹主义政策的持续性——在依赖易引发通胀的赤字货币化的过去民粹主义政权阴影下运作的中央银行，今天仍需要发出更强信号以表明其独立性和对价格稳定的承诺，从而有效锚定通胀预期。
