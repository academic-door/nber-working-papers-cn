Spillover Effects of Regulatory Inspections: Evidence from OSHA

监管检查的溢出效应：来自OSHA的证据

Matthew S. Johnson, David Levine, Michael W. Toffel

健康与医疗 美国

Abstract

Regulatory inspections might affect entities nearby those inspected. Such spillovers are difficult to identify because regulators target inspections and neighboring establishments face correlated shocks. We exploit the U.S. Occupational Safety and Health Administration’s Site-Specific Targeting (SST) program, which generated quasi-random variation in inspections among workplaces with injury rates near a discrete cutoff. An SST inspection reduced injuries at uninspected same-ZIP-code establishments by an estimated 11 percent—an effect roughly as large as the direct effect on the inspected establishment. Because spillovers extend to multiple nearby establishments, they avert several times as many injuries as the inspection’s direct effect.

中文摘要

监管检查可能会影响被检查对象附近的实体。此类溢出效应难以识别，因为监管机构会针对性地选择检查对象，且邻近机构面临相关冲击。我们利用美国职业安全与健康管理局的定点目标（SST）项目，该项目在伤害率接近某一离散阈值的场所之间产生了准随机的检查变异。一次SST检查使同一邮政编码区域内未被检查场所的伤害减少了约11%——这一效应大致与被检查场所受到的直接影响一样大。由于溢出效应扩展到多个邻近场所，其避免的伤害数量是检查直接效应的数倍。
