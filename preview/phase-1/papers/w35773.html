Compound Delay: The Causes and Consequences of Later Grandmotherhood

复合延迟：晚育祖母身份的原因与后果

Dora L. Costa, Matthew E. Kahn

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

We show how fertility delays compound across generations to raise age at first grandmotherhood. Under the 1960 first-birth schedule median grandmotherhood came at 44. Under the 2024 schedule it is 57, cutting the healthy years with grandchildren from 28 to 15. Longer life does not make up the loss of years when the grandmother is healthy enough to supply money and care. Delay is most pronounced among the college-educated, with reproductive-control access accounting for over half the fall in grandmotherhood by age 55 among White college graduates. When a daughter times her first birth to maximize her own payoff, the loss her choice imposes on her mother is external to her calculation. We call this the Rotten Adult Kid Problem. Delay raises both the price of a grandmother's care and wealth per grandchild.

中文摘要

我们展示了生育延迟如何跨代复合，从而提高首次成为祖母的年龄。在1960年的初育时间表下，成为祖母的中位年龄为44岁。在2024年的时间表下，这一年龄为57岁，使与孙辈共度的健康年限从28年缩短到15年。更长的寿命并不能弥补祖母健康到足以提供金钱和照护的年限损失。延迟在受过大学教育者中最为显著，在白人大学毕业生中，生殖控制的可及性解释了55岁前成为祖母比例下降的一半以上。当女儿为最大化自身收益而安排初育时间时，她的选择给母亲带来的损失是其计算之外的外部性。我们称之为“烂成年子女问题”。延迟既提高了祖母照护的价格，也提高了每个孙辈对应的财富。
