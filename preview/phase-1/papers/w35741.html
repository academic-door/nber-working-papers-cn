Diamond-Mirrlees meets Sims: Optimal Taxation with Rational Inattention

Diamond-Mirrlees与Sims相遇：理性疏忽下的最优税收

George-Marios Angeletos, Matias Bayas-Erazo

公共财政与税收 未指明区域

Abstract

We study optimal commodity taxation when consumers are rationally inattentive and the planner internalizes their attention costs. In our setting, consumers may underreact to taxes, may allocate attention unevenly across goods, and may display sparse behavior or mental accounting. Our main result is that this need not change tax design: in a benchmark, optimal taxes satisfy the same sufficient-statistics formulas as in classical public finance, regardless of the extent and endogeneity of inattention. Away from this benchmark, we offer a dual rationale for state-dependent taxes and a new lens on tax salience; but we still find no room for the adjustments emphasized in recent work on behavioral public finance.

中文摘要

我们研究当消费者具有理性疏忽且规划者将消费者的注意力成本内部化时的最优商品税。在我们的设定中，消费者可能对税收反应不足，可能在不同商品之间不均匀地分配注意力，并可能表现出稀疏行为或心理账户。我们的主要结果是，这不一定改变税收设计：在一个基准情形中，无论疏忽的程度和内生性如何，最优税收满足与经典公共财政相同的充分统计量公式。偏离这一基准时，我们为状态依赖税收提供了双重理由，并为税收显著性提供了新视角；但我们仍然没有发现近期行为公共财政研究中强调的调整空间。
