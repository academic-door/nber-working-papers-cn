Asset Privatization as Intergenerational Redistribution

资产私有化作为代际再分配

Kaiji Chen, Hanming Fang, Yang Tang

公共财政与税收 中国相关

Abstract

Rapid economic growth creates large differences in lifetime incomes across generations. This paper examines the intergenerational redistribution generated by subsidized access to appreciating public assets in rapidly growing economies. We show that providing incumbent generations with subsidized access to these assets before future growth is fully capitalized into market values gives them an early claim on subsequent economic growth, thereby redistributing resources from future to incumbent generations. In the context of housing privatization, subsequent capital gains on privatized housing enable homeowners to trade up, further amplifying housing demand, house prices, and intergenerational redistribution. We evaluate the early ownership and capital gains channels of asset-based redistribution in a quantitative equilibrium model calibrated to China's housing privatization. Relative to more standard pension-based redistribution, we show that asset-based redistribution delivers higher welfare for future cohorts while substantially reducing long-run fiscal burdens once economic growth unexpectedly slows.

中文摘要

快速经济增长造成了各代人终身收入的巨大差异。本文考察快速增长经济体中，通过补贴性获得增值公共资产所产生的代际再分配。我们表明，在未来增长完全资本化到市场价值之前，向当代人提供对这些资产的补贴性获得机会，使他们提前获得对随后经济增长的索取权，从而将资源从未来世代再分配给当代世代。在住房私有化的背景下，私有化住房随后的资本利得使房主能够换购升级，进一步放大住房需求、房价和代际再分配。我们在一个校准到中国住房私有化的定量均衡模型中评估资产型再分配的早期所有权渠道和资本利得渠道。相对于更标准的基于养老金的再分配，我们表明，一旦经济增长意外放缓，资产型再分配为未来队列带来更高福利，同时大幅降低长期财政负担。
