Buying a Station or Relaxing a Constraint? Spectrum Repacking and Dirac's Conjecture

购买电视台还是放松约束？频谱重打包与狄拉克猜想

Alex Chan

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

Spectrum repacking auctions can restore feasibility along two margins: retiring a station via buyout or relaxing a pairwise engineering restriction. I represent stations as vertices in a network and interference restrictions as edges between them, and so station buyouts delete vertices and engineering waivers relax edges. This paper shows that buying out any one station can reduce the number of channels needed, even though relaxing any single interference restriction cannot. The smallest feasible number of channels is $k$ . For every $k\geq4$, I show that there is a network in which removing any station saves one channel, while relaxing any single pairwise restriction does not. This main result follows directly from Dirac’s half-century-old conjecture on vertex-critical graphs, whose chromatic number falls after any vertex is removed. By constructing the missing counterexample at $k=4$ and combining it with earlier results for $k\geq5$, I show that at every $k\geq4$, some $k$-vertex-critical graph has no critical edge, resolving Dirac's conjecture. Market designers auditing feasibility link-by-link systematically can mistake combinatorial complementarity for remedy failure, overlooking station buyouts that unlock the clearing target.

中文摘要

频谱重打包拍卖可以通过两条边际恢复可行性：通过买断让电视台退役，或放松成对的工程限制。我将电视台表示为网络中的顶点，将干扰限制表示为它们之间的边，因此电视台买断删除顶点，工程豁免放松边。本文表明，买断任何一个电视台都可以减少所需频道数量，尽管放松任何单一干扰限制不能。最小可行频道数为 $k$。对于每个 $k\geq4$，我表明存在一个网络，其中移除任何电视台都能节省一个频道，而放松任何单一成对限制则不能。这一主要结果直接来自狄拉克半个世纪前关于顶点临界图的猜想，即移除任何顶点后色数下降。通过在 $k=4$ 时构造缺失的反例，并将其与 $k\geq5$ 的早期结果相结合，我表明在每个 $k\geq4$ 时，某个 $k$-顶点临界图没有临界边，从而解决了狄拉克猜想。逐边审计可行性的市场设计者可能会系统性地将组合互补性误认为补救失败，从而忽视了解锁清算目标的电视台买断。
