Universities as Incubators of Mass Uprising

大学作为大规模起义的孵化器

Sultan Mehmood, Ahmed Mushfiq Mobarak

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 孟加拉国、美国、越南

Abstract

We link high-frequency geocoded protest data during Bangladesh’s 2024 Monsoon Revolution, Facebook measures of protest activity and social connectivity, university enrollment and demographics to establish: (1) student-linked protests precede off-campus mobilization; (2) student concentration predicts both on- and off-campus protest, while non-student youth, educated adults, urbanization, and locations of “placebo” universities authorized but not yet operating do not; and (3) social connectivity to student protest locations predicts off-campus diffusion one week later. Universities appear to incubate mass uprising by making dissent visible, coordinated, and capable of spreading beyond campus. Such patterns are also evident in U.S. Vietnam War protests and in global data. That universities facilitate broader political mobilization can explain why governments often seek to restrict student activism.

中文摘要

我们将孟加拉国2024年季风革命期间的高频地理编码抗议数据、Facebook对抗议活动和社会连通性的测量、大学入学人数和人口统计数据联系起来，以确立以下结论：(1) 与学生相关的抗议先于校外动员；(2) 学生集中度同时预测校内和校外抗议，而非学生青年、受过教育的成年人、城市化以及已获授权但尚未运营的“安慰剂”大学所在地则不能预测；(3) 与学生抗议地点的社会连通性预测一周后的校外扩散。大学似乎通过使异议可见、协调并具备向校园外传播的能力来孵化大规模起义。这种模式在美国越战抗议和全球数据中也很明显。大学促进更广泛的政治动员，可以解释为什么政府往往试图限制学生行动主义。

Green Tastes, Beliefs, and Portfolios: Evidence from Synthetic Investors

绿色偏好、信念与投资组合：来自合成投资者的证据

Xindi He, Lubos Pastor

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We use LLMs as synthetic survey respondents to elicit green tastes, return beliefs, portfolio allocations, and investment motives from 480 retail and 60 institutional investor identities. Compared with retail, institutions report weaker tastes yet greener portfolio tilts, citing outside pressure. A greener tilt therefore need not indicate a stronger taste for greenness. Retail tastes depend mostly on political affiliation, institutional tastes on type and geography. Beliefs differ across the three ESG dimensions, with high-governance portfolios expected to outperform. We reconstruct survey responses under 2014--2025 information environments using date-masked information modules shuffled across years. Tastes vary little over time, but beliefs about green portfolios become more pessimistic after 2020. The two reconstructed series relate to different real-world data: tastes to measures of green demand, beliefs to measures of attention.

中文摘要

我们使用LLM作为合成调查受访者，从480个零售投资者和60个机构投资者身份中引出绿色偏好、回报信念、投资组合配置和投资动机。与零售投资者相比，机构投资者报告的偏好更弱，但投资组合的绿色倾斜更强，并 citing 外部压力。因此，更绿的倾斜不一定表明对绿色有更强的偏好。零售投资者的偏好主要取决于政治归属，机构投资者的偏好则取决于类型和地理位置。信念在三个ESG维度上存在差异，高治理投资组合预计将表现更优。我们使用按年份打乱、日期掩码的信息模块，重建2014—2025年信息环境下的调查回应。偏好随时间变化很小，但对绿色投资组合的信念在2020年后变得更加悲观。两个重建序列与不同的现实世界数据相关：偏好与绿色需求指标相关，信念与关注度指标相关。

Beyond the Numbers: Professional Forecasters’ Narratives about Inflation and Stock Market Performance

超越数字：专业预测者关于通胀与股市表现叙事

Christian Conrad, Julius Theodor Schoelkopf, Michael Weber, Frank Brueckbauer

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Professional forecasters' heterogeneous narratives about how inflation affects stock markets in 2023 rationalize their high disagreement regarding quantitative expectations for 12-month-ahead inflation and stock returns in December 2022. Professional forecasters causally update their return expectations in heterogeneous directions in response to information about the inflation outlook, depending on the narrative they entertain. Moreover, the narratives also affect their asset allocation in a hypothetical portfolio-choice experiment. Providing common signals does not necessarily lead to convergence of beliefs if agents subscribe to different narratives.

中文摘要

专业预测者关于2023年通胀如何影响股市的异质性叙事，解释了他们在2022年12月对12个月后通胀和股票回报的定量预期为何存在高度分歧。专业预测者会根据其所持叙事，对通胀前景信息做出异质性方向上的因果性回报预期更新。此外，这些叙事还影响他们在假设性投资组合选择实验中的资产配置。如果代理人认同不同叙事，提供共同信号并不必然导致信念趋同。

Demand-Driven Inflation

需求驱动型通胀

Domenico Giannone, Giorgio Primiceri

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Post-covid inflation was predominantly driven by unexpectedly strong demand forces---both in the United States and in the Euro Area. We establish this result using a sequence of structural VARs with progressively richer specifications, interpret it through the lens of standard AD-AS analysis and an estimated DSGE model, and provide independent support for this conclusion using survey forecasts and real-time data. These demand forces reflected a combination of surprisingly robust pent-up demand following the pandemic restrictions, stronger-than-expected expansionary fiscal policies, and an unusually accommodative monetary stance. Together, they mitigated the recessionary effects of adverse supply shocks and supported the recovery, albeit at the cost of higher inflation. The economic intuition is simple: When a central bank responds forcefully to inflationary pressures, as the Fed and the ECB have done in recent decades, the aggregate demand curve is relatively flat, so supply disturbances move real activity much more than inflation. A large inflation surge, therefore, requires a shift or rotation of the aggregate demand curve.

中文摘要

后疫情时期的通胀主要由意外强劲的需求力量驱动——无论在美国还是在欧元区都是如此。我们通过一系列逐步丰富设定的结构VAR确立这一结果，并通过标准AD-AS分析和估计的DSGE模型加以解释，同时利用调查预测和实时数据为这一结论提供独立支持。这些需求力量反映了以下因素的组合：疫情限制后意外强劲的积压需求、强于预期的扩张性财政政策，以及异常宽松的货币政策立场。它们共同缓解了不利供给冲击的衰退效应并支撑了复苏，尽管代价是更高的通胀。经济直觉很简单：当央行对通胀压力作出有力反应时，正如美联储和欧洲央行近几十年来所做的那样，总需求曲线相对平坦，因此供给扰动对实际活动的影响远大于对通胀的影响。因此，大规模通胀飙升需要总需求曲线的移动或旋转。

What Drives Censorship on College Campuses?

是什么驱动了大学校园中的自我审查？

Peter Arcidiacono, Cory Dowd, Matthew Lilley

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

There has been widespread concern about self-censorship on university campuses, and especially that conservative voices are being stifled. Using survey data from a private elite college, we show that self-censorship is not simply about being conservative: it is students with the most moderate views who self-censor the most. This pattern is associated with confidence and salience — students who are more confident in their views or who see an issue as especially important tend to hold more extreme views and to self-censor less. At the same time, conservatives self-censor at higher rates than liberals, and the same individual is more likely to self-censor on the views where they are relatively more conservative than on those where they are relatively more liberal; conservatives are also far more likely than liberals to cite concerns about social exclusion as a reason for self-censoring, and report self-censoring less outside the university community. Groups perceived as marginalized self-censor less than majority groups, and especially so on issues salient to them (e.g., Black students on BLM).

中文摘要

人们普遍担心大学校园中的自我审查，尤其是保守派声音受到压制。使用一所私立精英学院的调查数据，我们表明自我审查并不只是关于保守派身份：自我审查最多的是观点最温和的学生。这一模式与信心和显著性相关——对自己观点更有信心或认为某个问题特别重要的学生往往持有更极端的观点，且自我审查更少。与此同时，保守派的自我审查率高于自由派，同一个人在其相对更保守的观点上比在其相对更自由的观点上更可能自我审查；保守派也比自由派更可能将对社会排斥的担忧列为自我审查的原因，并报告在大学社区之外自我审查更少。被视为边缘化的群体比多数群体自我审查更少，尤其是在对其显著的问题上（例如黑人在BLM问题上）。

Coordinated R&D Programs and the Creation of New Industries

协调性研发项目与新产业的创造

Daniel P. Gross, Maria P. Roche

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

Large, mission-oriented R&D efforts sometimes generate substantial technological advances, but their commercial impacts vary. This paper uses one such effort—the U.S. World War II radar program—to explore how the outputs of such programs may contribute to later commercial development. Using archival records together with data on firm entry, patenting, and technical labor flows, we reconstruct the organization and outputs of wartime radar R&D, document the rapid postwar growth of a civilian industry, and evaluate which outputs were used in subsequent commercial activity. Codified knowledge (in publications and patents) diffused broadly, embodied knowledge (trained personnel) diffused more locally, and the component-system industrial architecture of the wartime program was mirrored in firms' postwar commercial positions. The evidence points to specific channels through which coordinated technology development programs can give shape to emerging high-tech industries.

中文摘要

大型、使命导向的研发努力有时会产生重大技术进展，但其商业影响各不相同。本文利用一项此类努力——美国二战雷达计划——来探讨此类项目的产出如何促进后来的商业发展。我们利用档案记录以及企业进入、专利和技术劳动力流动数据，重建战时雷达研发的组织和产出，记录一个民用产业的战后快速增长，并评估哪些产出被用于后续商业活动。编码化知识（出版物和专利中的知识）广泛扩散， embodied knowledge（受训人员）在更局部范围内扩散，而战时计划的组件—系统产业架构反映在企业战后的商业定位中。证据指向协调性技术开发项目能够塑造新兴高科技产业的特定渠道。

Fixed/Floating Interest Rate Parity

固定/浮动利率平价

Matthias Fleckenstein, Sven Klingler, Francis A. Longstaff

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

We present a new no-arbitrage relation which we denote as fixed/floating interest rate parity. This relation can be viewed as a domestic version of covered interest rate parity. We test this relation using fixed-rate bonds and floating-rate notes in the multi-trillion U.S. agency debt market and document significant violations. These violations are largely unrelated to other arbitrage spreads and measures of intermediary constraints. Instead, they are closely tied to the behavior of money market funds which hold the vast majority of agency floating-rate notes. Our results identify clientele effects as a source of persistent mispricing.

中文摘要

我们提出一个新的无套利关系，称之为固定/浮动利率平价。该关系可视为抛补利率平价的国内版本。我们使用规模达数万亿美元的美国机构债券市场中的固定利率债券和浮动利率票据检验这一关系，并记录了显著的偏离。这些偏离在很大程度上与其他套利利差和中介约束指标无关。相反，它们与持有绝大多数机构浮动利率票据的货币市场基金的行为密切相关。我们的结果将客户群效应识别为持续错误定价的一个来源。

Manufacturing Renaissance Meets Time-Inconsistent Tariffs

制造业复兴遇上时间不一致的关税

Christoph Boehm, Andrei A. Levchenko, Wei Xiang

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We analyze optimal tariffs in a dynamic version of the Venables (1987) framework with a time-to-build lag in firm creation. In this environment, trade protection is driven by the desire to increase firm creation at home at the expense of the foreign trade partner. When firm creation takes time, the benefits of protection accrue in the future, whereas the costs in the form of higher consumer prices occur immediately. Under certain parameterizations, a policymaker that can commit to a full time path of tariffs leaves trade unrestricted at date 0, and announces high tariffs coming on at a pre-specified future period. But there is a time inconsistency: the policymaker's short-run incentive is not to impose contemporaneous tariffs due to the adverse effect on prices. Thus, lack of commitment to future tariffs can preserve free(er) trade and derail a manufacturing renaissance.

中文摘要

我们在Venables（1987）框架的动态版本中分析最优关税，其中企业创建存在建设时滞。在这一环境中，贸易保护由以外国贸易伙伴为代价增加本国企业创建的愿望驱动。当企业创建需要时间时，保护的好处在未来实现，而更高消费价格形式的成本则立即发生。在某些参数化下，能够承诺完整关税时间路径的政策制定者在第0期不限制贸易，并宣布在预先指定的未来时期实施高关税。但存在时间不一致性：由于对价格的不利影响，政策制定者的短期激励是不征收当期关税。因此，缺乏对未来关税的承诺可以维持（更）自由的贸易，并使制造业复兴脱轨。

The Global Office: How Globalisation’s Frontier is Shifting from Factories to Offices

全球办公室：全球化的前沿如何从工厂转向办公室

Richard Baldwin

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

This paper argues that globalisation’s frontier has shifted from manufacturing GVCs to services GVCs: from the Global Factory to the Global Office. While the Factory remains larger in gross trade, the Office is growing much faster. A heatmap of trade in intermediates shows that the Factory is bloc-shaped and dominated by China, whereas the Office is star-shaped and dominated by America. Gravity estimates show that distance matters less for Office exports, but time zones matter more: each hour of time-zone separation reduces Office exports by roughly 4%, while having no effect on Factory exports. Importantly, all of the world’s export-linked net job creation between 2008 and 2022 occurred in digitally delivered services rather than manufacturing, and trade-linked service jobs command higher average pay globally. The Factory-to-Office shift is also reshaping development. Most emerging economies have seen export-led growth tilt towards services, but their service exports are not yet large enough to drive overall GDP growth the way manufacturing did historically. The big picture is that globalisation’s second unbundling is running out of steam even as its third unbundling is powering ahead. The paper, which raises more questions than it answers, closes with the conjecture that the future of trade is services.

中文摘要

本文认为，全球化的前沿已从制造业全球价值链转向服务业全球价值链：从全球工厂转向全球办公室。虽然工厂在总贸易中仍然更大，但办公室增长得快得多。中间品贸易热力图显示，工厂呈块状形态并由中国主导，而办公室呈星状形态并由美国主导。引力估计显示，距离对办公室出口的重要性较低，但时区更重要：每增加1小时时区差异使办公室出口减少约4%，而对工厂出口没有影响。重要的是，2008年至2022年间全球所有与出口相关的净就业创造都发生在数字化交付服务而非制造业中，且与贸易相关的服务岗位在全球范围内平均薪酬更高。从工厂到办公室的转变也在重塑发展。大多数新兴经济体的出口导向型增长已向服务业倾斜，但其服务出口尚不足以像制造业历史上那样推动整体GDP增长。总体图景是，全球化的第二次拆分正在失去动力，而其第三次拆分正在加速推进。本文提出的问题多于答案，最后以贸易的未来是服务业的猜想作结。

How Different are Different Differences-in-Differences? A Large-Scale Meta-Analysis

不同的双重差分有多大不同？一项大规模元分析

Peter Hull

NBER 工作论文 · AI 与数字经济 · 未指明区域

Abstract

I use AI-assisted replication to assess the empirical importance of negative weights in two-way fixed effects (TWFE) estimation, drawing on 1,346 staggered-adoption specifications from 108 economics papers. TWFE estimates are highly correlated with estimates from modern difference-in-differences methods, with observed sign reversals in 10.8–14.5% of comparisons and greater precision for TWFE. Hierarchical models that account for estimation noise imply a 91–98% correlation between estimands and a 0.6–2.1% probability of obtaining a statistically significant TWFE estimate with the opposite sign from the modern estimand. These findings suggest negative weights may be a less serious problem in practice, and illustrate how AI can support large-scale empirical assessments of new econometric methods.

Affirmative Action, Faculty Productivity and Caste Interactions: Evidence from Engineering Colleges in India

平权行动、教师生产率与种姓互动：来自印度工程学院的证据

Robert W. Fairlie, Saurabh Khanna, Prashant Loyalka, Gagandeep S. Sachdeva

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 印度

Abstract

Affirmative action programs are often criticized because of concerns that they result in lower worker productivity and efficiency losses. We study the relative productivity of workers benefiting from an aggressive affirmative action policy in a setting where hiring constraints are mandated upon employers and are typically likely to bind. In India, colleges are required to reserve approximately 50 percent of faculty hires for individuals from disadvantaged caste and social class groups. We collect and analyze data from a nationally representative sample of 50 engineering and technology colleges in India, some of which randomly assign students to classrooms. We find that reservation category faculty have lower levels of education, lower professorial ranks and fewer years of experience in academia than general category faculty who are not hired through reservations. Yet, even with lower qualifications, we do not detect meaningful differences in instructional quality between reservation category faculty and general category faculty across a wide range of measures that include course grades, follow-on course grades, standardized test scores, dropout, attendance, graduate school plans, and graduation. In fact, we find that, at least for immediate effects on course grades, students taught by reservation category faculty perform slightly better than students taught by general category faculty. We also estimate “teacher-like-me” effects of reservation category faculty on the relative course performance and longer-term outcomes of reservation category students and cannot reject the hypothesis of null effects. Furthermore, even in the face of potential discrimination and resentment against faculty hiring quotas, general category students perform slightly better in classrooms taught by reservation category faculty than general category faculty. Finally, estimates of differences between reservation category and general category faculty in several measures of research productivity and administrative service are small in magnitude and not statistically different from zero. The findings have implications for the heated debates over affirmative action programs found in many countries around the world and in India which is now the largest country in the world.

Unraveling the Cobweb of Global Imbalances: Drivers, Vulnerabilities and Adjustment Scenarios

解开全球失衡的蛛网：驱动因素、脆弱性与调整情景

Stefan Avdjiev, Kristin Forbes, Tsvetelina Nenova, Marjorie Santos

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

This paper analyses the evolution and drivers of the stock of global imbalances in order to assess associated vulnerabilities and potential adjustment scenarios. These imbalances have recently increased sharply, with a deterioration in the US net international investment position (NIIP) mirrored by NIIP improvements in most other major economies. We decompose these NIIP changes into their proximate drivers for a sample of 28 economies and find that financial factors (including valuation changes and investment income flows) are the primary drivers of global imbalances at both short and long horizons, while trade also plays a significant role (especially at long horizons). Valuation gains on international investment positions have supported economic growth in many countries since 2010, while also increasing their vulnerability to financial market adjustments. To assess the implications and potential risks, we simulate the spillovers through this cobweb of global imbalances from: (i) a sharp dollar depreciation, (ii) a repricing of equity markets, (iii) higher interest rates, and (iv) a halving of trade imbalances. These scenarios highlight the large international spillovers that would be associated with any reduction in global imbalances, the heterogeneous impact of various rebalancing mechanisms on individual countries and the limits of popular proposals for reducing these imbalances. The persistence of global imbalances underscores the importance of building resilience to the large spillovers that can occur through this cobweb of international exposures.

Can Home Visits Re-Engage Chronically Absent Students? Evidence from the LEAP Program

家访能否重新吸引长期缺勤学生？来自LEAP项目的证据

Eric Brunner, Shangyue Jiang, Stephen Ross

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

Beginning in the summer of 2021, the state of Connecticut launched the Learner Engagement and Attendance Program (LEAP) where home visits by school personnel were conducted with students identified as chronically absent from Connecticut’s 15 most disadvantaged school districts. Home visits were designed to connect students and their families directly with teachers to foster strong connections. We use student-level data from 2021–22 through 2023-24 to estimate event studies of the effect of LEAP on student attendance, chronic absenteeism, disciplinary incidents, and student achievement. Utilizing models that address selection into treatment timing over student observable and unobservable attributes, we find that students who received the LEAP intervention experienced approximately a 4 to 5 percentage point increase in attendance rates, a 13 to 15 percentage point decline in chronic absenteeism, and a 3 to 5 percentage point decline in the number of disciplinary incidents. Notably, initial event studies indicated increasing treatment effects over time, but after correcting for selection on unobservables treatment effects appeared constant over time. These effects were broad based, with similar impacts by gender, race and ethnicity, and free or reduced-price lunch status. These attendance and discipline results provide suggest that the LEAP intervention increased student noncognitive skills.
