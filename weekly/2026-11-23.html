Light Pollution: Effects on Sleep, Work, and Mental Health

光污染：对睡眠、工作与心理健康的影响

Joan Costa-i-Font, Janet Currie, Soodeh Saberian

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 加拿大、哥伦比亚、英国

Abstract

This study examines the impact of artificial streetlights on sleep, as well as downstream effects on mental health, absenteeism, and physical activity. We exploit variation resulting from the replacement of traditional sodium streetlights with light-emitting diode (LED) lights in Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada. Using data from the Canadian Community Health Survey, we examine discontinuities in difficulty sleeping at the municipal border with Vancouver, which did not adopt new lighting. We also estimate a within-Burnaby model exploiting the rollout of LED lights across neighborhoods, and a difference-in-differences design using matched Vancouver and Burnaby neighborhoods. The estimates show that conversion reduces difficulty sleeping immediately, and that improvements in mental health, absenteeism, and physical activity follow with a lag of two to three years. The largest gains occur with warmer-toned LEDs that more closely mimic natural light.

中文摘要

本研究考察人工路灯对睡眠的影响，以及对心理健康、缺勤和身体活动的下游效应。我们利用加拿大不列颠哥伦比亚省本拿比市将传统钠灯路灯替换为发光二极管（LED）灯所产生的外生变异。使用加拿大社区健康调查数据，我们考察与未采用新照明的温哥华市交界处的入睡困难断点。我们还估计了一个利用LED灯在各社区推广的本拿比市内模型，以及一个使用匹配的温哥华和本拿比社区的双重差分设计。估计结果显示，转换立即减少了入睡困难，而心理健康、缺勤和身体活动的改善在2至3年的滞后期后出现。最大收益出现在更接近自然光的暖色调LED灯上。

Supply-Side Drivers of Long-Acting Reversible Contraceptives: Evidence from Medicaid Beneficiaries

长效可逆避孕药具的供给侧驱动因素：来自医疗补助受益人的证据

Maya Rossin-Slater, Natalia Serna, Lin-Tung Tsai

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

U.S. federal law mandates that most insurers offer contraceptives for free to patients, yet nearly one in three women of reproductive age still report inadequate access to contraceptive services. With demand-side affordability barriers largely removed, supply-side factors—the actions of healthcare facilities and providers—may be critical in explaining this unmet need. This paper examines supply-side determinants of long-acting reversible contraceptive (LARC) use among Medicaid beneficiaries, a population for whom both LARCs and oral contraceptives (OCs) are covered at zero out-of-pocket cost. Unlike OCs, LARCs require clinician insertion and upfront device stocking, making them especially vulnerable to supply-side forces. Using national Medicaid claims data from 2015–2019 and a movers design that separates place-based from patient-based variation, we find that local supply-side factors explain 58.5 percent of geographic variation in LARC use—substantially more than the 37.7 percent explained for OCs. We then estimate the causal effects of a supply-side policy that substantially lowers the cost of stocking LARCs for safety-net providers: the 340B Drug Pricing Program. Leveraging the staggered timing of program participation across clinics in an event-study design, we find that the arrival of the first participating clinic in an enrollee's ZIP code increases LARC use by 4.3 percent without displacing OC use or increasing LARC removals. Together, these findings identify the supply side as both a key source of unmet contraceptive need and a promising target for policy to improve access to the most effective forms of contraception.

中文摘要

美国联邦法律规定大多数保险公司必须向患者免费提供避孕药具，但近三分之一育龄妇女仍报告避孕服务获取不足。在需求侧可负担性障碍基本消除的情况下，供给侧因素——医疗机构和提供者的行为——可能是解释这一未满足需求的关键。本文考察医疗补助受益人中长效可逆避孕药具（LARC）使用的供给侧决定因素，该人群的LARC和口服避孕药（OC）均以零自付费用覆盖。与OC不同，LARC需要临床医生置入和预先设备储备，使其特别容易受到供给侧力量的影响。使用2015—2019年全国医疗补助索赔数据以及将地点变异与患者变异分离的迁移者设计，我们发现当地供给侧因素解释了LARC使用地理变异的58.5%——大幅高于OC的37.7%。然后我们估计了一项大幅降低安全网提供者储备LARC成本的供给侧政策的因果效应：340B药品定价计划。利用事件研究设计中各诊所参与计划时间的交错性，我们发现参保人邮政编码区域内首家参与诊所的出现使LARC使用增加4.3%，且未取代OC使用或增加LARC取出。总之，这些发现将供给侧确定为未满足避孕需求的关键来源以及改善最有效避孕形式可及性的有前景的政策目标。

Beyond the First Best: Welfare and OPEC Market Power

超越最优：福利与OPEC市场势力

John Asker, Allan Collard-Wexler, Charlotte De Cannière, Jan De Loecker

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We introduce an empirical framework to quantify the welfare impact of market power, through coordinated production decisions by a cartel, while allowing for other distortions. We contrast two novel concepts – the marginal and the infra-marginal welfare loss – to a naive approach that fails to recognize the existence of other distortions operating alongside market power. We apply the framework to measure the impact of market power of OPEC in the global crude oil market. We leverage unique micro data on cost and production to quantify the deadweight loss and productive inefficiency due to the OPEC cartel. From 1970 onward, the naive approach implies that OPEC generated 2.2 trillion dollars of welfare losses, two-thirds from productive inefficiency and one-third from deadweight loss. Eliminating cost-minimization violations outside the cartel shrinks losses to around 0.7 trillion dollars, indicating that other frictions in the oil market are complementary to OPEC’s market power.

中文摘要

我们引入一个实证框架，通过卡特尔协调生产决策来量化市场势力的福利影响，同时允许其他扭曲存在。我们将两个新概念——边际福利损失和超边际福利损失——与未能认识到市场势力之外其他扭曲存在的朴素方法进行对比。我们将该框架应用于衡量OPEC在全球原油市场中的市场势力影响。我们利用关于成本和生产的独特微观数据来量化OPEC卡特尔导致的无谓损失和生产无效率。自1970年以来，朴素方法意味着OPEC造成了2.2万亿美元的福利损失，其中三分之二来自生产无效率，三分之一来自无谓损失。消除卡特尔之外的违反成本最小化行为将损失缩减至约0.7万亿美元，表明石油市场中的其他摩擦与OPEC市场势力具有互补性。

CFOs Meet LLMs

CFO与大型语言模型

John R. Graham, Campbell R. Harvey, Manish Jha

NBER 工作论文 · AI 与数字经济 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Business sentiment is closely watched, but measurement is slow and costly: surveys reach a limited number of firms, arrive periodically, and take time to field and compile. We show that large language models hold the potential to address some of these shortcomings. We prompt an LLM to role-play as the CFO of a specific company on a specific date, for every public-company CFO who responded to the Duke–Federal Reserve CFO Survey from 2002- 2025. We find that LLM-generated forecasts reasonably match the cross-section of CFO expectations, with accuracy increasing with the amount of information provided to the LLM. We discuss the potential as well as the hurdles to using digital twins to mimic individual decision-makers.

中文摘要

商业情绪受到密切关注，但测量缓慢且成本高昂：调查仅覆盖有限数量的企业，定期进行，且需要时间实地开展和汇总。我们表明大型语言模型有潜力解决其中一些不足。对于2002—2025年期间回应杜克—美联储CFO调查的每一位上市公司CFO，我们提示大型语言模型在特定日期角色扮演特定公司的CFO。我们发现大型语言模型生成的预测与CFO预期的横截面合理匹配，准确性随着提供给大型语言模型的信息量增加而提高。我们讨论了使用数字孪生模仿个体决策者的潜力以及障碍。

Chips & Tokens at the Fault Lines: Two Stacks in One Fragmenting World

断层线上的芯片与代币：一个碎片化世界中的两个技术栈

Fabio Natalucci, Alessandro Rebucci

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

The United States and China have each built the capacity to inflict serious economic damage on each other. This paper argues that the resulting mutual vulnerability now constrains both sides and prevents further economic decoupling. The paper maps this contest across two stacks: the real economy stack of computing, semiconductors and rare earths, and the financial stack of dollar dominance and digital finance. On the real side, an analysis of global earnings call reports, run through large language models, suggests that this mutual vulnerability was already shaping the global corporate narrative in 2025. On the financial side, the paper argues that digital finance could tip this balance either way. Portable U.S. dollar-denominated digital assets could deepen U.S. dollar dominance and erode China's capital controls, tilting the balance toward Washington and inviting more fragmentation. A drift of transactions onto offshore rails like Tether could do the opposite, eroding Washington’s enforcement power behind sanctions and, counterintuitively, restraining it. Both the U.S. and China, meanwhile, run internally inconsistent policies. The U.S. is closing its door to trade while keeping an open financial account, and China is doing the reverse. These configurations are ultimately unsustainable and could undermine the arsenals that sustain the equilibrium.

中文摘要

美国和中国都已建立起对彼此造成严重经济损害的能力。本文认为，由此产生的相互脆弱性现在约束着双方，并阻止进一步的经济脱钩。本文在两个技术栈上描绘这场竞争：计算、半导体和稀土构成的实体经济栈，以及美元主导地位和数字金融构成的金融栈。在实体方面，通过大型语言模型对全球财报电话会议报告的分析表明，这种相互脆弱性在2025年已经在塑造全球企业叙事。在金融方面，本文认为数字金融可能使这一平衡向任一方向倾斜。可携带的美元计价数字资产可能加深美元主导地位并侵蚀中国的资本管制，使平衡向华盛顿倾斜并招致更多碎片化。交易向Tether等离岸轨道漂移则可能产生相反效果，侵蚀华盛顿制裁背后的执法权力，并且反直觉地约束它。与此同时，美国和中国都运行着内部不一致的政策。美国在关闭贸易大门的同时保持金融账户开放，中国则相反。这些配置最终不可持续，并可能削弱维持均衡的武器库。

Are Republicans and Democrats Different Taxpayers? Evidence From Property Tax Appeals

共和党人和民主党人是不同的纳税人吗？来自房产税上诉的证据

Brad C. Nathan, Ricardo Perez-Truglia, Alejandro Zentner

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

Democrats and Republicans express some sharply different views about tax policy, but whether these ideological divisions translate into partisan differences in taxpayer behavior is far less clear. We study this question in the context of a common form of tax avoidance: property tax appeals. In a survey, we find that Americans, on average, believe Republican homeowners are more likely to appeal their property taxes than Democratic homeowners with similarly valued homes. Yet linking administrative property tax records from Dallas County with voting records reveals that this stereotype is not borne out in practice: the partisan gap in appeals is small, both in absolute terms (0.29 percentage points) and relative to several demographic gaps in appeals we use as benchmarks. Similar appeal rates could nonetheless conceal differences in motives. Using quasi-experimental and field experimental evidence, we find that Democrats and Republicans appeal for broadly similar reasons. In sum, despite differing tax policy preferences and public perceptions about their behavior, Democrats and Republicans are strikingly similar in both their propensity to appeal their property taxes and their underlying motives.

中文摘要

民主党人和共和党人对税收政策表达了一些截然不同的观点，但这些意识形态分歧是否转化为纳税人行为上的党派差异则远不清楚。我们在房产税上诉这一常见避税形式的背景下研究这个问题。在一项调查中，我们发现美国人平均认为共和党房主比拥有类似价值房屋的民主党房主更可能对其房产税提出上诉。然而，将达拉斯县的行政房产税记录与投票记录关联后发现，这种刻板印象在实践中并未得到证实：上诉中的党派差距很小，无论是绝对值（0.29个百分点）还是相对于我们用作基准的若干人口统计上诉差距而言。相似的上诉率仍可能掩盖动机差异。使用准实验和实地实验证据，我们发现民主党人和共和党人上诉的原因大体相似。总之，尽管税收政策偏好不同且公众对其行为的看法不同，民主党人和共和党人在上诉房产税的倾向及其潜在动机上惊人地相似。

Mortality Effects of the 2025 US Aid Freeze: Evidence from Zambia

2025年美国援助冻结的死亡率效应：来自赞比亚的证据

Torsten Figueiredo Walter, Dean Karlan, Niclas Moneke, Ana Radu, Khozya Zyambo

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国、赞比亚

Abstract

We study the impact of the 2025 sudden freeze then subsequent thaw of US foreign aid on HIV prevention, treatment, infection, and death. In Zambia, HIV-related aid was administered through two agencies. Activities under one were disrupted for several months, and those under the other much less. Comprehensive data from all Zambian health facilities reveal immediate differential reductions in preventive HIV measures that mostly later recovered, followed by more HIV-positive pregnant women, more HIV-exposed births, more opportunistic infections, and more recorded deaths. For example, after one year we observe a 14% [95% CI: 3, 25] increase in HIV-positive pregnant women and a 26% [95% CI: 8, 45] increase in deaths among people living with HIV who were receiving antiretroviral therapy.

中文摘要

我们研究2025年美国对外援助突然冻结及随后解冻对HIV预防、治疗、感染和死亡的影响。在赞比亚，HIV相关援助通过两个机构实施。其中一个机构的活动被中断了数月，而另一个机构的活动受影响小得多。来自赞比亚所有卫生机构的综合数据显示，预防性HIV措施立即出现差异性减少，随后大多恢复，接着出现更多HIV阳性孕妇、更多HIV暴露新生儿、更多机会性感染和更多记录死亡。例如，一年后我们观察到HIV阳性孕妇增加14%[95% CI：3, 25]，接受抗逆转录病毒治疗的HIV感染者死亡增加26%[95% CI：8, 45]。

Shrink Responsibly: Empirical Bayes in Economics

负责任地收缩：经济学中的经验贝叶斯方法

Peter Hull, Michal Kolesár

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Empirical researchers are often interested in the relative causal impact of individuals or institutions such as teachers, schools, hospitals, or neighborhoods. Regression-based estimates of such unit-specific parameters are often noisy; empirical Bayes (eB) methods can help isolate the signal from the estimation noise. This article provides an overview of eB methodology, showing how eB shrinkage can improve compound decisions—such as selecting individuals with the highest causal impacts—even when the working model motivating the shrinkage is misspecified. Not all analyses benefit from eB shrinkage, however, and we discuss which types of causal summaries are best estimated directly and how to do so robustly. We illustrate practical takeaways with a running analysis of public elementary and middle school value-added.

中文摘要

实证研究者常关注个体或机构（如教师、学校、医院或社区）的相对因果影响。基于回归的此类单位特定参数估计往往噪声较大；经验贝叶斯（eB）方法有助于从估计噪声中分离信号。本文概述eB方法，展示即使推动收缩的工作模型设定有误，eB收缩也能改善复合决策——例如选择因果影响最高的个体。然而，并非所有分析都能从eB收缩中受益；我们讨论哪些类型的因果汇总最适合直接估计，以及如何稳健地做到这一点。我们通过对公立中小学增值的连续分析来说明实际要点。

Long-run Uncovered Interest Parity in Emerging Markets

Alessandro Rebucci, Sinem Yagmur Toraman, Giorgio Valente, Nicolò Capirone

Abstract

Democratizing Venture Capital: Tax Incentives and the Geographic Expansion of Startup Investing

风险投资的民主化：税收激励与创业投资的地理扩张

Murillo Campello, Guilherme Junqueira

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Can tax subsidies expand the geographic reach of startup investing? Despite decades of growth, venture capital (VC) remains concentrated in a handful of metropolitan areas, leaving most of the country with limited access to startup financing. We study the 2009–2010 enhancement of the Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS) program, which eliminated federal capital gains taxes on investments in qualifying startups. By raising the payoff to success, the reform made riskier investments more attractive, including entry into unfamiliar markets. This incentive is especially strong for VC funds. Relative to similarly sized metros that already had VC, the reform raised the probability that a previously unserved metro received VC investment in qualifying sectors by 8 to 10 percentage points. There was no comparable increase in sectors excluded from QSBS or among angel investors, who receive the same tax benefit. Startups in newly served markets performed like those in other non-hub markets, created high-wage jobs, and attracted scientific and technical workers, some from established hubs. Our findings suggest that a sufficiently large subsidy can extend VC into markets it had long bypassed, even with no requirement on where startups are located.

中文摘要

税收补贴能扩大创业投资的地理覆盖范围吗？尽管经历了数十年的增长，风险投资（VC）仍集中在少数大都市区，使全国大部分地区获得创业融资的机会有限。我们研究2009–2010年合格小企业股票（QSBS）计划的强化，该计划取消了对合格初创企业投资的联邦资本利得税。通过提高成功的回报，该改革使风险更高的投资更具吸引力，包括进入不熟悉的市场。这种激励对VC基金尤其强烈。相对于已经拥有VC的类似规模大都市区，该改革使先前未获得服务的大都市区在合格行业获得VC投资的概率提高了8至10个百分点。在被排除在QSBS之外的行业或获得相同税收优惠的天使投资者中，没有可比的增长。新获得服务市场中的初创企业表现与其他非中心市场中的企业相似，创造了高工资就业，并吸引了科学和技术工作者，其中一些人来自成熟中心。我们的发现表明，足够大的补贴可以将VC扩展到它长期绕过的市场，即使对初创企业所在地没有要求。

Five Facts About Stablecoins

关于稳定币的五个事实

Lin William Cong, Gordon Y. Liao, Eswar S. Prasad, Tony Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We document five facts about payment stablecoins using wallet-level blockchain data from 2020 to 2025. First, stablecoins serve as a transaction currency: onchain volume exceeds Bitcoin’s despite a much smaller supply, velocity has risen sharply, and transactions have shifted toward low-value transfers. Second, using transaction timing to refine exchange-based attribution, we find activity spread across the Americas, Europe, and Asia and fewer cross-regional flows than the standard method implies. Third, we estimate a structural cash-management model on the holdings data and find that the costs of accessing stablecoin wallets have fallen since 2023. Fourth, stablecoins are becoming the settlement currency of a fast-growing onchain credit market, and their growth appears to reallocate rather than destroy credit. Fifth, smart contracts are involved in about a third of Ethereum stablecoin transactions and over two thirds of volume, and protocols such as x402 demonstrate sub-cent machine-to-machine payments. Stablecoins behave as tokenized money, with implications for financial markets and regulation.

中文摘要

我们使用2020年至2025年的钱包级区块链数据记录了关于支付稳定币的五个事实。第一，稳定币充当交易货币：尽管供应量小得多，链上交易量仍超过比特币，流通速度急剧上升，交易已转向低价值转账。第二，利用交易时间改进基于交易所的归属，我们发现活动分布在美洲、欧洲和亚洲，且跨区域流动比标准方法所暗示的要少。第三，我们在持有数据上估计了一个结构性现金管理模型，发现自2023年以来访问稳定币钱包的成本已经下降。第四，稳定币正成为快速增长的链上信贷市场的结算货币，其增长似乎是在重新分配而非摧毁信贷。第五，智能合约涉及约三分之一的以太坊稳定币交易和超过三分之二的交易量，而x402等协议展示了低于一美分的机器对机器支付。稳定币表现为代币化货币，对金融市场和监管具有影响。

Designing Low Emission Zones: Theory and Evidence

设计低排放区：理论与证据

Nano Barahona, Natalia Fabra, Imelda Imelda, Juan-Pablo Montero

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Low Emission Zones (LEZs), which exclude polluting vehicles from parts of a city, are spreading. Yet how their design affects welfare is poorly understood. Unlike price instruments, LEZs ration access inefficiently, imposing costs on inframarginal drivers of restricted vehicles. In our model, welfare is quasi-convex in LEZ size, yielding bang-bang principles: LEZs should be as large as possible or not implemented at all; cleaner vehicles (e.g., hybrids) could be exempt as transition technologies but later restricted, which may require planner commitment. Using new-car purchases, we find Madrid’s LEZ shifted buyers toward cleaner cars, but the surplus losses of switchers and inframarginal drivers exceeded emission savings.
