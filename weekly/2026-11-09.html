Policy Evaluation Beyond Significance Testing: Evidence from Chicago's ShotSpotter Contract

超越显著性检验的政策评估：来自芝加哥ShotSpotter合同的证据

Terence Chau, Philip J. Cook, Javier Lopez, Jens Ludwig, Alejandro Roemer, Adam K. Soliman

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Program impact estimates that are not statistically significant are often treated as providing little guidance for policy choice, even when economically important effects remain plausible. In such cases, the decision-relevant task is not simply to test whether the effect differs from zero, but to estimate the probability distribution of programmatic effects. The importance of going beyond hypothesis testing is especially clear when evaluations are underpowered relative to the effect needed for benefits to exceed costs. We illustrate this point using Chicago’s contract for ShotSpotter, an acoustical gunshot detection system that reduces police response times and may therefore have improved survival among shooting victims. Using a geographic RD design, we estimate that ShotSpotter reduced the shooting fatality rate by 2.5 percentage points, equivalent to roughly 49 fewer deaths per year. Although the estimate is not statistically significant, its implied benefit far exceeds the cost of the service. To have enough statistical power under the hypothesis testing approach to detect an effect of this size would require 87 years’ worth of Chicago shooting data. We instead implement a decision-relevant perspective using Bayesian analysis, treating the life-saving effect as a random variable. We find positive expected net benefits, with posterior probabilities that benefits exceed costs ranging from 70% under a flat prior and a $0.5 million value of statistical life to 99.6% under a no-harm prior and a $13.6 million value. The results illustrate how reliance on statistical significance can discard decision-relevant evidence when economically meaningful effects are difficult to detect precisely.

中文摘要

统计上不显著的项目影响估计通常被认为难以为政策选择提供指导，即使经济上重要的效应仍然可信。在此类情况下，与决策相关的任务不仅仅是检验效应是否异于零，而是估计项目效应的概率分布。当评估相对于使收益超过成本所需的效应而言统计功效不足时，超越假设检验的重要性尤为明显。我们利用芝加哥的ShotSpotter合同来说明这一点。ShotSpotter是一种声学枪声探测系统，可缩短警察响应时间，因而可能提高枪击受害者的存活率。采用地理断点回归设计，我们估计ShotSpotter使枪击致死率降低了2.5个百分点，相当于每年减少约49例死亡。尽管该估计在统计上不显著，其隐含收益远超服务成本。在假设检验方法下要有足够统计功效检测到这一规模的效应，需要87年的芝加哥枪击数据。我们转而采用贝叶斯分析实施与决策相关的视角，将挽救生命的效果视为随机变量。我们发现预期净收益为正，在平坦先验和50万美元统计生命价值下，收益超过成本的后验概率为70%；在无伤害先验和1360万美元价值下则为99.6%。结果表明，当经济上有意义的效应难以精确检测时，依赖统计显著性可能丢弃与决策相关的证据。

The Main Monetary Policy Shock

主要货币政策冲击

Christoph Boehm, Husang Kim, T. Niklas Kroner

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We propose a novel procedure to identify a main monetary policy (MMP) shock as the linear combination of monetary policy surprises that best explains macroeconomic fluctuations. Implemented with two standard high-frequency yield-curve surprises and the Boehm and Kroner (2024) non-yield shock, the MMP shock explains nontrivial business-cycle variation over the last 30 years, in contrast with prior findings. Moreover, the MMP shock differs markedly from a textbook monetary policy shock: a contractionary shock leaves interest rates mostly unchanged but persistently lowers headline prices and appreciates the dollar, while temporarily tightening financial conditions and depressing industrial production.

中文摘要

我们提出一种新方法，将主要货币政策（MMP）冲击识别为最能解释宏观经济波动的货币政策意外变化的线性组合。使用两个标准的高频收益率曲线意外变化以及Boehm和Kroner（2024）的非收益率冲击实施后，MMP冲击解释了过去30年中不可忽视的商业周期变异，这与先前发现形成对比。此外，MMP冲击与教科书式的货币政策冲击显著不同：紧缩性冲击使利率基本不变，但持续压低总体价格并使美元升值，同时暂时收紧金融条件并抑制工业生产。

AI as Shared Business Infrastructure: Firm Growth and the Distribution of Gains

作为共享商业基础设施的AI：企业成长与收益分配

Panle Jia Barwick, Lu Fang, Jinhan Liu, Huilan Xu, Zhe Yuan

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政

Abstract

Generative AI improves performance on individual tasks, but whether those gains translate into firm growth, and for which firms, remains an open question. We study a leading Chinese e-commerce platform that in 2024 embedded a generative AI system in the operating software firms use to run their businesses, drawing on centrally developed models and platform-wide data to deliver ready-to-use applications to every firm. Complete records of each firm’s AI use, linked to its monthly operations and sales, let us trace adoption through changes in business capabilities, such as reaching customers and introducing new products, to sales growth. Using a matched difference-in-differences design and focusing on firms with annual sales below RMB 5 million, we find that adoption raises monthly sales by 51.6%, primarily through a 47.0% increase in the number of orders; instrumental-variable estimates confirm these results. Adopters reach more customers, update their product lines faster, and deliver better product and service quality, with the largest proportional gains for the smallest firms. Two mechanisms help explain the results. First, AI lowers the cost of routine work: adopters produce more product content and change listings more often while spending less staff time per order, and the gains are largest for firms that faced the highest costs relative to sales before adoption. Second, AI reduces information frictions: adopters post richer product information, and the gains are largest in markets where demand is hard to predict and matching quality is poor, especially for small firms. At the market level, product categories with greater pre-existing information frictions see more sales by existing firms, more entry, and more new products. AI expands markets rather than merely shifting sales from non-adopters to adopters.

中文摘要

生成式AI改善了单项任务的绩效，但这些收益能否转化为企业成长、以及哪些企业受益，仍是未解问题。我们研究中国一家领先电商平台，该平台于2024年将生成式AI系统嵌入企业用于经营业务的运营软件中，依托集中开发的模型和平台范围的数据，向每家企业提供即用型应用。每家企业AI使用的完整记录与其月度运营和销售相链接，使我们能够追踪从商业能力变化（如触达客户和推出新产品）到销售增长的采用过程。采用匹配双重差分设计，聚焦年销售额低于500万元人民币的企业，我们发现采用使月销售额提高51.6%，主要通过订单数量增加47.0%实现；工具变量估计证实了这些结果。采用者触达更多客户、更快更新产品线，并提供更好的产品和服务质量，最小企业的比例收益最大。两种机制有助于解释这些结果。第一，AI降低常规工作成本：采用者生产更多产品内容、更频繁更换商品列表，同时每笔订单的员工时间减少，且对于采用前成本相对销售额最高的企业收益最大。第二，AI减少信息摩擦：采用者发布更丰富的产品信息，且在需求难以预测、匹配质量较差的市场中收益最大，对小企业尤其如此。在市场层面，既有信息摩擦更大的产品类别中，现有企业销售更多、进入更多、新产品更多。AI扩大市场，而不仅仅是将销售从非采用者转移给采用者。

Knowledge Without Taxation? Experimental Evidence from a Tax Education Program

无税收的知识？来自税收教育项目的实验证据

Anders Jensen, Nikhil Kumar, Konstantin Poensgen

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 加纳

Abstract

A property tax experiment in Ghana randomly provides tax education to property owners and frontline officials. The intervention raises tax knowledge, which spreads through social networks to untreated neighbors, but does not increase tax payments. Educated taxpayers and officials become more engaged in their interactions without developing more collaborative relationships. Taxpayers revise downward their beliefs about enforcement capacity and become more aware of legal ways to reduce their tax burden. Knowledge is a double-edged sword: it can stimulate citizens to engage with the tax system while exposing the state's limited capacity to enforce it.

中文摘要

加纳的一项房产税实验随机向房产所有者和一线官员提供税收教育。该干预提高了税收知识，知识通过社交网络传播至未受处理的邻居，但并未增加税款缴纳。受过教育的纳税人和官员在互动中更加投入，但并未形成更具合作性的关系。纳税人下调了对执法能力的信念，并更加了解减轻税负的合法途径。知识是一把双刃剑：它可以激励公民参与税收体系，同时暴露国家有限的执法能力。

Vigilance, Risk Vulnerability, and the Precautionary Augment

警觉性、风险脆弱性与预防性增量

Miles S. Kimball, Vincent A. Mastantuno

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Risk raises the expected marginal utility of a prudent agent. We define this proportional change as the precautionary augment, which measures risk-induced changes in the marginal value of saving, the Lucas Tree risk-free rate, and asset prices in a derivative-saturated market where market-state securities provide incomplete coverage against idiosyncratic risk. One agent is more vigilant than another if their precautionary augment is larger for all unfair risks. We derive and compare conditions for greater vigilance, showing how to implement the Diffidence Theorem geometrically. Because risk vulnerability is equivalent to decreasing vigilance, classic results about risk vulnerability emerge as corollaries.

中文摘要

风险提高了审慎主体的预期边际效用。我们将这一比例变化定义为预防性增量，它衡量风险引致的储蓄边际价值、Lucas树无风险利率以及衍生品饱和市场中资产价格的变化，在该市场中市场状态证券对特质风险提供不完整覆盖。若某主体对所有不公平风险的预防性增量都更大，则其比另一主体更警觉。我们推导并比较了更高警觉性的条件，展示了如何几何化地实施Diffidence定理。由于风险脆弱性等价于警觉性递减，关于风险脆弱性的经典结果作为推论出现。

When More Prenatal Care Looks Like Less: Selection, Information and Maternal and Infant Health

当更多产前护理看起来像更少：选择、信息与母婴健康

Chien-Tzu Cheng, Shin-Yi Chou, Hsien-Ming Lien

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 台灣

Abstract

We use Taiwan's 2003 SARS epidemic as a natural experiment, instrumenting prenatal care with gestational exposure to SARS-induced fear that reduced visits by over 20 percent. Adequate prenatal care reduces preventable maternal complications by 3.4 percentage points (26 percent), with no effect on infant outcomes. OLS estimates are perversely signed for maternal complications but overstate benefits for infant outcomes, consistent with competing patient- and provider-driven selection. Mechanism evidence points to prenatal care as a longitudinal information system: repeated contact facilitates detection of evolving risks and generates clinical information that informs delivery decisions, including delivery-mode routing and subsequent management at delivery.

中文摘要

我们利用台湾2003年SARS疫情作为自然实验，以孕期暴露于SARS引发的恐惧作为产前护理的工具变量，该恐惧使就诊次数减少超过20%。充足的产前护理使可预防的孕产妇并发症降低3.4个百分点（26%），对婴儿结局无影响。OLS估计对孕产妇并发症的符号反常，但高估了婴儿结局的收益，这与患者驱动和提供者驱动的竞争性选择一致。机制证据表明产前护理是一个纵向信息系统：反复接触有助于发现演变中的风险，并产生为分娩决策提供信息的临床信息，包括分娩方式路径安排和分娩时的后续管理。

No. 7论文详情NBER w35999

(In)Credible Development RCTs: False Discoveries, Low Power, and a Solution

（不）可信的发展经济学随机对照试验：错误发现、低统计功效及一种解决方案

Qingyuan Chai, Kevin Lang, Liang Zhong

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the statistical credibility of randomized controlled trials (RCTs) in development economics and the optimal allocation of resources to replication. Using 745 principal-hypothesis tests from 190 articles in leading economics journals, we find that actual t-statistics peak well below the theoretical benchmark of 2.8 expected under 80 percent power, though above values associated with p-hacking. This suggests many RCTs are underpowered. Estimating an empirical Bayes mixture model that accounts for selective publication, we find that approximately 30 percent of published rejections are false discoveries, with average directional power at only 40 percent. At conventional significance thresholds, the posterior probability of a false discovery reaches 66 percent. Doubling original sample sizes lowers the average false discovery rate by five percentage points, whereas tightening the significance threshold to 0.005 yields a larger reduction at the cost of total discoveries. Finally, we characterize a funder’s optimal replication policy under a fixed budget and an evidence standard. Under standard regularity conditions, replications concentrate in an intermediate interval of original test statistics, and the optimal replication sample size is single-peaked in the original t-statistic. Illustrative calibrations show that successful replications reduce aggregate false discovery rates to 1.8–7.8 percent, depending on the evidence standard.

中文摘要

我们研究发展经济学中随机对照试验（RCT）的统计可信度，以及将资源最优配置于重复实验的问题。利用来自领先经济学期刊190篇文章的745个主假设检验，我们发现实际t统计量的峰值远低于在80%统计功效下预期的理论基准2.8，但高于与p值操纵相关的数值。这表明许多RCT统计功效不足。通过估计一个考虑选择性发表的经验贝叶斯混合模型，我们发现约30%的已发表拒绝原假设是错误发现，平均方向性功效仅为40%。在常规显著性阈值下，错误发现的后验概率达到66%。将原始样本量翻倍可使平均错误发现率降低5个百分点，而将显著性阈值收紧至0.005则可在牺牲总发现数量的代价下带来更大的降低。最后，我们刻画了在固定预算和证据标准下资助者的最优重复实验政策。在标准正则条件下，重复实验集中于原始检验统计量的中间区间，且最优重复实验样本量关于原始t统计量呈单峰。示例性校准显示，成功的重复实验可将总体错误发现率降至1.8%–7.8%，具体取决于证据标准。

Sorting by Participation: A Field Experiment in a Stem-cell Registry

通过参与进行筛选：干细胞登记处的一项实地实验

Michael Haylock, Patrick Kampkötter, Mario Macis, Susanne Seitz, Robert Slonim, Daniel Wiesen

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Unavailability of stem-cell donors at the confirmatory typing (CT) stage, an operational milestone in the stem-cell donation process, reduces the effective supply of life-saving transplants for blood cancer patients worldwide. Although (potential) donors register voluntarily, requests may come much later, when circumstances and commitment have changed; keeping registered donors engaged and identifying who will follow through are therefore central managerial questions for registries. In a field experiment with a leading stem-cell registry, we randomize the framing of routine invitations to an engagement initiative. Relative to a business-as-usual letter, our experimental letters present the registry’s requests more saliently; two of them add identity framings inviting donors to join a “quickly available” or a “highly committed” donor team. The salience and identity framings significantly reduce initiative participation compared to the business-as-usual rate, yet intention-to-treat analyses show no detectable effect on average CT availability. Our central finding is that participation sorts donors by reliability: participants are unavailable in 12% of cases, versus 30% of non-participants. From a managerial perspective, opting in works as a reliability flag. Prioritizing participants in our experimental arms implies a relative increase of up to 7.5% more one-year survivors compared to not using this flag. Gains from flagging are likely to be larger for registries with lower availability.

理解AI如何重塑工作需要准确记录工人所执行的任务。工人往往最先看到自己的工作如何变化，因为新的职责和工具常常先出现在日常工作中，之后才进入正式描述。与工人的访谈有助于捕捉这些正在出现的任务变化。然而，现有方法支持对工人进行标准化评分，而与工人的开放式对话在大规模收集和综合方面仍然成本高昂。我们引入斯坦福任务网络（TaskNet），这是一个直接基于工人对日常工作叙述的职业分类体系。为构建TaskNet，我们开发了一个混合主动式系统，直接从工人处归纳任务清单。该系统进行访谈，将报告标准化为个人任务列表，并将这些列表汇总为职业任务清单。应用于涵盖不同教育要求和AI暴露程度的六个职业，我们的系统访谈了132名工人，从2726份报告中生成202条任务陈述，全部被O*NET中心分析师接受，相比之下，通过当前O*NET数据收集流程（新兴任务的主要替代来源）收集的工人自填任务建议约有65%被接受。每条陈述均由318名在职者通过7923次相关性和重要性评分进行验证。相对于O*NET 31.0数据库中抽样职业的任务数据，TaskNet覆盖了已发布的118项O*NET任务中的75%，并新增了79项无O*NET对应项的任务和58项细化现有陈述的任务。它覆盖了留出工人报告任务的89%，而O*NET为79%，并且覆盖了雇主招聘启事中描述活动的更大比例（72.5%对70.2%）。在下游，TaskNet在两个方向上改变了AI暴露估计，提高了软件开发者 and 律师这两个AI影响广受争议职业的估计。由于此类估计指导劳动力政策和培训，更完整和最新的任务列表有助于将这些努力指向最可能受影响的工人。TaskNet为在AI重塑工作之际保持职业信息最新并扎根于工人经验提供了基础。

Debiasing Law Enforcement Officers: Evidence from an Expressive Arts Intervention in India

消除执法人员的偏见：来自印度表达性艺术干预的证据

Sofia Amaral, Girija Borker, Nishith Prakash, María Micaela Sviatschi

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 印度

Abstract

Frontline state agents exercise substantial discretion over citizens’ access to public services, yet little is known about whether the attitudes and beliefs shaping this discretion can be changed among incumbent bureaucrats. Partnering with the Bihar Police - one of India’s largest states that operates under comparatively severe policing capacity constraints, we conduct a cluster randomized controlled trial across 417 police stations and roughly 2,500 active duty senior field police officers to evaluate a three-day expressive arts intervention, grounded in the Theatre of the Oppressed, designed to change police responses to gender-based violence (GBV). Approximately 7 months after baseline, treated officers exhibit more favorable attitudes toward GBV, greater empathy, and stronger legal knowledge. These changes translate into observed policing behavior: in standardized victim audits conducted one year later, officers in treated stations are 15 percentage points less likely to blame victims, dismiss fewer complaints, and provide more referrals, while administrative records show a 41 percent reduction in registration delays and, over a longer horizon, increased GBV reporting by citizens. Exploiting variation generated by officer transfers, we find suggestive evidence that these effects persist when treated officers relocate and diffuse to untreated officers working alongside treated colleagues. The effects also extend to the workplace: junior female officers working with treated senior officers report lower harassment and anxiety. These findings show that the attitudes shaping bureaucratic discretion are malleable and that changing how incumbent state agents exercise authority can improve both citizen-facing service delivery and the broader workplace environment.

中文摘要

一线国家代理人在公民获得公共服务方面拥有相当大的自由裁量权，但鲜有研究关注塑造这种自由裁量权的态度和信念能否在现任官僚中被改变。我们与比哈尔邦警察合作——该邦是印度最大的邦之一，在相对严峻的警务能力约束下运作——在417个警察局和约2,500名现役高级外勤警官中开展了一项整群随机对照试验，评估一项为期三天、以被压迫者剧场为基础的表达性艺术干预，旨在改变警察对性别暴力（GBV）的应对方式。基线后约7个月，受干预警官对GBV表现出更有利的态度、更强的同理心和更扎实的法律知识。这些变化转化为可观察的警务行为：在一年后进行的标准化受害者审计中，受干预警察局的警官指责受害者的可能性低15个百分点，驳回的投诉更少，提供的转介更多，而行政记录显示登记延迟减少了41%，并且在更长的时间范围内，公民的GBV报案有所增加。利用警官调动产生的变异，我们发现了提示性证据：当受干预警官调任并向与其共事的未受干预警官扩散时，这些效应会持续存在。效应还延伸至工作场所：与受干预高级警官共事的初级女警官报告称遭受的骚扰和焦虑更低。这些发现表明，塑造官僚自由裁量权的态度是可塑的，改变现任国家代理人行使权威的方式可以改善面向公民的服务提供和更广泛的工作场所环境。

Unconventional Monetary Policy and Deposit Retention

非常规货币政策与存款留存

Philip Strahan, Tuo Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 未指明区域

Abstract

The two dimensions of unconventional monetary policy — holding the short rate at the zero-lower bound (ZLB) and quantitative easing (QE) — shifted deposit composition toward more interest-sensitive customers. The ZLB drew these customers by creating rate compression, which limited the benefit of searching for high-yielding liquidity sources. QE also drew rate-sensitive, large depositors from the Federal Reserve’s purchases of bonds from non-banks. Once policy normalized, flow sensitivity and the deposit beta both increased with exposure to QE and the ZLB. We conclude that unconventional monetary policy made deposit retention costlier and riskier for banks.

中文摘要

非常规货币政策的两个维度——将短期利率维持在零下限（ZLB）和量化宽松（QE）——使存款构成向对利率更敏感的客户转移。ZLB通过造成利率压缩吸引了这些客户，这限制了寻找高收益流动性来源的收益。QE也吸引了对利率敏感的大额存款人，其来源是美联储从非银行机构购买债券。一旦政策正常化，流量敏感性和存款贝塔均随对QE和ZLB的暴露程度而上升。我们得出结论：非常规货币政策使银行的存款留存成本更高、风险更大。

Target What? The Foundations of Poverty Targeting

瞄准什么？贫困瞄准的基础

Rema Hanna, Emily Breza, Benjamin A. Olken, Abhijit Banerjee, Arun G. Chandrasekhar, Gideon Moore

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 印度、印度尼西亚

Abstract

Most government transfer programs aim to target the poor. Doing so implicitly assumes that the lowest-consumption households have the highest marginal social valuation, i.e., have the highest social welfare gain from an additional dollar. But this need not be the case. In 600 Indonesian villages, we ask over 5,500 villagers to rank other villagers on how well off they are and how much a modest cash transfer would help them, and compare these rankings to consumption and self-assessed welfare. Targeting consumption misses 77% of households with the highest marginal social valuation. Targeting self-assessed welfare misses 57%. Even targeting those the community ranks worst off in levels, not margins, misses 25%. Divergences between different notions of neediness are statistically predictable. In a randomized controlled trial, proxy means tests best target consumption, self-targeting ordeals capture self-assessed welfare levels, and community targeting comes closest to marginal social valuation. Targeting strategies inescapably embed normative stances.

中文摘要

大多数政府转移支付项目旨在瞄准穷人。这样做隐含地假设最低消费家庭具有最高的边际社会估值，即从额外一美元中获得最高的社会福利增益。但情况未必如此。在600个印度尼西亚村庄中，我们请超过5,500名村民对其他村民的富裕程度以及一笔适度现金转移对他们的帮助程度进行排序，并将这些排序与消费和自评福利进行比较。以消费为瞄准标准遗漏了77%具有最高边际社会估值的家庭。以自评福利为瞄准标准遗漏了57%。即使以社区在水平而非边际上排名最差的家庭为瞄准标准，也遗漏了25%。不同需求概念之间的分歧在统计上是可以预测的。在一项随机对照试验中，代理均值检验最能瞄准消费，自我瞄准的考验捕捉自评福利水平，而社区瞄准最接近边际社会估值。瞄准策略不可避免地嵌入了规范性立场。
