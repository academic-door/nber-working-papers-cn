How Does Capital Flow To Poor Countries? Evidence from Venture Capital and Private Equity

资本如何流向贫穷国家？来自风险投资和私募股权的证据

Emanuele Colonnelli, Marcio Cruz, Josh Lerner, Mariana Pereira-Lopez, Yuanchang Wei

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Why does so little private capital flow to poor countries? We study how venture capital and private equity investors evaluate firms across countries, using an experimental survey of 1,485 investors in 108 countries who are incentivized to evaluate hypothetical investment opportunities with randomized firm characteristics, locations, and investment terms. Investors respond similarly to firm fundamentals, but otherwise comparable firms attract substantially less interest in poorer countries. This poor-country penalty is much smaller among investors specialized in emerging markets. Detailed survey data show that emerging market and developed market investors have largely similar investment practices, but differ in how they obtain country information, assess and manage country risks, and exit investments. Investors reporting active country-risk management apply smaller country penalties, even after accounting for market focus. Our findings suggest that investors' specialization and investment practices shape how country risk affects investment: the same firm can face drastically different financing prospects depending on where it is located and which investors evaluate it.

中文摘要

为什么流向贫穷国家的私人资本如此之少？我们通过一项实验性调查，研究了风险投资和私募股权投资者如何评估不同国家的企业。该调查覆盖108个国家的1,485名投资者，并通过激励措施促使他们评估具有随机化企业特征、地点和投资条款的假设性投资机会。投资者对企业基本面的反应相似，但除此之外条件可比的企业在较贫穷国家获得的兴趣要低得多。这种贫穷国家惩罚在专注于新兴市场的投资者中要小得多。详细的调查数据显示，新兴市场和发达市场投资者的投资实践大体相似，但在获取国家信息、评估和管理国家风险以及退出投资的方式上存在差异。报告积极进行国家风险管理的投资者施加的国家惩罚较小，即使在考虑了市场专注度之后也是如此。我们的发现表明，投资者的专业化和投资实践塑造了国家风险如何影响投资：同一家企业可能因所在地不同以及由哪些投资者评估而面临截然不同的融资前景。

Will AI Growth Reach the Developing World?

AI增长会惠及发展中国家吗？

Daniel Björkegren, David Lagakos, Mitchell VanVuren

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 全球/多国

Abstract

This paper explores how economic growth driven by AI will impact the developing world. Our analysis is based on a growth model of a developed and developing economy where AI services are traded on the world market, and may substitute for or complement different labor inputs in production. Preferences allow for the possibility that demand for services provided by AI has an upper bound. The model predicts that developing countries may not benefit as much from AI-driven growth. First, they may lack complementary skills needed to fully utilize AI domestically. Second, they may be priced out of world markets due to their relatively lower productivity. We use our model to simulate how growth and welfare are affected by making workers more complementary to AI, subsidizing compute, speeding technology diffusion, and providing cash transfers. Numerical simulations of the model suggest that the potential for developing countries to be left behind – or having some workers lose in absolute terms – is substantial enough that researchers should study the impacts of AI on developing countries in real time.

中文摘要

本文探讨AI驱动的经济增长将如何影响发展中国家。我们的分析基于一个发达经济体和发展中经济体的增长模型，其中AI服务在世界市场上交易，并可能替代或互补生产中的不同劳动投入。偏好设定允许对AI所提供服务的需求存在上限的可能性。模型预测，发展中国家可能无法从AI驱动的增长中获益那么多。首先，它们可能缺乏在国内充分利用AI所需的互补技能。其次，由于相对较低的生产率，它们可能在世界市场中被价格挤出。我们使用模型模拟了使劳动者与AI更具互补性、补贴算力、加快技术扩散以及提供现金转移支付如何影响增长和福利。模型的数值模拟表明，发展中国家被甩在后面的可能性——或部分劳动者出现绝对损失的可能性——足够大，研究人员应当实时研究AI对发展中国家的影响。

The Recent and Impending Evolution of US Federal Support for State and Local Governments

美国联邦对州和地方政府支持的近期与即将到来的演变

Jeffrey Clemens, Anwita Mahajan

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 美国

Abstract

In the US federal system, the federal government provides substantial fiscal support to state and local governments. In this paper, we describe how the landscape of intergovernmental grants, which exceeded $1.2 trillion in 2025, evolved during and in the wake of the COVID-19 pandemic. We provide short-run evidence on how state and local government budgets have adapted as pandemic-era fiscal supports wound down. We further emphasize the pressures state and local budgets will face as several provisions of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA), which are intended to restrain intergovernmental grants, go into effect over the coming years.

Preconception Medicaid and Maternal and Infant Health: Evidence from Linked Administrative Data

孕前Medicaid与母婴健康：来自关联行政数据的证据

Genna Campain, Sarah Miller, Petra Persson, Maya Rossin-Slater, Laura Wherry, Katlyn N. King

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

Despite pregnancy-focused public insurance coverage in the U.S., low-income mothers and infants face persistent health disadvantages. Using linked birth, tax, and enrollment data, we examine the effects of preconception coverage from California's 2014 Medicaid expansion, comparing changes in outcomes for first-time mothers just below and above the income eligibility threshold. Expansion increased preconception Medicaid enrollment by 3.4 months (+23 percentage points in 12-month continuous coverage), first-month pregnancy enrollment by 24 percentage points, and adequate prenatal care and STI screening. Yet we find no improvements in maternal or infant health, suggesting preconception coverage alone cannot close health disparities.

中文摘要

尽管美国有针对怀孕的公共保险覆盖，低收入母亲和婴儿仍面临持续的健康劣势。利用关联的出生、税务和参保数据，我们考察了加州2014年Medicaid扩展带来的孕前覆盖的影响，比较了收入资格阈值两侧首次生育母亲的结果变化。扩展使孕前Medicaid参保增加了3.4个月（12个月连续覆盖增加23个百分点），妊娠第一个月参保增加24个百分点，并提高了充分产前护理和性传播感染筛查。然而，我们未发现母婴健康有所改善，这表明仅靠孕前覆盖无法弥合健康差距。

Clouds of Credit: Mapping the Financial Web Behind AI Compute

信贷之云：绘制AI算力背后的金融网络

Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh, Zijin Su

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We map the financial network supporting the AI buildout. Substantial equity cushions protect creditors against large obligations outstanding in mid-2026, but concentrated exposures and interconnected claims create weak links. Our calibrated model supports a large compute expansion in the coming years. The equilibrium investment exceeds the social optimum because firms do not internalize their effects on competitors' revenues. Debt finances nearly 60% of the expansion, increasing leverage, while non-recourse borrowing leaves project lenders exposed despite equity buffers. In the low-productivity state, losses reach $16.8 trillion across shareholders and creditors, with 40% loss-given-default on debt. Suppressing contractual payment spillovers among the key firms reduces private losses by approximately 30%, revealing substantial network propagation.

中文摘要

我们绘制了支撑AI建设的金融网络。大量股权缓冲保护债权人免受2026年中期巨额未偿债务的影响，但集中敞口和相互关联的债权形成了薄弱环节。我们校准的模型支持未来几年大规模的算力扩张。均衡投资超过社会最优，因为企业未将其对竞争对手收入的影响内部化。债务为扩张融资近60%，提高了杠杆率，而无追索权借款使项目贷款人尽管有股权缓冲仍面临敞口。在低生产率状态下，股东和债权人的损失达到16.8万亿美元，债务的违约损失率为40%。抑制关键企业之间的合同支付溢出可将私人损失减少约30%，揭示了显著的网络传播。

A Blessing in Disguise: The Long-Term Effects of Childhood Exposure to the Boll Weevil on Old-Age Male Longevity

因祸得福：童年接触棉铃象鼻虫对老年男性寿命的长期影响

Hamid Noghanibehambari, Jason Fletcher

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

The infamous agricultural pest, the boll weevil, spread across southern US counties between 1892 and 1920. It had devastating impacts on cotton production, a highly child-labor-intensive crop. This study examines how the arrival of the boll weevil and the subsequent reductions in child labor affect later-life mortality outcomes. Using Social Security Administration death records linked with the 1940 census, we document that the staggered spread of the boll weevil improved the longevity of exposed male children. Full exposure to the boll weevil is associated with an intent-to-treat effect of 1-3 months of higher longevity. We show that the estimated effects are robust across a wide array of specifications, are not driven by sociodemographic and socioeconomic compositional change in the final sample, and are not associated with pre-existing trends in the outcome. Further, we find larger impacts among Blacks, children of lower educated mothers, whose fathers work in the farming industry, and those born in high cotton intensity counties. Moreover, the effects appear to be substantially larger among children in states with less restrictive child labor policies, implying the role of these policies in reducing the adverse effects of child labor in the absence of the boll weevil in cotton production counties. Affected children are more likely to migrate, and their destination counties are more likely to be urban and have higher socioeconomic scores. The 1940 census suggests that the exposed children attain higher levels of education and have higher socioeconomic scores. We discuss the policy implications of these results in relation to child labor and school attendance policies in developing countries.

中文摘要

臭名昭著的农业害虫棉铃象鼻虫在1892年至1920年间蔓延至美国南部各县。它对棉花生产造成了毁灭性影响，而棉花是一种高度依赖童工的作物。本研究考察棉铃象鼻虫的到来及随后童工减少如何影响晚年死亡率结果。利用与社会保障局死亡记录相关联的1940年人口普查数据，我们记录了棉铃象鼻虫的交错蔓延改善了暴露男性儿童的寿命。完全暴露于棉铃象鼻虫与意向性治疗效应相关，即寿命延长1-3个月。我们表明，估计效应在广泛的一系列设定中稳健，并非由最终样本的社会人口和社会经济构成变化所驱动，也与结果的既有趋势无关。此外，我们发现黑人、母亲受教育程度较低者、父亲从事农业者以及出生在棉花密集度高县的儿童中影响更大。而且，在童工政策限制较少的州，儿童中的效应似乎大得多，这意味着在棉花生产县没有棉铃象鼻虫的情况下，这些政策在减少童工不利影响方面的作用。受影响的儿童更可能迁移，其目的地县更可能为城市且社会经济得分更高。1940年人口普查表明，暴露儿童获得了更高的教育水平并具有更高的社会经济得分。我们讨论了这些结果与发展中国家童工和入学政策相关的政策含义。

Learn to Earn: The Long-Term Effects of Foundational Learning

学会赚钱：基础学习的长期影响

Tahir Andrabi, Natalie Bau, Jishnu Das, Asim Ijaz Khwaja, Catherine Michaud-Leclerc

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 巴基斯坦

Abstract

Tracking Pakistani students over 15 years, we use two identification strategies to provide the first causal evidence linking learning in primary school to adult outcomes in a low-income country. Exposure to a school with higher test score value-added increases educational attainment and earnings 15 years later. A randomized information intervention that led to learning gains at the village level generated similar effects. Lifetime earnings gains across all students attending a 1sd better school total US$175,000, and for the information intervention, the marginal value of public funds is infinity for men. There is a strong economic case for investing in learning.

Childhood Learning and Adult Earnings in Four LMIC Cohorts

四个中低收入国家队列中的儿童期学习与成年收入

Jishnu Das, Yash Dhuldhoya, Marta Favara, Amory Gethin, Alan Sánchez, Abhijeet Singh

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 埃塞俄比亚、印度、巴基斯坦、秘鲁

Abstract

Researchers and policymakers in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs) have customarily relied on indirect evidence to justify investments in learning, either by linking test scores to economic growth or by connecting adult cognitive assessments to earnings. This reliance on indirect evidence largely stems from the scarcity of longitudinal data linking school-age learning to adult earnings. We address this gap by using data from Ethiopia, India, Pakistan, and Peru to estimate associations between foundational learning (FL), measured by test scores around age 12, and human capital and earnings in young adulthood. In Peru, India, and Pakistan, a one-standard-deviation increase in FL is associated with an 8-13 log point increase in adult earnings. Childhood scores also predict 1.1-1.5 additional years of schooling and 9.3-13.5 percentage points higher college or vocational attendance, as well as higher skills in adulthood. Interestingly, a linear-projection decomposition indicates that measured schooling and adult test scores explain between 18% (Peru) to 50-56% (India and Pakistan) of the FL-earnings gradient, suggesting that FL may raise earnings over and above the association with schooling and adult scores. Finally, FL can account for about 5-12 percent of the earnings gap between young Ethiopians and Indians in our sample, a number that could rise to 33 percent as returns to learning grow over the life cycle. Abstract

The Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) plans to implement a pilot switching 340B from an upfront discount model to a rebate model for drugs in the Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program (MDPNP). This study uses calibration methods to predict the impact of the rebate model pilot (RMP) on covered entities’ net revenues from the 340B program. I examine possible losses from averted duplicate discounts; denial of valid claims; manufacturers using data to exert influence; liquidity-related expenses; and administrative and staffing costs. I also project the effects of switching the entire 340B program to a rebate model. I project net revenue losses from the RMP of $1.5 billion. Expansion to all of 340B would increase the predicted losses to $22.8 billion, cutting net revenues from the program by 32%. The majority of losses will come from administrative and staffing costs, which do not benefit manufacturers, implying a net negative effect of switching to a rebate model. Covered entity losses from the RMP will be limited by the pilot only applying to a small percentage of 340B sales as well as restrictions HRSA’s guidance places on manufacturers. If the rebate model expands to all of 340B or if the restrictions are inadequately enforced, the losses will grow substantially.

中文摘要

美国卫生资源与服务管理局（HRSA）计划实施一项试点，将针对《医疗保险药品价格谈判计划》（MDPNP）内药品的340B从预先折扣模式转为返利模式。本研究采用校准方法预测返利模式试点（RMP）对覆盖实体从340B计划获得的净收入的影响。我考察了以下方面可能造成的损失：被避免的重复折扣；有效索赔被拒；制造商利用数据施加影响；与流动性相关的费用；以及行政和人员成本。我还预测了将整个340B计划转为返利模式的影响。我预计RMP造成的净收入损失为15亿美元。扩展至整个340B将使预测损失增至228亿美元，使该计划的净收入减少32%。大部分损失将来自行政和人员成本，这些成本并不使制造商受益，这意味着转为返利模式将产生净负面效应。RMP给覆盖实体造成的损失将因试点仅适用于一小部分340B销售以及HRSA指南对制造商施加的限制而有限。如果返利模式扩展至整个340B，或限制执行不到位，损失将大幅增加。
