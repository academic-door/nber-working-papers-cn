Inverted Gravity

反向引力

James E. Anderson, Mario Larch, Yoto V. Yotov

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We invert structural gravity to recover trade cost changes delivering targeted outcomes. For attainable welfare targets, one instrument per target yields a unique solution. At unchanged producer prices and to first order, each instrument must change by the welfare target divided by its own expenditure share; the required domestic change is about the size of the target, while the import change is several times larger. Inverting observed trade flows gives a separate trade cost change for each of the two directions in which a pair of countries trades. Applications to NAFTA and world trade illustrate the benefits of our approach.

中文摘要

我们反向运用结构引力模型，以恢复能够实现目标结果的贸易成本变化。对于可实现的福利目标，每个目标对应一个工具变量，可得到唯一解。在生产价格不变且取一阶近似的情况下，每个工具变量的变化必须等于福利目标除以其自身的支出份额；所需的国内变化约为目标规模，而进口变化则要大数倍。对观测到的贸易流进行反向运算，可为一对国家之间两个贸易方向分别得出不同的贸易成本变化。对NAFTA和世界贸易的应用说明了我们方法的优势。

When Cash Flows Turn Negative: Liquidity-Driven Selling by Pension Funds

当现金流转为负值：养老金基金的流动性驱动型抛售

Aleksandar Andonov, Kristy A. E. Jansen, Joshua Rauh

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

Pension funds are long-horizon investors with predictable liability structures. As such, they are often perceived as investors who can provide liquidity and stabilize markets. However, pension funds increasingly face negative operating cash flows as benefit payments exceed contributions. Using aggregate data on U.S. public pension funds combined with granular holdings obtained through public records requests, we document three findings that challenge the conventional view of pension funds as stabilizing investors. First, pension funds with more negative cash flows do not adjust their target asset allocations and maintain low allocations to safe and liquid assets. Second, because of these limited safe asset buffers, pension funds meet cash flow shocks primarily by selling equities, absorbing $0.67 per dollar of shock through equity sales. At the security level, pension funds sell across equities rather than following a safe and liquid-assets-first approach. Third, pension funds sell equities even when equity returns are negative, indicating that these trades reflect liquidity needs rather than portfolio rebalancing. Together, these findings show that pension funds have become regular sellers in equity markets rather than contrarian, stabilizing investors.

中文摘要

养老金基金是具有可预测负债结构的长期投资者。因此，它们常被视为能够提供流动性并稳定市场的投资者。然而，随着福利支付超过缴费，养老金基金日益面临负的经营现金流。利用美国公共养老金基金的总体数据，并结合通过公共记录申请获得的细粒度持仓数据，我们记录了三个发现，这些发现挑战了养老金基金作为稳定型投资者的传统观点。第一，现金流更为负面的养老金基金不会调整其目标资产配置，并维持对安全和流动性资产的低配置。第二，由于这些安全资产缓冲有限，养老金基金主要通过出售股票来应对现金流冲击，每1美元冲击中通过股票出售吸收0.67美元。在证券层面，养老金基金在股票之间进行出售，而不是遵循先出售安全和流动性资产的方法。第三，即使股票回报为负，养老金基金也会出售股票，这表明这些交易反映的是流动性需求，而不是投资组合再平衡。总之，这些发现表明，养老金基金已成为股票市场中的常规卖方，而不是逆势的稳定型投资者。

Do Judges Move Markets? Ideological Shifts in the Supreme Court and Macroeconomic Expectations

法官会推动市场吗？最高法院意识形态变化与宏观经济预期

Thomas Drechsel, Naixin Zhang

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 美国

Abstract

This paper studies how judicial ideology shapes financial markets' expectations about the macroeconomy. We use unexpected deaths of U.S. Supreme Court Justices as quasi-experimental shifts in the Court's ideological balance. High-frequency asset price responses reveal how markets perceive the macroeconomic consequences of these ideological shifts. We find that a more conservative Court is associated with lower inflation expectations, lower real activity expectations, and lower bond yields. A shift towards a more liberal Court induces the opposite responses. The finding that inflation and activity expectations respond with the same sign suggests that markets revise their expectations about aggregate demand, rather than aggregate supply. Moreover, contrasting the yield responses with estimates from studies of fiscal policy news indicates that a more conservative (liberal) Court leads markets to expect tighter (looser) future fiscal policy. We validate our quasi-experimental setup with sectoral asset price dynamics, such as high-frequency responses of clean energy and health care stocks. Taken together, our findings are consistent with markets interpreting changes in the Supreme Court's ideological composition as news about future fiscal expansions or contractions.

中文摘要

本文研究司法意识形态如何塑造金融市场对宏观经济的预期。我们使用美国最高法院大法官的意外死亡作为法院意识形态平衡的准实验性变化。高频资产价格反应揭示了市场如何看待这些意识形态变化的宏观经济后果。我们发现，更保守的法院与更低的通胀预期、更低的实际活动预期和更低的债券收益率相关。法院转向更自由派会引发相反的反应。通胀预期和活动预期以相同方向变化这一发现表明，市场修正的是总需求预期，而不是总供给预期。此外，将收益率反应与财政政策新闻研究的估计进行对比表明，更保守（自由派）的法院会使市场预期未来财政政策更紧缩（更宽松）。我们通过部门资产价格动态验证了我们的准实验设定，例如清洁能源和医疗保健股票的高频反应。总之，我们的发现与市场将最高法院意识形态构成变化解读为未来财政扩张或收缩的消息相一致。

Estimating the Impact of Targeted Public Policies with Limited Data

在数据有限的情况下估计有针对性的公共政策影响

Kelly M. Jones, Mayra Pineda-Torres

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Many public policies aim to support (or change the behavior of) a specific, targeted sub-group of the population. To measure the causal impacts of such policies, social scientists use quasi-experimental methods of policy evaluation, but are often hampered by data availability issues, especially regarding targeted populations, who are often disadvantaged and/or vulnerable. We use power calculations to quantify the challenge of detecting a policy’s impact on a small minority population based on population level data, using the impact of abortion bans on economic welfare as an example. We provide insight into this challenge and a roadmap for how researchers can calculate, ex-ante, whether their study is sufficiently powered to detect reasonably meaningful effect sizes.

中文摘要

许多公共政策旨在支持（或改变）人口中特定目标子群体的行为。为了衡量此类政策的因果影响，社会科学家使用准实验性的政策评估方法，但往往受到数据可得性问题的阻碍，尤其是关于目标人群的数据，因为他们通常处于不利地位和/或属于弱势群体。我们使用功效计算来量化基于总体层面数据检测政策对小型少数群体影响的挑战，并以堕胎禁令对经济福利的影响为例。我们深入分析了这一挑战，并为研究人员如何事前计算其研究是否具有足够功效以检测合理有意义的效应量提供了路线图。

Affirmative Action in Centralized College Admissions Systems

集中式大学招生系统中的平权行动

Nano Barahona, Cauê Dobbin, Sebastián Otero

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 巴西

Abstract

We study the consequences of affirmative action in college admissions. We develop an empirical framework that distinguishes the effects on marginally benefited and displaced students from the overall effects of the policy. The overall effects include equilibrium reallocations as benefited students leave seats that others fill and displaced students take seats elsewhere, displacing others in turn. We apply this framework to a large-scale policy in Brazil that required all federal institutions to reserve half their seats for racially and socioeconomically disadvantaged applicants. Exploiting admissions cutoffs, we find that marginally benefited targeted students attend and graduate from higher-quality programs. Their graduation gains are about twice the losses of marginally displaced non-targeted students, reflecting targeted students’ weaker outside options. To evaluate the overall effects, we estimate a model of degree choices and potential outcomes. Among all reallocated students, benefited students still gain nearly twice what displaced students lose, but equilibrium reallocations make the policy displace about 30% more students than it benefits. Non-targeted students’ losses therefore offset about two-thirds of targeted students’ graduation gains, leaving a modest net gain across all students.

中文摘要

我们研究大学招生中平权行动的后果。我们开发了一个实证框架，将边际受益学生和边际被挤出学生的影响与政策的总体影响区分开来。总体影响包括均衡再配置，因为受益学生离开的席位由其他人填补，而被挤出学生到其他地方占据席位，进而挤出其他人。我们将这一框架应用于巴西的一项大规模政策，该政策要求所有联邦机构为种族和社会经济弱势申请者保留一半席位。利用录取分数线，我们发现边际受益的目标学生进入并毕业于更高质量的项目。他们的毕业收益约为边际被挤出的非目标学生损失的两倍，这反映了目标学生较弱的外部选择。为了评估总体影响，我们估计了一个学位选择和潜在结果的模型。在所有被再配置的学生中，受益学生的收益仍接近被挤出学生损失的两倍，但均衡再配置使该政策挤出的学生数量比受益学生数量多约30%。因此，非目标学生的损失抵消了目标学生毕业收益的约三分之二，使所有学生总体上仅获得适度净收益。

Strategic Complementarity and Persistence in Linear-Quadratic Mean Field Games

线性二次平均场博弈中的策略互补性与持续性

Fernando E. Alvarez, David Argente

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 未指明区域

Abstract

We study the relation between strategic complementarities and aggregate persistence in linear-quadratic mean field games with interactions in states and actions. The framework provides local approximations to common macroeconomic problems, including capital accumulation and multiproduct pricing. We show that the equilibrium aggregate dynamics coincide with those of a single-agent linear-quadratic control problem in which state and action curvatures are adjusted for strategic interactions. This result gives conditions for equilibrium existence, establishes uniqueness, and links strategic interactions to aggregate adjustment. We establish conditions under which stronger state complementarities increase persistence, while stronger action complementarities reduce it, even in multidimensional problems whose underlying matrices are not simultaneously diagonalizable. Under these conditions, internalizing state complementarities makes the utilitarian planner’s allocation more persistent, while internalizing action complementarities makes it less persistent. With common shocks, we give conditions under which individual and aggregate dynamics can be combined to identify strategic interactions and model parameters. Finally, we extend the analysis to interactions through higher moments of the state distribution.

中文摘要

我们研究在状态和行动存在相互作用的线性二次平均场博弈中，策略互补性与总体持续性之间的关系。该框架为常见宏观经济问题提供了局部近似，包括资本积累和多产品定价。我们表明，均衡总体动态与单主体线性二次控制问题的动态一致，其中状态和行动曲率已根据策略互动进行调整。这一结果为均衡存在性给出了条件，确立了唯一性，并将策略互动与总体调整联系起来。我们建立了条件，在这些条件下，更强的状态互补性会增加持续性，而更强的行动互补性会降低持续性，即使是在其基础矩阵不可同时对角化的多维问题中也是如此。在这些条件下，将状态互补性内部化会使功利主义计划者的配置更具持续性，而将行动互补性内部化则会使其更不具持续性。对于共同冲击，我们给出了条件，在这些条件下，个体动态和总体动态可以结合起来识别策略互动和模型参数。最后，我们将分析扩展到通过状态分布更高阶矩产生的相互作用。

Measurement Error in Self-Reported Data on Stigmatized Behavior: Experimental Evidence on Intimate Partner Violence

污名化行为自报数据中的测量误差：亲密伴侣暴力的实验证据

Jorge M. Agüero, Ursula T. Aldana, Erica M. Field, Veronica Frisancho, Ignacio Rodriguez Hurtado, Javier Romero

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Error in the measurement of key development outcomes affects our understanding of socioeconomic phenomena and their policy implications. This paper focuses on measuring intimate partner violence (IPV), a public health issue affecting women globally. We introduce a novel, privacy-enhancing data collection method using a ballot, comparing it to standard survey techniques in which responses are not kept private from enumerators, and list experiments, an approach ensuring complete anonymity. Our findings show that improved privacy yields higher and more accurate IPV reports than both, especially among women with low education. By linking our data with two natural experiments, we demonstrate that the non-random nature of underreporting with standard methods can lead to biased estimates of IPV determinants.

中文摘要

关键发展结果测量中的误差会影响我们对社会经济现象及其政策含义的理解。本文聚焦于测量亲密伴侣暴力（IPV），这是一个全球影响女性的公共卫生问题。我们引入了一种新颖的、增强隐私的数据收集方法，使用投票箱，并将其与标准调查技术以及列表实验进行比较；前者受访者的回答不会对调查员保密，后者则确保完全匿名。我们的发现表明，与两者相比，改善隐私能带来更高且更准确的IPV报告，尤其是在受教育程度较低的女性中。通过将我们的数据与两个自然实验相链接，我们证明，标准方法下低报的非随机性可能导致对IPV决定因素的估计产生偏误。

Gains from Trade in Gun Policy: A Compensation-Based Benchmark

枪支政策中的贸易收益：基于补偿的基准

Bradley T. Shapiro, Sara Drango, Sarah Moshary

NBER 工作论文 · 国际贸易 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Policies restricting gun sales face substantial political opposition and constitutional constraints. In this paper, we ask how costly it would be to induce potential hand-gun buyers to choose less lethal alternatives. Pairing stated-choice experiments with a behavioral model of firearm choice, we estimate the payments required to induce substitution away from handguns toward long guns or no gun purchase. We then compare these payments with the resulting mortality benefits. We find substantial gains from trade: many prospective handgun buyers require relatively little compensation to switch and targeted compensation can avert homicides at a social cost well below conventional estimates of the value of a statistical life. The extent to which these gains can be realized through compensation, however, depends on the state’s information about which consumers would change their choices and at what price. Without this information, uniform subsidies pay consumers who would choose an alternative anyway and overcompensate those willing to switch for less, sharply increasing government expenditure and reducing the optimal scale of compensation.

中文摘要

限制枪支销售的政策面临巨大的政治反对和宪法约束。在本文中，我们追问：诱导潜在手枪购买者选择致命性较低的替代品需要付出多大成本。我们将陈述性选择实验与枪支选择的行为模型相结合，估计诱导人们从手枪转向长枪或不购买枪支所需的支付金额。然后，我们将这些支付与由此带来的死亡率收益进行比较。我们发现存在巨大的贸易收益：许多潜在手枪购买者只需相对较少的补偿就愿意转换，而定向补偿能够以远低于传统统计生命价值估计的社会成本避免凶杀。然而，这些收益能在多大程度上通过补偿实现，取决于州政府是否掌握哪些消费者会改变选择以及以什么价格改变的信息。若缺乏这一信息，统一补贴会向那些无论如何都会选择替代品的人支付，并对那些愿意以更低价格转换的人过度补偿，从而急剧增加政府支出并降低最优补偿规模。

Industrial Policy via the IPO Market

通过IPO市场实施的产业政策

Zhiguo He, Yiming Qian, Xi Sun, Wang Renxuan

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 美国

Abstract

This paper studies how China uses access to public equity markets as an instrument of industrial policy. We find that policy-favored status, rather than industry Tobin's Q, predicts IPO activity, in contrast to the United States. The effect is economically large: our estimates imply that favored industries had 287% more IPOs and 148% higher IPO proceeds than non-favored industries over 2002 to 2022. We develop a framework to interpret these findings: the government shapes the number of listing opportunities available across industries, while market forces determine which firms obtain these opportunities based on firm fundamentals. Consistent with our framework, policy favor also predicts a valuation premium which persists after listing.

中文摘要

中文摘要

关键接入医院（CAH）项目根据1997年《平衡预算法案》设立，向小型农村医院提供基于成本的Medicare报销，以换取对住院能力和服务范围的限制。我们使用合成双重差分设计估计CAH认定对医院财务、能力和组织结果的影响，该设计利用了1999—2002年州级CAH可用性的交错推出。我们发现，CAH认定使营业利润率提高3到4个百分点，而流动性和资本投资没有变化，因为基于成本的报销在收入得以维持的同时，费用随规模下降而下降。认定还降低了住院规模，总床位、产科能力和护理人员显著下降。急诊科就诊量也明显下降，而门诊量基本不变。与此同时，CAH认定减少了医院关闭，对合并没有显著影响。这些结果指向一个州内医院之间的“生存与规模”权衡，即基于认定的补贴使少数医院免于关闭，同时导致更多转为CAH的医院普遍缩减住院规模。由于转为CAH的医院削减的住院能力多于被避免关闭所保留的能力，该项目可能在保持医院开放的同时，降低农村住院总能力。
