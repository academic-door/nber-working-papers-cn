High and Rising Institutional Concentration of Award-Winning Economists

获奖经济学家机构集中度的高企与上升

Richard B. Freeman, Danxia Xie, Hanzhe Zhang, Hanzhang Zhou

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We analyze the academic affiliations of nearly 6,000 award-winning researchers in 18 major fields in the natural sciences, engineering, and social sciences from the 1820s to the 2020s, focusing on the 1960s onward. The analysis reveals a trend of declining concentration in the institutional affiliations of award-winning researchers, shifting from a few science-strong universities in high-income countries to a more diverse set of institutions across the world. The decline in concentration is observed in all fields except one: economics. The institutional affiliations of prizewinning economists have become more concentrated over time, making economics the most concentrated field. We associate the higher concentration of prizewinning work in economics with the field’s stronger sorting by institutional prestige, its lower reliance on specialized equipment and instruments, and its assessment of findings based on a synthesis of evidence rather than on decisive experiments or proofs. We discuss the benefits and costs of this high and rising institutional concentration of prizewinning economists.

中文摘要

我们分析了1820年代至2020年代、涵盖自然科学、工程学和社会科学18个主要领域中近6000名获奖研究者的学术机构隶属关系，重点关注1960年代以后。分析揭示了一个趋势：获奖研究者的机构隶属集中度在下降，从高收入国家的少数科学强校转向全球更多样化的机构。这种集中度下降在除一个领域外的所有领域都被观察到：经济学。获奖经济学家的机构隶属关系随时间变得更加集中，使经济学成为集中度最高的领域。我们将经济学获奖工作更高的集中度与该领域更强的机构声望排序、对专业设备和仪器的较低依赖，以及基于证据综合而非决定性实验或证明来评估研究结果联系起来。我们讨论了获奖经济学家这种高企且上升的机构集中度的收益与成本。

Tax Planning and Multinational Behavior

税收筹划与跨国公司行为

Rosanne Altshuler, Lysle Boller, Kevin A. Roberts, Juan Carlos Suárez Serrato

NBER 工作论文 · 公共财政与税收 · 美国

Abstract

We use newly linked IRS data to study how US multinationals use tax-planning structures that exploit mismatches between US and foreign tax laws. In 2016, these structures accounted for 36% of foreign profits despite being adopted by only 18% of firms. We develop and estimate a structural model using adoption patterns and changes in foreign effective tax rates, domestic and foreign investment, and global R&D following adoption. The model implies substantial heterogeneity in adoption costs and benefits and shows that restricting tax planning creates tradeoffs between tax revenue and economic activity that are amplified by global R&D linkages.

我们使用新近链接的IRS数据，研究美国跨国公司如何利用美国与外国税法之间的错配来实施税收筹划结构。2016年，这些结构占外国利润的36%，尽管仅有18%的企业采用了它们。我们利用采用模式以及采用后外国有效税率、国内外投资和全球研发的变化，开发并估计了一个结构模型。该模型意味着采用成本和收益存在显著异质性，并表明限制税收筹划会在税收收入和经济活动之间产生权衡，而这种权衡因全球研发联系而被放大。

In Utero Exposure to the 1982 Tylenol Recall and Neurodevelopmental Outcomes

1982年泰诺召回事件的子宫内暴露与神经发育结局

Christopher M. Worsham, Charles Bray, Joshua Rothman, Brian Kwan, Anupam Jena

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 美国

Abstract

Observational research into the relationship between maternal acetaminophen use during pregnancy and a child’s subsequent risk of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) or autism has been limited by confounding factors. Using two large national U.S. insurance claims databases and a novel natural experiment, we assessed differences in prevalence of ADHD and autism among children whose gestational periods were impacted by a nationwide Tylenol recall issued in 1982, following deaths from Tylenol capsules containing potassium cyanide. Prevalence of ADHD and autism was not significantly different in birth cohorts exposed to the recall during gestation, when maternal acetaminophen use would have been reduced; interrupted time series models showed no significant differences compared to underlying temporal diagnostic trends. These findings are inconsistent with a clinically meaningful effect of maternal acetaminophen use on subsequent development of neurodevelopmental disorders.

中文摘要

关于孕期母亲使用对乙酰氨基酚与孩子随后患注意力缺陷多动障碍（ADHD）或自闭症风险之间关系的观察性研究，一直受到混杂因素的限制。我们使用两个大型美国全国保险索赔数据库和一项新的自然实验，评估了妊娠期受到1982年全国性泰诺召回事件影响的儿童中ADHD和自闭症患病率的差异；该召回发生在含有氰化钾的泰诺胶囊导致死亡之后。在妊娠期暴露于召回事件的出生队列中，当时母亲的对乙酰氨基酚使用本应减少，ADHD和自闭症的患病率没有显著差异；中断时间序列模型显示，与潜在的时间诊断趋势相比没有显著差异。这些发现与母亲使用对乙酰氨基酚对随后神经发育障碍发展具有临床意义效应的观点不一致。

Estimating Heterogeneous Impacts with Endogenous Participation and Non-Compliance

在内生参与和不依从情况下估计异质性影响

Orazio Attanasio, Lina Cardona-Sosa, Ziqian Liu

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 加纳

Abstract

This paper considers the estimation of heterogeneous impacts within a Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT) in a situation where heterogeneous status is endogenous and is not fully observed in the control sample. We show how to use information in the treatment sample and a noisy signal on the heterogeneous status to estimate meaningful impacts under a minimal set of assumptions. We provide an application of this approach to a data set from Ghana, collected around a clustered RCT to evaluate the impacts of an early-years intervention, differentiating the impact between households that participate actively in the program from those who participate only passively, as recipients. We show that, for some outcomes, the properly estimated heterogeneous impacts differ substantially from those computed with a naive estimator.

中文摘要

The Effects of Long-Term Exposure to Pollution on Health

长期暴露于污染对健康的影响

Joshua S. Graff Zivin, Johnny Huynh, Timothy Frank Justicz, Adriana Lleras-Muney, Matthew J. Neidell

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 美国

Abstract

We provide the first causal evidence on how long-term exposure to fine particulate matter (PM2.5) at contemporary ambient levels contributes to the development of chronic disease in a young, initially healthy population. Our analysis links detailed personnel records for roughly 150,000 U.S. Army soldiers to high-resolution annual measures of PM2.5. Because soldiers enter service exceptionally healthy and receive uniform access to care, new diagnoses reflect pollution’s physiological impact rather than preexisting conditions or differential health care access. They are also required to relocate based on the needs of the Army, which provides us with quasi-random variation in initial duty-station assignments that we use as an instrument for average annual pollution exposure over a soldier’s career. We find that sustained PM2.5 exposure substantially increases circulatory disease, particularly hypertension, with mixed evidence on respiratory conditions that are sensitive to specification. Exposure over shorter durations, by contrast, has little detectable effect, a pattern consistent with the cumulative nature of pollution’s health burden. These findings demonstrate that even moderate long-term exposure to air pollution can erode health capital in ways that are invisible to studies focused on short-run variations or mortality alone, and they highlight the importance of regulatory standards that account for chronic exposure risks.

中文摘要

我们提供了首个因果证据，说明在当代环境水平下长期暴露于细颗粒物（PM2.5）如何促成一个年轻且初始健康人群中慢性疾病的发展。我们的分析将大约150,000名美国陆军士兵的详细人事记录与高分辨率年度PM2.5测量数据相链接。由于士兵入伍时极为健康，并且获得统一的医疗服务，新诊断反映的是污染的生理影响，而不是既存疾病或医疗服务获取差异。他们还根据陆军需要被要求迁移，这为我们提供了初始驻地分配的准随机变异，我们将其用作士兵职业生涯中年均污染暴露的工具变量。我们发现，持续的PM2.5暴露显著增加循环系统疾病，尤其是高血压，而对呼吸系统疾病的证据则因设定不同而混合。相比之下，较短时间的暴露几乎没有可检测到的影响，这一模式与污染健康负担的累积性质一致。这些发现表明，即使是中等的长期空气污染暴露，也会以仅关注短期变化或死亡率的研究所看不见的方式侵蚀健康资本，并凸显了考虑慢性暴露风险的监管标准的重要性。

Aligning Platform and User Incentives in Social Media Age Restrictions

在社交媒体年龄限制中协调平台与用户激励

Leonardo Bursztyn, Rafael Jiménez-Durán, Aaron Leonard, Filip Milojević, Christopher Roth

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Several countries are adopting social media bans for teenagers, yet early evidence suggests limited adherence to the age-based restrictions. Such bans ask platforms to remove profitable customers and teens to give up access to their online social networks. We study an alternative regulatory regime in which platforms remain liable for teens on the adult tier but may offer a safe tier that removes restricted features, such as algorithmic feeds, while preserving interactions with their relevant network. Provision of the safe tier and enforcement are complementary: teens can adhere without giving up access to their network, and platforms can retain displaced adult-tier users as lower-margin customers. This policy design makes the teen decision to avoid the adult tier less dependent on peer dynamics and increases teen welfare for a relevant range of regulatory penalties.

中文摘要

Voluntary Corporate Climate Targets in a Patchwork Policy Landscape

碎片化政策格局下的企业自愿气候目标

Joseph E. Aldy, Michael W. Toffel, Alison J. Ong, Lavender Yang

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Many large, publicly-traded companies adopt voluntary greenhouse gas emission reduction commitments in the presence of a fragmented public policy landscape. To explore the potential emission implications of such commitments, we develop a framework for understanding how firms’ voluntary emission targets interact with carbon pricing, regulatory mandates, subsidies, and heterogeneous policies across political jurisdictions. This analysis highlights the difficulty of distinguishing incremental voluntary emission abatement from policy-induced emission reductions. We introduce the Salata Institute Corporate Climate Targets Database, which we produced from public domain reports published by Russell 3000 firms over 2000-2024. The database documents the growth, form, scope, and sectoral distribution of voluntary greenhouse gas emission targets, including net-zero, carbon-neutral, carbon-reduction, energy-related, and qualitative commitments. Comparing this database with CDP, a non-profit organization formerly known as the Carbon Disclosure Project and the most common source of corporate emission targets used in the research literature, reveals substantial omissions in their survey-based data, especially in recent years. We show that voluntary corporate targets often mirror government-established goals and public policies, and some firms benchmark their internal carbon prices to existing carbon taxes and emission trading systems. The paper concludes by outlining empirical challenges and future research needs for evaluating the emission impacts of voluntary commitments.

Abstract

A critical tension in global governance is that trade agreements have evolved largely in isolation from climate policy. This paper shows that the two domains generate systematic cross-externalities: larger gains from trade are associated with greater climate externalities imposed on partners, while linking carbon taxes to trade agreements produces distributive externalities that undermine the balance of trade concessions. To address this tension, we present a framework to integrate harmonized carbon pricing into the WTO subject to institutional and political-feasibility constraints. We explore two linkage designs. The first is a centralized Climate Fund, where all members apply a common demand-side carbon tax and contribute a portion of the revenues to the fund, capped by fiscal constraints. The Fund then reallocates these contributions to balance the tax burden across countries, subject to informational constraints. Quantitative analysis shows that even a simple fund allocation rule can sustain a carbon price of $138 per ton of CO2, reducing global emissions by 54%. The main binding constraint is informational: knowing the precise incidence of the carbon tax across countries ex ante would raise the feasible price to $265. The second is a decentralized design without transfers. Each member meets a carbon tax revenue floor but retains full discretion over its mix of demand-side and supply-side taxes. Energy exporters choose supply-side taxes while energy importers opt for demand-side taxes. These choices organically balance the tax burden and yield a 47% reduction in global emissions, almost rivaling the centralized Climate Fund.

Many regression analyses use observations that are correlated within uncorrelated clusters and/or are correlated in distance or other spatial measure. Such data are often positively correlated. A companion paper, Cameron and Miller (2026), presents inference when correlation is spatially dampening in the distance between observations. The two papers are intended to provide a guide to empirical researchers.
