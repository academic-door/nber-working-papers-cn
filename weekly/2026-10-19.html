The Why and How of Foreign Aid

对外援助的动因与方式

Dean Karlan

NBER 工作论文 · 其他主题 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Foreign aid has become a melting pot of motivations and policies, where differently-motivated actors often converge on recommended policies while the similarly-motivated diverge. Breaking down the “why” and the “how” can illuminate real tensions and reveal false ones. The “why” asks which problems to tackle and who benefits: the recipient, the donor through economic, political, or soft-power returns, or everyone through global public goods. The “how” asks which strategy most cost-effectively addresses a chosen problem. Evidence is central to the “how”: use it where it exists, produce it where it does not. In particular, approaches which improve state capacity to use and generate evidence can satisfy multiple objectives: strengthen recipient countries, benefit donors, and provide a global public good of knowledge. Ultimately, the “how” is not a linear process, but rather an iterative one of learning, adopting, adapting, repeating, and then sharing so others can learn as well.

中文摘要

对外援助已成为动机与政策的熔炉，不同动机的行为主体常常在推荐政策上趋同，而动机相似者却出现分歧。拆解“为什么”与“怎么做”可以揭示真实的张力并识别虚假的张力。“为什么”追问要解决哪些问题以及谁受益：受援国、通过经济、政治或软实力回报的援助国，还是通过全球公共品使所有人受益。“怎么做”追问哪种策略能以最具成本效益的方式解决选定的问题。证据对“怎么做”至关重要：在已有证据之处使用证据，在缺乏证据之处生产证据。特别是，提升国家使用和生成证据能力的方法可以满足多重目标：增强受援国、惠及援助国，并提供知识的全球公共品。最终，“怎么做”不是线性过程，而是学习、采纳、调整、重复、再分享以使他人也能学习的迭代过程。

Correcting for Heterogeneity in the Use of Subjective Scales

校正主观量表使用中的异质性

Daniel J. Benjamin, Kristen B. Cooper, Ori Heffetz, Miles S. Kimball, Jiannan Zhou

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Analyses of survey data may be confounded if people use response scales differently. We formalize and generalize the theory behind using “calibration questions,” such as vignettes, to correct for scale-use heterogeneity. Finding, in our (N ≈ 10,000) and existing data, that the mapping between respondents’ scales is approximately linear, we develop a simple estimator for scale-use-corrected regressions. We validate it using subjective-scale ratings of height and weight and illustrate it with life satisfaction regressions, where correction leaves the main qualitative findings intact but can substantially change coefficient ratios, for example tripling the estimated cost of unemployment relative to income.

中文摘要

如果人们以不同方式使用回答量表，调查数据分析可能会受到混杂。我们形式化并推广了使用“校准问题”（如情境 vignettes）来校正量表使用异质性的理论。在我们的数据（N ≈ 10,000）和现有数据中发现受访者量表之间的映射近似线性后，我们开发了一个用于量表使用校正回归的简单估计量。我们使用身高和体重的主观量表评分对其进行验证，并用生活满意度回归进行说明，其中校正使主要定性发现保持不变，但可能大幅改变系数比率，例如使失业相对于收入的估计成本增至三倍。

Too Little too Late? Nationwide Carbon Trading in China

太少太迟？中国全国碳交易

Shuang Zhang, Douglas Almond

NBER 工作论文 · 环境与气候 · 印度、印度尼西亚、菲律宾

Abstract

China launched a nationwide, rate-based carbon market in 2021. Covering the world’s top CO2 emitter, compliance depends on the intensity of carbon emissions rather than an absolute cap. We draw on the theoretical literature of rate-based systems to provide the first empirical evidence on an early adopter. Using plant-level data from satellite imagery and machine learning methods, we find that high-efficiency power plants increased electricity generation and CO2 emissions by approximately 5% relative to low-efficiency plants. Because its carbon market regulates nearly all coal plants in China, we focus on the differential responses by plant efficiency, finding no evidence of a differential reduction in emission intensity (though these estimates are imprecise). Relative to coal plants in Indonesia and the Philippines, which had no carbon market, China’s high-efficiency plants also increased emissions, while the estimate for its low-efficiency plants is small and insignificant. This pattern of responses suggests that emissions increases of high-efficiency plants exceeded substitution away from low-efficiency plants. Underscoring the production stimulus to more efficient Chinese plants, air quality worsened downwind of high- efficiency plants relative to low-efficiency plants, compromising air quality in relatively clean areas of China. If China's goal is to reduce its carbon emissions, then benchmarks will need to be tightened within the existing system or an alternative system that does not simultaneously incentivize electricity production will need to be adopted.

中文摘要

中国于2021年启动了全国性的基于强度的碳市场。该市场覆盖全球最大二氧化碳排放国，其履约取决于碳排放强度而非绝对上限。我们借鉴基于强度体系的理论文献，提供了关于早期采用者的首个实证证据。利用来自卫星影像的工厂级数据和机器学习方法，我们发现高效率电厂相对于低效率电厂将发电量和二氧化碳排放量增加了约5%。由于其碳市场几乎监管中国的所有燃煤电厂，我们聚焦于按电厂效率的差异化反应，未发现排放强度差异化下降的证据（尽管这些估计不精确）。相对于没有碳市场的印度尼西亚和菲律宾燃煤电厂，中国的高效率电厂也增加了排放，而对其低效率电厂的估计较小且不显著。这种反应模式表明，高效率电厂的排放增加超过了从低效率电厂的替代。突显对中国更高效电厂的生产刺激的是，高效率电厂下风向的空气质量相对于低效率电厂恶化，损害了中国相对清洁地区的空气质量。如果中国的目标是减少碳排放，那么需要在现有体系内收紧基准，或采用不会同时激励发电的替代体系。

Staying Connected: Experimental Effects of Coach-Delivered Text Messages in an Adult High School

保持联系：成人高中教练发送短信的实验效应

Alberto Ortega, Pietro Pellerito, Patrick S. Turner

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 美国

Abstract

More than 23 million adults in the United States lack a high school diploma, and most who return to school drop out before graduating. We evaluate a randomized intervention of adult high school students in which coaches delivered text messages that encouraged persistence. We find that assignment to treatment increased graduation by 4.9 percentage points, and the effect of message receipt among compliers is 9.3 percentage points, a 41 percent increase relative to the control mean. The intervention operated on the intensive margin by increasing the number of courses completed, but did not affect student enrollment. The effects of the intervention are concentrated among students assigned to experienced coaches, who customized their messages more extensively. We contextualize the economic impact of the low-cost intervention by comparing labor market earnings of graduates to non-graduates, which implies an earnings benefit of $149 per treated student over four years.

中文摘要

美国有超过2300万成年人没有高中文凭，而大多数重返校园者在毕业前辍学。我们评估了一项针对成人高中学生的随机干预，其中教练发送鼓励坚持的短信。我们发现，被分配到处理组使毕业率提高了4.9个百分点，而在遵从者中收到短信的效应为9.3个百分点，相对于控制组均值提高了41%。该干预通过增加完成课程数量在集约边际上发挥作用，但未影响学生入学。干预效应集中在被分配到经验丰富的教练的学生中，这些教练更广泛地定制了短信。我们通过比较毕业生与非毕业生的劳动力市场收入来说明这一低成本干预的经济影响，这意味着每名受处理学生在四年内获得149美元的收益。

Will Medical Care Strangle or Save the Federal Budget?

医疗保健会扼杀还是拯救联邦预算？

David M. Cutler

NBER 工作论文 · 健康与医疗 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Forecasts of the federal budget depend greatly on trends in medical spending. Since 1970, medical spending has nearly tripled as a share of gross domestic product (GDP). However, spending growth has barely exceeded GDP growth since 2010. This difference in growth rates makes forecasting spending difficult. I present scenarios for future spending derived from considering the sources of spending increase, ranging from rapid increases in new, expensive treatments to a reduction in spending due to cost-reducing technology. Even modest increases in spending relative to GDP are likely to overwhelm the federal government’s ability to pay for medical care using current tax instruments.

中文摘要

联邦预算预测在很大程度上取决于医疗支出的趋势。自1970年以来，医疗支出占国内生产总值（GDP）的比重几乎增至三倍。然而，自2010年以来，支出增长仅略高于GDP增长。这种增长率差异使支出预测变得困难。我提出了未来支出的情景，这些情景源自对支出增长来源的考虑，范围从新的昂贵治疗方法的快速增长到因降低成本的技术而减少支出。即使支出相对于GDP的温和增长，也可能压倒联邦政府使用当前税收工具支付医疗保健的能力。

Understanding Migration Responses to Local Shocks

理解对地方冲击的迁移反应

Kirill Borusyak, Rafael Dix-Carneiro, Brian K. Kovak

NBER 工作论文 · 宏观与货币财政 · 巴西、德国

Abstract

A commonly used migration regression relates population changes to local labor demand shocks, often yielding small or statistically insignificant coefficients despite sizable effects on labor market outcomes. We argue that such findings need not imply that mobility is unresponsive to local economic conditions. Rather, conventional migration regressions can be biased, as they do not reflect how workers choose among competing locations. In a parsimonious equilibrium model, local population growth depends on differences between local shocks and those in relevant destinations; when these shocks are correlated, estimated responses are attenuated. Moreover, with industry-level shocks, standard shift-share measures misrepresent locations’ effective exposure, leading to further attenuation. Using simulation experiments based on Brazilian and German administrative data, we show attenuation can exceed 50% and that conventional regression estimates can be highly imprecise. We propose theory-grounded empirical approaches that correct the biases, deliver estimates with a clear structural interpretation, and improve the precision of estimated mobility responses.

中文摘要

一种常用的迁移回归将人口变化与地方劳动力需求冲击联系起来，尽管对劳动力市场结果有相当大的影响，却常常产生较小或统计上不显著的系数。我们认为，此类发现并不必然意味着流动性对地方经济状况无反应。相反，传统迁移回归可能存在偏误，因为它们未反映工人如何在竞争性地点之间进行选择。在一个简约的均衡模型中，地方人口增长取决于地方冲击与相关目的地冲击之间的差异；当这些冲击相关时，估计反应会被衰减。此外，对于行业层面冲击，标准移动份额指标误表了地点的有效暴露，导致进一步衰减。使用基于巴西和德国行政数据的模拟实验，我们表明衰减可能超过50%，且传统回归估计可能高度不精确。我们提出有理论依据的实证方法，以校正偏误、提供具有清晰结构解释的估计，并提高估计流动性反应的精确度。

The Role of Organization in Firm-Sponsored Training

组织在企业资助培训中的作用

David J. Deming, Raffaella Sadun, Christopher T. Stanton

NBER 工作论文 · 教育与劳动 · 全球/多国

Abstract

Some firms are more likely than others to invest in workers’ skills. We document this phenomenon and discuss its implications in light of models of firm-sponsored skills training. The data show that better managed firms, even in the same industries and labor markets, are more likely to train, while training clusters around either managerial or frontline worker development. We argue that organizational differences provide incentives for firms to train beyond traditional explanations that focus on labor market frictions. We illustrate how organizational heterogeneity can generate training investments through a knowledge hierarchy framework where top managers with different skills build bespoke organizations tailored to their unique needs. This in turn makes general skill firm-specific, allowing firms to capture returns on training investment.

中文摘要

一些企业比其他企业更倾向于投资于员工技能。我们记录了这一现象，并结合企业资助技能培训的模型讨论了其含义。数据显示，即使在相同行业和劳动力市场中，管理更好的企业也更可能提供培训，而培训则围绕管理者或一线员工的发展形成集群。我们认为，组织差异为企业提供了培训激励，这超出了聚焦于劳动力市场摩擦的传统解释。我们通过一个知识层级框架说明组织异质性如何产生培训投资：在该框架中，拥有不同技能的顶层管理者构建量身定制的组织，以满足其独特需求。这反过来使通用技能变得企业特定，从而使企业能够获取培训投资的回报。

Venture Capital and Customer Capital

风险投资与客户资本

Martin Aragoneses, Young Soo Jang, Amir Sufi

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 未指明区域

Abstract

Venture capital specializes in the financing of customer capital intensive firms—firms with higher sales and marketing expense to revenue ratios, a higher wage bill for sales and marketing employees, and a higher tendency to apply for trademarks—and they do so more than other financial intermediaries. Venture capitalists target high customer capital industries with low knowledge capital but avoid high knowledge capital industries that lack customer capital. Firms tilt investment toward customer capital leading up to the initial venture capital investment, after which their scale accelerates rapidly. Venture capitalists focus on financing customer capital because such investments require tolerance for substantial risks of failure in pursuit of extreme upside potential, as characterized by power law returns. This return profile fits customer capital investments more closely than investments in knowledge capital. Venture capitalists that sprinkle small investments across a large number of firms, colloquially referred to as the “spray and pray” strategy, target customer capital industries for investment, whereas venture capitalists that commit larger amounts to a small number of firms target knowledge capital industries. The venture capital focus on customer capital reflects a shift away from its traditional focus on knowledge capital intensive firms over the past 30 years.

中文摘要

风险投资专门为客户资本密集型企业融资——这些企业具有更高的销售与营销费用占收入比率、更高的销售与营销员工工资支出，以及更高的商标申请倾向——而且它们比其他金融中介更常这样做。风险投资家瞄准客户资本高但知识资本低的行业，而避开缺乏客户资本的知识资本高行业。企业在首次风险投资进入之前将投资向客户资本倾斜，此后其规模迅速加速扩张。风险投资家专注于为客户资本融资，因为此类投资需要容忍追求极端上行潜力过程中的重大失败风险，其特征是幂律回报。这种回报特征比知识资本投资更贴近客户资本投资。将小额投资分散于大量企业的风险投资家——俗称“撒胡椒面”策略——瞄准客户资本行业进行投资，而将较大金额投入少数企业的风险投资家则瞄准知识资本行业。风险投资对客户资本的关注反映了过去30年其从传统上聚焦知识资本密集型企业发生的转变。

Firms' Investment and the Real Transmission of the Global Financial Cycle

企业投资与全球金融周期的实体传导

Caitlin Hegarty, Matias Moretti, Pablo Ottonello, Diego J. Perez

NBER 工作论文 · 金融与资产定价 · 全球/多国

Abstract

We study how global financial conditions transmit to firms’ investment. We document that increases in risk premia are followed by large investment contractions, particularly among firms with higher default risk. We develop a quantitative heterogeneous-firm open-economy model where firms finance investment with defaultable debt priced by risk-averse global investors. We identify a strong risk channel linking changes in the global price of risk to financing costs through firms’ quantity of risk—the sensitivity of debt repayment to global shocks. This introduces a risk dimension to stabilization policy: smoothing cash flows reduces firms’ quantity of risk and dampens international financial transmission.
