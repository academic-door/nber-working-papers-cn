Optimal Taxation of Indistinguishable Activities

不可区分活动的最优征税

Jacob Goldin, Sebastian Koehne, Nicholas Lawson

公共财政与税收 未指明区域

Abstract

Optimal tax policy frequently prescribes different tax treatments for activities that are difficult in practice for tax administrators to distinguish, such as expenditures undertaken for business versus personal purposes. We study the optimal design of income tax deductions for such expenditures under the assumption that a common deduction rate must be applied to both forms of the expenditure. The Pareto optimal common deduction turns on the relative compensated spending response for each margin, weighted by the income tax wedge. We also characterize the welfare gain from distinguishing between the two expenditure forms as a harmonic mean of the compensated responses. We illustrate both results with an application to air travel expenses.

中文摘要

最优税收政策经常对税务管理者在实践中难以区分的活动规定不同的税收待遇，例如为商业目的与个人目的发生的支出。我们研究在此类支出上所得税扣除的最优设计，假设必须对两种形式的支出适用共同的扣除率。帕累托最优的共同扣除取决于每一边际的相对补偿性支出反应，并以所得税楔子加权。我们还刻画了区分两种支出形式所带来的福利收益，其等于补偿性反应的调和平均数。我们用航空旅行支出的应用来说明这两个结果。
