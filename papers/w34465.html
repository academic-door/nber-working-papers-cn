Facilitated enrollment in public health insurance: Effects on prenatal care and birth outcomes

促进公共健康保险参保：对产前护理和出生结局的影响

Janet Currie, Lucy G. Hackett, Fernanda Marquez-Padilla

健康与医疗 墨西哥

Abstract

Many countries are expanding public health insurance for the uncovered, but administrative barriers can reduce enrollments. However, lowering barriers may insure people with marginal health benefits and crowd out services to incumbents. We use novel longitudinal administrative childbirth data to examine the impact of Medical Insurance Century XXI (SMS), a children’s program that auto-enrolled family members in Mexico’s Seguro Popular (SP) health insurance. SMS increased SP coverage among pregnant women, especially those who previously delivered in private hospitals. This group increased the utilization of prenatal care, without significant changes in birth outcomes, and without negative spillover effects on incumbent women.

中文摘要

许多国家正在为未参保人群扩大公共健康保险，但行政障碍可能会减少参保。然而，降低障碍可能会为边际健康效益的人群提供保险，并挤占对现有参保人的服务。我们使用新颖的纵向行政分娩数据来检验墨西哥“二十一世纪医疗保险”（SMS）的影响，这是一个儿童项目，自动将家庭成员纳入墨西哥的“全民健康保险”（Seguro Popular, SP）。SMS提高了孕妇的SP覆盖率，尤其是那些以前在私立医院分娩的孕妇。这一群体增加了产前护理的利用，但出生结局没有显著变化，也没有对现有参保女性产生负面溢出效应。
