Balancing Growth in a Warming World

在变暖的世界中平衡增长

Adrien Bilal, Thibault Ingrand, Diego R. Känzig

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper develops a theory of balanced growth and optimal climate policy in an integrated assessment framework. Balanced growth is possible despite climate change, but at a slower rate. Two distinct policy regimes emerge because emissions permanently raise carbon stocks. For low damages, optimal growth coincides with the laissez-faire and carbon is priced at a constant rate. For high damages, optimal policy caps temperature growth to prevent escalating losses and achieve faster economic growth than in the laissez-faire by pricing carbon at an increasing rate. We quantify our model and show that empirical damage estimates span both regimes. For low damages, optimal policy achieves a 1% welfare gain with 3 to 8°C of warming per century and no change in long-run growth. For high damages, optimal policy raises annual growth by 0.8 percentage points and achieves a 26% welfare gain by capping warming at 0.4°C per century.

中文摘要

本文在综合评估框架下发展了平衡增长与最优气候政策的理论。尽管存在气候变化，平衡增长仍是可能的，但速度较慢。由于排放会永久性地提高碳存量，因此出现了两种不同的政策机制。对于低损害，最优增长与自由放任一致，碳定价为恒定比率。对于高损害，最优政策通过以递增的比率对碳定价，限制温度增长以防止损失升级，并实现比自由放任更快的经济增长。我们对模型进行了量化，并表明实证损害估计跨越了两种机制。对于低损害，最优政策在每世纪3至8摄氏度的变暖情况下实现1%的福利收益，且长期增长不变。对于高损害，最优政策通过将变暖限制在每世纪0.4摄氏度，使年增长率提高0.8个百分点，并实现26%的福利收益。
