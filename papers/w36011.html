Investment and Governance: Through The Lens of Sustainability

投资与治理：可持续性视角

Jitendra Aswani, Roberto Rigobon

环境与气候 未指明区域

Abstract

Using 3,944 sustainable bond issuances by public firms from 2013 to 2022, we examine the causal effect of sustainable investment on governance adoption. A heteroskedasticity-based identification strategy reveals that a 1% increase in sustainable debt (by total debt) raises the likelihood of sustainable governance adoption by 9.2%. Such adoption improves environmental performance by 16% (structural estimates). These effects are confined to non-financial firms; for financial firms, governance adoption alone is in-sufficient, as sustainable loan borrowers environmental performance does not improve. Firms with stronger environmental performance are less likely to adopt sustainable governance. Results are robust to alternative definitions and extensive specification checks.

中文摘要

我们使用2013年至2022年间上市公司发行的3,944笔可持续债券，考察可持续投资对治理采纳的因果效应。基于异方差性的识别策略显示，可持续债务（占总债务）每增加1%，采纳可持续治理的可能性提高9.2%。这种采纳使环境绩效提高16%（结构估计）。这些效应仅限于非金融企业；对于金融企业，仅采纳治理是不够的，因为可持续贷款借款人的环境绩效并未改善。环境绩效较强的企业更不可能采纳可持续治理。结果在替代定义和大量设定检验下保持稳健。
