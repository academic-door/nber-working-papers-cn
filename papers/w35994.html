NBER Working Paper w35994所在周报

The Impact of Climate Change on Fisheries

气候变化对渔业的影响

Fabio Cevenini, Christopher Costello, Carlo Fezzi

环境与气候 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper provides the first causal estimate of the effect of ocean temperature variations on fishing revenue. We develop a simple theoretical model underpinning an empirical specification estimated on 2.5 million spatially explicit observations of fishing revenue from all European vessels for 2013–2022. The identification strategy leverages a novel metric, Marine Degree Days, which quantifies species-specific temperature exposure relative to biologically established thermal tolerance intervals. Within this framework we address key identification challenges, including spatial selection and ecological interdependencies across species. We find that deviations from species’ thermal preferences lead to significant losses in fishing revenue, with heat exposure causing the most pronounced impacts. These effects are mediated by species’ vertical mobility and ecological characteristics. Projections across different climate scenarios point to widespread revenue declines by 2100, reaching €560 million annual losses in the warmest scenario, though impacts are highly spatially heterogeneous. Lower-income, equatorial areas are expected to suffer the most, while some colder regions experience gains.

中文摘要

本文首次提供了海洋温度变化对渔业收入影响的因果估计。我们构建了一个简单的理论模型，作为实证设定的基础，该设定基于2013–2022年所有欧洲船只250万个空间明确的渔业收入观测值进行估计。识别策略利用了一个新指标——海洋度日，该指标量化了相对于生物学确立的热耐受区间的物种特定温度暴露。在此框架内，我们解决了关键的识别挑战，包括空间选择和物种间的生态相互依赖。我们发现，偏离物种的热偏好会导致渔业收入的显著损失，其中热暴露造成的影响最为显著。这些效应由物种的垂直移动性和生态特征所中介。不同气候情景下的预测表明，到2100年收入将普遍下降，在最暖情景下达到每年5.6亿欧元的损失，尽管影响在空间上高度异质。低收入赤道地区预计受害最深，而一些较冷地区则经历收益。
