The Impact of Central Bank Stock Purchases: Evidence from Discontinuities in Policy Rules

央行股票购买的影响：来自政策规则不连续性的证据

Masao Fukui, Masayuki Yagasaki

宏观与货币财政 日本

Abstract

We trace the impact of central bank stock purchases by exploiting discontinuities in the Bank of Japan's policy rule, which triggers purchases when stock market index movements fall below a threshold. In normal times, the purchases raise the long-term interest rate while leaving no detectable impact on stock prices. After the introduction of yield curve control, which pegs the long-term yield at 0%, interest rates stop responding and stock prices rise robustly following the purchases. Although these results are inconsistent with the inelastic market hypothesis in its original form, general equilibrium extensions with endogenous interest rates explain our findings.

中文摘要

我们通过利用日本银行政策规则中的不连续性来追踪央行股票购买的影响；当股票市场指数变动低于某一阈值时，该规则会触发购买。在正常时期，这些购买会提高长期利率，同时对股价没有可检测到的影响。在引入将长期收益率钉在0%的收益率曲线控制之后，利率不再响应，而股价在购买后稳健上升。尽管这些结果与原形式的市场非弹性假说不一致，但带有内生利率的一般均衡扩展能够解释我们的发现。
