Anti-Harassment Policy and the Startup Labor Market

反骚扰政策与初创企业劳动力市场

Jun Chen, Song Ma, Feng Zhang

教育与劳动 美国

Abstract

This paper examines how anti-harassment reforms that weaken non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) in sexual harassment cases affect the startup labor market. Using a staggered difference-in-differences design and LinkedIn data on over 50,000 U.S. VC-backed startups from 2014--2022, we find that NDA reforms reduce female hiring by about 8% annually without affecting male hiring, with effects concentrated among junior women and in small or male-dominated startups. Treated firms also experience more male manager departures, promote more women, and receive less VC funding. While NDA-weakening laws raise perceived legal risk and reduce female hiring, they trigger organizational restructuring that advances women into leadership.\n

中文摘要

本文考察了削弱性骚扰案件中保密协议（NDAs）的反骚扰改革如何影响初创企业劳动力市场。利用交错双重差分设计以及2014年至2022年间超过50,000家美国风险投资支持的初创企业的领英数据，我们发现NDA改革使女性雇佣人数每年减少约8%，而对男性雇佣没有影响，其效应集中在初级女性和小型或男性主导的初创企业中。受处理的企业也经历了更多的男性管理者离职，晋升了更多女性，并获得更少的风投资金。虽然削弱NDA的法律提高了感知的法律风险并减少了女性雇佣，但它们触发了组织重组，从而将女性推入领导岗位。
