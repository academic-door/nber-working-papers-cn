Instrumental Variables with Time-Varying Exposure: New Estimates of Revascularization Effects on Quality of Life

具有时变暴露的工具变量：血运重建对生活质量影响的新估计

Joshua Angrist, Bruno Ferman, Carol Gao, Peter Hull, Otavio L. Tecchio, Robert W. Yeh

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper develops instrumental variables (IV) estimators for dynamic causal effects in randomized trials with imperfect compliance. These methods are applied to a randomized trial that assigned patients with ischemic heart disease to either an invasive treatment arm centered on revascularization or a control group meant to receive non-invasive medical therapy. As is common in such “strategy trials,” many participants assigned to treatment remained untreated while many assigned to control crossed over into treatment. Protocol non-compliance causes ITT estimates to diverge from the effect of treatment received, while conventional per-protocol analyses that condition on treatment received are compromised by selection bias. Extending the static potential-outcomes IV framework, the methods here identify average causal effects of treatment for dynamic compliers, the set of trial participants who comply with trial protocol at different follow-up horizons. IV estimates of revascularization effects on compliers’ quality of life are markedly larger and more sustained than previously reported ITT and per-protocol estimates. We also show how to estimate average characteristics and marginal potential outcome means for dynamic compliers. These results are used to explain confounding in as-treated per-protocol estimates.

中文摘要

本文为不完全依从的随机试验中的动态因果效应开发了工具变量（IV）估计量。这些方法应用于一项随机试验，该试验将缺血性心脏病患者分配到以血运重建为中心的侵入性治疗组或旨在接受非侵入性药物治疗的对照组。正如这类“策略试验”中常见的情况，许多被分配到治疗组的参与者未接受治疗，而许多被分配到对照组的参与者则转向接受治疗。方案不依从导致意向性治疗（ITT）估计偏离实际接受治疗的效果，而传统的按方案分析（以接受治疗为条件）则因选择偏倚而受损。本文扩展了静态潜在结果IV框架，所提出的方法识别了动态依从者（即在不同随访时间点遵守试验方案的试验参与者集合）的平均因果治疗效果。对依从者血运重建对生活质量影响的IV估计明显大于且更持久于先前报告的ITT和按方案估计。我们还展示了如何估计动态依从者的平均特征和边际潜在结果均值。这些结果用于解释按治疗分析中的混杂偏倚。
