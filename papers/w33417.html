Ancient Epics in the Television Age: Religious Identity and the Rise of Hindu Nationalism in India

电视时代的古代史诗：宗教认同与印度教民族主义在印度的兴起

Dean Yang, Alessandro Saia, Akhila Kovvuri, Resuf Ahmed, Paul Brimble

教育与劳动 印度

Abstract

How does strengthened religious identity affect society and politics? We study the impact of “Ramayan,” the massively popular televised version of the Hindu epic. We conduct difference-in-differences analyses, measuring Ramayan exposure with TV signal strength in Indian localities at the time of airing in 1987. Localities with higher Ramayan exposure saw persistently strengthened Hindu religious identity, measured by the names given to newborns; increases in Hindu-Muslim communal violence during the Hindu-nationalist mobilization of the early 1990s; and longer-run increases in electoral success for the Hindu nationalist Bharatiya Janata Party. A battery of tests indicates these effects reflect exposure to Ramayan specifically, rather than television generally. Areas with greater exposure also saw increased presence of Hindu religious schools, a potential channel behind the long-run persistence of effects.

中文摘要

强化的宗教认同如何影响社会与政治？我们研究印度教史诗《罗摩衍那》广受欢迎的电视版的影响。我们采用双重差分分析，以1987年播出时印度各地的电视信号强度衡量《罗摩衍那》的暴露程度。暴露程度较高的地区，印度教宗教认同持续增强（以新生儿命名衡量）；在1990年代初印度教民族主义动员期间，印度教-穆斯林社群暴力事件增加；长期来看，印度教民族主义政党印度人民党的选举成功率上升。一系列检验表明，这些效应具体反映了对《罗摩衍那》的暴露，而非泛泛的电视影响。暴露程度较高的地区，印度教宗教学校的数量也有所增加，这可能是效应长期持续的一个潜在渠道。
