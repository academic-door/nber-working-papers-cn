How Do Financial Conditions Affect Professional Conduct? Evidence from Opioid Prescriptions

财务状况如何影响职业行为？来自阿片类药物处方的证据

Isil Erel, Shan Ge, Pengfei Ma

金融与资产定价 全球/多国

Abstract

Can personal finances affect professional conduct? We find that healthcare providers increase opioid prescriptions when experiencing unfavorable home value changes, a proxy for wealth shocks. Results hold with office-by-year fixed effects and hold for providers living far from their offices, largely ruling out patient demand explanations. Effects are stronger among lower-income providers, those practicing in lower-income ZIP codes, and those treating minority patients. Higher opioid prescriptions predict more future business. Providers with lower housing returns also perform more preference-sensitive surgeries, but not urgent ones. Our findings show how personal financial pressure shapes professional conduct, with implications beyond healthcare. \n\n

中文摘要

个人财务状况会影响职业行为吗？我们发现，当医疗服务提供者经历不利的房屋价值变化（财富冲击的代理变量）时，他们会增加阿片类药物的处方。结果在包含办公室×年份固定效应的模型中依然成立，并且对于居住地远离其办公室的提供者同样成立，这在很大程度上排除了患者需求解释。这种效应在低收入提供者、在低收入邮政编码区执业的提供者以及治疗少数族裔患者的提供者中更强。更高的阿片类药物处方预示着未来更多的业务。住房回报率较低的提供者也进行更多偏好敏感型手术，但非紧急手术。我们的发现表明个人财务压力如何塑造职业行为，其影响超出了医疗保健领域。
