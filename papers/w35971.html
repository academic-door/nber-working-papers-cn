Coase Meets Negishi: Property Rights, Welfare Weights, and Distributional Consequences in Climate Economics

科斯与根岸相遇：气候经济学中的产权、福利权重与分配后果

Simon F. Lang, Matthew D. Gordon, Matthew J. Kotchen

环境与气候 全球/多国

Abstract

We develop a property-rights based approach for defining welfare weights in economic models of climate change. Whether there is a "right to pollute" or a "right to no pollution" is increasingly contested in legislatures, courts, and international climate negotiations. We show how these notions of property rights can provide the basis for "Coasean welfare weights" that are applicable in multilateral public goods settings. Distributional questions are addressed through positive analysis, conditional on property rights, rather than normative views about redistribution. The implied transfers are consistent with a bargained solution over the net benefits of addressing climate change and reflect the value of property rights over emissions. The approach defines efficient allocations that generate Pareto improvements, in contrast to commonly used Negishi weights. We implement the approach in a global integrated assessment model to quantify what countries have at stake in ongoing political and legal debates about emissions-related property rights.

中文摘要

我们提出一种基于产权的方法，用于在气候变化经济模型中定义福利权重。是否存在“污染权”或“不受污染权”在立法机构、法院和国际气候谈判中日益受到争议。我们展示了这些产权概念如何为适用于多边公共品情境的“科斯式福利权重”提供基础。分配问题通过实证分析加以处理，以产权为条件，而非基于关于再分配的规范性观点。隐含的转移支付与就应对气候变化的净收益达成的谈判解相一致，并反映了排放产权的价值。该方法定义了产生帕累托改进的有效配置，与常用的根岸权重形成对比。我们在一个全球综合评估模型中实施该方法，以量化各国在关于排放相关产权的持续政治和法律辩论中的利害关系。
