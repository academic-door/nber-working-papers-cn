Gender Gaps: International Evidence on the Role of Family Policy

性别差距：家庭政策作用的国际证据

Francine D. Blau, Lawrence Kahn, Freddy Bachmann, Helen Burkhardt, Leonardo Peñaloza-Pacheco, David Titus

教育与劳动 卢森堡、美国

Abstract

We use Luxembourg Income Study and International Social Survey Programme microdata on 27 countries over the 1986-2019 period, augmented by policy and labor market information, to estimate the impact of family-friendly policies on gender gaps in employment, wages, earnings, and part-time work, as well as child penalties in employment and earnings. We find that longer duration of parental leave generally lowers gender gaps in wages, employment, earnings, and child penalties in employment and earnings, except for high levels of this entitlement, where further increases in leave raise gender gaps. We also find that the right to request a part-time work schedule raises relative female part-time employment but lowers employment and earnings gender gaps. Higher government expenditures for childcare lower employment, earnings, and part-time employment gender gaps as well as child employment and earnings penalties. These results are generally stronger for the less educated than for the highly educated, possibly reflecting higher costs of taking time off for highly educated women. Finally, expansion of family-friendly policies over this period can account for 24-33% of our sample’s reduction in gender gaps in employment and earnings and 46-76% of the reduction in child penalties in these outcomes. The slower expansion of such policies in the United States than elsewhere accounts for 6-15% of the slower reduction in US gender employment and earnings gaps and 20% of the slower fall in the US child employment penalty.

中文摘要

我们使用卢森堡收入研究和国际社会调查项目关于27个国家在1986-2019年期间的微观数据，并辅以政策和劳动力市场信息，估计家庭友好政策对就业、工资、收入和兼职工作中的性别差距，以及就业和收入中的子女惩罚的影响。我们发现，更长的育儿假期限总体上降低了工资、就业、收入中的性别差距以及就业和收入中的子女惩罚，但在该权利水平很高时除外——此时进一步增加假期会扩大性别差距。我们还发现，申请兼职工作安排的权利提高了女性相对兼职就业，但降低了就业和收入性别差距。更高的政府儿童保育支出降低了就业、收入和兼职就业性别差距，以及子女就业和收入惩罚。这些结果在受教育程度较低者中通常强于受教育程度较高者，这可能反映了受教育程度较高的女性休假的成本更高。最后，这一时期家庭友好政策的扩张可以解释我们样本中就业和收入性别差距缩小的24-33%，以及这些结果中子女惩罚缩小的46-76%。美国此类政策扩张慢于其他地方，这解释了美国性别就业和收入差距缩小较慢的6-15%，以及美国子女就业惩罚下降较慢的20%。
