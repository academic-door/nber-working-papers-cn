Federal Eminent Domain and Interstate Energy Infrastructure: Evidence from History

联邦征用权与州际能源基础设施：来自历史的证据

Zachary Liscow, Joseph S. Shapiro, Heidi L. Williams

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

We analyze a historical episode to generate evidence on how federal eminent domain authority affected construction of energy transportation infrastructure. The 1947 amendment to the Natural Gas Act gave federally approved interstate natural gas pipelines the power to acquire rights of way by eminent domain when negotiations with landowners failed. We compare construction of interstate gas pipelines with construction of oil pipelines and of within-state gas pipelines, neither of which was directly affected by the reform, using newly digitized historical government records and trade press listings. After 1947, annual additions to interstate gas pipeline mileage rose nearly sixfold while oil pipeline growth held steady, and the gain in interstate mileage was nearly five times the gain for within-state pipelines. Both comparisons suggest that the amendment substantially increased interstate gas pipeline construction. We discuss what this historical evidence implies for current congressional proposals to provide federal eminent domain authority for interstate electricity transmission.

中文摘要

我们分析一个历史事件，以生成关于联邦征用权如何影响能源运输基础设施建设证据。1947年《天然气法》修正案赋予联邦批准的州际天然气管道在与土地所有者谈判失败时通过征用权获得通行权的权力。我们使用新数字化的历史政府记录和行业媒体列表，比较州际天然气管道建设与石油管道和州内天然气管道建设，后两者均未直接受改革影响。1947年后，州际天然气管道里程的年增量增长了近六倍，而石油管道增长保持稳定，州际里程的增益几乎是州内管道增益的五倍。两项比较均表明，该修正案大幅增加了州际天然气管道建设。我们讨论这一历史证据对当前国会提案为州际电力传输提供联邦征用权的启示。
