Exorbitant Privilege of the Periodic Table? Geoeconomics, Endogenous Centrality and Strategic Minerals for the Green Transition

元素周期表的过高特权？地缘经济学、内生中心性与绿色转型的战略矿产

Josh Kirk, Evgenia Passari, Hélène Rey

宏观与货币财政 中国相关 美国

Abstract

Trade networks underpinning the energy transition are endogenous economic objects, and their structure is a source of market power. Using bilateral trade data for thirteen electrification metals over 1995–2023, we show that trade in these materials has reorganised into a hub-and-spoke system centred on China, in sharp contrast to the more diffuse, multilateral structure of fossil fuels. This centrality did not follow from resource endowments: China holds few of the underlying reserves. It was built through processing investment, industrial policy, and commodity-targeted development finance, an equilibrium outcome rather than a geographical accident. We formalise this logic in a stylised model in which a country chooses how central to become, and show that centrality can map into pricing power: the response of the world price of critical minerals to a supply cut is stronger when the network is more concentrated, the downstream demand and the competitive fringe supply are more inelastic. Constructing text-based, commodity-specific supply shocks and estimating local projections, we find that supply contractions in critical metals raise consumer inflation in the European Union and the United States by roughly twice as much as comparable fossil-fuel shocks, and more persistently. Centrality is therefore not merely a descriptive feature of the new resource map: it can create leverage over global prices. The green transition reduces strategic dependence on hydrocarbon exporters but reconstitutes it around the architecture of mineral supply chains.

中文摘要

支撑能源转型的贸易网络是内生的经济对象，其结构是市场势力的来源。利用1995–2023年间十三种电气化金属的双边贸易数据，我们表明这些材料的贸易已重组为一个以中国为中心的轴辐式体系，这与化石燃料更为分散、多边的结构形成鲜明对比。这种中心性并非源于资源禀赋：中国持有的基础储量很少。它是通过加工投资、产业政策和针对特定商品的开发性金融建立起来的，是一种均衡结果而非地理偶然。我们在一个程式化模型中形式化了这一逻辑，其中一国选择其中心化程度，并表明中心性可以转化为定价权：当网络更集中、下游需求和竞争性边缘供给更缺乏弹性时，关键矿产世界价格对供给削减的反应更强。构建基于文本的、特定商品的供给冲击并估计局部投影，我们发现关键金属的供给收缩使欧盟和美国的消费者通胀上升幅度约为可比化石燃料冲击的两倍，且更持久。因此，中心性不仅仅是新资源地图的一个描述性特征：它可以对全球价格产生杠杆作用。绿色转型减少了对碳氢化合物出口国的战略依赖，但围绕矿产供应链的架构重新构建了这种依赖。
