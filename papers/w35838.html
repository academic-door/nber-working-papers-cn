When Does Bad News Stick? Topic-Level Asymmetry in Narrative Dynamics

坏消息何时会持续？叙事动态中的主题层面不对称性

Isha Agarwal, Wentong Chen, Eswar S. Prasad

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

When does bad news stick? This paper shows that the persistence of media sentiment depends not only on whether news is good or bad, but also on what the news is about. Using the complete Wall Street Journal archive from 1990 to 2025, we construct monthly positive and negative sentiment indices for twelve economic topics and estimate topic-specific sentiment dynamics. Bad-news innovations generate larger cumulative responses than positive-sentiment innovations in every topic, indicating that negativity asymmetry is a broad feature of media sentiment. But the size of this asymmetry varies sharply across topics. The bad-minus-positive persistence gap is largest in security and human rights, firms and corporate strategy, health and public safety, and real estate and financial stability. Bad-news persistence is also sizable in data-rich topics such as financial markets, real estate, and corporate news, suggesting that frequent information arrival does not necessarily lead narratives to revert quickly. These findings show that aggregate sentiment indices mask substantial topic-level heterogeneity in the asymmetric dynamics of narratives, with potential implications for how persistent negative sentiment shapes expectations, asset prices, and real economic outcomes.

中文摘要

坏消息何时会持续？本文表明，媒体情绪的持续性不仅取决于新闻是好是坏，还取决于新闻涉及什么内容。使用 1990 年至 2025 年完整的《华尔街日报》档案，我们为 12 个经济主题构建了月度正面和负面情绪指数，并估计了主题特定的情绪动态。在每个主题中，坏消息冲击产生的累积响应都大于正面情绪冲击，表明负面性不对称是媒体情绪的一个广泛特征。但这种不对称的幅度在不同主题之间差异显著。坏消息与正面消息的持续性差距在安全与人权、企业与企业战略、健康与公共安全，以及房地产与金融稳定中最大。坏消息的持续性在数据丰富的主题中也相当可观，例如金融市场、房地产和企业新闻，这表明频繁的信息到来并不一定使叙事迅速回归。这些发现表明，总体情绪指数掩盖了叙事不对称动态中显著的主题层面异质性，并对持续性负面情绪如何塑造预期、资产价格和实际经济结果具有潜在含义。
