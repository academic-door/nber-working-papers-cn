The Returns to International Migration in a Free Mobility Setting: Evidence from Austria and Germany

自由流动环境下的国际移民回报：来自奥地利和德国的证据

Andrea Weber, David Card, Wolfgang Dauth, Wolfgang Frimmel, Julia Schmieder, Rudolf Winter-Ebmer

教育与劳动 奥地利、德国

Abstract

How big are the gains to international migration? How do they vary across migrants? We study these questions using linked administrative data for Austria and Germany, focusing on migrants who were steadily employed 2-4 years before their move and using their previous co-workers as a comparison group for a difference-in-differences design. We combine data from the origin and destination countries for 5 years after the move, allowing us to assess the gains for those who remain in the destination and those who return. On average, migrants from Austria to Germany experience an immediate and persistent rise of around 13% in their daily wage relative to their matched co-workers, while migrants from Germany to Austria gain about 4%. The higher returns for Austrians are mainly driven by higher average wages in Germany, rather than by differential selection of migrants. Consistent with a dynamic learning model, the returns to migration are largest for migrants who remain in the destination country, smaller for those who return after 1-4 years, and zero or even slightly negative for those who return in less than a year. They are also larger for migrants who follow established cross-border networks between workplaces. To help interpret our findings we conduct a parallel analysis of domestic migrants who move to a larger city (Vienna or one of the 5 largest cities in Germany). “Big city” movers experience gains in wages of about 5% for Austrians and 10% for Germans. Again the gains are largest for those who remain in the destination city, with smaller effects for those who move on.

中文摘要

国际移民的收益有多大？这些收益在不同移民之间如何变化？我们利用奥地利和德国的关联行政数据研究这些问题，重点关注在迁移前2-4年稳定就业的移民，并使用其此前的同事作为双重差分设计的对照组。我们结合了来源国和目的地国在迁移后5年的数据，从而能够评估留在目的地者和返回者的收益。平均而言，从奥地利迁移到德国的移民相对于其匹配同事，日工资立即且持续上升约13%，而从德国迁移到奥地利的移民收益约为4%。奥地利人更高的回报主要由德国更高的平均工资驱动，而非移民的差异性选择。与动态学习模型一致，移民回报对留在目的地国的移民最大，对1-4年后返回者较小，对不到一年就返回者为零甚至略为负。对于遵循工作场所之间既有跨境网络的移民，回报也更大。为帮助解释我们的发现，我们对迁往更大城市（维也纳或德国5个最大城市之一）的国内移民进行了平行分析。“大城市”迁移者的工资收益对奥地利人约为5%，对德国人约为10%。同样，留在目的地城市者收益最大，继续迁移者效应较小。
