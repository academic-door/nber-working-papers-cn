Shrink Responsibly: Empirical Bayes in Economics

负责任地收缩：经济学中的经验贝叶斯方法

Peter Hull, Michal Kolesár

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

Empirical researchers are often interested in the relative causal impact of individuals or institutions such as teachers, schools, hospitals, or neighborhoods. Regression-based estimates of such unit-specific parameters are often noisy; empirical Bayes (eB) methods can help isolate the signal from the estimation noise. This article provides an overview of eB methodology, showing how eB shrinkage can improve compound decisions—such as selecting individuals with the highest causal impacts—even when the working model motivating the shrinkage is misspecified. Not all analyses benefit from eB shrinkage, however, and we discuss which types of causal summaries are best estimated directly and how to do so robustly. We illustrate practical takeaways with a running analysis of public elementary and middle school value-added.

中文摘要

实证研究者常关注个体或机构（如教师、学校、医院或社区）的相对因果影响。基于回归的此类单位特定参数估计往往噪声较大；经验贝叶斯（eB）方法有助于从估计噪声中分离信号。本文概述eB方法，展示即使推动收缩的工作模型设定有误，eB收缩也能改善复合决策——例如选择因果影响最高的个体。然而，并非所有分析都能从eB收缩中受益；我们讨论哪些类型的因果汇总最适合直接估计，以及如何稳健地做到这一点。我们通过对公立中小学增值的连续分析来说明实际要点。
