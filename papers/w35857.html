NBER Working Paper w35857所在周报

Capital-Skill Complementarity Beyond Firm Boundaries

超越企业边界的资本-技能互补性

Gert Bijnens, Anders Humlum, Stijn Vanormelingen

教育与劳动 比利时

Abstract

New machinery has long been argued to raise the relative demand for skilled labor, yet firm-level evidence finds no such shift at investing firms. We resolve this puzzle by looking beyond firm boundaries. Linking Belgian data on capital investments, buyer–supplier transactions, and worker skills, we show that the skill bias of new machinery arises entirely at external suppliers. Following machinery investment spikes, purchases from suppliers raise firms’ total demand for high-STEM workers by 45%, three times the rise for other skill groups, while in-house hiring raises it by less than 5%, evenly across skill groups. This external demand comes first from machine integrators and later from complementary services such as software, and is stronger for smaller firms, lumpier investments, and rapidly advancing capital, consistent with fixed and adjustment costs of hiring high-STEM workers in-house. In a general-equilibrium model estimated on our event studies, the rise in machinery per worker from 2003 to 2021 raises relative demand for high-STEM workers by 8.8 log points and their wage premium by 3.1. Measured on payrolls alone, both would fall.

中文摘要

长期以来，人们认为新机器会提高对熟练劳动力的相对需求，但企业层面的证据并未在投资企业中发现这种转变。我们通过将目光投向企业边界之外来解决这一谜题。通过链接比利时关于资本投资、买方-供应商交易和工人技能的数据，我们表明新机器的技能偏向完全出现在外部供应商处。在机器投资激增之后，从供应商处的采购使企业对高STEM工人的总需求提高了45%，是其他技能群体增幅的三倍，而内部招聘使其提高不到5%，且在各技能群体之间分布均匀。这种外部需求首先来自机器集成商，随后来自软件等互补服务，并且在规模较小的企业、更块状的投资以及快速进步的资本中更强，这与在企业内部雇用高STEM工人所面临的固定成本和调整成本相一致。在一个基于我们事件研究估计的一般均衡模型中，2003年至2021年每名工人对应的机器数量上升使对高STEM工人的相对需求提高了8.8个对数点，并使他们的工资溢价提高了3.1。仅以工资单来衡量，两者都会下降。
