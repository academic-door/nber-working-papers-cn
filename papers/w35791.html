Here, There, and Everywhere: Remote Work’s Impact on Employment in the UK

此处、彼处与无处不在：远程工作对英国就业的影响

Ian Burn, Melissa D. Gentry, Joanna Lahey

教育与劳动 英国

Abstract

Remote work is often touted as a way for disabled and older workers to remain in the labour market. Post-Covid, the incidence of remote work has increased in the UK. Following Bloom et al. (2026), we test the effects of remote work on employment for these two groups using the pandemic’s increase in work from home rates as a quasi-experiment. We compare occupations with high growth in work from home (WFH) to those with lower growth in work from home, with growth rates measured among the non-disabled or younger. We then instrument using pre-pandemic remote work scores from Dingle and Neiman (2020). We find significant evidence that rising WFH increases employment of disabled and older workers in the UK. We additionally find decreases in commute time and wages for both groups, but no effect on hours worked.

中文摘要

远程工作常被吹捧为残疾和年长劳动者留在劳动力市场的一种方式。新冠疫情后，英国远程工作的发生率有所上升。遵循Bloom等（2026）的方法，我们利用疫情期间居家办公率的上升作为准实验，检验远程工作对这两个群体就业的影响。我们比较居家办公（WFH）增长较高的职业与居家办公增长较低的职业，增长率是在非残疾或较年轻群体中衡量的。然后我们使用Dingle和Neiman（2020）的疫情前远程工作得分作为工具变量。我们发现显著证据表明，居家办公的上升增加了英国残疾和年长劳动者的就业。我们还发现这两个群体的通勤时间和工资均有所下降，但对工作时长没有影响。
