Hidden Debt Revelations

隐性债务揭示

Sebastian Horn, David Mihalyi, Philipp Nickol, César Sosa-Padilla

金融与资产定价 全球/多国

Abstract

How reliable are public debt statistics? This paper quantifies the magnitude, characteristics, and timing of hidden debt by tracking ex post revisions to sovereign debt statistics. We assemble a comprehensive new database of 119 editions, or vintages, of League of Nations, United Nations, IMF, and World Bank debt statistics, covering up to 187 countries over more than a century. We show that public debt is systematically underreported, with upward revisions averaging roughly one percent of GDP across advanced and developing economies, external and domestic debt, and all reporting systems we examine. Hidden debt accumulates in good times and tends to be revealed in bad times, often during IMF programs and sovereign defaults. Over the past one hundred years, nearly half of all sovereign defaults have coincided with substantial upward revisions to reported public debt. We incorporate these patterns into a quantitative sovereign default model and show that hidden debt raises default risk and borrowing costs, and thereby contributes to debt intolerance. Differences in the reliability of debt data across countries account for roughly 20% of the observed gap in debt-carrying capacity between low and high-income economies.

中文摘要

公共债务统计数据有多可靠？本文通过追踪主权债务统计数据的事后修正，量化了隐性债务的规模、特征和时机。我们汇编了一个全面的新数据库，包含国际联盟、联合国、国际货币基金组织和世界银行债务统计数据的119个版本，覆盖了超过一个世纪的、多达187个国家。我们表明，公共债务被系统性低估，在发达经济体和发展中经济体、外部债务和国内债务以及我们检查的所有报告系统中，向上修正平均约占GDP的1%。隐性债务在繁荣时期积累，并往往在萧条时期被揭示，通常是在国际货币基金组织计划和主权违约期间。在过去一百年中，近一半的主权违约与报告的公共债务大幅向上修正同时发生。我们将这些模式纳入一个定量的主权违约模型，并表明隐性债务提高了违约风险和借贷成本，从而导致了债务不耐受。各国债务数据可靠性的差异解释了低收入和高收入经济体之间债务承载能力差距的约20%。
