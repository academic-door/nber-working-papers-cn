Unconventional Monetary Policy and Deposit Retention

非常规货币政策与存款留存

Philip Strahan, Tuo Yang

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

The two dimensions of unconventional monetary policy — holding the short rate at the zero-lower bound (ZLB) and quantitative easing (QE) — shifted deposit composition toward more interest-sensitive customers. The ZLB drew these customers by creating rate compression, which limited the benefit of searching for high-yielding liquidity sources. QE also drew rate-sensitive, large depositors from the Federal Reserve’s purchases of bonds from non-banks. Once policy normalized, flow sensitivity and the deposit beta both increased with exposure to QE and the ZLB. We conclude that unconventional monetary policy made deposit retention costlier and riskier for banks.

中文摘要

非常规货币政策的两个维度——将短期利率维持在零下限（ZLB）和量化宽松（QE）——使存款构成向对利率更敏感的客户转移。ZLB通过造成利率压缩吸引了这些客户，这限制了寻找高收益流动性来源的收益。QE也吸引了对利率敏感的大额存款人，其来源是美联储从非银行机构购买债券。一旦政策正常化，流量敏感性和存款贝塔均随对QE和ZLB的暴露程度而上升。我们得出结论：非常规货币政策使银行的存款留存成本更高、风险更大。
