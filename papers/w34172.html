Domestic Outsourcing and Young-worker Entry into the Formal Sector

国内外包与年轻工人进入正规部门

Mayara Felix, Michael B. Wong

教育与劳动 巴西

Abstract

We provide evidence that domestic outsourcing increases young-worker entry into the formal sector. Leveraging a pair of 1993–1994 Brazilian reforms that reduced the relative cost of outsourcing security guards, a triple-differences design shows that the reforms increased formal guard employment by 4% and hiring from unemployment or informality by 7%, while reallocating formal employment from older to younger workers and leaving demographic-adjusted wages unchanged. Census data corroborate the rise in formality, driven by the youngest cohorts. The compositional shift mirrors a general pattern in Brazil’s matched employer–employee records: conditional on total firm size, employers with greater occupation-specific scale hire younger workers, paid less at entry and more likely to be entering formal employment for the first time. The evidence is most consistent with contract firms supplying at scale the capabilities needed to hire productive workers from outside the formal sector—a demand-side channel for increasing formal sector employment.

中文摘要

我们提供了国内外包增加年轻工人进入正规部门的证据。利用1993-1994年巴西两项降低外包保安相对成本的改革，一个三重差分设计表明，改革使正规保安就业增加了4%，从失业或非正规部门招聘增加了7%，同时将正规就业从年长工人重新分配给年轻工人，并使经人口调整后的工资保持不变。人口普查数据证实了正规性的上升，这由最年轻的队列驱动。这种构成转变反映了巴西匹配雇主-雇员记录中的一般模式：在给定企业总规模的情况下，具有更大职业特定规模的雇主雇佣更年轻的工人，他们的入职工资更低，更可能是首次进入正规就业。证据最符合合同公司大规模提供从正规部门外部雇佣生产性工人所需能力的观点——这是增加正规部门就业的需求侧渠道。
