The Impact of the Great Recession on Mortality: A Dynamic Factor Analysis

大衰退对死亡率的影响：动态因子分析

Amy Finkelstein, Matthew J. Notowidigdo, Enzo Profili

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

We use a dynamic factor time series model to estimate the national mortality impact of the Great Recession. In aggregate, the Great Recession reduced the average annual, age-adjusted mortality rate by about 2 percent, with effects persisting for at least a decade. In a calibrated model of the welfare effects of the estimated impacts of the Great Recession on consumption and mortality, we find that the welfare gain from the estimated mortality decline is quantitatively significant relative to the welfare loss from the estimated consumption decline. The estimated mortality decline is also quantitatively close to prior mortality estimates using cross-sectional variation in the severity of the Great Recession, implying limited general equilibrium effects of the Great Recession on mortality. More broadly, our findings suggest that prior evidence from local labor markets that recessions are good for health can be extrapolated to the likely mortality consequences of nationwide recessions.

中文摘要

我们使用动态因子时间序列模型来估计大衰退对全国死亡率的影响。总体而言，大衰退使年均年龄调整死亡率下降约2%，且影响持续至少十年。在一个校准的福利效应模型中，该模型纳入估计的大衰退对消费和死亡率的影响，我们发现估计的死亡率下降所带来的福利收益相对于估计的消费下降所带来的福利损失在数量上是显著的。估计的死亡率下降在数量上也接近先前使用大衰退严重程度横截面变异得到的死亡率估计，这意味着大衰退对死亡率的总体均衡效应有限。更广泛地说，我们的发现表明，先前来自地方劳动力市场的证据——即衰退对健康有益——可以外推到全国性衰退可能的死亡率后果。
