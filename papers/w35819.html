Timing, Trading Frictions, and the Limits of Subsidy Capture in Livestock Risk Protection

时机、交易摩擦与牲畜风险保护中的补贴捕获限度

Yifei Zhang, Andrew Keller, Shawn Arita, Sandro Steinbach

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

Publicly subsidized insurance programs can create incentives for subsidy capture when insured products closely resemble privately traded financial instruments. Livestock Risk Protection (LRP) is one such case because it provides price insurance through a contract that closely resembles an exchange-traded put option. Using LRP endorsement records and matched CME options data for 2020–2025, we document that endorsement activity clusters around CME option expiration dates, especially for feeder cattle and lean hogs. Because producer-level derivatives positions are unobserved, however, timing patterns alone cannot identify subsidy capture. We therefore simulate returns to combined LRP-CME strategies using observed contract terms, option prices, and trading frictions, including bid-ask spreads, margin requirements, and financing costs. We find that the subsidy-created premium wedge is observable at purchase, but realized gains are small, commodity-specific, and concentrated in low-volatility environments where realized volatility falls below implied volatility. Even in those favorable cases, combined strategies substantially worsen downside outcomes relative to holding LRP alone. Additional evidence shows that clustering persists across volatility regimes and after the 2025 revisions to the LRP Handbook, suggesting that observed timing patterns may reflect contract alignment and routine hedging practices, instead of widespread economically meaningful subsidy capture.

中文摘要

当受保产品与私人交易的金融工具高度相似时，公共补贴保险项目可能产生补贴捕获激励。牲畜风险保护（LRP）就是这样一个案例，因为它通过一种与交易所交易看跌期权高度相似的合约提供价格保险。使用2020—2025年的LRP背书记录和匹配的CME期权数据，我们记录了背书活动集中在CME期权到期日附近，尤其是育肥牛和瘦肉猪。然而，由于生产者层面的衍生品头寸不可观测，仅凭时机模式无法识别补贴捕获。因此，我们使用观察到的合约条款、期权价格和交易摩擦（包括买卖价差、保证金要求和融资成本）来模拟LRP-CME组合策略的收益。我们发现，补贴创造的保费楔子在购买时是可观察的，但实现收益很小、具有商品特异性，并集中在已实现波动率低于隐含波动率的低波动环境中。即使在这些有利情形下，与单独持有LRP相比，组合策略也会显著恶化下行结果。额外证据表明，这种聚集在不同波动率状态下以及在2025年《LRP手册》修订之后仍然持续，这表明观察到的时机模式可能反映的是合约对齐和常规套期保值实践，而不是广泛存在且具有经济意义的补贴捕获。
