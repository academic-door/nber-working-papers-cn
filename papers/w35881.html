Does Remote Work Increase Fertility?

远程工作会增加生育率吗？

Andrew C. Johnston

教育与劳动 美国

Abstract

Does working from home increase fertility? I study the 2020 expansion of remote work by comparing changes in births across U.S. college graduates whose majors differed in pre-pandemic teleworkability. Measuring exposure by college major avoids relying on occupations workers may have chosen in response to the expansion. Among married women, a one-standard-deviation increase in teleworkability reduced annual birth rates by 2 percent and increased full-time employment by 1.3 percent. I find no statistically significant effects on fertility among men or unmarried women, or on marriage or divorce. Accounting exercises suggest that increased employment can explain about half of the decline in births. The results suggest that returning to pre-pandemic telework levels would raise the total fertility rate by 0.09 births per woman.

中文摘要

在家工作会增加生育率吗？我通过比较疫情前远程工作可行性不同的专业美国大学毕业生之间的出生变化，研究2020年远程工作的扩张。以大学专业衡量暴露程度，避免依赖于工人可能为响应这一扩张而选择的职业。在已婚女性中，远程工作可行性增加一个标准差使年出生率降低2%，并使全职就业增加1.3%。我没有发现对男性或未婚女性的生育率，或对结婚或离婚有统计显著影响。核算练习表明，就业增加可以解释约一半的出生下降。结果表明，恢复到疫情前的远程工作水平将使总和生育率提高每名女性0.09个孩子。
