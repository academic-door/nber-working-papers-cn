A Beckerian Theory of Taxation

贝克尔式税收理论

Margaret Chen, Mikhail Golosov, Ilia Krasikov

公共财政与税收 未指明区域

Abstract

We study taxation in matching environments in which both equilibrium sorting and the sharing of match surplus are endogenous. Tax reforms lead to complex adjustments in sorting and surplus sharing patterns across the whole economy. We develop a general approach for characterizing these equilibrium adjustments using matching elasticities, which are primitive objects that describe how agents' matching choices are affected by pecuniary payoffs in partial equilibrium. Applying this approach to a workhorse model of family formation, we first show that the sorting and surplus sharing responses are closely linked to complementarities in the creation of economic surplus, as first emphasized by Becker (1973). We then describe the effects of arbitrary tax reforms on tax revenues, the utilities of different agents, and social welfare; design tests to assess Pareto efficiency of existing tax systems; derive formulas that characterize optimal taxes; and propose numerical techniques to compute them. In a calibrated model, we find that optimal marginal tax rates are substantially lower and less progressive than in the Mirrleesian benchmark.

中文摘要

我们研究匹配环境中的税收问题，其中均衡排序和匹配剩余的分享均为内生。税制改革会导致整个经济中排序和剩余分享模式的复杂调整。我们开发了一种通用方法，利用匹配弹性来刻画这些均衡调整，匹配弹性是描述代理人匹配选择如何受局部均衡中货币收益影响的原始对象。将该方法应用于家庭形成的标准模型，我们首先表明排序和剩余分享反应与经济剩余创造中的互补性密切相关，正如Becker（1973）首次强调的那样。然后我们描述任意税制改革对税收收入、不同代理人效用和社会福利的影响；设计评估现有税制帕累托效率的检验；推导刻画最优税收的公式；并提出计算它们的数值技术。在一个校准模型中，我们发现最优边际税率显著低于Mirrleesian基准，且累进性更低。
