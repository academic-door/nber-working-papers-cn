When Microenterprises Grow, Are Consumers Better Off? Evidence from Large Loans to Microenterprises in Chile

微型企业成长时，消费者是否受益？来自智利对微型企业大额贷款的证证据

Dean Karlan, Natalia Rigol, Benjamin N. Roth

金融与资产定价 智利

Abstract

Evaluations of microenterprise credit typically measure effects only on borrowing firms. But what about their customers? If microenterprises sell relatively undifferentiated goods and services, as is often hypothesized, credit may simply reallocate sales across firms and create little consumer benefit. In a randomized controlled trial in Chile, large loans increased treated firms’ profits by USD 292 per month, a 13.4% increase. Customer survey data indicate even larger benefits for customers: a gain of USD 494 per month in consumer surplus. Furthermore, using a sample of more than 125,000 non-treated firms operating in the same markets, we find little evidence of business stealing. The welfare gains from credit expansion thus extend well beyond the borrowers themselves.

中文摘要

对微型企业信贷的评估通常只衡量对借款企业的影响。但它们的客户呢？如果微型企业销售相对无差异的商品和服务，正如常被假设的那样，信贷可能只是重新分配企业之间的销售，几乎不会创造消费者收益。在智利的一项随机对照试验中，大额贷款使受处理企业的利润每月增加292美元，增幅为13.4%。客户调查数据表明，客户获得的收益更大：消费者剩余每月增加494美元。此外，使用在同一市场中经营的超过125,000家未受处理企业的样本，我们几乎未发现业务被窃取的证据。因此，信贷扩张带来的福利收益远远超出借款人本身。
