Beveridgean Phillips Curve

贝弗里奇式菲利普斯曲线

Pascal Michaillat, Emmanuel Saez

宏观与货币财政 美国

Abstract

This paper develops a Beveridgean model of the Phillips curve. While the New Keynesian Phillips curve is based on monopolistic pricing under price-adjustment costs, the Beveridgean Phillips curve is based on directed-search pricing under price-adjustment costs. Under directed search, prices respond to slack instead of marginal costs. The resulting Phillips curve has three properties that match recent US evidence. First, it delivers the divine coincidence: inflation is on target whenever unemployment is at its efficient level—the full-employment rate of unemployment (FERU), the geometric mean of the unemployment and vacancy rates. Second, it is decreasing and convex in the unemployment-inflation plane: inflation responds more strongly to unemployment when the labor market is inefficiently tight than when it is inefficiently slack. The convexity is inherited from the Beveridge curve, which makes unemployment a decreasing and convex function of tightness. Third, shifts of the Beveridge curve move the FERU and therefore shift the unemployment-inflation Phillips curve. The model also implies that the Fed's dual mandate is internally consistent, that a soft landing from an inefficiently tight labor market is possible, and that the zero lower bound is not topsy-turvy.

中文摘要

本文发展了一个贝弗里奇式菲利普斯曲线模型。新凯恩斯菲利普斯曲线基于价格调整成本下的垄断定价，而贝弗里奇式菲利普斯曲线基于价格调整成本下的定向搜寻定价。在定向搜寻下，价格对松弛程度而非边际成本作出反应。由此得到的菲利普斯曲线具有三个与近期美国证据相符的性质。第一，它实现了神圣巧合：只要失业处于其有效水平——充分就业失业率（FERU），即失业率与职位空缺率的几何平均值——通胀就处于目标水平。第二，它在失业-通胀平面上是递减且凸的：当劳动力市场无效率地紧张时，通胀对失业的反应强于劳动力市场无效率地松弛时。这种凸性继承自贝弗里奇曲线，后者使失业成为紧张程度的递减且凸函数。第三，贝弗里奇曲线的移动会移动FERU，从而移动失业-通胀菲利普斯曲线。该模型还意味着美联储的双重使命在内部是一致的，从无效率紧张劳动力市场实现软着陆是可能的，且零下限并非颠倒错乱。
