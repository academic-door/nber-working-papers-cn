Doing Better at Doing Good: Lessons from a Bayesian Meta-Analysis After 20 Years of Poverty Graduation Programs

在行善上做得更好：20年扶贫毕业项目后的贝叶斯元分析经验

Samantha Carter, Edward Jee, Dean Karlan, Patrick Premand

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

Multifaceted poverty Graduation programs that aim to increase income generating activities for low-income households are being rapidly adopted and scaled by nonprofit organizations and governments. This paper conducts a Bayesian hierarchical meta-analysis of 49 studies (encompassing 81 treatment variations) on poverty Graduation programs, defined as containing at least three of five components: consumption support, savings facilitation, skills training, coaching, and a lumpsum transfer. The analysis finds large gains across economic and psychosocial outcomes: monthly consumption rises by 38 USD PPP (11 percent), income by 28 USD PPP (15 percent), alongside improvements in savings, assets, food security, mental health, and women’s empowerment. Beyond these average treatment effects, three results stand out. First, programs with large transfers (at least 400 USD PPP) have larger treatment effects but are not more cost-effective. Second, more expensive programs generate larger treatment effects, but the improvements are not commensurate with the cost increases; thus, lower cost programs tend to be more cost-effective. Third, programs implemented from 2016 onward perform considerably better than those implemented prior to 2016: benefit-cost ratios from 2016 onward are 5.39, compared to 2.89 through 2015. Neither changes in cost nor in high-level design (components) explain this upward trend. Considering other heavily studied interventions, the results show a similar upward trend for treatment effects on behavior change from financial education but not for unconditional cash transfers. The paper hypothesizes a mechanism of continuous sector-community learning and improved implementation quality.

中文摘要

旨在增加低收入家庭创收活动的多维度扶贫毕业项目正被非营利组织和政府迅速采用并扩大规模。本文对49项研究（涵盖81种处理变体）进行了贝叶斯分层元分析，这些研究涉及扶贫毕业项目，其定义为至少包含五个组成部分中的三个：消费支持、储蓄便利、技能培训、辅导和一次性转移支付。分析发现经济和社会心理结果均有大幅改善：月度消费增加38美元PPP（11%），收入增加28美元PPP（15%），同时储蓄、资产、粮食安全、心理健康和妇女赋权也有所改善。除这些平均处理效应外，有三项结果尤为突出。第一，转移支付规模较大（至少400美元PPP）的项目处理效应更大，但成本效益并不更高。第二，成本更高的项目产生更大的处理效应，但改善幅度与成本增加不成比例；因此，低成本项目往往更具成本效益。第三，2016年及以后实施的项目表现明显优于2016年之前实施的项目：2016年及以后的效益成本比为5.39，而截至2015年为2.89。成本变化和高层设计（组成部分）变化均无法解释这一上升趋势。考虑其他被大量研究的干预措施，结果显示金融教育对行为改变的处理效应存在类似上升趋势，但无条件现金转移支付则没有。本文提出一个机制假设：持续的行业-社区学习和实施质量改善。
