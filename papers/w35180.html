The Persistence of Power: How Family Origins Shape Political Representation and Policy

权力的持续性：家庭出身如何塑造政治代表性与政策

Eric Chyn, Katherine Cohen, Kareem Haggag, Bryan A. Stuart

公共财政与税收 美国

Abstract

In the United States, long hailed as the land of opportunity, is access to political office truly open across society, or do the most privileged children disproportionately rise to enter political life? This question speaks to a longstanding concern that elite families may entrench themselves in positions of power, reproducing a form of hereditary privilege within a democratic system. We study the family backgrounds of U.S. politicians over the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries and show that children from wealthy and privileged households have been substantially overrepresented in elected office. This imbalance has persisted over time and, at the highest levels of office, varies little across political parties. To test the durability of elite political advantage, we exploit the sudden economic shock caused by the end of slavery. Despite the large and concentrated losses at the top of the wealth distribution, the children of slaveholders continued to enter government at high rates. Finally, we examine whether politicians’ socioeconomic origins shape policy by constructing a new sample of close elections linked to detailed information on U.S. House candidates’ family backgrounds. Comparing otherwise similar districts in which a candidate from a high socioeconomic status family narrowly wins rather than loses, we find that districts represented by higher status candidates are less likely to support pro-tax positions in roll-call voting. Together, the evidence across our analyses shows that family background strongly predicts entry into political office and has measurable consequences for policy choices.

中文摘要

在美国这个长期被誉为机遇之地的国家，进入政治职位的途径是否真正向社会各阶层开放，还是最特权家庭的子女不成比例地进入政治生活？这个问题涉及一个长期存在的担忧，即精英家庭可能在权力位置上自我固化，在民主制度内再生产一种世袭特权。我们研究了19世纪末20世纪初美国政治家的家庭背景，并表明来自富裕和特权家庭的子女在民选职位中的代表性一直显著过高。这种不平衡持续存在，并且在最高层级的职位上，不同政党之间的差异很小。为检验精英政治优势的持久性，我们利用了奴隶制废除所带来的突然经济冲击。尽管财富分配顶端的损失巨大且集中，奴隶主的子女仍以高比例继续进入政府。最后，我们通过构建一个新的、与详细的美国众议院候选人家庭背景信息相关联的势均力敌选举样本，来检验政治家的社会经济出身是否塑造政策。比较那些来自高社会经济地位家庭的候选人以微弱优势获胜而非落败的、其他方面相似的选区，我们发现，由更高地位候选人代表的选区在唱名表决中支持增税立场的可能性更低。综合来看，我们分析中的证据表明，家庭背景强烈预测进入政治职位的可能性，并对政策选择产生可衡量的影响。
