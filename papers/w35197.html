Global Policy Spillovers: How Environmental Policies Propagate through Product Attributes

全球政策溢出效应：环境政策如何通过产品属性传播

Koichiro Ito, James M. Sallee, Caleb Halvorson-Fried, Andrew Smith

环境与气候 日本、美国

Abstract

When firms design products for global markets, domestic policies can propagate internationally by inducing changes in product attributes. We call this phenomenon attribute propagation and develop a framework to assess its economic significance. Using difference-in-differences analysis and structural estimation, we find that a fuel-economy subsidy in Japan led to substantial improvements in fuel economy in the United States. The impact is first-order—most CO2 emissions reductions from the Japanese policy arise through its effects in the United States. Our findings suggest that conventional economic analysis may understate the global impacts of many economic policies.

中文摘要

当企业为全球市场设计产品时，国内政策可以通过引起产品属性的变化而在国际上传播。我们将这种现象称为属性传播，并开发了一个框架来评估其经济意义。利用双重差分分析和结构估计，我们发现日本的燃油经济性补贴导致了美国燃油经济性的显著改善。这种影响是一阶的——日本政策带来的大部分二氧化碳减排都源于其在美国的影响。我们的研究结果表明，传统经济分析可能低估了许多经济政策的全球影响。
