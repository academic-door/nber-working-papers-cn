Haste or Waste? The Role of Presale in Residential Housing

仓促还是浪费？预售在住宅市场中的作用

Ziyang Chen, Maggie Rong Hu, Ginger Zhe Jin, Qiyao Zhou

其他主题 中国相关

Abstract

This paper provides the first theoretical framework and empirical evidence on housing presale policies. Constructing a novel dataset across 270 major cities in mainland China, we identify 3,179 unfinished residential projects begun between 2010 and 2019 from a government-run citizen complaint portal. Stricter presale criteria (when developers may begin presale) and stricter post-sale supervision of construction funds both predict fewer unfinished projects, but only the presale criterion slows new housing development. A back-of-the-envelope analysis suggests the current policy bundle lies inside the Pareto frontier: tightening supervision alone could cut unfinished projects by 55% without slowing development.

中文摘要

本文提供了关于住房预售政策的首个理论框架和实证证据。通过构建覆盖中国大陆270个主要城市的新数据集，我们从政府运营的市民投诉门户网站中识别出2010年至2019年间开工的3,179个未完工住宅项目。更严格的预售标准（即开发商何时可以开始预售）和更严格的售后建设资金监管都能预测更少的未完工项目，但只有预售标准会减缓新住房开发。一项粗略估算分析表明，当前的政策组合位于帕累托前沿之内：仅加强监管就能在不减缓开发的情况下将未完工项目减少55%。
