Impact of Adults’ College Completion on their Parents’ Mortality: Evidence from the 1993 Korean Higher Education Reform

成年人大学完成对其父母死亡率的影响：来自1993年韩国高等教育改革的证据

Ah-Reum Lee, William H. Dow, Jacqueline M. Torres

健康与医疗 韩国

Abstract

A large literature examines the economic and health returns to college education. We extend this literature by studying the upward intergenerational effects of children’s education on parental mortality. We exploit Korea’s 1993 Higher Education Reform, which we estimate raised college completion by 13 percentage points among eldest children in the 1974–1983 birth cohorts relative to the prior 10-year cohort. Using data from the Korean Longitudinal Study of Aging, reduced-form estimates indicate a 27% (s.e.=10%) reduction in mortality among parents whose oldest child was in the reform-affected cohort. Because the reform affected college completion but not other education levels or quality, we use it as an instrumental variable (IV) for child college completion. IV estimates indicate a 20% (s.e.=7%) reduction in parental mortality for children induced to complete college by the reform. These parents received greater financial support and contact from their children and had lower smoking and higher physical activity, although these mechanisms explain only a small share of the mortality benefit. Effects were larger for parents of daughters in reduced-form analyses, although IV estimates did not differ significantly by child gender. Future research should further investigate the mechanisms through which children’s college education affects parental mortality.

中文摘要

大量文献考察了大学教育的经济和健康回报。我们通过研究子女教育对父母死亡率的向上代际效应来拓展这一文献。我们利用韩国1993年高等教育改革，我们估计该改革使1974—1983年出生队列中长子女的大学完成率相对于此前10年队列提高了13个百分点。使用韩国老龄化纵向研究的数据，简约式估计表明，其最年长子女属于受改革影响队列的父母，死亡率降低了27%（标准误=10%）。由于该改革影响了大学完成但不影响其他教育水平或质量，我们将其作为子女大学完成的工具变量（IV）。IV估计表明，对于因改革而被诱导完成大学的孩子，其父母死亡率降低了20%（标准误=7%）。这些父母从子女处获得了更多的经济支持和联系，并且吸烟更少、体育活动更多，尽管这些机制只能解释死亡率收益的一小部分。在简约式分析中，对女儿父母的影响更大，尽管IV估计并未因子女性别而存在显著差异。未来研究应进一步探究子女大学教育影响父母死亡率的机制。
