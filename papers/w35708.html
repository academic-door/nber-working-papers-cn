“Non-Marginal” Investor Beliefs

“非边际”投资者的信念

William N. Goetzmann, Dasol Kim, Robert J. Shiller

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper provides evidence of how the beliefs of investors who step out of the market, or “non-marginal” investors, influence asset prices. Using more than two decades of respondent-level investor surveys, we construct wedge measures that quantify the distance between subjective investor beliefs and option-implied benchmarks. We use self-identified proxies for market participation to document that these wedges correspond with the beliefs of non-marginal investors. Non-marginal investors are generally more pessimistic in their market return forecasts and perceive greater crash risks than the pricing population. Subjective crash beliefs are a key determinant of participation, even when controlling for expected return beliefs. This heterogeneity, in part, explains the negative association between average expected returns estimated from surveys and future realized market returns. In the cross-section, stocks with greater sensitivity to non-marginal beliefs earn lower returns, particularly where disagreement is high. Taken together, the results provide evidence of a composition channel in which belief-driven exit concentrates risk among fewer investors.

中文摘要

本文提供了关于退出市场的投资者（即“非边际”投资者）的信念如何影响资产价格的证据。利用超过二十年的受访者层面投资者调查，我们构建了楔形指标，用以量化主观投资者信念与期权隐含基准之间的距离。我们使用自我识别的市场参与代理变量来记录这些楔形与非边际投资者信念的对应关系。非边际投资者通常在其市场回报预测上更为悲观，并且比定价人群感知到更大的崩盘风险。主观崩盘信念是参与度的关键决定因素，即使在控制预期回报信念时也是如此。这种异质性部分解释了调查估计的平均预期回报与未来已实现市场回报之间的负相关关系。在横截面上，对非边际信念敏感度更高的股票获得较低回报，尤其是在分歧较大的情况下。总体而言，结果提供了构成渠道的证据，在该渠道中，信念驱动的退出将风险集中在较少的投资者身上。
