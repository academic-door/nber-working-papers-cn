NBER Working Paper w36047所在周报

Regret, Survival Beliefs, and Retirement Recommendations

后悔、生存信念与退休建议

Abigail Hurwitz, Olivia S. Mitchell

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine how information about others’ retirement regret affects annuitization and savings recommendations. In a survey experiment, participants received information about regret over not purchasing annuities or not saving enough for retirement. Responses were concentrated among individuals who underestimated their survival probabilities relative to population benchmarks, consistent with a survival belief-updating mechanism. Among underestimators, annuity regret information increased annuitization recommendations by 16.9 percentage points (approximately 38%), while savings regret information reduced recommendations to significantly increase saving by 15.4 percentage points (approximately 28%). Less regret-averse individuals reduced aggressive savings recommendations, while the positive response to annuity regret information was concentrated among more risk-averse respondents. The annuity effect was strongest among respondents with greater annuity knowledge than less knowledgeable respondents.

中文摘要

我们考察关于他人退休后悔的信息如何影响年金化建议和储蓄建议。在一项调查实验中，参与者收到了关于未购买年金或未为退休储蓄足够资金的后悔信息。反应集中在那些相对于人口基准低估自身生存概率的个体中，这与生存信念更新机制相一致。在低估者中，年金后悔信息使年金化建议提高了16.9个百分点（约38%），而储蓄后悔信息使显著增加储蓄的建议降低了15.4个百分点（约28%）。较不厌恶后悔的个体减少了对激进储蓄建议的支持，而对年金后悔信息的积极反应集中在风险厌恶程度较高的受访者中。年金效应在年金知识较多的受访者中比在知识较少的受访者中最强。
