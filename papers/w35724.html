Risk Perceptions of E-Cigarettes and the Evaluation of EVALI Messaging

电子烟风险认知与EVALI信息传播效果评估

Lawrence Jin, Donald S. Kenkel, Alan D. Mathios, Grace N. Phillips, Revathy Suryanarayana, Hua Wang

健康与医疗 中国相关 澳大利亚、印度、印度尼西亚、日本、马来西亚、瑞典、英国、美国

Abstract

With the growth of sales of e-cigarettes and other alternative nicotine products, governments face the new challenge of crafting public health messages that provide information to consumers about these less risky but not risk-free products. Bayesian learning serves as the theoretical framework for our analysis of how smokers form their perceptions of the risks of e-cigarettes based on the risks of smoking. This paper consists of two parts. First, we conduct a cross-country descriptive analysis of government messaging and smokers’ perceptions of e-cigarette risks. We study a diverse group of eight countries: Australia, China, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, Sweden, the U.K. and the U.S. We find that public health messages range widely across the countries. In online cross-country surveys of adult smokers, on average perceptions of e-cigarette risks are associated with their perceptions of the risk of smoking and with their countries’ messaging. Notably, in all eight countries many smokers incorrectly believe that e-cigarettes are relatively more harmful than smoking and over-estimate the absolute risks of e-cigarettes. In the second part of our study, we report the results of online randomized experiments that compare the effects of different e-cigarette risk messaging about an outbreak of lung injuries (given the acronym EVALI). The U.K. public health message about the low risk of e-cigarettes relative to smoking reduced smokers’ incorrect risk perceptions, while the U.S. public health message increased incorrect risk perceptions.

中文摘要

随着电子烟及其他替代尼古丁产品销售的增长，政府面临新的挑战：如何制定公共卫生信息，向消费者传达这些风险较低但并非无风险的产品信息。贝叶斯学习构成了我们分析吸烟者如何基于吸烟风险形成对电子烟风险认知的理论框架。本文由两部分组成。首先，我们对政府信息传播和吸烟者对电子烟风险的认知进行了跨国描述性分析。我们研究了八个不同的国家：澳大利亚、中国、印度尼西亚、日本、马来西亚、瑞典、英国和美国。我们发现各国的公共卫生信息差异很大。在对成年吸烟者进行的在线跨国调查中，平均而言，对电子烟风险的认知与其对吸烟风险的认知以及其所在国家的信息传播有关。值得注意的是，在所有八个国家中，许多吸烟者错误地认为电子烟比吸烟相对更有害，并高估了电子烟的绝对风险。在研究的第二部分，我们报告了在线随机实验的结果，该实验比较了关于肺损伤爆发（缩写为EVALI）的不同电子烟风险信息的效果。英国关于电子烟相对于吸烟风险较低的公共卫生信息减少了吸烟者错误的风险认知，而美国的公共卫生信息则增加了错误的风险认知。
