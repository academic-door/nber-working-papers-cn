NBER Working Paper w36060所在周报

Light Pollution: Effects on Sleep, Work, and Mental Health

光污染：对睡眠、工作与心理健康的影响

Joan Costa-i-Font, Janet Currie, Soodeh Saberian

环境与气候 加拿大、哥伦比亚、英国

Abstract

This study examines the impact of artificial streetlights on sleep, as well as downstream effects on mental health, absenteeism, and physical activity. We exploit variation resulting from the replacement of traditional sodium streetlights with light-emitting diode (LED) lights in Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada. Using data from the Canadian Community Health Survey, we examine discontinuities in difficulty sleeping at the municipal border with Vancouver, which did not adopt new lighting. We also estimate a within-Burnaby model exploiting the rollout of LED lights across neighborhoods, and a difference-in-differences design using matched Vancouver and Burnaby neighborhoods. The estimates show that conversion reduces difficulty sleeping immediately, and that improvements in mental health, absenteeism, and physical activity follow with a lag of two to three years. The largest gains occur with warmer-toned LEDs that more closely mimic natural light.

中文摘要

本研究考察人工路灯对睡眠的影响，以及对心理健康、缺勤和身体活动的下游效应。我们利用加拿大不列颠哥伦比亚省本拿比市将传统钠灯路灯替换为发光二极管（LED）灯所产生的外生变异。使用加拿大社区健康调查数据，我们考察与未采用新照明的温哥华市交界处的入睡困难断点。我们还估计了一个利用LED灯在各社区推广的本拿比市内模型，以及一个使用匹配的温哥华和本拿比社区的双重差分设计。估计结果显示，转换立即减少了入睡困难，而心理健康、缺勤和身体活动的改善在2至3年的滞后期后出现。最大收益出现在更接近自然光的暖色调LED灯上。
