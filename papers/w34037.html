Government Monitoring of Health Care Quality: Evidence from the Nursing Home Sector

政府对医疗保健质量的监管：来自疗养院部门的证据

Yiqun Chen, Marcus Dillender

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

In contracting out, monitoring is an important policy tool to extract information on firm quality and incentivize quality provision. This paper examines a central quality inspection of nursing homes, a sector with significant welfare implications but widespread public concerns about care quality. We find that nursing homes exhibit strong responses to the inspection in multiple core dimensions. However, nearly all responses drop immediately once the inspection is completed. While inspection ratings are unlikely to reflect nursing homes’ absolute quality given the strategic responses, they nonetheless predict nursing homes’ relative quality: admission to higher-rated nursing homes lowers patient mortality.

中文摘要

在合同外包中，监管是提取企业质量信息和激励质量供给的重要政策工具。本文考察了对疗养院的核心质量检查，该部门具有显著的福利影响，但护理质量普遍受到公众关注。我们发现疗养院在多个核心维度上对检查表现出强烈反应。然而，几乎所有反应在检查完成后立即消失。尽管鉴于策略性反应，检查评级不太可能反映疗养院的绝对质量，但它们确实能预测疗养院的相对质量：入住评级较高的疗养院可降低患者死亡率。
