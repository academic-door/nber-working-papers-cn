Homeownership and Attention to Inflation: Evidence from Information Treatments

住房所有权与对通胀的关注：来自信息干预实验的证据

Jessica Piccolo, Yuriy Gorodnichenko

宏观与货币财政 美国

Abstract

Whose inflation expectations drive spending decisions? Using randomized information treatments on U.S. households, we show that the answer depends on homeownership status. Renters revise their inflation expectations strongly in response to new information but do not adjust durable purchases. Homeowners update their beliefs more modestly but significantly increase durable spending when they do. This negative covariance between belief updating and spending response implies that average estimates conflate groups with opposite behavior. We trace the asymmetry to differences both in attention to inflation and in the available margins of adjustment: homeowners act through durables, while renters, who are more likely to be liquidity constrained, increase nondurable spending in the short run. These findings have implications for the design of central bank communication, the measurement of expectation-spending elasticities, and the modeling of heterogeneous attention in HANK frameworks.

中文摘要

谁的通货膨胀预期驱动支出决策？通过对美国家庭进行随机信息干预实验，我们表明答案取决于住房所有权状态。租房者在收到新信息后，会大幅修正其通胀预期，但不会调整耐用品购买。房主对信念的更新幅度较为温和，但当他们确实更新信念时，会显著增加耐用品支出。信念更新与支出反应之间的这种负协方差意味着，平均估计混淆了行为相反的不同群体。我们将这种不对称性追溯到对通胀关注度的差异以及可调整边际的差异：房主通过耐用品采取行动，而租房者（更可能面临流动性约束）则在短期内增加非耐用品支出。这些发现对中央银行沟通的设计、预期-支出弹性的测量以及HANK框架中异质性关注的建模具有启示意义。
