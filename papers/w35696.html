Loan Rates as Incentive Instruments

贷款利率作为激励工具

Kinda Cheryl Hachem

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

Loan rates are usually viewed as prices that shape borrower incentives, but they can also serve another role in a principal-agent problem by determining how informative repayment is about unobservable effort inside the bank. I study how the two roles interact: because loan rates move repayment probabilities differently for different borrowers, the bank designs its wage contract and its loan rate menu jointly. I show that the cost-minimizing wage contract is non-monotone, including pay after default, and that the bank tilts loan rates toward levels that make rewarded outcomes most diagnostic of correct assignment. When failure pay is restricted, the bank instead re-prices some loans toward the rate at which repayment probabilities are most similar across borrowers and may ultimately take more risk to conserve on agency costs. I show that a capital requirement introduced to correct a leverage externality caps failure pay in this way and, under a countercyclical capital requirement, the economy can enter a permanent two-point cycle in which banks alternate between non-monotone contracts with less risk-taking and monotone contracts with more.

中文摘要

贷款利率通常被视为塑造借款人激励的价格，但它们也可以在委托-代理问题中发挥另一作用，即决定还款信息在多大程度上揭示银行内部的不可观测努力。我研究这两种作用如何相互作用：由于贷款利率对不同借款人的还款概率影响不同，银行会联合设计其工资合同和贷款利率菜单。我表明，成本最小化的工资合同是非单调的，包括违约后支付，并且银行将贷款利率倾斜到使奖励结果最能反映正确分配的水平。当失败支付受到限制时，银行反而会将某些贷款重新定价到借款人之间还款概率最相似的利率水平，并可能最终承担更多风险以节省代理成本。我表明，为纠正杠杆外部性而引入的资本要求以这种方式限制了失败支付，并且在逆周期资本要求下，经济可能进入一个永久性的两点周期，银行在风险承担较少的非单调合同和风险承担较多的单调合同之间交替。
