Does Fertility Stabilize At Low Levels? Evidence from Period, Cohort, and Subnational Data

生育率会在低水平上稳定吗？来自时期、队列和次国家数据的证据

Diane Coffey, Michael Geruso, Dean Spears, Sangita Vyas

其他主题 印度、日本、美国

Abstract

Many population projections used by international and government agencies assume that, in today's low-fertility societies, future fertility rates will stop declining and roughly stabilize. This paper assesses the evidence for an equilibrium point or rebound from low levels. We show that there is no robust evidence for a systematic fertility rebound, or for a stable equilibrium point at any fertility level conventionally assumed. Instead, where and when fertility has fallen low, it has tended to keep falling. This is true across a range of data sources, time periods, and fertility measures: United Nations data on period total fertility rates for all country-years from 1950 to 2023; Human Fertility Database data on completed fertility for cohorts born between 1935 and 1975 in 31 countries; and fertility rates in subnational geographies in Japan, India, and the United States. We reconcile these results with a prior literature that documented fertility reversals among some low-fertility countries in the early 2000s. Our findings provide an empirical basis for re-examining the equilibrium and rebound assumptions embedded in widely used population projections.

中文摘要

国际和政府机构使用的许多人口预测假设，在当今低生育率社会中，未来生育率将停止下降并大致稳定。本文评估了关于低水平均衡点或反弹的证据。我们表明，没有稳健证据支持系统性生育率反弹，也没有证据支持在任何通常假设的生育率水平上存在稳定均衡点。相反，在生育率降至低水平的地方和时期，它往往继续下降。这一点在一系列数据来源、时间段和生育率指标中都成立：联合国关于1950年至2023年所有国家-年份的时期总和生育率数据；人类生育率数据库关于31个国家1935年至1975年出生队列的完成生育率数据；以及日本、印度和美国次国家地区的生育率。我们将这些结果与先前记录2000年代初一些低生育率国家生育率反转的文献相协调。我们的发现为重新审视广泛使用的人口预测中嵌入的均衡和反弹假设提供了经验基础。
