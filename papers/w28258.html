Supply Shocks in a Heterogeneous-Firm New Keynesian Model: The Entry Multiplier

异质性企业新凯恩斯模型中的供给冲击：进入乘数

Florin O. Bilbiie, Marc J. Melitz

理论与方法 未指明区域

Abstract

We study productivity shocks in a New Keynesian model with endogenous entry, selection, and nominal rigidities. Adjustment along the extensive margin fundamentally alters the transmission of TFP shocks. Under sticky prices, productivity disturbances generate a large “entry multiplier”: firm entry responds much more strongly than under flexible prices, even when output is allocatively efficient to first order. Introducing wage stickiness breaks this neutrality. Adverse TFP shocks reduce profits, trigger exit, and generate a negative output gap while remaining inflationary. Productivity shocks therefore behave as true supply shocks, without resorting to ad hoc cost-push or markup disturbances. Unlike standard markup shocks, TFP shocks in our framework imply procyclical profits and entry, consistent with the data. When wages are sufficiently sticky, expansionary monetary policy raises entry, closes the negative output gap, and improves welfare. The model remains analytically tractable and isomorphic to the standard New Keynesian framework, with entry and selection appearing as simple wedges.

中文摘要

我们研究了具有内生进入、选择和名义刚性的新凯恩斯模型中的生产率冲击。沿着扩展边际的调整从根本上改变了全要素生产率冲击的传导。在价格粘性下，生产率扰动会产生巨大的“进入乘数”：企业进入的反应远强于灵活价格下的情况，即使产出在一阶条件下是配置有效的。引入工资粘性打破了这种中性。不利的全要素生产率冲击会降低利润，引发退出，并在保持通胀的同时产生负的产出缺口。因此，生产率冲击表现为真正的供给冲击，而无需诉诸于特设的成本推动或加成率扰动。与标准的加成率冲击不同，我们框架中的全要素生产率冲击意味着顺周期的利润和进入，这与数据一致。当工资具有足够粘性时，扩张性货币政策会提高进入率，缩小负的产出缺口，并改善福利。该模型保持了解析可处理性，并与标准新凯恩斯框架同构，进入和选择表现为简单的楔子。
