Bureaucratic Rigidity and Battle Outcomes: Evidence from Military Logistics in the Ming Dynasty

官僚刚性与战斗结果：来自明代军事后勤的证据

Jingjing Chen, Jianlei Han, Shing-Yi Wang, Yongxiang Wang

其他主题 中国相关 未指明区域

Abstract

Bureaucratic rules meant to improve accountability can undermine state effectiveness. We study a 1492 Ming dynasty reform that shifted military grain delivery from salt merchants to a state relay system with prefecture-to-prefecture handovers and fixed routes. Afterward, court records document more supply shortages at formerly merchant-supplied garrisons than at state-supplied ones. Using 406 defensive battles against foreign invaders, we find the reform reduced victory rates near these garrisons. Declines were largest where resupply mattered most (longer wars, walled-city defenses) and where the state’s rules were most costly (routes crossing more prefectures or exposed to floods, droughts, or rugged terrain).

中文摘要

旨在提高问责的官僚规则可能削弱国家效能。我们研究1492年明朝的一项改革，该改革将军事粮食运送从盐商转向国家接力系统，实行府到府的交接和固定路线。此后，宫廷记录显示，原由商人供应的卫所比国家供应的卫所出现更多供应短缺。利用406场抵御外敌入侵的防御战，我们发现该改革降低了这些卫所附近的胜率。下降在补给最重要（更长战争、城墙防御）以及国家规则成本最高（路线跨越更多府或易受洪水、干旱或崎岖地形影响）的地方最大。
