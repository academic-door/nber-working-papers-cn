Autonomy and Technology Adoption

自主性与技术采纳

Benjamin Bushong, Carolina Corral, Xavier Giné, Aprajit Mahajan, Enrique Seira

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

We examine whether autonomy increases adherence to expert recommendations in technology adoption. In a context where farmers overuse fertilizer, we ran a field experiment that combined recommendations with either a restrictive subsidy tied to expert-recommended inputs or a flexible subsidy preserving farmer autonomy over input choice. In the short run, farmers adopted expert recommendations at similar rates regardless of subsidy autonomy and reduced fertilizer over-use by two-thirds. In the longer run, after the intervention ended, farmers with autonomy were significantly more likely to persist with the expert recommendations. A laboratory experiment with the same core structure, a tighter autonomy manipulation, and a different population replicates our main results and tests competing mechanisms. Formal mechanism tests are consistent with memory fully mediating the effect of autonomy on persistence, and persistence fully mediating the effect on long-run performance. Our results suggest that preserving choice can enhance the long-term effectiveness of expert advice and targeted subsidies.

中文摘要

我们考察了自主性是否会提高技术采纳中对专家建议的遵从度。在农民过度使用化肥的情境下，我们开展了一项实地实验，将建议与两种补贴相结合：一种是限制性补贴，与专家推荐的投入品绑定；另一种是灵活性补贴，保留农民对投入品选择的自主权。短期内，无论补贴自主性如何，农民采纳专家建议的比率相似，并将化肥过度使用减少了三分之二。在干预结束后的较长期内，拥有自主权的农民坚持采用专家建议的可能性显著更高。一个具有相同核心结构、更严格的自主性操控和不同受试群体的实验室实验，重复了我们的主要结果，并检验了竞争性机制。正式的机制检验与以下结论一致：记忆完全中介了自主性对坚持性的影响，而坚持性又完全中介了其对长期绩效的影响。我们的结果表明，保留选择权可以增强专家建议和定向补贴的长期有效性。
