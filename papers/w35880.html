The Relationship between Income and Health: Evidence from Nationwide Health Exams in Korea

收入与健康之间的关系：来自韩国全国健康检查的证据

Liran Einav, Amy Finkelstein, Sehyun Hong

健康与医疗 韩国

Abstract

We examine how physical health, health behaviors, and mortality vary with income for prime-age adults. To do so, we leverage population-wide, individual-level cardiovascular health exams linked to income tax records and subsequent mortality for a representative sample of about 350,000 Koreans aged 47--57. For both men and women, there is a negative disease-income gradient for high blood pressure, high blood glucose, and high body mass index, but not for high cholesterol. Disease-income gradients are steeper for women than men, and tend to be concentrated in the top half of the income distribution; similar patterns hold for health behavior-income gradients. Subsequent mortality-income gradients are also concentrated in the top half of the income distribution, but are steeper for men than women. Although physical health and behavior measures are highly predictive of subsequent mortality, differences in these measures by income explain very little of the income-mortality gradient, even for cardiovascular mortality. Our findings suggest that something other than differences in disease burden at prime age drives most of the income-mortality gradient at older ages.

中文摘要

我们考察壮年成年人的身体健康、健康行为和死亡率如何随收入变化。为此，我们利用与所得税记录和随后死亡率相关联的全人口、个体层面心血管健康检查，样本为代表约350,000名年龄47–57岁的韩国人。对于男性和女性，高血压、高血糖和高体重指数都存在负的疾病-收入梯度，但高胆固醇不存在。女性的疾病-收入梯度比男性更陡，并且往往集中在收入分布的上半部分；健康行为-收入梯度也存在类似模式。随后的死亡率-收入梯度也集中在收入分布的上半部分，但男性比女性更陡。尽管身体健康和行为指标对随后死亡率具有高度预测性，但这些指标按收入的差异只能解释收入-死亡率梯度的很小部分，即使对心血管死亡率也是如此。我们的发现表明，除壮年疾病负担差异之外的其他因素驱动了老年收入-死亡率梯度的大部分。
