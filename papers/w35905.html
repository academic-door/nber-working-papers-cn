NBER Working Paper w35905所在周报

Credit Markets with New Screening Technologies

具有新筛选技术的信贷市场

Maryam Farboodi, Péter Kondor, Pablo Kurlat

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

We study equilibrium market structure in a model of credit-market competition with correlated or uncorrelated screening errors and endogenous screening technologies. A pool externality arises because rejected borrowers reapply elsewhere, so lenders’ screening and pricing choices affect competitors’ applicant pools. With independent errors, a single market segment emerges, with rates rising smoothly as lenders who choose better technologies lend to more adversely selected borrowers. With correlated screening errors, potentially stemming from similar AI systems and overlapping financial-sector data, ex-ante identical lenders instead specialize across qualitatively distinct segments, generating a hockey-stick interest-rate schedule: segments with uniform rates but heterogeneous borrower quality coexist with segments featuring uniform quality but dispersed rates, echoing traditional banking, fintech, and high-rate indiscriminate lending. We show how technological and regulatory changes affecting screening costs, including frontier AI innovation and various forms of open banking, unevenly affect financial access and can either benefit or harm financially excluded borrowers.

中文摘要

我们研究一个信贷市场竞争模型的均衡市场结构，该模型具有相关或不相关的筛选误差以及内生筛选技术。由于被拒绝的借款人会到别处重新申请，因此产生池外部性，贷款机构的筛选和定价选择会影响竞争对手的申请池。在独立误差下，出现单一市场细分，利率随着选择更好技术的贷款机构向更多逆向选择的借款人放贷而平滑上升。在相关筛选误差下（可能源于相似的人工智能系统和重叠的金融部门数据），事前相同的贷款机构反而在性质不同的细分市场之间专业化，产生曲棍球杆式利率曲线：统一利率但借款人质量异质的细分市场与质量统一但利率分散的细分市场并存，呼应传统银行、金融科技和高利率无差别放贷。我们展示影响筛选成本的技术和监管变化（包括前沿人工智能创新和各种形式的开放银行）如何不均衡地影响金融可得性，并可能使被金融排斥的借款人受益或受损。
