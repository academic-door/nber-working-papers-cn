Avoiding Regulation: Evidence from Nursing Homes

规避监管：来自养老院的证据

Alden Cheng, Riley League

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

We study strategic responses by firms to threshold-based regulations. We document extensive bunching in response to additional staffing requirements for California nursing homes with at least 100 beds. To estimate the regulation’s effect on staffing, we develop a novel treatment effects estimator for threshold-based regulations under precise manipulation, by decomposing firms into response types based on counterfactual and realized staffing and size, separating causal effects from selection. We estimate that higher staffing above the threshold is nearly entirely due to selection, with low-staffing facilities manipulating their size to avoid the regulation. These size distortions limit access, particularly for disadvantaged patients.

中文摘要

我们研究企业对基于阈值的监管的策略性反应。我们记录了加利福尼亚州拥有至少100张床位的养老院在应对额外人员配备要求时出现的大量聚集现象。为了估计该监管对人员配备的影响，我们开发了一种新的处理效应估计量，用于在精确操纵下基于阈值的监管，通过根据反事实和实际人员配备及规模将企业分解为反应类型，将因果效应与选择分离。我们估计，超过阈值以上的更高人员配备几乎完全源于选择，低人员配备的设施操纵其规模以规避监管。这些规模扭曲限制了可及性，尤其是对弱势患者而言。
