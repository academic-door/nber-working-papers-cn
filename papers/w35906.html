Home-Foreign Elasticity Functions and the Gains from Trade

本国—外国弹性函数与贸易收益

Jonathan Vogel

国际贸易 全球/多国

Abstract

Gains from trade are typically calculated using a constant trade elasticity estimated from substitution across foreign countries. For general homothetic preferences, we show how to calculate these gains from the initial domestic expenditure share and the home-foreign elasticity function along a price path to autarky. While this function generally depends on the specific price path, under weak separability it does not. We provide conditions for its nonparametric identification and estimate sector-level elasticity functions using tariff changes implied by trade agreements. Across WIOD and OECD ICIO data, estimated home-foreign elasticities generally rise with domestic expenditure shares. Using estimated elasticity functions in a multi-sector model with intermediate inputs, the median gain across the WIOD countries is 19.37 log points, compared with 24.43 when each country-sector elasticity is held fixed at its initial value.

中文摘要

贸易收益通常使用从外国国家间替代估计的恒定贸易弹性来计算。对于一般位似偏好，我们展示如何从初始国内支出份额以及沿价格路径至自给自足的本国—外国弹性函数来计算这些收益。虽然该函数通常取决于具体价格路径，但在弱可分性下则不然。我们提供其非参数识别的条件，并利用贸易协定所隐含的关税变化估计部门层面的弹性函数。在WIOD和OECD ICIO数据中，估计的本国—外国弹性通常随国内支出份额上升。在包含中间投入的多部门模型中使用估计的弹性函数，WIOD国家的收益中位数为19.37个对数点，而将每个国家—部门弹性固定在其初始值时则为24.43。
