Early Results from the RESET Demonstration Project: Inflation in Food at Home

RESET示范项目的早期结果：家庭食品通胀

Steve Coffey, Gabriel Ehrlich, John C. Haltiwanger, Ron S. Jarmin, David Johnson, Tod Johnson, Matthew D. Shapiro

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper presents early results from the RESET Demonstration Project, an initiative that constructs price and sales indices using item-level transactions data. The project’s overarching goal is to demonstrate the feasibility and advantages of leveraging modern transactions data for economic measurement by pointing toward a future in which official economic statistics are rebuilt on 21st-century data and technology. The paper introduces new inflation measures for Food at Home and evaluates them on two fronts: how they compare to current official statistics and what they offer as novel measures that capture more accurately the dynamics of consumer choice. The paper also addresses the challenges of producing reliable and credible official statistics from item-level transactions data and reports findings on recent Food at Home inflation trends.

中文摘要

本文介绍了RESET示范项目的早期结果，该倡议使用项目级交易数据构建价格和销售指数。该项目的总体目标是通过指向一个以21世纪数据和技术重建官方经济统计的未来，来证明利用现代交易数据进行经济测量的可行性和优势。本文引入了家庭食品的新通胀指标，并从两个方面对其进行评估：它们与当前官方统计相比如何，以及作为能够更准确捕捉消费者选择动态的新指标，它们提供了什么。本文还讨论了从项目级交易数据生产可靠且可信官方统计所面临的挑战，并报告了近期家庭食品通胀趋势的发现。
