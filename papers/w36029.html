Childhood Learning and Adult Earnings in Four LMIC Cohorts

四个中低收入国家队列中的儿童期学习与成年收入

Jishnu Das, Yash Dhuldhoya, Marta Favara, Amory Gethin, Alan Sánchez, Abhijeet Singh

教育与劳动 埃塞俄比亚、印度、巴基斯坦、秘鲁

Abstract

Researchers and policymakers in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs) have customarily relied on indirect evidence to justify investments in learning, either by linking test scores to economic growth or by connecting adult cognitive assessments to earnings. This reliance on indirect evidence largely stems from the scarcity of longitudinal data linking school-age learning to adult earnings. We address this gap by using data from Ethiopia, India, Pakistan, and Peru to estimate associations between foundational learning (FL), measured by test scores around age 12, and human capital and earnings in young adulthood. In Peru, India, and Pakistan, a one-standard-deviation increase in FL is associated with an 8-13 log point increase in adult earnings. Childhood scores also predict 1.1-1.5 additional years of schooling and 9.3-13.5 percentage points higher college or vocational attendance, as well as higher skills in adulthood. Interestingly, a linear-projection decomposition indicates that measured schooling and adult test scores explain between 18% (Peru) to 50-56% (India and Pakistan) of the FL-earnings gradient, suggesting that FL may raise earnings over and above the association with schooling and adult scores. Finally, FL can account for about 5-12 percent of the earnings gap between young Ethiopians and Indians in our sample, a number that could rise to 33 percent as returns to learning grow over the life cycle. We interpret these descriptive findings in light of recent causal evidence on the learning-to-earnings pipeline and discuss the implications for policy investments in FL.

中文摘要

低收入和中等收入国家（LMICs）的研究者和政策制定者通常依赖间接证据来证明投资于学习的合理性，方法是将考试成绩与经济增长联系起来，或将成人认知评估与收入联系起来。这种对间接证据的依赖在很大程度上源于将学龄期学习与成年收入联系起来的纵向数据稀缺。我们利用埃塞俄比亚、印度、巴基斯坦和秘鲁的数据来填补这一空白，估计以12岁左右的考试成绩衡量的基础学习（FL）与成年早期人力资本和收入之间的关联。在秘鲁、印度和巴基斯坦，FL每提高1个标准差，与成年收入增加8-13个对数点相关。儿童期成绩还预测受教育年限增加1.1-1.5年，大学或职业教育的就读率提高9.3-13.5个百分点，以及成年后技能更高。有趣的是，线性投影分解表明，测得的学校教育和成人考试成绩解释了FL-收入梯度的18%（秘鲁）至50-56%（印度和巴基斯坦），这表明FL可能在 schooling 和成人成绩关联之外提高收入。最后，FL可以解释我们样本中埃塞俄比亚青年和印度青年之间收入差距的约5-12%，随着学习回报在整个生命周期中增长，这一数字可能升至33%。我们结合近期关于学习到收入管道的因果证据来解读这些描述性发现，并讨论对FL政策投资的启示。
