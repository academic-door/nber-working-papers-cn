NBER Working Paper w35909所在周报

The Robust Properties of Economies with Endogenous Production Networks

内生生产网络经济的稳健性质

Simone Cerreia-Vioglio, Roberto Corrao, Joel P. Flynn, Giacomo Lanzani

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

We characterize equilibria in a canonical class of economies with endogenous production networks, showing that log-prices equal a min-max over networks of a primitive affine function of log-productivities. This formula captures how resilience and fragility interact: the "min" reflects resilience from substituting suppliers, while the "max" reflects fragility from complementarity-induced rigidity. Applying these results, we generalize Hulten's theorem, finding that local Domar weights are generally insufficient to describe propagation of even small shocks. Moreover, we characterize how microeconomic volatility affects GDP, finding that smooth changes in Domar weights overstate its adverse effects by omitting option value from switching suppliers.

中文摘要

我们刻画了一类具有内生生产网络的典型经济中的均衡，表明对数价格等于网络上的极小极大值，该极小极大值是对数生产率的原始仿射函数。这一公式捕捉了韧性与脆弱性如何相互作用：“极小”反映了替代供应商带来的韧性，而“极大”反映了互补性引起的刚性所带来的脆弱性。应用这些结果，我们推广了Hulten定理，发现局部Domar权重通常不足以描述即使是小冲击的传播。此外，我们刻画了微观经济波动如何影响GDP，发现Domar权重的平滑变化通过忽略转换供应商的期权价值而高估了其不利影响。
