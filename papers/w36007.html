NBER Working Paper w36007所在周报

Horizontal Equity of Taxation: Citizen Beliefs and Policy Preferences

税收的横向公平：公民信念与政策偏好

Pierre Bachas, Christopher A. Hoy, Anders Jensen, Mahvish Shaukat

公共财政与税收 巴基斯坦

Abstract

Taxes are harder to enforce on the self-employed than on wage earners, creating horizontal inequities among taxpayers with similar incomes. Using tax microsimulation and household survey data from 29 developing countries, we show that horizontal inequities in actual tax systems are comparable in magnitude to vertical equity and are exacerbated by progressive reforms that fall disproportionately on wage earners. We then conduct surveys and randomized information experiments in Pakistan to study public perceptions of these inequities. Although providing information raises fairness concerns, it does not increase support for reforms reducing it, revealing an important political constraint to improving tax equity.

中文摘要

对自雇者征税比工薪收入者更难，从而在收入相近的纳税人之间造成横向不平等。我们使用来自29个发展中国家的税收微观模拟和家庭调查数据表明，实际税制中的横向不平等在量级上与纵向公平相当，并且会因累进改革而加剧，因为这些改革不成比例地落在工薪收入者身上。随后我们在巴基斯坦开展调查和随机信息实验，研究公众对这些不平等的看法。尽管提供信息提高了公平关切，但并未增加对减少不平等的改革的支持，这揭示了改善税收公平的一个重要政治约束。
