The GDPR and SDK Usage In Android Mobile Apps

GDPR与安卓移动应用中的SDK使用

Ginger Zhe Jin, Ziqiao Liu, Liad Wagman

其他主题 美国

Abstract

Using data on Software Development Kits (SDKs), we study how SDK usage by Android apps changed after the European Union rolled out the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in May 2018. Relative to US-only apps, the number of non-Google SDKs used per app in five major European countries (EU5) exhibits a 3.6% decline in EU5-only apps after the GDPR, and an 8.7% decline in apps multihoming US and EU5. These effects are mostly driven by lower-ranked apps. Using monthly active users as weights to compute average user exposure to SDKs across app categories and country groups, we find no significant reduction in the average SDK exposure in EU5-only or multihoming apps relative to US-only apps. Overall, these findings suggest that the GDPR had only limited effects on the SDK layer of Android apps and did not materially reduce user exposure to the most prevalent SDK ecosystems.

中文摘要

利用软件开发工具包（SDK）的数据，我们研究了2018年5月欧盟推出《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）后，安卓应用对SDK的使用如何变化。相对于仅在美国运营的应用，GDPR后，在五个主要欧洲国家（EU5）仅运营的应用中，每个应用使用的非谷歌SDK数量下降了3.6%，而在美国和EU5同时运营（多归属）的应用中下降了8.7%。这些影响主要由排名较低的应用驱动。以月度活跃用户为权重计算各应用类别和国家组中用户的平均SDK暴露量，我们发现EU5专属或多归属应用相对于仅美国应用，其平均SDK暴露量没有显著减少。总体而言，这些发现表明GDPR对安卓应用的SDK层影响有限，并未实质性减少用户对最普遍SDK生态系统的暴露。
