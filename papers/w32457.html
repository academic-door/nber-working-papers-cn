Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC), Bank Deposits, and Digital Payments: First Evidence from a Live Retail CBDC

中央银行数字货币（CBDC）、银行存款和数字支付：来自活跃零售CBDC的首个证据

Marco Di Maggio, Pulak Ghosh, Soumya Kanti Ghosh, Nishant Vats, Andrew Wu

宏观与货币财政 印度

Abstract

We present the first empirical evidence on how a live retail central bank digital currency (CBDC) affects bank funding, lending, and payments, using the rollout of India’s digital rupee. Exploiting quasi-experimental variation from the Reserve Bank of India’s staggered authorization of lead banks and centrally coordinated CBDC marketing campaigns at a large public-sector bank, we show that CBDC eligibility reduces retail deposits by 2.7%, concentrated in liquid savings accounts, while illiquid term deposits remain unchanged. Credit falls initially but quickly recovers, consistent with banks adjusting their funding mix. On the payments side, CBDC crowds out deposit-backed digital rails rather than cash usage. Early adoption is skewed toward wealthier, uninsured, and more educated depositors. A simple sufficient-statistics framework implies that the net welfare effect is positive, with household convenience and safety gains outweighing the costs of lost deposit franchise value, temporary credit contraction, and more expensive wholesale funding.

中文摘要

我们利用印度数字卢比的推广，提供了关于活跃的零售中央银行数字货币（CBDC）如何影响银行资金、贷款和支付的第一个实证证据。利用印度储备银行交错授权牵头银行以及一家大型国有银行中央协调的CBDC营销活动所产生的准实验变化，我们表明，CBDC的资格使零售存款减少了2.7%，主要集中在流动性储蓄账户，而非流动性定期存款保持不变。信贷最初下降，但很快恢复，这与银行调整其融资结构相一致。在支付方面，CBDC挤出了基于存款的数字支付轨道，而非现金使用。早期采用者偏向于更富有、未投保和受教育程度更高的储户。一个简单的充分统计量框架表明，净福利效应为正，家庭便利性和安全性收益超过了存款特许经营权价值损失、暂时性信贷收缩以及更昂贵的批发融资成本。
