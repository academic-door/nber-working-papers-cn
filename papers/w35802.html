The Political Foundations of Industrial Policy: Emergence, Endurance, and Exhaustion

产业政策的政治基础：兴起、延续与耗尽

Lottie Field, Réka Juhász, Michele Zampa

其他主题 意大利、美国

Abstract

We study how politics shapes industrial policy through two paradigmatic twentieth-century cases: the Cassa per il Mezzogiorno in Italy (1950–1984) and Kennedy's Moonshot in the United States (1961–1972). Drawing on historical and primary political sources, we show that each policy originated in a broader political project rather than in an economic diagnosis: the Christian Democrats' pursuit of national integration and democratic consolidation, and Kennedy's New Frontier. We introduce the concept of industrial policy vision - a publicly articulated, transformational objective that political leaders attach to a set of ideals and pursue through state-led intervention in the economy - and develop a five-component definition that makes it empirically tractable. We then trace each vision through decades of newspaper coverage, combining topic modeling and sentiment analysis with systematic close reading. In both cases, public debate proceeded largely within the frame the vision set, granting the policies tolerance for setbacks and expense. The cases diverge in their fates: the Moonshot's bounded vision was redeemed on schedule, while the Cassa's open-ended vision was exhausted by accumulated failure and the policy outlived it only briefly. This divergence points to our central conclusion: while a vision holds, it sets the terms on which an industrial policy's performance is judged, including what counts as failure and how much time the policy is given to deliver.

中文摘要

我们通过20世纪两个典型案例研究政治如何塑造产业政策：意大利的南方基金（1950—1984年）和美国的肯尼迪登月计划（1961—1972年）。基于历史资料和原始政治文献，我们表明，每项政策都源于更广泛的政治工程，而非经济诊断：基督教民主党追求国家整合与民主巩固，以及肯尼迪的“新边疆”。我们引入“产业政策愿景”这一概念——一种公开阐述的、变革性目标，政治领导人将其与一套理想相联系，并通过国家主导的经济干预加以追求——并发展出一个包含五个组成部分的定义，使其在实证上可操作。随后，我们通过数十年的报纸报道追踪每种愿景，将主题建模和情感分析与系统性的细读相结合。在两个案例中，公共辩论基本上都在愿景设定的框架内进行，从而赋予这些政策对挫折和开支的容忍度。两个案例的命运出现分化：登月计划有边界的愿景按计划得到兑现，而南方基金开放式的愿景则因累积的失败而耗尽，政策在其之后仅短暂存续。这种分化指向我们的核心结论：只要一种愿景仍然成立，它就设定了评判产业政策表现的条款，包括什么算作失败，以及给予政策多长时间来交付成果。
