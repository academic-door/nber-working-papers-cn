Are Republicans and Democrats Different Taxpayers? Evidence From Property Tax Appeals

共和党人和民主党人是不同的纳税人吗？来自房产税上诉的证据

Brad C. Nathan, Ricardo Perez-Truglia, Alejandro Zentner

公共财政与税收 美国

Abstract

Democrats and Republicans express some sharply different views about tax policy, but whether these ideological divisions translate into partisan differences in taxpayer behavior is far less clear. We study this question in the context of a common form of tax avoidance: property tax appeals. In a survey, we find that Americans, on average, believe Republican homeowners are more likely to appeal their property taxes than Democratic homeowners with similarly valued homes. Yet linking administrative property tax records from Dallas County with voting records reveals that this stereotype is not borne out in practice: the partisan gap in appeals is small, both in absolute terms (0.29 percentage points) and relative to several demographic gaps in appeals we use as benchmarks. Similar appeal rates could nonetheless conceal differences in motives. Using quasi-experimental and field experimental evidence, we find that Democrats and Republicans appeal for broadly similar reasons. In sum, despite differing tax policy preferences and public perceptions about their behavior, Democrats and Republicans are strikingly similar in both their propensity to appeal their property taxes and their underlying motives.

中文摘要

民主党人和共和党人对税收政策表达了一些截然不同的观点，但这些意识形态分歧是否转化为纳税人行为上的党派差异则远不清楚。我们在房产税上诉这一常见避税形式的背景下研究这个问题。在一项调查中，我们发现美国人平均认为共和党房主比拥有类似价值房屋的民主党房主更可能对其房产税提出上诉。然而，将达拉斯县的行政房产税记录与投票记录关联后发现，这种刻板印象在实践中并未得到证实：上诉中的党派差距很小，无论是绝对值（0.29个百分点）还是相对于我们用作基准的若干人口统计上诉差距而言。相似的上诉率仍可能掩盖动机差异。使用准实验和实地实验证据，我们发现民主党人和共和党人上诉的原因大体相似。总之，尽管税收政策偏好不同且公众对其行为的看法不同，民主党人和共和党人在上诉房产税的倾向及其潜在动机上惊人地相似。
