The Investment Channel of Monetary Policy: Disentangling Firm Heterogeneity

货币政策的投资渠道：解构企业异质性

Thomas Drechsel, Daniel Lewis, Davide Melcangi, Laura Pilossoph

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

To study monetary policy transmission at the firm level, researchers typically consider heterogeneity along a small number of firm characteristics, such as size or leverage. We instead estimate the full distribution of firms’ investment responses to monetary policy, using a clustering regression framework. Our novel approach captures multidimensional and unobservable heterogeneity across firms and time. We find that investment by most firms in most time periods responds little to monetary policy. Only about 5% of firm-time observations are associated with a strong response. We then investigate the sources of heterogeneity ex post. We find that most of the variation in investment sensitivity arises within firms over time. Although several firm characteristics predict responsiveness to monetary policy, the majority of heterogeneity remains unexplained by firm observables. We demonstrate that our empirical estimates provide new targets for disciplining structural models of firm heterogeneity.

中文摘要

为研究企业层面的货币政策传导，研究者通常考虑少数几个企业特征（如规模或杠杆率）上的异质性。我们则采用聚类回归框架，估计企业对货币政策投资反应的完整分布。我们的新方法捕捉了企业间和企业跨时间的多维且不可观测的异质性。我们发现，大多数企业在大多数时期的投资对货币政策反应甚微。仅有约5%的企业-时间观测值与强烈反应相关。随后我们事后考察异质性的来源。我们发现，投资敏感性的大部分变异来自企业自身随时间的变化。尽管若干企业特征可以预测对货币政策的反应程度，但大部分异质性仍无法由企业可观测变量解释。我们证明，我们的实证估计为约束企业异质性结构模型提供了新的目标。
