NBER Working Paper w35823所在周报

Optimal Population Growth in the Diamond OLG Model

Diamond OLG模型中的最优人口增长

Laurence M. Ball

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper studies the effects of population growth on welfare in an OLG model, in which lower population growth raises output per worker but also reduces the ratio of workers to retirees. Under fairly mild restrictions on the model’s parameters, there exists an optimal population-growth rate—one that maximizes steady-state welfare. This growth rate may well be negative: it may be optimal for the population to shrink over time. The introduction of a pay-as-you-go social security system reduces the optimal population-growth rate.

中文摘要

本文研究了人口增长对OLG模型中福利的影响。在该模型中，较低的人口增长会提高每工人产出，但也会降低劳动者与退休人员之比。在模型参数受到相当温和限制的情况下，存在一个最优人口增长率——它使稳态福利最大化。该增长率很可能为负：人口随时间收缩可能是最优的。引入现收现付社会保障制度会降低最优人口增长率。
