Past Automation and Future AI: How Weak Links Tame the Growth Explosion

过去的自动化与未来的AI：弱环节如何抑制增长爆炸

Charles I. Jones, Christopher Tonetti

宏观与货币财政 美国

Abstract

How much past growth is due to automation, and what does this imply for the future of AI? We perform task-based growth accounting and show that capital task productivity has grown about 5 percentage points per year faster than labor task productivity in the U.S. economy. The main benefit of automation is using rapidly improving machines instead of slowly-improving humans on more tasks. With these historical measures, we calibrate a growth model in which the production of goods and ideas is endogenously automated. Growth accelerates and can explode. However, the acceleration is slowed by "weak links," tasks still performed by labor.

中文摘要

过去增长中有多少归因于自动化，这对AI的未来意味着什么？我们进行基于任务的增长核算，并表明在美国经济中，资本任务生产率比劳动任务生产率每年快约5个百分点。自动化的主要好处是在更多任务上使用快速改进的机器而非缓慢改进的人类。利用这些历史度量，我们校准了一个商品和思想生产内生自动化的增长模型。增长加速并可能爆炸。然而，加速被“弱环节”——仍由劳动执行的任务——所减缓。
