Aging and Structural Change: Cost Disease in the Care Sector

老龄化与结构变迁：护理部门中的成本病

Nick Pretnar, Finn Kydland, Tonya Y. Kydland

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

For most of the service sector, market productivity growth has pulled production out of the home. We show that formal nursing care is an exception because its productivity growth has been negative. A two-sector general equilibrium overlapping generations model with informal care attributes the rise in formal care's relative price to this decline, not demography. Care/non-care complementarity dominates formal/informal complementarity. As formal prices rise, elders substitute non-care consumption for formal care, shifting care onto informal caregivers. Population aging need not slow growth if non-care productivity rises or entering cohorts grow and its effects are offset by declining care needs.

中文摘要

对于大多数服务业而言，市场生产率增长已将生产从家庭中拉出。我们表明，正规护理是一个例外，因为其生产率增长为负。一个包含非正规护理的两部门一般均衡世代交叠模型将正规护理相对价格的上升归因于这一下降，而非人口结构。护理/非护理之间的互补性主导了正规/非正规之间的互补性。随着正规价格上升，老年人以非护理消费替代正规护理，将护理转移给非正规照护者。如果非护理生产率上升或进入队列增长，且其影响被下降的护理需求所抵消，人口老龄化未必会减缓增长。
