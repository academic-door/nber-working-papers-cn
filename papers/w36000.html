When More Prenatal Care Looks Like Less: Selection, Information and Maternal and Infant Health

当更多产前护理看起来像更少：选择、信息与母婴健康

Chien-Tzu Cheng, Shin-Yi Chou, Hsien-Ming Lien

健康与医疗 中国相关 台灣

Abstract

We use Taiwan's 2003 SARS epidemic as a natural experiment, instrumenting prenatal care with gestational exposure to SARS-induced fear that reduced visits by over 20 percent. Adequate prenatal care reduces preventable maternal complications by 3.4 percentage points (26 percent), with no effect on infant outcomes. OLS estimates are perversely signed for maternal complications but overstate benefits for infant outcomes, consistent with competing patient- and provider-driven selection. Mechanism evidence points to prenatal care as a longitudinal information system: repeated contact facilitates detection of evolving risks and generates clinical information that informs delivery decisions, including delivery-mode routing and subsequent management at delivery.

中文摘要

我们利用台湾2003年SARS疫情作为自然实验，以孕期暴露于SARS引发的恐惧作为产前护理的工具变量，该恐惧使就诊次数减少超过20%。充足的产前护理使可预防的孕产妇并发症降低3.4个百分点（26%），对婴儿结局无影响。OLS估计对孕产妇并发症的符号反常，但高估了婴儿结局的收益，这与患者驱动和提供者驱动的竞争性选择一致。机制证据表明产前护理是一个纵向信息系统：反复接触有助于发现演变中的风险，并产生为分娩决策提供信息的临床信息，包括分娩方式路径安排和分娩时的后续管理。
