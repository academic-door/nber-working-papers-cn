Pricing Power in the Mortgage Market: Evidence from GSE Fee Waivers

抵押贷款市场的定价能力：来自政府支持企业费用豁免的证据

Neil Bhutta, Benjamin J. Keys, Adithya Raajkumar

国际贸易 未指明区域

Abstract

Market power may arise not only from firms, but also from individual salespeople who can exploit informational advantages over consumers. We explore this phenomenon in the mortgage market by examining a Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac fee waiver targeted at lower-income first-time homebuyers. We estimate that mortgage intermediaries capture over 60% of $1.5 billion in total fee waivers during 2023–2024, rather than fully passing these savings through to borrowers. Pass-through varies substantially across loan officers: those with a track record of originating expensive mortgages (independent of lender pricing) pass through less than 20% of the waiver. Our evidence suggests that loan officers retain incentives to steer borrowers into higher-rate mortgages, despite post-crisis regulations designed to limit such practices.

中文摘要

市场势力不仅可能来自企业，也可能来自能够利用对消费者信息优势的个体销售人员。我们通过考察针对低收入首次购房者的房利美/房地美费用豁免，在抵押贷款市场中探讨这一现象。我们估计，在2023—2024年期间，抵押贷款中介获取了15亿美元总费用豁免中的超过60%，而非将这些节省完全转嫁给借款人。转嫁率在贷款专员之间差异很大：那些有发放高成本抵押贷款记录（与贷款机构定价无关）的贷款专员转嫁的豁免不足20%。我们的证据表明，尽管危机后监管旨在限制此类做法，贷款专员仍保留将借款人引导至更高利率抵押贷款的激励。
