Explaining Realized Returns: Roll’s R2 40 Years Later

解释已实现收益：Roll的R2四十年后

Bryan T. Kelly, Yinan Su, Chao Zhang

宏观与货币财政 美国

Abstract

Can we explain asset price movements even with ex post observations of their drivers? Reinterpreting Roll’s (1988) AFA Presidential Address, “R2,” as a modern empirical exercise, we show that conventional macroeconomic, cash-flow, expectations, and flow variables deliver out-of-sample R2 values that are often negative and otherwise below 10% for monthly U.S. equity returns and are unavailable at the daily frequency. We use generative AI to extract newspaper-stated reasons from Wall Street Journal market summaries and filter out price and return language. These reasons explain around 30–40% of daily and monthly returns out of sample. Their content maps to interpretable themes, including corporate news, macroeconomic conditions, monetary policy, sentiment, and geopolitics.

中文摘要

即使事后观察到资产价格的驱动因素，我们能否解释资产价格变动？将Roll（1988）AFA主席演讲“R2”重新解释为一项现代实证研究，我们表明，传统的宏观经济、现金流、预期和流量变量对美国月度股票收益提供的样本外R2值往往为负，否则低于10%，并且在日度频率上不可获得。我们使用生成式AI从《华尔街日报》市场摘要中提取报纸陈述的原因，并过滤掉价格和收益语言。这些原因在样本外解释了约30–40%的日度和月度收益。其内容映射到可解释的主题，包括公司新闻、宏观经济状况、货币政策、情绪和地缘政治。
