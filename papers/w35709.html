Signals, Steps, and Setbacks: Age Discrimination in a Laddered Labor Market

信号、阶梯与挫折：阶梯式劳动力市场中的年龄歧视

Shuaizhang Feng, Lars Lefgren, Jieyi Liu, Brennan Platt, Xiaoyu Xia

教育与劳动 中国相关

Abstract

We study age discrimination in a labor market with a job ladder, where worker productivity is revealed over time. Using data from a large Chinese recruitment platform that links job postings, applications, and employer responses, we document sharp declines in applications and callbacks at posted age cutoffs. Discrimination operates in both directions: older applicants are disadvantaged in entry-level jobs, while younger applicants are less likely to be hired into managerial roles. A model of statistical discrimination with imperfect signal screening explains these patterns. Consistent with the model, older applicants to entry-level jobs are negatively selected relative to their peers.

中文摘要

我们研究了具有职业阶梯的劳动力市场中的年龄歧视，其中工人生产率随时间推移而显现。利用来自中国大型招聘平台的数据（该平台将职位发布、申请和雇主回应联系起来），我们记录了在公布的年龄截止点处申请和回电的急剧下降。歧视在两个方向上都起作用：年长申请者在入门级工作中处于劣势，而年轻申请者被聘用到管理岗位的可能性较低。一个具有不完美信号筛选的统计歧视模型解释了这些模式。与该模型一致，申请入门级工作的年长申请者相对于其同龄人存在负向选择。
