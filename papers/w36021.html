Impacts of AI in Higher Education: A Panel Survey of Undergraduates

人工智能在高等教育中的影响：本科生面板调查

Bruce Sacerdote, Mason Parris-Bacon

教育与劳动 美国

Abstract

We report findings from the first wave of a survey of undergraduates including separate samples of Dartmouth students, graduating high school seniors, and U.S. college students more broadly. Students report extensive use of AI both for tasks which are likely harmful to learning and tasks which are likely helpful to learning. Students report much higher peer misuse of AI relative to reports of own misuse. Ninety-two percent of students agree or strongly agree that use of AI is harmful to writing. Use of graded out-of-class assignments has become increasingly unfair due to cheating with AI. But students see the value of using AI to answer conceptual questions in real time, create practice quizzes and study guides and to enable meaningful and efficient progress on research projects. Eighty-four percent of Dartmouth respondents report that AI is changing the nature of jobs in their desired (future) field as opposed to eliminating jobs. AI’s perceived value and harms vary widely by academic discipline suggesting that use, rules, and approaches must also vary greatly based on the discipline and context. Students are largely self-taught in AI but have strong demand for incorporating AI effectively in the curriculum.

中文摘要

我们报告了一轮本科生调查的发现，样本包括达特茅斯学院学生、即将毕业的高中生以及更广泛的美国大学生等独立样本。学生报告在可能有害于学习的任务和可能有助于学习的任务中都广泛使用AI。学生报告同伴滥用AI的程度远高于自己滥用AI的报告。92%的学生同意或强烈同意使用AI对写作有害。由于利用AI作弊，计分课外作业已变得越来越不公平。但学生看到了使用AI实时回答概念性问题、创建练习测验和学习指南以及使研究项目取得有意义且高效进展的价值。84%的达特茅斯受访者报告，AI正在改变其期望（未来）领域的工作性质，而非消除工作。AI的感知价值和危害因学科而异，差异很大，表明使用、规则和方法也必须因学科和情境而大不相同。学生在AI方面 largely 是自学的，但对将AI有效纳入课程有强烈需求。
