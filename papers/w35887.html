Road Pricing under Heterogeneity

异质性下的道路定价

Itai Ater, Brad Ross, Adi Shany, Shoshana Vasserman

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

Congestion pricing policies are widely debated and critics often argue that toll costs outweigh congestion relief. We show theoretically that welfare gains depend on two forces: the extent of driver heterogeneity—both in preferences and in exposure to highly demanded roads and times of day—and the extent of revenue recycling. We quantify the impact of both forces using data from a large-scale experiment that tracked driving behavior with and without usage-based congestion pricing incentives for nearly 10,000 drivers over two years. Using a model of time-varying traffic equilibrium with heterogeneous drivers and a granular road network, we show that a congestion pricing policy using the experiment's price schedule would raise welfare by as much as adding a new lane to every highway and would raise enough revenue to fully compensate losers. Moreover, the extent of driver heterogeneity entails that optimal prices can improve net welfare with only a small fraction of toll revenue.

中文摘要

拥堵定价政策广受争议，批评者常常认为通行费成本超过拥堵缓解收益。我们从理论上表明，福利收益取决于两种力量：驾驶员异质性的程度——既包括偏好，也包括对高需求道路和时段的暴露程度——以及收入循环使用的程度。我们利用一项大规模实验的数据量化这两种力量的影响；该实验在两年内追踪了近10,000名驾驶员在有和没有基于使用量的拥堵定价激励下的驾驶行为。使用一个包含异质性驾驶员和细粒度道路网络的时变交通均衡模型，我们表明，采用实验价格方案的拥堵定价政策将带来的福利提升，相当于为每条高速公路新增一条车道，并且筹集的收入足以完全补偿受损者。此外，驾驶员异质性的程度意味着，最优价格只需很小一部分通行费收入就能改善净福利。
