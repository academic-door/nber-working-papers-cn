Education and Gender Voting Polarization: The Effect of Compulsory Schooling on Sex-Based Preferences for the Democratic and Republican Parties

教育与性别投票极化：义务教育法对民主党和共和党性别偏好的影响

Philip DeCicca, Harry Krashinsky, Erik T. Nesson

教育与劳动 美国

Abstract

One of the most notable developments in U.S. electoral politics over the last 40 years is the increasing divide between men and women voters. While men and women had roughly equivalent levels of support for both the Democratic and Republican parties in 1980, more recent surveys have revealed a roughly 10 to 15-percentage-point gap in preference for the Democratic party among women relative to men. We explain this result by using a variety of econometric strategies to establish a novel connection between compulsory schooling laws (CSLs), educational attainment, and diverging political preferences by sex. First, we show that the timing of this divergence is coincident with recent changes in CSLs, and that this divergence is almost entirely due to changes in sex-related preferences for the Democratic party in states where there exists a much stronger effect of CSLs on average educational attainment -- very little change in sex-related preferences for the Democratic party is present in other states. Furthermore, in states with more-impactful CSLs, changes in CSLs and their associated increased educational attainment explain almost all of the diverging support for political parties and differences in opinion over major policy issues by sex, such as gun control and abortion. Altogether, these new facts present an original explanation that relies upon the effect of education to explain the evolution of gender political preferences in the U.S. over the last 40 years.

中文摘要

过去40年美国选举政治最显著的发展之一是男性和女性选民之间日益扩大的分歧。1980年，男性和女性对民主党和共和党的支持水平大致相当，而更近期的调查显示，女性相对于男性对民主党的偏好存在约10至15个百分点的差距。我们通过使用多种计量经济学策略，建立义务教育法（CSLs）、教育 attainment 与按性别分化的政治偏好之间的新颖联系，来解释这一结果。首先，我们表明这一分歧的时间与近期CSLs的变化相吻合，并且这一分歧几乎完全归因于在CSLs对平均教育 attainment 影响强得多的州中，对民主党偏好的性别相关变化——在其他州，对民主党的性别相关偏好几乎没有变化。此外，在CSLs影响更大的州，CSLs的变化及其相关的教育 attainment 提高几乎解释了按性别分化的政党支持以及枪支管制和堕胎等重大政策议题意见差异的全部。总之，这些新事实提出了一个依赖教育效应来解释过去40年美国性别政治偏好演变的原创性解释。
