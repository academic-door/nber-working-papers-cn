The Effects of Private Plan Subsidies on Enrollment: Evidence from Medicare Advantage

私人计划补贴对参保的影响：来自Medicare Advantage的证据

Paul D. Jacobs, Timothy J. Layton

宏观与货币财政 美国

Abstract

Some social insurance programs, including the Medicare program in the United States, allow beneficiaries to choose between public and private insurance firms. In these types of programs, the government will often provide a voucher to those who opt for private firms. A key policy decision is the level of this voucher, and a key input to that decision is the elasticity of enrollment in the public versus the private option with respect to the size of the voucher. We estimate this elasticity in the Medicare program, where recent work has provided remarkably little evidence on how Medicare Advantage (MA) enrollment responds to payment despite the program’s rapid growth. We leverage the fact that the formula determining county-level “benchmark” payments (vouchers) involves coarse thresholds based on county-level spending and counties occasionally move across these thresholds, providing a shock to the vouchers unrelated to beneficiary demand. Using several complementary estimation strategies, we find that a 1 percent increase in a county’s benchmark raises MA enrollment by roughly half a percentage point within three years, implying an elasticity of about 2. Applying this estimate to a policy counterfactual that sets benchmarks at 95 percent of county fee-for-service spending implies a decline in MA enrollment of about 6.1 percentage points, or roughly one-quarter of our sample. We also provide suggestive evidence, consistent with prior work, that the increase in benchmarks gets passed through in the form of additional benefits, providing a mechanism for the enrollment increases.

中文摘要

一些社会保险项目，包括美国的Medicare项目，允许受益人在公共和私人保险公司之间进行选择。在这类项目中，政府通常会向选择私人公司的人提供代金券。一个关键的政策决策是代金券的水平，而该决策的一个关键输入是公共选项与私人选项的参保人数相对于代金券规模的弹性。我们在Medicare项目中估计这一弹性，尽管该项目快速增长，但近期研究提供的关于Medicare Advantage（MA）参保如何对支付作出反应的证据却出奇地少。我们利用这样一个事实：决定县级“基准”支付（代金券）的公式涉及基于县级支出的粗略阈值，且县偶尔会跨越这些阈值，从而提供了一种与受益人需求无关的代金券冲击。使用多种互补的估计策略，我们发现一个县的基准提高1%会使MA参保人数在三年内增加约半个百分点，意味着弹性约为2。将这一估计应用于一个将基准设定为县按服务收费支出95%的政策反事实，意味着MA参保人数下降约6.1个百分点，或约我们样本的四分之一。我们还提供了与先前研究一致的提示性证据，即基准的增加会以额外福利的形式传导，为参保增加提供了一种机制。
