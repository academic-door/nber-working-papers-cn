Deep Learning as a Projection Method for Solving Economic Models

深度学习作为求解经济模型的投影方法

Kenneth L. Judd, Karl Schmedders

理论与方法 未指明区域

Abstract

Deep learning is increasingly presented in economics as a new paradigm for solving dynamic models, superseding the classical projection methods of numerical economics. We argue that this dichotomy rests on a misclassification. The neural-network solvers emphasized in this literature are least-squares projection methods with an adaptive nonlinear parameterization. Placing deep learning inside the projection family reorients the agenda. The issue is not a contest between paradigms, but suggests a single toolkit using a variety of approximation methods, residual criteria, and evaluation designs to be chosen on the merits for the model at hand. In low and moderate dimensions, linear-basis and sparsegrid methods are often more accurate, faster, and easier to verify than a trained neural network. In high dimensions, an appropriate choice for the space of permissible functions will often avoid the curse of dimensionality.

中文摘要

在经济学中，深度学习日益被呈现为求解动态模型的新范式，取代了数值经济学的经典投影方法。我们认为，这种二分法建立在错误分类之上。这一文献所强调的神经网络求解器，是具有自适应非线性参数化的最小二乘投影方法。将深度学习置于投影方法族之内会重新调整研究议程。问题不在于范式之间的竞赛，而是提示我们使用单一工具箱，根据手头模型择优选择各种近似方法、残差准则和评估设计。在低维和中维情形下，线性基和稀疏网格方法通常比训练后的神经网络更准确、更快速且更容易验证。在高维情形下，对允许函数空间的适当选择往往能够避免维数灾难。
