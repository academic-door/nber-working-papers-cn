Does Food Assistance Drive Spending on Sugary Foods? Evidence from the Pandemic Electronic Benefit Transfer Program

食品援助会推动含糖食品支出吗？来自疫情期间电子福利转移计划的证据

Georgina Cisneros, Brandyn F. Churchill

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

We provide evidence on the extent to which food assistance affects spending on sugary items by studying the Pandemic Electronic Benefit Transfer (P-EBT) program, which provided funds to families of children who lost access to free and reduced-price school meals due to COVID-19 pandemic school closures. Leveraging state-level variation in the timing of P-EBT disbursements using a stacked difference-in-differences identification strategy with NielsenIQ Consumer Panel data, we find that P-EBT disbursement was associated with a 16-percent increase in weekly spending on sugary items among SNAP-eligible households with school-aged children. Notably, this increase exceeded the percentage increase in overall food spending for this group, suggesting that SNAP-eligible households with school-aged children allocated a disproportionately large share of their P-EBT-driven purchases toward sugary items relative to their pre-period baseline. We do not detect changes among households unlikely to have received P-EBT benefits or in placebo disbursement years.

中文摘要

我们通过研究疫情期间电子福利转移（P-EBT）计划，提供食品援助在多大程度上影响含糖商品支出的证据。该计划因 COVID-19 疫情期间学校关闭，向失去免费和减价学校餐机会的儿童家庭提供资金。利用 NielsenIQ Consumer Panel 数据，并采用堆叠双重差分识别策略，利用各州 P-EBT 发放时间差异，我们发现 P-EBT 发放与有学龄儿童的 SNAP 合格家庭每周含糖商品支出增加 16% 相关。值得注意的是，这一增幅超过了该群体总体食品支出的百分比增幅，表明有学龄儿童的 SNAP 合格家庭相对于其前期基线，将 P-EBT 驱动购买中不成比例地更大份额分配给了含糖商品。我们未在不太可能获得 P-EBT 福利的家庭中或安慰剂发放年份中检测到变化。
