The Global Office: How Globalisation’s Frontier is Shifting from Factories to Offices

全球办公室：全球化的前沿如何从工厂转向办公室

Richard Baldwin

宏观与货币财政 中国相关 美国

Abstract

This paper argues that globalisation’s frontier has shifted from manufacturing GVCs to services GVCs: from the Global Factory to the Global Office. While the Factory remains larger in gross trade, the Office is growing much faster. A heatmap of trade in intermediates shows that the Factory is bloc-shaped and dominated by China, whereas the Office is star-shaped and dominated by America. Gravity estimates show that distance matters less for Office exports, but time zones matter more: each hour of time-zone separation reduces Office exports by roughly 4%, while having no effect on Factory exports. Importantly, all of the world’s export-linked net job creation between 2008 and 2022 occurred in digitally delivered services rather than manufacturing, and trade-linked service jobs command higher average pay globally. The Factory-to-Office shift is also reshaping development. Most emerging economies have seen export-led growth tilt towards services, but their service exports are not yet large enough to drive overall GDP growth the way manufacturing did historically. The big picture is that globalisation’s second unbundling is running out of steam even as its third unbundling is powering ahead. The paper, which raises more questions than it answers, closes with the conjecture that the future of trade is services.

中文摘要

本文认为，全球化的前沿已从制造业全球价值链转向服务业全球价值链：从全球工厂转向全球办公室。虽然工厂在总贸易中仍然更大，但办公室增长得快得多。中间品贸易热力图显示，工厂呈块状形态并由中国主导，而办公室呈星状形态并由美国主导。引力估计显示，距离对办公室出口的重要性较低，但时区更重要：每增加1小时时区差异使办公室出口减少约4%，而对工厂出口没有影响。重要的是，2008年至2022年间全球所有与出口相关的净就业创造都发生在数字化交付服务而非制造业中，且与贸易相关的服务岗位在全球范围内平均薪酬更高。从工厂到办公室的转变也在重塑发展。大多数新兴经济体的出口导向型增长已向服务业倾斜，但其服务出口尚不足以像制造业历史上那样推动整体GDP增长。总体图景是，全球化的第二次拆分正在失去动力，而其第三次拆分正在加速推进。本文提出的问题多于答案，最后以贸易的未来是服务业的猜想作结。
