NBER Working Paper w35694所在周报所在月度合集

Comparisons

比较

Thomas Graeber, Benjamin Enke

公共财政与税收 未指明区域

Abstract

Economic decisions are often shaped by comparison points such as reference points, goals, expectations and peers. A long-standing puzzle is that decisions sometimes increase in these comparators (assimilation) and sometimes decrease in them (contrast). We develop a simple taxonomy that predicts the sign. The idea is that when absolute evaluations—translating a decision input into an output—are difficult, people use comparison points as information about how to navigate tradeoffs. This logic predicts that decisions decrease in input comparators—those tied to exogenous decision inputs (e.g., a typical hourly wage)—and increase in output comparators—those tied to endogenous decisions or outcomes (e.g., typical daily earnings). We test and confirm these predictions in experiments on labor supply, investment and belief updating, involving both social and expectations-based comparison points. We further show that comparison effects weaken both when decision uncertainty is experimentally reduced and when comparators are less informative, consistent with a mechanism of comparisons as information. Finally, a classification of the prior literature suggests that the input-vs.-output taxonomy organizes the sign of comparison effects across more than 100 experimental and field observational studies.

中文摘要

经济决策常常受到参照点、目标、期望和同伴等比较点的影响。一个长期存在的谜团是，决策有时会随着这些比较点的提高而增加（同化效应），有时又会随之减少（对比效应）。我们提出了一个简单的分类法来预测其方向。核心思想是，当绝对评估——即将决策输入转化为输出——较为困难时，人们会将比较点作为如何权衡取舍的信息。这一逻辑预测，决策会随着输入型比较点（与外部给定的决策输入相关，如典型的小时工资）的增加而减少，并随着输出型比较点（与内生决策或结果相关，如典型的日收入）的增加而增加。我们在涉及社会性比较点和基于期望的比较点的劳动供给、投资和信念更新实验中检验并证实了这些预测。我们进一步表明，当实验性地降低决策不确定性以及当比较点的信息含量较低时，比较效应都会减弱，这与“比较作为信息”的机制相符。最后，对既有文献的分类表明，输入型与输出型的分类法能够解释超过100项实验和实地观察研究中比较效应的方向。
