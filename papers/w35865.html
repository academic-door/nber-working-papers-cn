NBER Working Paper w35865所在周报

Financing the AI Buildout

为人工智能建设融资

Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh

AI 与数字经济 美国

Abstract

Artificial intelligence is driving a large expansion in data center capacity, power infrastructure, and specialized computing equipment. We estimate that a 1 GW AI campus costs about $41 billion. A 188 GW U.S. buildout completed by 2032 would require total investment of nearly$9 trillion and amount to annual investment of 3.2 percent of GDP over 2025–2032. As hyperscaler capital expenditures outgrow internal cash flow, financing is shifting toward leases, joint ventures, project debt, private credit, securitization, and special-purpose vehicles. These structures expand funding capacity but also raise asset-level leverage, obscure contingent obligations, and expose investors to tenant concentration, technological obsolescence, execution delays, and uncertain residual values.

中文摘要

人工智能正在推动数据中心容量、电力基础设施和专用计算设备的大规模扩张。我们估计，一个1 GW的AI园区成本约为410亿美元。到2032年完成的188 GW美国建设将需要近9万亿美元的总投资，相当于2025-2032年间每年投资占GDP的3.2%。随着超大规模企业的资本支出增长超过内部现金流，融资正转向租赁、合资企业、项目债务、私人信贷、证券化和特殊目的载体。这些结构扩大了融资能力，但也提高了资产层面的杠杆率，模糊了或有义务，并使投资者面临租户集中度、技术过时、执行延迟和剩余价值不确定等风险。
