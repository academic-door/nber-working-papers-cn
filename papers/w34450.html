Demand Elasticity in Dynamic Asset Pricing

动态资产定价中的需求弹性

Zhiguo He, Péter Kondor, Jessica S. Li

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

The demand-system approach identifies asset demand slopes using residual supply shocks, presuming these shocks move expected returns but not risk. In dynamic economies, risk is endogenous: investors retrade, so demand depends on the joint distribution of current and future returns, which changes as investors absorb a shock. The shock therefore changes the demand curve it traces, violating the exclusion restriction. In a calibrated multi-asset dynamic model, the measured slope is roughly 40% of its conceptual counterpart, implying substantially steeper demand curves than standard estimates suggest. The bias persists even if shocks are infinitesimal and transitory. We discuss potential empirical remedies.

中文摘要

需求系统方法利用剩余供给冲击来识别资产需求斜率，并假定这些冲击会改变预期收益但不会改变风险。在动态经济中，风险是内生的：投资者会重新交易，因此需求取决于当前和未来收益的联合分布，而这一分布会随着投资者吸收冲击而改变。因此，冲击改变了它所描绘的需求曲线，违反了排除性限制。在一个校准的多资产动态模型中，测得的斜率约为其概念对应物的40%，这意味着需求曲线比标准估计所暗示的要陡峭得多。即使冲击是无穷小的和暂时的，这种偏差仍然存在。我们讨论了潜在的实证补救措施。
