The Innovation Race: Experimental Evidence on Advanced Technologies

创新竞赛：关于先进技术的实验证据

Zoë B. Cullen, Ester Faia, Elisa Guglielminetti, Ricardo Perez-Truglia, Concetta Rondinelli

金融与资产定价 意大利

Abstract

We present a large-scale field experiment testing for strategic complementarities in firms’ technology adoption. Our experiment was embedded in a Bank of Italy survey covering around 3,000 firms. We elicited firms’ beliefs about competitors’ adoption of two advanced technologies: artificial intelligence (AI) and robotics. We randomly provided half of the sample with a signal about adoption among comparable firms. Relative to this signal, most firms substantially underestimated competitors’ adoption, and those provided with the information updated their expectations about competitors’ future adoption. The information increased firms’ intended future adoption of robotics: a 1 pp increase in the share of competitors expected to adopt advanced technologies causes a 0.732 pp increase in the firm’s intention to adopt robotics by 2027. Our findings provide causal evidence on coordination in innovation and illustrate how information frictions shape technology diffusion.

中文摘要

我们展示了一项大规模实地实验，测试企业技术采用中的战略互补性。我们的实验嵌入在意大利银行的一项调查中，该调查覆盖了约3,000家企业。我们引出了企业对竞争对手采用两种先进技术——人工智能（AI）和机器人技术——的信念。我们随机向一半样本提供了关于可比企业采用情况的信号。相对于该信号，大多数企业严重低估了竞争对手的采用率，而那些获得信息的企业更新了他们对竞争对手未来采用的预期。该信息增加了企业未来采用机器人技术的意向：竞争对手预期采用先进技术的比例每增加1个百分点，企业到2027年采用机器人技术的意向就会增加0.732个百分点。我们的发现提供了关于创新协调的因果证据，并说明了信息摩擦如何塑造技术扩散。
