Tracking Inequality: Teachers and the Allocation of Educational Opportunities

追踪不平等：教师与教育机会的分配

Michela Carlana, Francesca Miserocchi, Eleonora Patacchini

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

Professionals routinely make consequential decisions for others without systematically observing how those decisions turn out. Whether such decisions reflect accurate forecasting or systematically distorted beliefs has important implications for equality of opportunity. We study this question in the context of teachers' recommendations for high school tracks by combining nationwide administrative data, randomized vignette experiments, belief elicitation, and a field experiment. Holding students' academic performance and interests constant, teachers are significantly less likely to recommend demanding academic tracks when students come from disadvantaged socioeconomic backgrounds. This differential treatment is accompanied by systematically inaccurate beliefs: teachers substantially overestimate the probability that high-achieving disadvantaged students will fail in academically demanding schools. We then provide teachers with personalized feedback on their previous recommendations and objective information on the realized success of disadvantaged students. The intervention substantially reduces socioeconomic gaps in recommendations for high-achieving students. Effects are concentrated among boys assigned to teachers with the largest prior recommendation gaps and the most inaccurate beliefs. Among the high-achieving disadvantaged boys most affected by the intervention, these changes translate into higher enrollment in demanding academic tracks, without detectable adverse effects on short-run academic performance.

中文摘要

专业人士经常为他人做出影响深远的决定，却没有系统地观察这些决定的结果。这些决定是反映了准确的预测，还是反映了系统性扭曲的信念，对机会平等具有重要意义。我们结合全国范围的行政数据、随机小插曲实验、信念诱导和实地实验，在教师对高中分流的推荐背景下研究这个问题。在控制学生的学业成绩和兴趣的情况下，当学生来自弱势的社会经济背景时，教师推荐要求严格的学术轨道的可能性显著降低。这种差异对待伴随着系统性不准确的信念：教师显著高估了高成就的弱势学生在学业要求严格的学校中失败的概率。然后，我们向教师提供关于他们先前推荐的个性化反馈，以及关于弱势学生实际成功的客观信息。该干预显著缩小了高成就学生推荐中的社会经济差距。这些效应集中在那些先前推荐差距最大且信念最不准确的教师所分配的男生中。在受干预影响最大的高成就弱势男生中，这些变化转化为更高比例的严格学术轨道入学率，而对短期学业成绩没有可检测的不利影响。
