Pollution Monitoring and the Pricing of Environmental Risk in Firms

污染监测与企业环境风险定价

Namrata Kala, Jalal Sani, Nemit Shroff, Pengdong Zhang

环境与气候 中国相关

Abstract

Pricing firms’ environmental risk is central to ensuring that capital is efficiently allocated. We examine whether an increase in the salience of pollution information affects the beliefs and perceived valuations of financially sophisticated agents—financial analysts—regarding the environmental risks faced by the firms they cover. Exploiting the staggered rollout of China's ambient air quality monitoring program, we show that when monitoring is introduced in a city, analysts based there become substantially more likely to discuss air pollution. These changes in attention are reflected in stock prices: reports issued by treated analysts generate more negative market reactions for brown firms and more positive reactions for green firms, with little change for environmentally neutral firms. These price responses do not subsequently reverse. Treated analysts increase their information acquisition through corporate site visits and issue less favorable assessments of brown firms and more favorable assessments of green firms. Our findings show that ambient environmental monitoring can affect asset prices by making environmental risks more salient and inducing greater information production about their implications for firm value.

中文摘要

为企业环境风险定价对于确保资本有效配置至关重要。我们考察污染信息显著性的提高是否影响财务资深代理人——金融分析师——对其所覆盖企业面临的环境风险的信念和感知估值。利用中国环境空气质量监测计划的交错推广，我们表明当某城市引入监测时，位于该城市的分析师变得显著更可能讨论空气污染。这些注意力变化反映在股价中：受处理分析师发布的报告对棕色企业产生更负面的市场反应，对绿色企业产生更正面的反应，而对环境中性企业变化甚微。这些价格反应随后并未逆转。受处理分析师通过企业实地调研增加信息获取，并对棕色企业发布更不利的评估、对绿色企业发布更有利的评估。我们的发现表明，环境监测可以通过使环境风险更加显著并诱导更多关于其对企业价值影响的信息生产来影响资产价格。
