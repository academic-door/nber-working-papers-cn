Crime in Covid Times

新冠时期的犯罪

Jens Ludwig

其他主题 美国

Abstract

What caused the historically unique volatility in American homicides since 2019, driven by gun homicides? While there is no shortage of candidate explanations, a coherent understanding has been elusive because of the widely held view that gun violence, like other crimes, stems from a rational weighing of benefits and costs as in Becker (1968). This model does a generally poor job of explaining recent trends in homicide. Behavioral economics helps explain what the Becker model cannot. This view starts with the fact that most shootings don’t further some larger goal like robbery or gang wars over drug turf (“instrumental violence”); they’re arguments settled with guns (“expressive violence”). Why do arguments start or escalate? The behavioral model points to automatic cognition that is fast, effortless but sometimes prone to error. The pandemic increased automaticity by increasing distress – a cognitive “bandwidth tax.” Recent homicide trends, driven by changes in expressive violence, are mirrored by similar trends for deaths from drug overdose, car crashes, and suicide (especially for Black Americans, the group most affected by violence) – “deaths of decision-making.” Also relevant is a large rise and fall in gun carrying, which might partially stem from Becker-like mechanisms: Large changes in handgun sales and police stops, both of which may also have been affected by the killing of George Floyd. Since American gun violence is mostly arguments with guns, no wonder homicide trends are due mostly to changes in the number of arguments and the odds a given argument had a gun present.

中文摘要

是什么导致了自2019年以来美国凶杀案历史上独特的波动，且主要由枪支凶杀案驱动？虽然候选解释并不缺乏，但由于人们普遍认为枪支暴力与其他犯罪一样，源于如Becker（1968）所述的理性权衡收益与成本，因此一直难以形成连贯的理解。该模型在解释近期凶杀趋势方面总体表现不佳。行为经济学有助于解释Becker模型无法解释的内容。这一观点始于一个事实：大多数枪击事件并不服务于某种更大的目标，如抢劫或为毒品地盘而进行的帮派战争（“工具性暴力”）；它们是用枪支解决的争执（“表达性暴力”）。为什么争执会开始或升级？行为模型指向快速、毫不费力但有时容易出错的自动认知。疫情通过增加痛苦——一种认知“带宽税”——提高了自动性。近期由表达性暴力变化驱动的凶杀趋势，与药物过量、车祸和自杀（尤其是受暴力影响最大的群体美国黑人）死亡人数的类似趋势相呼应——“决策死亡”。同样相关的是枪支携带的大幅上升和下降，这可能部分源于类似Becker的机制：手枪销售和警察拦截的巨大变化，这两者也可能受到乔治·弗洛伊德被杀事件的影响。由于美国枪支暴力主要是用枪支进行的争执，难怪凶杀趋势主要归因于争执数量的变化以及某一争执发生时现场有枪的几率。
