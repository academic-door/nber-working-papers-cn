Are Some Angels Better than Others?

有些天使投资人比其他天使投资人更好吗？

Johan Karlsen, Katja Kisseleva, Aksel Mjøs, David T. Robinson

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

We explore how the returns to angel investing relate to the financial, human, and social capital of the individual investors. Better-performing angels earn their higher returns through greater access to right-tail outcomes, not by avoiding losses. Wealthier and better financially connected angels invest in larger firms, but the returns to nonfinancial capital are substantial. Angels with relevant business experience earn higher returns than others in the same firm, especially when they take board seats. Social connections to founders and outside investors are also important. These findings have important implications for household finance and entrepreneurship policy.

中文摘要

我们探讨了天使投资的回报如何与个人投资者的财务资本、人力资本和社会资本相关联。表现更好的天使投资人通过获得更多右尾结果的机会获得更高回报，而非通过避免损失。更富有且财务联系更广的天使投资人投资于规模更大的公司，但非财务资本的回报也相当可观。拥有相关商业经验的天使投资人在同一家公司中比其他投资人获得更高回报，尤其是当他们担任董事会席位时。与创始人和外部投资者的社会联系也很重要。这些发现对家庭金融和创业政策具有重要意义。
