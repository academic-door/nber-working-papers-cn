Affirmative Action in Centralized College Admissions Systems

集中式大学招生系统中的平权行动

Nano Barahona, Cauê Dobbin, Sebastián Otero

教育与劳动 巴西

Abstract

We study the consequences of affirmative action in college admissions. We develop an empirical framework that distinguishes the effects on marginally benefited and displaced students from the overall effects of the policy. The overall effects include equilibrium reallocations as benefited students leave seats that others fill and displaced students take seats elsewhere, displacing others in turn. We apply this framework to a large-scale policy in Brazil that required all federal institutions to reserve half their seats for racially and socioeconomically disadvantaged applicants. Exploiting admissions cutoffs, we find that marginally benefited targeted students attend and graduate from higher-quality programs. Their graduation gains are about twice the losses of marginally displaced non-targeted students, reflecting targeted students’ weaker outside options. To evaluate the overall effects, we estimate a model of degree choices and potential outcomes. Among all reallocated students, benefited students still gain nearly twice what displaced students lose, but equilibrium reallocations make the policy displace about 30% more students than it benefits. Non-targeted students’ losses therefore offset about two-thirds of targeted students’ graduation gains, leaving a modest net gain across all students.

中文摘要

我们研究大学招生中平权行动的后果。我们开发了一个实证框架，将边际受益学生和边际被挤出学生的影响与政策的总体影响区分开来。总体影响包括均衡再配置，因为受益学生离开的席位由其他人填补，而被挤出学生到其他地方占据席位，进而挤出其他人。我们将这一框架应用于巴西的一项大规模政策，该政策要求所有联邦机构为种族和社会经济弱势申请者保留一半席位。利用录取分数线，我们发现边际受益的目标学生进入并毕业于更高质量的项目。他们的毕业收益约为边际被挤出的非目标学生损失的两倍，这反映了目标学生较弱的外部选择。为了评估总体影响，我们估计了一个学位选择和潜在结果的模型。在所有被再配置的学生中，受益学生的收益仍接近被挤出学生损失的两倍，但均衡再配置使该政策挤出的学生数量比受益学生数量多约30%。因此，非目标学生的损失抵消了目标学生毕业收益的约三分之二，使所有学生总体上仅获得适度净收益。
