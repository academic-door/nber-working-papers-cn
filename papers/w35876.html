Survival versus Scale: The Effects of Critical Access Hospital Designation

生存与规模：关键接入医院认定的影响

Ian M. McCarthy, Katie Leinenbach

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

The Critical Access Hospital (CAH) program, established under the Balanced Budget Act of 1997, provides cost-based Medicare reimbursement to small rural hospitals in exchange for limits on inpatient capacity and service scope. We estimate the effects of CAH designation on hospital finances, capacity, and organizational outcomes using a synthetic difference-in-differences design that exploits the staggered 1999– 2002 rollout of state-level CAH availability. We find that CAH designation raises operating margins by three to four percentage points, with no change in liquidity or capital investment, as cost-based reimbursement sustains revenue while expenses fall with scale. Designation also reduces inpatient scale, with significant declines in total beds, obstetric capacity, and nursing staff. Emergency department visits also decline meaningfully, while outpatient volume is largely unchanged. At the same time, CAH designation reduces hospital closures, with no meaningful effect on mergers. These results point to a “survival versus scale” tradeoff across hospitals within a state, wherein designation-based subsidies avert closure for a small number of hospitals while inducing widespread inpatient downsizing among the many more hospitals that convert. Because converting hospitals shed more inpatient capacity than averted closures preserve, the program may reduce aggregate rural inpatient capacity even as it keeps hospitals open.

中文摘要

关键接入医院（CAH）项目根据1997年《平衡预算法案》设立，向小型农村医院提供基于成本的Medicare报销，以换取对住院能力和服务范围的限制。我们使用合成双重差分设计估计CAH认定对医院财务、能力和组织结果的影响，该设计利用了1999—2002年州级CAH可用性的交错推出。我们发现，CAH认定使营业利润率提高3到4个百分点，而流动性和资本投资没有变化，因为基于成本的报销在收入得以维持的同时，费用随规模下降而下降。认定还降低了住院规模，总床位、产科能力和护理人员显著下降。急诊科就诊量也明显下降，而门诊量基本不变。与此同时，CAH认定减少了医院关闭，对合并没有显著影响。这些结果指向一个州内医院之间的“生存与规模”权衡，即基于认定的补贴使少数医院免于关闭，同时导致更多转为CAH的医院普遍缩减住院规模。由于转为CAH的医院削减的住院能力多于被避免关闭所保留的能力，该项目可能在保持医院开放的同时，降低农村住院总能力。
