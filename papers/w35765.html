The Evolving Marriage Gap in Children’s Outcomes: Family Structure and Young Adult Outcomes Revisited

儿童结果中不断演变的婚姻差距：重新审视家庭结构与青年成人结果

Melissa Schettini Kearney, Phillip B. Levine, Alden Q. Barson

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

Much of the existing research on the relationship between family structure and children’s outcomes has focused on average effects. Kearney and Levine (2017) instead proposed a heterogeneous framework in which the gap in outcomes between children of married and unmarried parents varies according to the resource context, namely a mother’s own resources, the net resources that a partner would contribute, and the outcome-specific returns to those resources. Using data from the 2013 Panel Study of Income Dynamics (PSID), they found evidence consistent with the model’s predictions. We revisit this question using the 2023 PSID to examine how these relationships have changed over the past decade. We find that the association between childhood family structure and young adult poverty has greatly diminished, whereas the association with college completion persists and is concentrated among children of mothers with higher levels of education. These findings suggest that although the economic circumstances of single mothers and their children have generally improved in recent decades, disparities in household resources and higher-order educational outcomes by family structure remain.

中文摘要

现有关于家庭结构与儿童结果之间关系的研究大多集中于平均效应。Kearney和Levine（2017）则提出了一个异质性框架，其中已婚和未婚父母子女之间的结果差距随资源背景而变化，即母亲自身资源、伴侣将贡献的净资源，以及这些资源的特定结果回报。他们使用2013年收入动态面板研究（PSID）的数据，发现了与该模型预测一致的证据。我们使用2023年PSID重新审视这一问题，以考察这些关系在过去十年中如何变化。我们发现，童年家庭结构与青年成人贫困之间的关联已大大减弱，而与大学毕业的关联仍然存在，并集中在受教育程度较高的母亲的子女中。这些发现表明，尽管近几十年来单身母亲及其子女的经济状况总体上有所改善，但按家庭结构划分的家庭资源和更高阶教育结果方面的差距仍然存在。
