NBER Working Paper w35803所在周报

Injury, Workload, and the Strategic Response of Coaches in the NBA

NBA中的伤病、工作量与教练的战略反应

Kala Krishna, Sergey Lychagin, Lewis S. McLean

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

Worker effort increases contemporaneous output but may result in injury or burnout. Directing effort away from the workers most exposed to injury would reduce this risk, but information on the exposure is often hidden. We ask whether managers act on such private information, studying the NBA as a data-rich workplace. Instrumenting workload with teammate absence, we find that work causally raises injury risk, which contrasts with the spurious negative correlation commonly found in sports medicine studies. We also show that coaches rest players in response to injury risk shocks. By acting on this private information, coaches can raise player productivity.

中文摘要

工人努力会增加当期产出，但可能导致伤病或倦怠。将努力从最容易受伤的工人身上转移开本可降低这一风险，但关于暴露程度的信息往往是隐藏的。我们研究管理者是否会依据此类私人信息采取行动，并将NBA作为一个数据丰富的工作场所。以队友缺阵作为工作量的工具变量，我们发现工作会因果性地提高受伤风险，这与运动医学研究中常见的虚假负相关形成对比。我们还表明，教练会针对伤病风险冲击让球员休息。通过依据这种私人信息采取行动，教练可以提高球员生产率。
