NBER Working Paper w35973所在周报

Risk and Return in Government Bonds

政府债券的风险与回报

Matteo Leombroni, Carolin Pflueger, Adi Sunderam

金融与资产定价 美国

Abstract

Does the compensation investors require for holding government bonds vary with their risk? We measure expected excess returns using market yields and professional forecasts of future yields, showing that they are tightly linked to a standard measure of risk: bonds’ betas with respect to the stock market. In a month-by-maturity panel of U. S. Treasuries from 1988 to 2024, the correlation between expected excess returns and bond–stock betas is 67%. The correlation is driven by variation in market yields rather than forecasters’ expectations. The relationship between realized excess returns and betas is weak in a univariate regression, but it becomes economically large and significant when we control for monetary-policy and macroeconomic forecast errors. Similar results obtain in an international panel of developed countries from 1989 to 2024. Our findings suggest that variation in risk premia driven by changing bond risk accounts for more than half of both the decline in U.S. long-term bond yields since the mid-1980s and their increase since 2020.

中文摘要

投资者持有政府债券所要求的补偿是否随其风险而变化？我们使用市场收益率和对未来收益率的专业预测来度量预期超额收益，表明它们与一个标准风险度量紧密相关：债券相对于股票市场的贝塔。在1988年至2024年美国国债的月度-期限面板中，预期超额收益与债券-股票贝塔之间的相关性为67%。这一相关性由市场收益率的变化而非预测者的预期驱动。在单变量回归中，已实现超额收益与贝塔之间的关系较弱，但当我们控制货币政策与宏观经济预测误差后，它在经济上变得很大且显著。在1989年至2024年发达国家的国际面板中也得到类似结果。我们的发现表明，由债券风险变化驱动的风险溢价变动，解释了自1980年代中期以来美国长期债券收益率下降以及自2020年以来其上升的超过一半。
