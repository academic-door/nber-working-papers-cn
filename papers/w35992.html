Policy Deterrence: Strategic Investment in U.S. Broadband

政策威慑：美国宽带领域的战略投资

Karam Kang, Mo Xiao

其他主题 美国

Abstract

Can firms influence policy through investment? Using 2010--2019 U.S. broadband deployment and state policy data, we find that large internet service providers expand capacity more in electorally competitive counties. Greater large-provider capacity in swing counties is associated with fewer subsequent entry- and investment-facilitating policies, especially rights-of-way accommodations that reduce deployment barriers faced by smaller providers. To interpret these patterns, we develop a model in which a large firm's capacity can reduce the policymaker's gain from intervention. In the model, policy deterrence raises the large firm's market share and can reduce total deployment.

中文摘要

企业能否通过投资影响政策？利用2010--2019年美国宽带部署和州级政策数据，我们发现大型互联网服务提供商在选举竞争激烈的县扩张容量的幅度更大。摇摆县中大型提供商容量越大，随后出台的促进进入和投资的政策越少，尤其是减少小型提供商部署障碍的路权便利政策。为解释这些模式，我们构建了一个模型，其中大型企业的容量会降低政策制定者从干预中获得的收益。在该模型中，政策威慑提高了大型企业的市场份额，并可能减少总部署量。
