Rational Inattention to Discrete Choices with Stable Priors

具有稳定先验的离散选择的理性疏忽

Bruno Pellegrino

理论与方法 未指明区域

Abstract

How does prior information affect discrete choice? In the seminal rational inattention model of Matějka and McKay (2015), multinomial logit arises from the discrete choice of agents who are uncertain about choice payoffs and who have access to a flexible information acquisition technology (RI-logit). A key limitation of this powerful framework is the lack of known solutions that allow the decision maker's prior information to vary across choices: obtaining such solutions has remained an open problem in this literature. In this paper, I solve the RI-logit model analytically for two related families of priors known respectively as Positive Stable and Tempered Stable distributions. In my solution, the decision maker's prior information enters the choice probabilities through a choice-specific parameter, which can be read as shifting either the ex-ante expected utility of a risk-neutral decision maker, or the ex-ante riskiness perceived by a risk-averse one. These results complete the RI-logit framework by separating prior information from information that is endogenously acquired, and expand its empirical applications by making it possible to study how choice probabilities react to changes in prior information.

中文摘要

先验信息如何影响离散选择？在Matějka和McKay (2015)的开创性理性疏忽模型中，多项式Logit模型源于对选择收益不确定且能够使用灵活信息获取技术的代理人的离散选择（RI-logit）。这个强大框架的一个关键局限性是缺乏已知的解决方案，无法让决策者的先验信息在不同选择之间变化：获得此类解决方案在该文献中一直是一个未解决的问题。在本文中，我针对两类相关的先验族（分别称为正稳定分布和 tempered 稳定分布）解析地求解了RI-logit模型。在我的解决方案中，决策者的先验信息通过一个特定于选择的参数进入选择概率，该参数可以被解读为改变风险中性决策者的事前预期效用，或风险厌恶者感知的事前风险。这些结果通过将先验信息与内生获取的信息分开，完善了RI-logit框架，并通过使研究选择概率如何响应先验信息的变化成为可能，扩展了其实证应用。
