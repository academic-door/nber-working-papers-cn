NBER Working Paper w35912所在周报

Upgrading Financial Markets

升级金融市场

Francis Annan, Seth Garz

金融与资产定价 塞拉利昂

Abstract

Expanding access to financial services may not be enough to develop markets in low-income settings: providers also need to be held accountable to consumers. We test this in a field experiment across 195 rural Sierra Leonean communities, upgrading Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs) into mobile money and bank agents, cross-randomized with a monthly consumer feedback mechanism. Upgrading expands access, but feedback drives large operational improvements (+20.5 pp, 85%), particularly in privacy and consumer protection, raising usage and revenues. Upgraded VSLAs also show higher savings digitization and higher incomes, evidence that disciplining agent actions benefits not only consumers but also the principal that finances and stands behind its agent. Interpreted through a framework of delegated intermediation, we show accountability, not infrastructure alone, aligns provider incentives with consumer welfare and is central to designing scalable financial intermediaries.

中文摘要

扩大金融服务可及性可能不足以在低收入环境中发展市场：服务提供者还需要对消费者负责。我们在塞拉利昂195个农村社区开展了一项实地实验，将乡村储蓄和贷款协会（VSLA）升级为移动货币和银行代理点，并与每月消费者反馈机制交叉随机化。升级扩大了可及性，但反馈带来了巨大的运营改进（+20.5个百分点，85%），特别是在隐私和消费者保护方面，提高了使用率和收入。升级后的VSLA还表现出更高的储蓄数字化程度和更高的收入，这证明约束代理行为不仅有利于消费者，也有利于为其代理提供融资并为其背书的委托人。通过委托 intermediation 框架进行解释，我们表明问责制——而非仅基础设施——使提供者激励与消费者福利相一致，并且是设计可扩展金融中介的核心。
