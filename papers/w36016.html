An Odd Approximation of Inflation for State Dependent Pricing Models

状态依存定价模型的一种通胀奇次近似

Luca Gagliardone, Mark Gertler, Simone Lenzu, Joris Tielens, Al-Mahdi Ebsim

宏观与货币财政 美国

Abstract

We derive an aggregate state-space representation of state-dependent pricing models with random menu costs and strategic complementarities. The representation, derived from first principles, replaces the cross-sectional distribution of price gaps with a finite set of its moments (the first three), while preserving the key economic forces of state-dependent pricing--movements in the frequency of price adjustment and selection. Along the impulse response, inflation is an odd (cubic) polynomial in the average price gap. Embedded in a general equilibrium setting, the model can be solved by perturbation, filtered, and estimated with standard DSGE tools, and it matches well the global solution across shock sizes. Applied to the 2021--22 inflation surge in the United States and the euro area, it shows that much of the variation that linear models would attribute to cost-push shocks is in fact endogenous amplification through the frequency of price changes and selection.

中文摘要

我们推导了具有随机菜单成本和战略互补性的状态依存定价模型的加总状态空间表示。该表示从第一性原理推导而来，用价格缺口的有限个矩（前三个）替代其横截面分布，同时保留状态依存定价的关键经济力量——价格调整频率的变动和选择效应。沿脉冲响应，通胀是平均价格缺口的奇次（三次）多项式。嵌入一般均衡环境后，该模型可以通过扰动法求解、滤波，并用标准DSGE工具估计，且在不同冲击规模下与全局解吻合良好。应用于2021–22年美国及欧元区的通胀飙升，结果表明，线性模型会归因于成本推动冲击的很大一部分变异，实际上是通过价格变化频率和选择效应产生的内生放大。
