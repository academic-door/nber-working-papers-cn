The Positive Spillovers to Risky Investments in Vacant, Abandoned, and Disinvested Properties

对空置、废弃和被撤资房产进行风险投资的正向溢出效应

Edward W. Chen, Reagan L. Lengefeld, Omar Isaac Asensio

环境与气候 格鲁吉亚

Abstract

This study examines a housing program that created affordable infill developments on formerly vacant, abandoned, or disinvested properties in Savannah, Georgia. Savannah is a major economic hub anchored by the Port of Savannah, where many urban neighborhoods remain impacted by disinvestment and climate risk. Using two decades of record-linked property and tax data, this study quantifies local economic spillovers from this public intervention, revealing previously hidden social and economic benefits of infill housing policies. These benefits include an 11% increase in the market value of properties immediately neighboring an infill development site and an increase of up to 35% in areas with a high density of infill activity, pointing to block-level revitalization in climate-disadvantaged and distressed areas.

中文摘要

本研究考察了佐治亚州萨凡纳市一项住房项目，该项目在先前空置、废弃或被撤资的房产上创建了可负担的填充式开发项目。萨凡纳是以萨凡纳港为依托的主要经济中心，许多城市社区仍受到撤资和气候风险的影响。本研究利用二十年记录关联的房产和税收数据，量化了这一公共干预带来的本地经济溢出效应，揭示了填充式住房政策此前隐藏的社会和经济收益。这些收益包括：紧邻填充式开发项目场地的房产市场价值增加了11%，而在填充式活动高度密集的地区，增幅最高可达35%，表明气候劣势和困境地区出现了街区层面的复兴。
