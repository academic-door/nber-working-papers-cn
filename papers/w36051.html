NBER Working Paper w36051所在周报

Climate Risk, Real Estate, and Property Insurance

气候风险、房地产和财产保险

Benjamin L. Collier, Benjamin J. Keys, Philip Mulder

环境与气候 未指明区域

Abstract

Growing climate risk threatens long-lived, fixed physical assets like real estate. To navigate this risk, homeowners and prospective buyers rely on market signals, including property values and insurance costs. The effectiveness of these market signals depends on whether standard economic assumptions hold. In this review, we assess whether housing and insurance markets accurately reflect climate risk, and whether market participants receive and respond to those signals. A rapidly growing literature documents substantial frictions in each part of the chain from climate risk to prices, with downstream consequences for adaptation and recovery. We conclude that these frictions call into question whether the price signals provided by real estate and insurance markets are likely to steer households into outcomes that are resilient to growing climate risk.

中文摘要

日益增长的气候风险威胁着房地产等长期固定实物资产。为应对这一风险，房主和潜在买家依赖市场信号，包括房产价值和保险成本。这些市场信号的有效性取决于标准经济学假设是否成立。在本综述中，我们评估住房和保险市场是否准确反映气候风险，以及市场参与者是否接收并响应这些信号。快速增长的文献记录了从气候风险到价格这一链条每个环节中的重大摩擦，并对适应和恢复产生下游后果。我们得出结论，这些摩擦使人们质疑房地产和保险市场提供的价格信号是否可能引导家庭走向能够抵御日益增长的气候风险的结果。
