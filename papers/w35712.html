The Limits of Verifiability: Credibility and Flexibility in Communication

可验证性的边界：沟通中的可信度与灵活性

Alessandro Lizzeri, Yichuan Lou, Jacopo Perego

国际贸易 未指明区域

Abstract

We compare verifiable and unverifiable communication in a sender–receiver setting with partially aligned preferences, where the sender knows more than what her evidence can prove. We identify a credibility–flexibility tradeoff: tying the sender’s claims to her evidence enhances her credibility, but limits how flexibly she can communicate when her evidence does not accurately reflect her private information. This tradeoff changes the economics of verifiable disclosure. When preferences are sufficiently aligned, full evidence disclosure is neither sustainable in equilibrium nor efficient. We show that verifiability facilitates communication when preferences are sufficiently misaligned, but hinders it when they are sufficiently aligned, suggesting that institutions that impose verification need not al- ways improve information transmission. Finally, we study how verifiable and unverifiable communication can complement one another when both are available: unverifiable communication can contextualize verifiable evidence when the latter is misleading.

中文摘要

我们在发送者—接收者框架下比较了可验证与不可验证的沟通，其中偏好部分一致，且发送者所知信息超出其证据所能证明的范围。我们识别出一种可信度—灵活性权衡：将发送者的陈述与其证据绑定会增强其可信度，但当证据未能准确反映其私人信息时，这会限制其沟通的灵活性。这一权衡改变了可验证披露的经济学逻辑。当偏好足够一致时，完全证据披露在均衡中既不可持续，也非有效率。我们表明，当偏好足够不一致时，可验证性促进沟通；但当偏好足够一致时，可验证性反而阻碍沟通，这意味着施加验证要求的制度并不总能改善信息传递。最后，我们研究了当可验证与不可验证沟通同时可用时，二者如何互补：当可验证证据具有误导性时，不可验证沟通可为其提供情境背景。
