Mutual Party Extremism

相互的政党极端主义

Ivo Welch

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

It is well known that candidates may have to stake out more partisan positions to win their primaries. These then also determine their success in the general election. The equilibrium consequences of primaries are less well understood. There is a robust externality — the primary trap — that can sustain multiple equilibria (along a band). Candidate extremism (moderation) on one side facilitates candidate extremism (moderation) on the other. Electorate polarization and turnout are less important than other candidates’ choices. The paper also discusses how other considerations can alter the set of equilibria.

中文摘要

众所周知，候选人可能不得不采取更具党派性的立场以赢得初选。这些立场随后也决定了他们在普选中的成功。初选的均衡后果则不那么为人所知。存在一种强大的外部性——初选陷阱——可以维持多重均衡（沿一个区间）。一方的候选人极端主义（温和主义）会促进另一方的候选人极端主义（温和主义）。选民两极分化和投票率不如其他候选人的选择重要。本文还讨论了其他考虑因素如何改变均衡集。
