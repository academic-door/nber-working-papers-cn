Does Income Affect Health? Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial of a Guaranteed Income

收入影响健康吗？来自一项保障收入随机对照试验的证据

Sarah Miller, Elizabeth Rhodes, Alexander W. Bartik, David E. Broockman, Patrick K. Krause, Eva Vivalt

健康与医疗 美国

Abstract

We study a randomized trial in which 1,000 low-income U.S. adults age 21 to 40 received $1,000 per month for three years, from November 2020 until October 2023, while 2,000 control participants received $50 monthly over that same period. The transfer produced large but short-lived improvements in stress and food security and increased hospital, emergency department, and dental care use. However, it did not improve self-reported physical health, clinical biomarkers, or mental health after the first year, and we can rule out small effects on many outcomes. We also find no effects on the health of children in participants’ households.

中文摘要

我们研究了一项随机试验，其中1,000名年龄在21至40岁之间的低收入美国成年人从2020年11月到2023年10月连续三年每月获得1,000美元，而2,000名对照组参与者在同一时期每月获得50美元。这笔转移支付在压力和食品安全方面产生了显著但短暂的改善，并增加了医院、急诊科和牙科护理的使用。然而，它并未改善第一年后的自我报告身体健康、临床生物标志物或心理健康，并且我们可以排除对许多结果的微小影响。我们还发现对参与者家庭中儿童的健康没有影响。
