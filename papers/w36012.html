Last-Mile Delivery and the Future of Aid

最后一公里交付与援助的未来

Erika Deserranno, Andrea Guariso, Nancy Qian

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

Foreign aid seeks to provide basic services, such as health care and education, to poor and vulnerable populations. Its effectiveness depends on last-mile delivery: whether those services reach the people who need them. This article reviews evidence on two key aspects of last-mile delivery. The first concerns the growing role of non-state providers, especially NGOs, and its implication for state’s capacity to deliver services and coordinate across providers. The second concerns the selection, motivation, and management of frontline workers who provide the services directly to beneficiaries. We argue that decisions about aid allocation and withdrawal should account for the but can quickly erode. need to preserve last-mile delivery capacity, which takes time and resources to build.

中文摘要

对外援助旨在为贫困和脆弱人群提供医疗和教育等基本服务。其有效性取决于最后一公里交付：这些服务能否到达需要它们的人手中。本文回顾了关于最后一公里交付两个关键方面的证据。第一个方面涉及非国家提供者（尤其是非政府组织）日益增长的作用，及其对国家提供服务和协调各提供者能力的影响。第二个方面涉及直接向受益人提供服务的一线工作人员的选择、激励和管理。我们认为，关于援助分配和退出的决策应考虑到维护最后一公里交付能力的需要，而这种能力的建设需要时间和资源，但也可能迅速侵蚀。
