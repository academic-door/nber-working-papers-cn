Do Online Job Postings Capture Job Vacancies?

在线职位发布能否捕捉职位空缺？

Michael Dalton, Lisa B. Kahn, Andreas I. Mueller

教育与劳动 美国

Abstract

We ask whether online job postings capture U.S. job vacancies, matching near-universal Burning Glass (BG) postings to the representative Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey (JOLTS). BG converged toward JOLTS over 2007–2022, and its representativeness varies across establishment characteristics. We reweight BG to align postings with JOLTS openings along observables, and assess impacts on prior BG findings. Because large establishments account for most openings and are well represented, the level and cyclicality of skill demand survive our adjustment. But BG overstates labor market concentration (the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index) nearly threefold, because small and mid-sized establishments post less online.

中文摘要

我们探讨在线职位发布是否能捕捉美国职位空缺，将近乎普遍的Burning Glass（BG）职位发布与代表性的职位空缺和劳动力流动调查（JOLTS）进行匹配。在2007年至2022年间，BG向JOLTS收敛，其代表性因机构特征而异。我们重新加权BG，使职位发布在可观测特征上与JOLTS空缺对齐，并评估对先前BG研究结果的影响。由于大型机构占空缺的大部分且代表性良好，技能需求的水平和周期性在我们的调整后仍然成立。但BG将劳动力市场集中度（赫芬达尔-赫希曼指数）夸大了近三倍，因为中小型机构在线发布的职位较少。
