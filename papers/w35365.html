Shaping Major Choice: The Role of High School Counselors

塑造专业选择：高中辅导员的作用

Kennedy Johnston, Jonathan Meer, Danila Serra

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

Existing studies show that high school counselors can significantly influence students' graduation rates and college enrollment; less is known about their ability to direct students toward particular fields of study. We evaluate an information intervention aimed at increasing counselors' awareness of economics, a major often associated with misconceptions about its content and career opportunities, and characterized by substantial under-representation of women and racial and ethnic minorities. Counselors from randomly selected Texas high schools were invited to participate in a one-day information workshop on the economics major. We evaluate the impact of the intervention on students' major preferences and outcomes using application and admissions data from a large public university attended by many graduates from the treatment schools, as well as enrollment and course-taking records from the Texas Education Research Center. The intervention led to substantial increases in interest in economics at the college application stage, particularly among high-achieving women, but did not lead to significant changes in college major outcomes. We conclude that high school counselors can play an important role in shaping students' field-of-study preferences, but translating preferences into enrollment requires additional exposure and reinforcement.

中文摘要

现有研究表明，高中辅导员能显著影响学生的毕业率和大学入学率；但关于他们引导学生选择特定研究领域的能力，我们知之甚少。我们评估了一项信息干预措施，旨在提高辅导员对经济学专业的认识——该专业常因对其内容和职业机会的误解而备受困扰，并且女性及少数族裔群体的代表性严重不足。来自随机选择的德克萨斯州高中的辅导员被邀请参加为期一天的经济学专业信息研讨会。我们利用一所大型公立大学的申请和录取数据（该大学接收了许多来自处理组学校的毕业生），以及德克萨斯教育研究中心的注册和课程记录，评估了该干预措施对学生专业偏好和结果的影响。该干预措施显著增加了大学申请阶段对经济学的兴趣，尤其是在成绩优异女性中，但并未导致大学专业结果的显著变化。我们得出结论，高中辅导员在塑造学生研究领域偏好方面可以发挥重要作用，但将偏好转化为实际注册需要额外的接触和强化。
