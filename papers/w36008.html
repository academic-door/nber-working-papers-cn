NBER Working Paper w36008所在周报

Monetary policy in a Phelps–Winter Economy

Phelps–Winter经济中的货币政策

Bruce C. Greenwald, Joseph E. Stiglitz

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

We use the Phelps-Winter customer market—providing a better theory of price determination for large parts of the economy than the standard model—to show that raising interest rates induces higher prices, as firms value current profits more, and are more willing to lose customers for short-term gains. These inflationary effects can be significant in relevant ranges of interest rates; they exist in the short run even when interest rate hikes succeed in cutting aggregate demand; and that they are amplified by policy uncertainty and the uncertainty about the effects of interest rate changes, both of which have increased as conventional policy has demonstrably failed badly to keep inflation at the 2% target. Empirical studies confirm this low level of ineffectiveness of raising interest rates in reducing inflation. The weakening effectiveness of monetary policy was predicted by our earlier work on banks and the supply of credit, as a result of the elimination of interest prohibition on deposits; and further suggested by work on the structural transformation of the economy, as customer markets become more important.

中文摘要

我们使用Phelps-Winter客户市场——它为经济中很大一部分提供了比标准模型更好的价格决定理论——表明提高利率会引发更高价格，因为企业更看重当前利润，并更愿意为短期收益而失去客户。这些通胀效应在相关利率区间内可能很显著；即使加息成功削减总需求，它们在短期内也存在；而且它们会被政策不确定性以及利率变化影响的不确定性放大，而随着常规政策在将通胀保持在2%目标方面明显严重失败，这两种不确定性都已上升。实证研究证实了加息在降低通胀方面处于这种低水平的无效性。货币政策有效性的减弱曾被我们早期关于银行和信贷供给的研究所预测，其原因是存款利息禁令的取消；关于经济结构转型的研究进一步表明，随着客户市场变得更加重要，这一减弱也会出现。
