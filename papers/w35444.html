Risk Design: AI and Prediction Beyond Screening in Insurance Markets

风险设计：保险市场中超越筛选的AI与预测

Alex Chan

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

I study insurance when contracts can change residual risk. Under complete contracting, prevention is supplied by its least-cost source. Prediction and prevention can lower claims but the best prevention package may attract the people most likely to need it. Competition then creates a risk-design trilemma: plans may have to weaken prevention, abandon separation, or subsidize high-risk enrollment. Adverse selection penalizes prevention only when high-risk consumers value it more, not when it lowers insurers’ claims. Because broader coverage lets insurers capture more of those savings, it can increase certified prevention. If patients or firms cannot replace missing services privately, screening changes more than enrollment and premiums: it leaves society with more avoidable losses overall.

中文摘要

我研究了当合同可以改变残余风险时的保险问题。在完全合同下，预防由其最低成本来源提供。预测和预防可以降低索赔，但最佳的预防组合可能会吸引最可能需要它的人群。竞争随后产生了一个风险设计三难困境：计划可能不得不削弱预防、放弃分离，或补贴高风险参保。逆向选择仅在当高风险消费者更看重预防时惩罚预防，而非当预防降低保险公司索赔时。因为更广泛的覆盖使保险公司能够获取更多此类节省，它可以增加经认证的预防。如果患者或企业无法私下替换缺失的服务，筛选改变的不仅仅是参保和保费：它使社会总体上留下更多可避免的损失。
