fereg: Fast High-Dimensional Fixed-Effects Regressions in Stata

fereg：Stata中的快速高维固定效应回归

Johannes F. Schmieder

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

Regressions with many fixed effects are central to applied research, but fitting them to large datasets can be slow. In this article, I present the commands fereg and feppml, which fit linear and Poisson pseudo-maximum-likelihood regressions with high-dimensional fixed effects. Both commands use familiar Stata syntax and pass the computation to a compiled plugin. The plugin chooses among projection, Krylov, and multigrid solvers according to the structure of the fixed effects, checks every result before posting it, and runs on several processor cores. The commands support multiple and interacted fixed effects, group-specific slopes, regression weights, robust and multiway clustered standard errors, saved fixed effects, and prediction. Two simulated examples illustrate their use. In simulated designs with up to ten million observations and default stopping settings, fereg was 6–204 times faster than reghdfe and feppml 11–103 times faster than ppmlhdfe. The commands were also faster than fixest (R) and PyFixest (Python) in all 53 simulated configurations and than the Julia packages FixedEffectModels.jl and GLFixedEffectModels.jl in 52 of 53, with geometric-mean time ratios between 3.0 and 7.7. On administrative data with 1.2 million workers and 117,000 firms, fereg and feppml were at least 3.4 times faster than every other command at every number of cores.

中文摘要

包含许多固定效应的回归是应用研究的核心，但在大型数据集上拟合它们可能很慢。本文介绍命令fereg和feppml，它们拟合具有高维固定效应的线性回归和泊松伪最大似然回归。两个命令都使用熟悉的Stata语法，并将计算传递给编译插件。该插件根据固定效应的结构在投影、Krylov和多网格求解器之间选择，在发布每个结果前进行检查，并在多个处理器核心上运行。这些命令支持多重和交互固定效应、组别特定斜率、回归权重、稳健和多向聚类标准误、保存固定效应以及预测。两个模拟示例说明了它们的用法。在最多1000万观测值且使用默认停止设置的模拟设计中，fereg比reghdfe快6–204倍，feppml比ppmlhdfe快11–103倍。这些命令在所有53个模拟配置中也快于fixest（R）和PyFixest（Python），并在53个配置中的52个快于Julia包FixedEffectModels.jl和GLFixedEffectModels.jl，几何平均时间比在3.0到7.7之间。在包含120万工人和117,000家企业的行政数据上，fereg和feppml在每个核心数下都至少比其他所有命令快3.4倍。
