Do Markets Believe in Transformative AI?

市场是否相信变革性人工智能？

Isaiah Andrews, Maryam Farboodi

AI 与数字经济 美国

Abstract

We examine US bond yields around 30 major AI model release dates from four frontier labs between November 2022 and December 2025. Long-term Treasury and TIPS yields fall around these releases and remain lower for weeks, with the declines concentrated around releases that a crowdsourced forecast interprets as good news about the rate of AI progress. These changes are statistically significant according to a variety of small-sample valid statistical tests, and suggest that investors may take seriously the possibility of transformative impacts from AI.

中文摘要

我们考察了2022年11月至2025年12月期间四个前沿实验室发布30个主要AI模型日期前后的美国债券收益率。长期国债和通胀保值国债（TIPS）收益率在这些发布事件前后下降，并在数周内保持较低水平，其中降幅集中在那些被众包预测解读为AI进展速度利好消息的发布事件上。根据多种小样本有效统计检验，这些变化具有统计显著性，表明投资者可能认真对待AI产生变革性影响的可能性。
