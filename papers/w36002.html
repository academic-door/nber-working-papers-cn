Knowledge Without Taxation? Experimental Evidence from a Tax Education Program

无税收的知识？来自税收教育项目的实验证据

Anders Jensen, Nikhil Kumar, Konstantin Poensgen

公共财政与税收 加纳

Abstract

A property tax experiment in Ghana randomly provides tax education to property owners and frontline officials. The intervention raises tax knowledge, which spreads through social networks to untreated neighbors, but does not increase tax payments. Educated taxpayers and officials become more engaged in their interactions without developing more collaborative relationships. Taxpayers revise downward their beliefs about enforcement capacity and become more aware of legal ways to reduce their tax burden. Knowledge is a double-edged sword: it can stimulate citizens to engage with the tax system while exposing the state's limited capacity to enforce it.

中文摘要

加纳的一项房产税实验随机向房产所有者和一线官员提供税收教育。该干预提高了税收知识，知识通过社交网络传播至未受处理的邻居，但并未增加税款缴纳。受过教育的纳税人和官员在互动中更加投入，但并未形成更具合作性的关系。纳税人下调了对执法能力的信念，并更加了解减轻税负的合法途径。知识是一把双刃剑：它可以激励公民参与税收体系，同时暴露国家有限的执法能力。
