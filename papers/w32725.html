Production Function Estimation Using Subjective Expectations Data

利用主观预期数据估计生产函数

Agnes Norris Keiller, Áureo de Paula, John Van Reenen

其他主题 英国

Abstract

Standard proxy methods for estimating production functions in the Olley and Pakes (1996) tradition require assumptions on input choices. We introduce a new method that exploits (increasingly available) data on firms’ expectations of their future output and inputs that allows us to obtain consistent production function parameter estimates while relaxing these input demand assumptions. In contrast to both proxy and dynamic panel methods like Blundell and Bond (2000), our proposed estimator can be implemented on a single cross-section of data and Monte Carlo simulations show it outperforms alternative estimators when firms’ material input choices are subject to optimization error. Implementing a range of production function estimators on UK panel data, we find our proposed estimator yields results that are either similar to or more credible than commonly-used alternatives. These differences are larger in industries where material inputs appear harder to optimize. We show that the share of cross-firm TFP dispersion accounted for by persistent productivity differences is substantially larger when calculated using parameter estimates from our proposed estimator.

中文摘要

在Olley和Pakes（1996）传统中估计生产函数的标准代理变量方法需要对投入选择做出假设。我们引入了一种新方法，利用（日益可得的）企业对其未来产出和投入的预期数据，使我们能够在放宽这些投入需求假设的同时获得一致的生产函数参数估计。与代理变量方法和动态面板方法（如Blundell和Bond（2000））相比，我们提出的估计器可以在单一横截面数据上实施，并且蒙特卡洛模拟表明，当企业的物质投入选择存在优化误差时，我们的估计器优于其他估计器。在使用英国面板数据实施一系列生产函数估计器后，我们发现我们提出的估计器产生的结果与常用替代方法相似或更为可信。在物质投入似乎更难优化的行业中，这些差异更大。我们表明，当使用我们提出的估计器得到的参数估计时，由持久生产率差异解释的企业间全要素生产率（TFP）离散份额要大得多。
