Firm Pay, Amenities, and Inequality

企业薪酬、福利与不平等

Sydnee Caldwell, Ingrid Haegele, Jörg Heining

教育与劳动 德国

Abstract

We estimate firm-specific amenity valuations using discrete choice experiments in a survey of German workers linked to administrative records. Workers rank hypothetical offers from real firms they would consider joining; randomized wages identify ex-ante, money-metric valuations. Valuations vary across firms and demographic groups, yet a single index predicts firm size and employee satisfaction. High-wage firms provide no worse amenities: valuations rise with wage premia across firms and are approximately orthogonal when weighted by employment. Accounting for amenities widens between-firm inequality by roughly 20 percent in variance terms. Gender differences in valuations explain part of women’s sorting into lower-wage firms.

中文摘要

我们利用与行政记录关联的德国工人调查中的离散选择实验，估计了企业特定的福利估值。工人对他们会考虑加入的真实企业所提供的假设性工作机会进行排序；随机化的工资水平识别出事前、货币度量的估值。估值在不同企业和人口群体间存在差异，但单一指数能预测企业规模和员工满意度。高工资企业提供的福利并不更差：估值随企业间的工资溢价上升，且按就业加权后近似正交。考虑福利因素后，企业间不平等（以方差衡量）扩大了约20%。性别间的估值差异部分解释了女性流向低工资企业的现象。
