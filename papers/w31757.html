NBER Working Paper w31757所在周报所在月度合集

Managing to Learn

管理学习

Sabrin A. Beg, Anne E. Fitzpatrick, Adrienne M. Lucas

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

To improve public services at scale, public sector managers must encourage reticent civil servants to enact effective reforms. We show, through a randomized controlled trial, that equipping existing school managers with specific tools that reinforce effective teacher training improves school management and student learning and decreases teacher turnover. Additional general management training differentially improves management but not student test scores. Management and test score improvements persisted. We resolve conflicting results regarding the role of management in public sector productivity and demonstrate how public sector managers are a pathway to scale by enhancing reform effectiveness through personal effort.

中文摘要

为了大规模改善公共服务，公共部门管理者必须鼓励沉默寡言的公务员实施有效的改革。我们通过一项随机对照试验表明，为现有的学校管理者配备强化有效教师培训的具体工具，可以改善学校管理、提高学生学习成绩并降低教师流动率。额外的通用管理培训能显著改善管理，但不能提高学生考试成绩。管理和考试成绩的改善得以持续。我们解决了关于管理在公共部门生产率中作用的相互矛盾的结果，并证明了公共部门管理者如何通过个人努力增强改革效果，从而成为扩大规模的途径。
