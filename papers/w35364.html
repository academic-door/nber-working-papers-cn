Identity Uncertainty

身份不确定性

Anujit Chakraborty, Arkadev Ghosh, Matt Lowe, Gareth Nellis

教育与劳动 印度

Abstract

Many group identities that influence economic behavior are imperfectly observed. Individuals and institutions often conceal identity markers to limit discrimination. Yet concealment also creates uncertainty about group membership, hampering coordination in social interaction. To study this tradeoff, we paired high- and low-caste men for collaborative data entry work in North India. We randomly assigned each mixed-caste pair to either be: (i) introduced by full names, making caste common knowledge; (ii) introduced by first names only, making caste disclosure a choice; or (iii) instructed not to disclose caste. The two concealment conditions substantially reduce the accuracy of beliefs about a partner’s caste and confidence in those beliefs. They also weaken workplace relations, lowering trust, willingness to interact, and perceived productivity—consistent with identity helping structure social coordination. Evidence on mechanisms shows that identity concealment inhibits authentic interaction, making workers less able to express their “true selves,” while certainty about a partner’s identity is associated with stronger workplace ties. Concealment leaves a sizable caste disparity in higher-status role assignment intact, implying minimal impacts on discrimination. We conclude that where group identities are socially entrenched, reducing their legibility may undermine intergroup relations.

中文摘要

许多影响经济行为的群体身份并不能被完美观察到。个人和机构常常隐藏身份标记以限制歧视。然而，隐藏也造成了关于群体成员身份的不确定性，从而妨碍了社会互动中的协调。为了研究这种权衡，我们在印度北部将高种姓和低种姓男性配对进行协作数据录入工作。我们随机将每对混合种姓配对分配到以下三种情况之一：(i) 以全名介绍，使种姓成为公共知识；(ii) 仅以名字介绍，使种姓披露成为一种选择；或 (iii) 被指示不得披露种姓。两种隐藏条件显著降低了关于伙伴种姓信念的准确性以及对这些信念的信心。它们还削弱了工作场所关系，降低了信任、互动意愿和感知生产力——这与身份有助于构建社会协调的观点一致。关于机制的证据表明，身份隐藏抑制了真实互动，使工作者更难以表达其“真实自我”，而对伙伴身份的确定性则与更强的工作场所联系相关。隐藏使得在较高地位角色分配中存在的显著种姓差距保持不变，这意味着对歧视的影响微乎其微。我们得出结论，在群体身份已根深蒂固的社会中，降低其可辨识度可能会破坏群体间关系。
