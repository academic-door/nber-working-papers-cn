Limits to Arbitrage and Prediction Market Efficiency: the Case of PredictIt

套利限制与预测市场效率：PredictIt案例

Eric Zitzewitz

其他主题 美国

Abstract

PredictIt is a CFTC-regulated US prediction market focused on political outcomes. It was very successful in attracting participants in the 2016-22 period, running liquid markets on topics predecessors had tried unsuccesfully. During this period, investors were limited to $850 positions in each contract, which limited arbitrage trading. PredictIt exhibited much larger pricing biases than previously studied prediction markets. Bid prices often persistently added to more than 110, implying an immediate arbitrage profit even after fees. Prices exhibited an overpricing of longshots that was larger than in other prediction markets. The experience of PredictIt suggests that limiting stakes may undermine the ability of prediction markets to accurately price event probabilities.

中文摘要

PredictIt是一个受CFTC监管的美国预测市场，专注于政治结果。它在2016-22年期间非常成功地吸引了参与者，在前人未能成功的话题上运行了流动性市场。在此期间，投资者在每个合约中的头寸被限制在850美元，这限制了套利交易。PredictIt表现出的定价偏差远大于此前研究的预测市场。买价经常持续加总超过110，意味着即使扣除费用后也存在即时套利利润。价格表现出对冷门结果的过度定价，且程度大于其他预测市场。PredictIt的经验表明，限制投注规模可能削弱预测市场准确定价事件概率的能力。
