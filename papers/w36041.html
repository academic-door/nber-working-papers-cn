Does Treating Addiction Rebuild Lives? Evidence from Buprenorphine

治疗成瘾能重建生活吗？来自丁丙诺啡的证据

Sarah Eichmeyer, Aljoscha Janssen, Jonathan Zhang

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

Does treating addiction improve economic circumstances as well as health? Using two decades of Veterans Health Administration records on over 140,000 patients with opioid use disorder and consumer credit data, we exploit staggered facility-level buprenorphine adoption and quasi-random emergency physician assignment. A five-percentage-point increase in the share of clinicians with buprenorphine prescribing experience doubles overdose survivors’ long-term medication use and reduces two-year mortality by an estimated 3.7 percentage points. Instrumenting receipt yields a 22 percent credit score gain. At $300,000 per statistical life-year, estimated survival benefits alone exceed added medical costs within three years

中文摘要

治疗成瘾能改善经济状况以及健康吗？使用退伍军人健康管理局二十年来对超过140,000名阿片类药物使用障碍患者的记录和消费者信用数据，我们利用设施层面丁丙诺啡采用的交错推行和准随机的急诊医生分配。具有丁丙诺啡处方经验的临床医生比例增加五个百分点，使过量幸存者的长期药物使用翻倍，并使两年死亡率估计降低3.7个百分点。对接受治疗进行工具变量估计，得到22%的信用评分提升。按每统计生命年300,000美元计算，仅估计的生存收益就在三年内超过增加的医疗成本
