Navigating Medicaid: Experimental Evidence on Administrative Burden and Coverage Loss

应对医疗补助计划：关于行政负担与保障覆盖损失的实验证据

Rebecca Mary Myerson, Allison Espeseth, Laura Dague

公共财政与税收 美国

Abstract

In a large-scale field experiment, we tested methods to inform Medicaid enrollees with an imminent renewal deadline about available assistance. Supplementing written outreach – which was sent to all beneficiaries – with a call reminding them about available assistance increased Medicaid renewal by 1 percentage point (1.5%), and reduced procedural denials by 1.1 percentage points, including significant effects for tribal members, children, lower-income people, and people with chronic disease. These findings suggest administrative frictions reduce coverage for eligible people, including those with chronic disease. The data also provide practical lessons for outreach.

中文摘要

在一项大规模实地实验中，我们测试了告知即将面临续期截止日期的医疗补助计划参保人可获得援助的方法。在向所有受益人发送书面通知的基础上，额外增加电话提醒其可获得援助，使医疗补助续保率提高了1个百分点（1.5%），并使程序性拒绝率降低了1.1个百分点，其中包括对部落成员、儿童、低收入人群和慢性病患者产生的显著影响。这些发现表明，行政摩擦减少了符合条件人群（包括慢性病患者）的保障覆盖。这些数据也为外展工作提供了实际经验。
