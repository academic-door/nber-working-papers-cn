Green Subsidies with Demand Distortions

存在需求扭曲的绿色补贴

Susanna B. Berkouwer, Joshua T. Dean

环境与气候 肯尼亚

Abstract

Credit constraints and other demand distortions are pervasive in lower-income contexts. A model of clean technology adoption shows that demand distortions can increase the welfare gains from green subsidies. This arises through two channels: shifting the marginal adopter toward higher private and social benefits, and increasing demand elasticity. We test this by cross-randomizing purchase price subsidies, usage subsidies, and access to an interest-free loan for a US$84 induction stove among 2,134 charcoal users in Kenya. Usage subsidies of 75% have a precise zero effect on adoption and usage. Purchase price subsidies, however, generate large welfare gains. Credit constraints raise the subsidy’s efficacy by 35%–73%, up to US$10–US$12 in observable welfare gain per subsidy dollar. This demand distortion also lowers the subsidy cost of abatement by 41%, to US$12 per tCO2e. This operates through the two channels identified in the theory: credit constraints raise the marginal adopter’s positive externality by 19% and fuel savings by 30%, and lower the subsidy cost per marginal adoption by 29%. In a counterfactual without demand distortions, the abatement cost rises to US$137 per tCO2e. Contexts with large demand distortions could offer many cost-effective opportunities for climate change mitigation financing.

中文摘要

信贷约束和其他需求扭曲在低收入环境中普遍存在。一个清洁技术采用模型表明，需求扭曲可以增加绿色补贴的福利收益。这通过两个渠道产生：将边际采用者转向更高的私人和社会收益，以及增加需求弹性。我们通过在肯尼亚2,134名木炭使用者中交叉随机化购买价格补贴、使用补贴和获得无息贷款（用于一台84美元的电磁炉）来检验这一点。75%的使用补贴对采用和使用具有精确的零效应。然而，购买价格补贴产生了巨大的福利收益。信贷约束将补贴的有效性提高了35%–73%，每1美元补贴带来高达10–12美元的可观察福利收益。这种需求扭曲还将减排的补贴成本降低了41%，降至每吨二氧化碳当量12美元。这通过理论中确定的两个渠道发挥作用：信贷约束将边际采用者的正外部性提高了19%，燃料节省提高了30%，并将每边际采用的补贴成本降低了29%。在没有需求扭曲的反事实中，减排成本上升至每吨二氧化碳当量137美元。具有巨大需求扭曲的环境可能为气候变化减缓融资提供许多成本有效的机会。
