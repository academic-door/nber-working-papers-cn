The Neoclassical Theory of Firm Investment and Taxes: A Reassessment

企业投资与税收的新古典理论：重新评估

Gabriel Chodorow-Reich

公共财政与税收 未指明区域

Abstract

Drawing on decades of research in public and macroeconomics, this article offers a neoclassical synthesis of the effect of corporate taxation on firm investment and aggregate outcomes. The microeconometric literature has (often unwittingly) studied two distinct comparative statics of the firm’s first order condition for capital. The first holds output fixed and identifies the capital-labor substitution elasticity (Eisner and Nadiri, 1968). The other holds fixed variables external to the firm (Coen, 1969). A consensus range for the Coen short-run relationship implies plausible values for parameters of the firm’s revenue function, validating the neoclassical theory. The long-run, general equilibrium elasticity of capital instead involves a third comparative static, holding labor fixed. This elasticity depends on the parameters identified from the firm-level regressions, connecting the microeconometric evidence to macroeconomic questions. Using this framework, I quantitatively assess the effect of corporate taxation on aggregate capital, output, wages, and tax revenue, and compare to existing approaches including policy models.

中文摘要

借鉴公共经济学和宏观经济学数十年的研究，本文对企业税对企业投资和总体结果的影响提供了一个新古典综合。微观计量文献（常常无意中）研究了企业资本一阶条件的两个不同的比较静态。第一个在产出固定的情况下，识别资本-劳动替代弹性（Eisner和Nadiri, 1968）。另一个则固定企业外部的变量（Coen, 1969）。Coen短期关系的共识范围意味着企业收入函数参数的合理值，从而验证了新古典理论。长期一般均衡的资本弹性则涉及第三个比较静态，即劳动固定。该弹性取决于从企业层面回归中识别出的参数，将微观计量证据与宏观经济问题联系起来。利用这一框架，我定量评估了企业税对总资本、产出、工资和税收收入的影响，并与包括政策模型在内的现有方法进行比较。
