Aggregate-Surplus Maximizing Monetary Policy

总剩余最大化的货币政策

David Baqaee, Ariel Burstein, Joao Guerreiro

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

We study optimal monetary policy in a general heterogeneous-agent New Keynesian (HANK) economy. Instead of maximizing a social welfare function, the policymaker maximizes the joint surplus of all households, without taking a stance on how that surplus should be distributed. This is the policy that maximizes resources left over if winners from reform were to compensate losers. We derive the approximate optimal policy using linear-quadratic methods. The quadratic objective function nests the standard New Keynesian loss function and adds losses from incomplete markets and heterogeneous labor wedges. We show that optimal policy is the sum of time-dependent and state-contingent components. When labor wedges are homogeneous, monetary policy cannot improve household insurance: the time-dependent component is zero, and optimal policy coincides with the representative-agent New Keynesian (RANK) benchmark. Departures from RANK arise from heterogeneous labor wedges. In that case, the time-dependent component temporarily alleviates incomplete-market distortions but vanishes over time. The state-contingent component solves a dual-mandate problem, as in RANK, but with a higher weight on output-gap stabilization relative to inflation stabilization. In a calibrated model, heterogeneous labor wedges raise this weight by about 37 percent. We also find that a dual mandate with this higher weight achieves almost all of the aggregate-surplus gains from the full optimal policy and requires no compensatory transfers between winners and losers.

中文摘要

我们研究一般异质性代理人新凯恩斯（HANK）经济中的最优货币政策。政策制定者不是最大化社会福利函数，而是最大化所有家庭的联合剩余，不对该剩余应如何分配采取立场。这是使改革赢家补偿输家后剩余资源最大化的政策。我们使用线性-二次方法推导近似最优政策。二次目标函数嵌套了标准新凯恩斯损失函数，并增加了来自不完全市场和异质性劳动楔子的损失。我们表明最优政策是时间依赖成分和状态依存成分之和。当劳动楔子同质时，货币政策无法改善家庭保险：时间依赖成分为零，最优政策与代表性代理人新凯恩斯（RANK）基准一致。偏离RANK源于异质性劳动楔子。在这种情况下，时间依赖成分暂时缓解不完全市场扭曲，但随时间消失。状态依存成分解决双重使命问题，如RANK中一样，但相对于通胀稳定赋予产出缺口稳定更高的权重。在一个校准模型中，异质性劳动楔子将该权重提高约37%。我们还发现，具有该更高权重的双重使命实现了完整最优政策几乎所有的总剩余收益，且不需要赢家和输家之间的补偿性转移。
