NBER Working Paper w36046所在周报

Valuing the Cost of Incomplete Global Markets

评估全球市场不完全的成本

Karen K. Lewis, Edith X. Liu

金融与资产定价 全球/多国

Abstract

Deviations from complete financial markets in cross-border investments generate economic welfare costs. This paper shows how to value these costs using standard theory and data based on three main results. Our first result shows how welfare valuations can be derived from a general wealth-to-dividend ratio. Next, we establish how aggregate consumption and exchange rate data can be used to uncover diversifiable goods allocations across countries. Finally, we demonstrate how asset pricing data and models can be used to value deviations from complete markets. The paper outlines a range of applications that fall under this framework, including financial intermediary constraint models.

中文摘要

跨境投资中偏离完全金融市场的现象会产生经济福利成本。本文基于三个主要结果，展示了如何利用标准理论和数据来评估这些成本。我们的第一个结果展示了如何从一般的财富-股息比率推导出福利估值。接下来，我们确立了如何利用总消费和汇率数据来揭示各国之间可分散的商品配置。最后，我们展示了如何利用资产定价数据和模型来评估对完全市场的偏离。本文概述了属于该框架的一系列应用，包括金融中介约束模型。
