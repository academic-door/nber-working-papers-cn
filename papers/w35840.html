NBER Working Paper w35840所在周报

Asymmetric Reversals

非对称反转

Federico Baldi-Lanfranchi, Pierre Collin-Dufresne, Kent D. Daniel

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

Short-term return reversal is one of the most robust asset-pricing anomalies, and is commonly linked to liquidity provision. We decompose individual firm stock returns into two distinct components: SYS, the component of returns that can be linked to systematic risk and public information releases; and an orthogonal residual RES. The RES component reverses, while the SYS component exhibits continuation. Moreover, the residual reversals are highly asymmetric: positive residual shocks reverse much more slowly than negative shocks. A return factor based on asymmetric idiosyncratic reversal subsumes the idiosyncratic volatility factor and a broad set of other short-horizon anomalies. Our findings suggest that asymmetric idiosyncratic reversal (AIR) is the primary driver of short-term return predictability.

中文摘要

短期收益反转是最稳健的资产定价异象之一，通常与流动性提供相关。我们将单个公司股票收益分解为两个不同组成部分：SYS，即可与系统性风险和公共信息发布相联系的收益部分；以及正交残差RES。RES部分发生反转，而SYS部分表现出延续性。此外，残差反转高度非对称：正向残差冲击的反转速度远慢于负向冲击。基于非对称特质反转的收益因子涵盖了特质波动率因子以及广泛的其他短期限异象。我们的发现表明，非对称特质反转（AIR）是短期收益可预测性的主要驱动因素。
