How Curvy is the Phillips Curve?

菲利普斯曲线有多弯曲？

Philip Bunn, Lena Anayi, Emily Barnes, Nicholas Bloom, Aaron Jalca, Paul Mizen, Gregory Thwaites, Ivan Yotzov

宏观与货币财政 英国、美国

Abstract

Macro evidence suggests a convex relationship between inflation and slack, but causal identification is challenging. Using large firm-panel surveys in the UK and US, we show that price responses to demand shocks are convex at the firm level, across hypothetical survey shocks, realised sales shocks, and COVID-induced demand shocks. Convexity is strongest when firms and industries face higher inflation, fades beyond two years, and applies to cost shocks. We rationalise these findings with a menu-cost model in which trend inflation moves firms closer to price-increase thresholds. We estimate that this nonlinearity explains about one quarter of the 2022 inflation overshoot.

中文摘要

宏观证据表明通胀与产出缺口之间存在凸性关系，但因果识别具有挑战性。利用英国和美国的大型企业面板调查，我们表明价格对需求冲击的反应在企业层面是凸性的，这体现在假设性调查冲击、实际销售冲击以及新冠疫情引发的需求冲击中。当企业和行业面临更高通胀时，凸性最强，超过两年后减弱，并且也适用于成本冲击。我们用一个菜单成本模型来解释这些发现，在该模型中，趋势通胀使企业更接近提价阈值。我们估计这种非线性关系解释了2022年通胀超调的大约四分之一。
