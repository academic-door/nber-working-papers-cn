Optimal Line-Drawing for Legal Rules

法律规则的最优划线

Jacob Goldin, David Weisbach

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

Line drawing is a pervasive feature of legal systems. Across many domains, the applicable legal treatment turns on whether an activity falls above or below some threshold. We characterize the economic forces that determine the optimal placement of such thresholds, both generally and in specific legal settings involving monetary and non-monetary sanctions, prohibitions, and mandates. We apply our results to two important line-drawing questions in private law: the optimal due-care threshold for negligence liability and the optimal threshold for whether a shortfall in contract performance constitutes breach. In both applications, the optimal line takes the form of a population-level marginal cost-benefit rule, with the relevant costs and benefits weighted over the actors whose behavior the line induces to change.

中文摘要

划线是法律体系的一个普遍特征。在许多领域，适用的法律处理取决于某项活动是高于还是低于某一阈值。我们刻画了决定此类阈值最优位置的经济力量，既包括一般情形，也包括涉及金钱与非金钱制裁、禁止和命令的具体法律情境。我们将研究结果应用于私法中的两个重要划线问题：过失责任的最优注意义务阈值，以及合同履行不足是否构成违约的最优阈值。在这两个应用中，最优界线均采取总体层面边际成本-收益规则的形式，相关成本与收益根据该界线所引致行为改变的主体进行加权。
