Beyond Technocracy: Toward a Holistic Approach to Central Bank Trust

超越技术官僚：迈向中央银行信任的整体性方法

Sandra Eickmeier, Luba Petersen

宏观与货币财政 德国

Abstract

We examine public trust in the European Central Bank (ECB) and its determinants using data from the Bundesbank Household Panel survey for Germany. Drawing on insights from political science and psychology, we move beyond technocracy and develop a more holistic account of central bank trust. We show that trust in the ECB is associated with both perceived competence—defined as effectiveness in maintaining price stability and reliance on rules, scientific analysis and data—and perceived values—particularly the integrity of senior policymakers, honest communication and broader societal concern. Respondents who report that competence matters more report significantly higher trust in the ECB. In contrast, those who place greater importance on values tend to express lower trust. Trust in the ECB is also strongly associated with trust in political institutions more generally and, to a lesser extent, interpersonal trust. Together, our findings offer a fresh perspective on trust in central banks, indicating that it is necessary to move beyond a purely technocratic approach to central bank policy. Importantly, such efforts are fully consistent with—and may even support central banks’ existing mandates and institutional independence.

中文摘要

我们利用德国联邦银行家庭面板调查（Bundesbank Household Panel survey）的数据，考察公众对欧洲中央银行（ECB）的信任及其决定因素。借鉴政治学和心理学的见解，我们超越技术官僚视角，构建了一个更全面的央行信任解释框架。我们发现，对欧洲央行的信任既与感知能力（定义为在维持物价稳定方面的有效性，以及对规则、科学分析和数据的依赖）相关，也与感知价值观（尤其是高层政策制定者的诚信、诚实的沟通和更广泛的社会关切）相关。报告称能力更重要的受访者对欧洲央行的信任度显著更高。相比之下，那些更重视价值观的受访者往往表达出较低的信任度。对欧洲央行的信任也与对政治机构的一般信任密切相关，并在较小程度上与人际信任相关。综合来看，我们的研究结果为央行信任提供了新视角，表明有必要超越纯粹技术官僚式的央行政策方法。重要的是，这些努力与央行现有的使命和机构独立性完全一致，甚至可能对其有所支持。
