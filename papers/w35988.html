NBER Working Paper w35988所在周报

The Hidden Sharpe Ratio

隐藏的夏普比率

Mikhail Chernov, Bryan T. Kelly, Semyon Malamud, Johannes Schwab

金融与资产定价 美国

Abstract

We estimate the maximal Sharpe ratio in large cross-sections of assets directly, without constructing the efficient portfolio. Covariance estimation in this setting requires regularization, which suppresses the contribution of small-eigenvalue directions. Our estimator progressively recovers this contribution, yielding tight upper and lower bounds with asymptotic confidence intervals. We further show that ridge-regularized Markowitz portfolios, the construction underlying many existing high-Sharpe benchmarks, deliver out-of-sample Sharpe ratios that fall short of the true maximum by an explicit, non-vanishing multiplicative factor. Applied to U.S. equities, the method delivers an estimated maximal annualized Sharpe ratio of approximately 7.4, or 5.0 once micro-cap stocks are excluded, substantially exceeding existing portfolio-based benchmarks of 1.0–4.0. Adding corporate bonds raises the maximum, though not by a statistically significant margin. Nonlinear portfolio weights dominate linear ones, and characteristics introduced after 2004 contribute little additional Sharpe ratio.

中文摘要

我们直接估计大型资产横截面中的最大夏普比率，而无需构建有效投资组合。在这种情形下，协方差估计需要正则化，这会抑制小特征值方向的贡献。我们的估计量逐步恢复这一贡献，产生带有渐近置信区间的紧致上下界。我们进一步表明，岭正则化Markowitz投资组合——许多现有高夏普基准所基于的构建方式——其样本外夏普比率低于真实最大值一个明确的、非消失的乘数因子。应用于美国股票，该方法给出的估计最大年化夏普比率约为7.4，排除微型股后为5.0，大幅超过现有基于投资组合的基准1.0–4.0。加入公司债券会提高最大值，但幅度在统计上不显著。非线性投资组合权重优于线性权重，而2004年之后引入的特征对夏普比率的额外贡献很小。
