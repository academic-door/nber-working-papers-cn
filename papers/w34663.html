The Impacts of Parole Supervision

假释监督的影响

Luke D. Brinkman, Andrew Jordan, Derek Neal

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

We study a reform to parole supervision that reduced supervision terms for some Illinois parolees from 12 to 6 months. Using difference-in-differences, we find that the reform reduced prison re-entry rates. Sharp drops in technical revocation rates drove these reductions. Rates of prison re-admissions linked to new crimes were unchanged. Cook County court records confirm these findings. The reform reduced parole costs without creating measurable harms to public safety. We develop a conceptual framework that shows how shorter terms of supervision could reduce rates of criminal activity, and we show how this framework informs on-going debates about parole supervision policies.

中文摘要

我们研究了一项假释监督改革，该改革将伊利诺伊州部分假释人员的监督期限从12个月缩短至6个月。使用双重差分法，我们发现该改革降低了重返监狱率。技术性撤销率的急剧下降推动了这些减少。与新犯罪相关的重新入狱率没有变化。库克县法院记录证实了这些发现。该改革降低了假释成本，且未对公共安全造成可衡量的损害。我们构建了一个概念框架，说明较短的监督期限如何可能降低犯罪活动率，并展示了该框架如何为当前关于假释监督政策的辩论提供信息。
