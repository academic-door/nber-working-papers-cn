Targeted Acceleration in Middle School Math: Impacts on College Entry, Degree Completion, and STEM

初中数学中的定向加速：对大学入学、学位完成和STEM的影响

David Card, Laura Giuliano

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

We study an accelerated 3-year math curriculum offered in the middle schools of a large urban district that enables students to finish Algebra I and Geometry before high school. Entry to the program is based on 5th-grade math test scores, with a cutoff at the 80th percentile. Continuing eligibility requires at least a B grade in the previous year; successful completion of all three years is highly correlated with an index of non-cognitive skills. Following students for 6 years after high school graduation, we find that the impacts of admission vary widely by non-cognitive skill and gender. Marginally eligible boys with high non-cognitive skills have a 33 percentage point (ppt.) increase in the probability of completing calculus by 11th grade and a 12 ppt. increase in the probability of completing a major in STEM or business within 6 years; boys with lower noncognitive skills take more advanced math classes in high school but have no significant effects on their college outcomes. Like their male peers, marginally eligible girls with high non-cognitive skills are more likely to complete calculus by 11th grade (+17 ppt.). Their rate of graduating with a STEM or business major is unaffected, but they switch from social sciences to the core STEM majors (science, engineering and math). Girls in the two lower terciles of non-cognitive skill are also strongly affected by entry to the program, with higher participation in advanced math classes in high school, higher rates of entry to selective 4-year colleges, and higher rates of completing a STEM or business major (+12 ppt).

中文摘要

我们研究了一个大型城市学区初中提供的加速3年数学课程，该课程使学生能够在高中之前完成代数I和几何。进入该项目基于5年级数学考试成绩，截止线在第80百分位。继续资格要求前一年至少获得B等级；成功完成全部三年与非认知技能指数高度相关。在高中毕业后的6年内跟踪学生，我们发现录取的影响因非认知技能和性别而差异很大。具有高非认知技能的边缘合格男孩在11年级前完成微积分的概率增加33个百分点（ppt.），在6年内完成STEM或商科专业的概率增加12个百分点；非认知技能较低的男孩在高中选修更多高级数学课程，但对其大学结果没有显著影响。与其男性同龄人一样，具有高非认知技能的边缘合格女孩在11年级前完成微积分的可能性更高（+17个百分点）。她们以STEM或商科专业毕业的比率未受影响，但她们从社会科学转向核心STEM专业（科学、工程和数学）。处于非认知技能最低两个三分位组的女孩也强烈受到进入该项目的影响，在高中参与高级数学课程的比例更高，进入选择性4年制大学的比例更高，完成STEM或商科专业的比例更高（+12个百分点）。
