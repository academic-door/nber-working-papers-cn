Governance Regimes, Economic Mobility, and the Composition of Public Investment: Evidence from the Democratization of Villages in India

治理体制、经济流动性与公共投资构成：来自印度村庄民主化的证据

Andrew D. Foster, Mark R. Rosenzweig

宏观与货币财政 印度

Abstract

We develop a quantifiable general-equilibrium model of village governance, motivated by India’s democratization, to study how political institutions shape public goods provision and economic development. The model features two groups, landowners and landless workers, with different preferences over public goods such as irrigation, schools, and employment programs. Households from both groups can enter nonfarm entrepreneurship, but face credit-dependent entry costs. We compare oligarchy, where landowners control public goods decisions, with democracy, where electoral competition determines policy based on the relative sizes of the two groups. The calibrated model predicts that democratization’s effects on public goods and growth are not uniform; they depend on population composition and the ease of occupational mobility through access to credit. Using a 37-year village-level panel from India (1971–2006) and exploiting variation in democratization driven by state and national reforms, we find strong support for these predictions. Financial intermediation shifts landless households’ preferences from wage-enhancing policies toward schooling, increasing nonfarm business entry. As a result, democracy provides fewer schools than oligarchy when credit is unavailable, but more schools when credit is accessible. We also show that transfers to the landless increase schooling under democracy but reduce it under oligarchy. These findings highlight how governance structures and economic conditions jointly determine the effectiveness of development policies.

中文摘要

我们以印度的民主化为动机，开发一个可量化的村庄治理一般均衡模型，以研究政治制度如何塑造公共品提供与经济发展。该模型包含两个群体——地主和无地工人——它们对灌溉、学校和就业项目等公共品有不同偏好。两个群体的家庭都可以进入非农创业，但面临依赖信贷的进入成本。我们比较寡头制（地主控制公共品决策）与民主制（选举竞争根据两个群体的相对规模决定政策）。校准后的模型预测，民主化对公共品和增长的影响并非一致；它们取决于人口构成以及通过信贷获得职业流动性的难易程度。使用印度1971–2006年37年的村庄级面板，并利用由邦和国家改革驱动的民主化变异，我们发现对这些预测的有力支持。金融中介将无地家庭的偏好从提高工资的政策转向学校教育，增加非农企业进入。结果，在信贷不可得时，民主制提供的学校少于寡头制，但在信贷可得时提供更多学校。我们还表明，对无地者的转移支付在民主制下增加学校教育，但在寡头制下减少学校教育。这些发现凸显了治理结构与经济条件如何共同决定发展政策的有效性。
