The Allocative Cost of War: A View to a Kill...ing of Productivity

战争的配置成本：生产率之殇

Marvin Amann, Yuriy Gorodnichenko, Oleksandr Talavera

金融与资产定价 俄罗斯、乌克兰

Abstract

Why is war so economically costly? Our answer is that modern war lowers output not only by destroying productive factors, but also by making the surviving economy work less efficiently. Using uniquely comprehensive firm-level data collected during Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, we document a dramatic collapse in allocative productivity in Ukraine. To sharpen identification and explore key mechanisms (including the role of war intensity, internal displacement, reallocation, and macroeconomic instability), we exploit spatial heterogeneity across Ukrainian districts and comparisons with Russian aggression in 2014 and the 2008 Global Financial Crisis. Our key policy implication is that restoring allocative efficiency–and preparing institutional arrangements that facilitate rapid reallocation in times of stress–is critical for sustaining economic capacity, defense, and national security.

中文摘要

为什么战争的经济代价如此高昂？我们的答案是，现代战争不仅通过摧毁生产要素来降低产出，还使幸存的经济体运行效率降低。利用2022年俄罗斯全面入侵乌克兰期间收集的独特且全面的企业层面数据，我们记录了乌克兰配置生产率的急剧崩溃。为了加强识别并探索关键机制（包括战争强度、国内流离失所、重新配置和宏观经济不稳定的作用），我们利用了乌克兰各地区间的空间异质性，并与2014年俄罗斯的侵略和2008年全球金融危机进行了比较。我们的关键政策含义是，恢复配置效率——并准备在压力时期促进快速重新配置的制度安排——对于维持经济能力、国防和国家安全至关重要。
