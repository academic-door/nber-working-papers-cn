What Can (and Cannot) Be Learned about Systemic Racial Discrimination from Randomized Controlled Trials?

从随机对照试验中可以（和不可以）了解关于系统性种族歧视的什么？

Dania V. Francis, Rob Gillezeau, Damon Jones, Angelino C. G. Viceisza

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs) have played a major role in detecting direct forms of racial discrimination. In this essay, we ask whether RCTs can (or cannot) play an equally important role in the case of systemic racial discrimination. We propose a conceptual model of systemic racial discrimination drawing from prior definitions of systemic racism. We then consider a range of possibilities and limitations for how RCTs can help understand systemic racial discrimination, including: (1) the feasibility of manipulating systemic racial discrimination on a large scale; (2) smaller scale approaches; (3) conducting RCTs across different structural settings; (4) the manipulation of the salience of systemic forces; (5) the role of information in reducing systemic discrimination; (6) the manipulation of the salience of racial identity; and, finally, (7) examination of strategic responses to systemic racial discrimination. In discussing limitations, we point to other approaches that may fill in the gaps left by RCTs in studying systemic racial discrimination.

中文摘要

随机对照试验（RCT）在检测直接形式的种族歧视方面发挥了重要作用。在本文中，我们询问RCT是否能够（或不能够）在系统性种族歧视的情况下发挥同样重要的作用。我们借鉴先前关于系统性种族主义的定义，提出一个系统性种族歧视的概念模型。然后，我们考虑RCT如何帮助理解系统性种族歧视的一系列可能性和局限性，包括：（1）大规模操纵系统性种族歧视的可行性；（2）较小规模的方法；（3）在不同结构环境中进行RCT；（4）操纵系统性力量的显著性；（5）信息在减少系统性歧视中的作用；（6）操纵种族身份的显著性；最后，（7）考察对系统性种族歧视的战略反应。在讨论局限性时，我们指出其他方法可能填补RCT在研究系统性种族歧视时留下的空白。
