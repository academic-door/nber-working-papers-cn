NBER Working Paper w36018所在周报

Decoding Success: Phonics-based Instruction and Student Achievement

解码成功：基于自然拼读的教学与学生成绩

Alex Hollingsworth, Alberto Ortega, Nikolaos Zirogiannis

教育与劳动 美国

Abstract

Reading is a foundational skill, yet fewer than one in three US fourth graders read at a proficient level. How best to teach reading and improve educational attainment has been an active policy debate for decades. Recently, phonics-based approaches have surged in popularity, even though the largest attempt to put similar methods into classrooms was previously abandoned out of concerns that the program was ineffective. This paper studies Reading First, a nearly $1 billion per year federal program established under No Child Left Behind that promoted phonics-based instruction in K-3 classrooms. Exploiting administrative data from Texas and program eligibility formulas, we estimate effects via difference-in-differences and instrumental variables specifications. We find that eligibility increased third-grade reading test scores by 0.1 standard deviations and that effects persisted through at least the eighth grade. An analysis of digitized exam questions shows that treated students outperformed peers on questions that required basic decoding skills and those with more words, a benefit that also drove improvements in math scores. We follow exposed cohorts into early adulthood, finding that the program increased on-time high school graduation, college enrollment, four-year college completion, and adult earnings at age 25.

中文摘要

阅读是一项基础技能，但美国四年级学生中阅读达到熟练水平的不足三分之一。如何最好地教授阅读并提高教育成就，数十年来一直是政策辩论的活跃议题。近年来，基于自然拼读的方法人气激增，尽管此前将类似方法引入课堂的最大规模尝试因担心该项目无效而被放弃。本文研究《阅读优先》（Reading First），这是《不让一个孩子掉队》法案下设立的每年近10亿美元的联邦项目，旨在在K-3课堂推广基于自然拼读的教学。利用德克萨斯州的行政数据和项目资格公式，我们通过双重差分和工具变量设定估计效应。我们发现，资格使三年级阅读测试成绩提高了0.1个标准差，且效应至少持续到八年级。对数字化试题的分析表明，受处理学生在需要基本解码技能的题目以及字数更多的题目上表现优于同伴，这一收益也推动了数学成绩的提升。我们追踪受暴露队列进入成年早期，发现该项目提高了按时高中毕业率、大学入学率、四年制大学毕业率以及25岁时的成年收入。
