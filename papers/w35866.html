NBER Working Paper w35866所在周报

Gang Crackdowns

打击帮派

Carlos Schmidt-Padilla, Nikita Melnikov, María Micaela Sviatschi

金融与资产定价 萨尔瓦多

Abstract

What are the consequences of restoring state authority in territories governed by criminal organizations? Combining household surveys and administrative records with detailed maps of former gang-controlled areas, we study how El Salvador’s 2022 State of Exception, which ended two decades of gang territorial control, reshaped Salvadoran society. We find that the removal of gang control integrated previously segmented labor markets, improving mobility, employment outcomes, household income, and financial inclusion. Areas with greater pre-2022 gang exposure also experienced larger increases in business formation and property investment. However, the legacy of criminal governance persists through residence-based discrimination: employers remain less willing to hire otherwise identical applicants from former gang-controlled neighborhoods. Finally, although overall support for the crackdowns remains high, individuals who witnessed police abuse report substantially greater distrust of the police and the national government, suggesting that abuses of state authority may undermine the political sustainability of tough-on-crime policies.

中文摘要

在犯罪组织控制的领土上恢复国家权威会带来什么后果？我们结合家庭调查和行政记录与前帮派控制区域的详细地图，研究了萨尔瓦多2022年的紧急状态（结束了长达二十年的帮派领土控制）如何重塑了萨尔瓦多社会。我们发现，帮派控制的消除整合了此前分割的劳动力市场，改善了流动性、就业结果、家庭收入和金融普惠。2022年前帮派暴露程度更高的地区，企业创办和房地产投资增长也更大。然而，犯罪治理的遗产通过基于居住地的歧视持续存在：雇主仍然不太愿意雇用来自前帮派控制社区的其他方面相同的求职者。最后，尽管对打击行动的总体支持率仍然很高，但目睹过警察滥权的个人报告对警察和国家政府的信任度显著更低，这表明国家权威的滥用可能削弱强硬打击犯罪政策的政治可持续性。
