Export Controls and the Economics of National Security

出口管制与国家安全经济学

Jingting Fan, Kyle Handley, Nuno Limão

国际贸易 美国

Abstract

Export controls restrict trade to limit adversaries’ access to goods that contribute to military capability, but their effectiveness depends on how trade and production adjust across countries. We combine new data on U.S. export control classifications and licensing outcomes with a quantitative general equilibrium model featuring dual-use production, input-output linkages, and separate civilian and military final expenditure. We estimate large export and entry frictions from these controls. These effects are substantially smaller for U.S. exports to members of multilateral export control regimes (MECRs): the differential is equivalent to an export cost of 15 to 29 log points. Relative to a counterfactual without differential controls on adversaries, multilateral controls reduce their imports of controlled products far more than unilateral U.S. controls. Only 7% of the reduction in controlled exports under the multilateral regime is replaced by other countries, compared with 28% under unilateral U.S. policy. Multilateral controls reduce adversaries’ real military expenditure by increasing their military price index. The security impact, measured as U.S. real military expenditure relative to adversaries, rises by about 1 log point under multilateral controls but only 0.14 log points under unilateral controls, with both imposing a similar civilian real cost. Even a U.S. embargo fails to reproduce the multilateral security outcome.

中文摘要

出口管制限制贸易以限制对手获取有助于军事能力的商品，但其有效性取决于贸易和生产如何在各国之间调整。我们将美国出口管制分类和许可结果的新数据与一个量化一般均衡模型相结合，该模型具有两用生产、投入-产出联系以及单独的民用和军用最终支出。我们从这些管制中估计出巨大的出口和进入摩擦。这些效应对美国向多边出口管制机制（MECR）成员的出口要小得多：差异相当于15至29个对数点的出口成本。相对于不对对手实施差别管制的反事实，多边管制比美国单边管制更多地减少对手对受管制产品的进口。在多边机制下受管制出口减少中只有7%被其他国家替代，而在美国单边政策下为28%。多边管制通过提高对手的军事价格指数来减少其实际军事支出。以美国实际军事支出相对于对手衡量，安全影响在多边管制下上升约1个对数点，但在单边管制下仅上升0.14个对数点，而两者施加相似的民用实际成本。即使美国禁运也无法复制多边安全结果。
