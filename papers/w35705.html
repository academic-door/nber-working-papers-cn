Unemployment Insurance in Macroeconomic Stabilization with Imperfect Expectations

不完全预期下宏观经济稳定中的失业保险

Bence Bardóczy, Joao Guerreiro

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

Automatic stabilizers can respond to a recession without a new policy decision, but their effects on aggregate demand may still only arrive with delay. We study the transmission lag for unemployment insurance extensions in a heterogeneous-agent New Keynesian model that closely matches the incidence of unemployment risk and the response of household spending to job loss and UI extensions. More generous UI stimulates demand largely by relaxing precautionary saving, so households must anticipate future unemployment risk and benefit duration. To quantify this channel, we work directly with survey expectations, using a measured history of forecast errors and revisions, rather than committing to a specific model of belief formation. Our estimated model implies a significant Expectations-Driven Efficacy Lag. The direct effect of UI extensions on consumption peaks only after UI duration has begun to recede. In general equilibrium, the consumption multiplier is about 0.6 on impact, about half its value under full-information rational expectations, but the policy becomes more effective by the end of the first year of the recession.

中文摘要

自动稳定器可以在无需新政策决策的情况下应对衰退，但其对总需求的影响可能仍会延迟显现。我们研究了一个异质性代理人新凯恩斯模型中失业保险延长的传导时滞，该模型紧密匹配了失业风险的分布以及家庭支出对失业和失业保险延长的反应。更慷慨的失业保险主要通过放松预防性储蓄来刺激需求，因此家庭必须预期未来的失业风险和福利持续时间。为了量化这一渠道，我们直接使用调查预期，利用预测误差和修正的实测历史，而不是依赖于特定的信念形成模型。我们的估计模型意味着一个显著的预期驱动的效能时滞。失业保险延长对消费的直接影响仅在福利持续时间开始缩短后才达到峰值。在一般均衡中，消费乘数在冲击时约为0.6，约为完全信息理性预期下数值的一半，但该政策在衰退第一年结束时变得更加有效。
