NBER Working Paper w35789所在周报

(How) Do We Teach Emotions?

我们（如何）教授情绪？

Anjali Adukia, Matthew Bonci, Paula Dastres Gallardo, Emileigh Harrison, Jake Nicoll, Teodora Szasz

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

Emotional intelligence constitutes a key component of human capital, shaped partially by educational materials. Using machine learning and generative AI tools, we examine emotional content in public-school textbooks and children’s literature. A stark mismatch emerges: text exposes children to a broad emotional range, but images overwhelmingly depict happiness and calm, regardless of emotions described in text on the same page. Nearly half of pages show zero overlap between the emotions described in text and those shown in images. This pattern persists across time, contexts, and identities. Household purchases and library inventories suggest content may be endogenously shaped by consumer demand favoring “positive” cover imagery, implying market forces narrow the emotional landscape children encounter.

中文摘要

情商是人力资本的关键组成部分，部分由教育材料塑造。我们使用机器学习和生成式AI工具，考察公立学校教科书和儿童文学中的情绪内容。一个鲜明的错配浮现出来：文本让孩子接触到广泛的情绪范围，但图像压倒性地描绘快乐和平静，无论同一页文本中描述的情绪如何。近一半的页面显示文本描述的情绪与图像展示的情绪之间零重叠。这一模式在时间、情境和身份特征上持续存在。家庭购买和图书馆库存表明，内容可能由偏好“积极”封面图像的消费者需求内生塑造，这意味着市场力量缩小了儿童所接触的情绪图景。
