Large Language Models as Voting Mechanisms

作为投票机制的大语言模型

Raša Karapandža, Yaw Nyarko

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) increasingly provide economic and financial advice. We develop a microeconomic framework for understanding this role. We prove that an LLM acts like a voting rule: each input text is an election, possible continuations are candidates, and output probabilities are vote shares derived from the training corpus. Such rules can generate Condorcet cycles, implying intransitive pairwise recommendations and potential money-pump exploitation. Using ChatGPT-4o, we identify millions of cycles in pairwise investment choices among S&P 100 firms. Our theorems establish the voting interpretation on the training data and support its use off-sample. We also show that correcting cycles ex post is generally infeasible. Finally, temperature changes the voting rule: at T = 1, outputs are sampled in proportion to vote shares; lower temperatures favor the plurality winner, whereas higher temperatures flatten probabilities toward uniformity.

中文摘要

大语言模型（LLMs）日益提供经济和金融建议。我们构建了一个微观经济学框架来理解这一作用。我们证明，LLM 的行为类似于一种投票规则：每个输入文本是一场选举，可能的续写是候选人，输出概率是源自训练语料的得票份额。此类规则可能产生孔多塞循环，意味着成对建议不具有传递性，并可能被用于“抽水机”式套利。使用 ChatGPT-4o，我们在 S&P 100 公司的成对投资选择中识别出数百万个循环。我们的定理在训练数据上确立了投票解释，并支持其样本外使用。我们还表明，事后纠正循环通常是不可行的。最后，温度会改变投票规则：在 T = 1 时，输出按得票份额比例抽样；较低温度有利于多数获胜者，而较高温度则使概率趋于均匀。
