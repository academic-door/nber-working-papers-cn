Debiasing Law Enforcement Officers: Evidence from an Expressive Arts Intervention in India

消除执法人员的偏见：来自印度表达性艺术干预的证据

Sofia Amaral, Girija Borker, Nishith Prakash, María Micaela Sviatschi

环境与气候 印度

Abstract

Frontline state agents exercise substantial discretion over citizens’ access to public services, yet little is known about whether the attitudes and beliefs shaping this discretion can be changed among incumbent bureaucrats. Partnering with the Bihar Police - one of India’s largest states that operates under comparatively severe policing capacity constraints, we conduct a cluster randomized controlled trial across 417 police stations and roughly 2,500 active duty senior field police officers to evaluate a three-day expressive arts intervention, grounded in the Theatre of the Oppressed, designed to change police responses to gender-based violence (GBV). Approximately 7 months after baseline, treated officers exhibit more favorable attitudes toward GBV, greater empathy, and stronger legal knowledge. These changes translate into observed policing behavior: in standardized victim audits conducted one year later, officers in treated stations are 15 percentage points less likely to blame victims, dismiss fewer complaints, and provide more referrals, while administrative records show a 41 percent reduction in registration delays and, over a longer horizon, increased GBV reporting by citizens. Exploiting variation generated by officer transfers, we find suggestive evidence that these effects persist when treated officers relocate and diffuse to untreated officers working alongside treated colleagues. The effects also extend to the workplace: junior female officers working with treated senior officers report lower harassment and anxiety. These findings show that the attitudes shaping bureaucratic discretion are malleable and that changing how incumbent state agents exercise authority can improve both citizen-facing service delivery and the broader workplace environment.

中文摘要

一线国家代理人在公民获得公共服务方面拥有相当大的自由裁量权，但鲜有研究关注塑造这种自由裁量权的态度和信念能否在现任官僚中被改变。我们与比哈尔邦警察合作——该邦是印度最大的邦之一，在相对严峻的警务能力约束下运作——在417个警察局和约2,500名现役高级外勤警官中开展了一项整群随机对照试验，评估一项为期三天、以被压迫者剧场为基础的表达性艺术干预，旨在改变警察对性别暴力（GBV）的应对方式。基线后约7个月，受干预警官对GBV表现出更有利的态度、更强的同理心和更扎实的法律知识。这些变化转化为可观察的警务行为：在一年后进行的标准化受害者审计中，受干预警察局的警官指责受害者的可能性低15个百分点，驳回的投诉更少，提供的转介更多，而行政记录显示登记延迟减少了41%，并且在更长的时间范围内，公民的GBV报案有所增加。利用警官调动产生的变异，我们发现了提示性证据：当受干预警官调任并向与其共事的未受干预警官扩散时，这些效应会持续存在。效应还延伸至工作场所：与受干预高级警官共事的初级女警官报告称遭受的骚扰和焦虑更低。这些发现表明，塑造官僚自由裁量权的态度是可塑的，改变现任国家代理人行使权威的方式可以改善面向公民的服务提供和更广泛的工作场所环境。
