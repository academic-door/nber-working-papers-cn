The Effects of Years of Health Insurance on the Health of Older Adults

医疗保险年限对老年人健康的影响

Yu Liu, Kevin Callison, Robert Kaestner

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

Whether longer exposure to health insurance improves health remains an open empirical question. This study examines the health effects of an additional year of health insurance coverage by exploiting differential changes in coverage pre-to-post implementation of the Affordable Care Act (ACA) for those who were and were not insured prior to the ACA. Using longitudinal data from the Health and Retirement Study (HRS), we instrument for insurance duration using baseline uninsured status interacted with the post-ACA period. We find that the ACA increased insurance coverage by approximately one additional year among previously uninsured respondents relative to those insured at baseline. Instrumental-variable estimates provide no statistically significant evidence of improvements in self-rated health, depressive symptoms, or hypertension over a four-year follow-up period. These findings are robust across alternative specifications. Overall, results provide little evidence that longer insurance coverage translates into measurable short-term health improvements, while leaving open the possibility of modest or longer-term benefits.

中文摘要

更长的医疗保险暴露是否改善健康仍是一个未决的实证问题。本研究利用《平价医疗法案》（ACA）实施前后，对ACA之前已参保和未参保者保险覆盖率的差异变化，考察额外一年医疗保险覆盖的健康效应。使用《健康与退休研究》（HRS）的纵向数据，我们以基线未参保状态与ACA后时期的交互项作为保险持续时间的工具变量。我们发现，相对于基线已参保的受访者，ACA使先前未参保受访者的保险覆盖增加了约一年。工具变量估计在四年随访期内未提供自评健康、抑郁症状或高血压改善的统计显著证据。这些发现在替代设定下稳健。总体而言，结果几乎没有证据表明更长的保险覆盖转化为可测量的短期健康改善，同时保留了 modest 或长期益处的可能性。
