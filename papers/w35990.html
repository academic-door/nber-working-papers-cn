Financial Advisors and Retirees' Risk-taking

财务顾问与退休人员的风险承担

Allison T. Cole, Maarten Meeuwis, Alessandro Previtero, Noah Stoffman

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

Advised retirees keep about two-thirds of their portfolios in equities well into old age—30 to 40 percentage points more than non-advised retirees, target-date funds, or common rules of thumb. These allocations reflect active choices rather than inertia, and they adjust gradually toward a new advisor’s investment style following advisor switches. Equity shares rise with relationship tenure, and advisor influence is strongest among clients with low financial knowledge or risk tolerance, consistent with trust facilitating risk-taking. Given retirees’ modest financial wealth relative to their safe pension income, we rationalize these portfolios in a standard life-cycle model with moderate risk aversion. Modeling advisors as “money doctors” who correct clients’ pessimistic beliefs about the equity premium, we find that, relative to a no-advice counterfactual, the welfare gains from higher risk-taking are of the same order of magnitude as the cost of advice.

中文摘要

接受顾问建议的退休人员在步入老年后仍将约三分之二的资产组合配置于股票——比未接受建议的退休人员、目标日期基金或常见经验法则高出30至40个百分点。这些配置反映的是主动选择而非惯性，并且在更换顾问后会逐渐调整至新顾问的投资风格。股票份额随关系持续时间上升，且顾问影响力在金融知识或风险承受能力较低的客户中最强，这与信任促进风险承担相一致。鉴于退休人员相对于其安全养老金收入的金融财富较为有限，我们在标准生命周期模型中以中等风险厌恶程度合理化了这些资产组合。将顾问建模为纠正客户对股票溢价悲观信念的“货币医生”，我们发现，相对于无建议的反事实情形，更高风险承担带来的福利收益与建议成本处于同一数量级。
