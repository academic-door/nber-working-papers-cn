The Early Impacts of AI on Employment among Recent College Graduates

AI对近期大学毕业生就业的早期影响

Robert W. Fairlie, Jane Wu

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

The impact of AI on the employment prospects of recent college graduates is hotly debated with no consensus on the magnitude of impacts nor even the timing of those potential impacts. Using CPS microdata, we provide the first estimates of the effects of AI on the unemployment of recent college graduates in June, July and August 2026. We provide evidence suggesting that unemployment rates are especially high for summer months and that 2026 might be the first year of widespread enough AI use in the workplace to detect impacts of AI on recent college graduates, the group argued to be most vulnerable to AI replacement. Taking an agnostic approach to defining treatment timing, we find that unemployment rates did not spike in summer 2026 relative to summer months in previous years and did not rise in a significant way relative to older college graduates or young workers without a college degree. We also provide the first analysis of an expanded definition of unemployment that includes those who report “wanting a job” which adds nearly two percentage points to the unemployment rate of recent college graduates but we find no evidence of a statistically significant increase in summer 2026 even after adding these “sidelined unemployed.” We estimate difference-in-differences and event-study interaction models using both older college graduates and young non-college graduates as comparison groups and do not find evidence of an increase in relative unemployment rates. Finally, we estimate models in which we interact 2026 unemployment with AI exposure and remote work availability by occupation and find some evidence of a positive relationship with remote work availability.

中文摘要

AI对近期大学毕业生就业前景的影响引发了激烈争论，对于影响幅度乃至这些潜在影响的时间都没有共识。使用CPS微观数据，我们首次估计了AI对2026年6月、7月和8月近期大学毕业生失业的影响。我们提供的证据表明，夏季月份的失业率特别高，并且2026年可能是AI在工作场所中的使用广泛到足以检测出AI对近期大学毕业生影响的第一个年份，而这一群体被认为最容易受到AI替代。对处理时点采取不可知的方法，我们发现，与往年夏季月份相比，2026年夏季失业率并未飙升，并且相对于年长的大学毕业生或没有大学学位的年轻工人，也没有显著上升。我们还首次分析了扩展定义的失业，其中包括报告“想要工作”的人，这使近期大学毕业生的失业率增加近2个百分点，但即使加入这些“被边缘化的失业者”，我们也没有发现2026年夏季出现统计显著上升的证据。我们使用年长大学毕业生和年轻非大学毕业生作为比较组，估计了双重差分和事件研究交互模型，并未发现相对失业率上升的证据。最后，我们估计了将2026年失业率与按职业划分的AI暴露和远程工作可得性进行交互的模型，并发现一些证据表明其与远程工作可得性存在正相关关系。
