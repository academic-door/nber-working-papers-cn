Mergers and Quality Provision in Healthcare: Evidence from Nursing Homes

医疗保健中的并购与质量供给：来自疗养院的证据

Pinka Chatterji, Chun-Yu Ho, Wenqing Li

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper examines the impact of mergers between nursing home chains and independent facilities on quality of care, utilizing facility-level data from 1999–2019. Using a staggered difference-in-differences framework, we find that acquired facilities experience an 8% reduction in health deficiency citations in the three-year period post-merger. This effect persists for at least five years and is more pronounced in cross-market mergers, as well as mergers involving smaller or low-deficiency chains. Estimating a structural model suggests that this decline in deficiencies is driven by post-merger reductions in the marginal costs of serving residents. However, this cost efficiency is accompanied by a decline in direct care hours. Furthermore, the reduction in deficiencies is concentrated on low-severity categories, suggesting that mergers improve regulatory compliance rather than clinical outcomes. Consistently, we find no meaningful impact of mergers on residents’ health outcomes.\n\n

中文摘要

本文利用1999–2019年的机构层面数据，考察了疗养院连锁机构与独立机构之间的并购对护理质量的影响。通过使用交错双重差分框架，我们发现被收购机构在并购后三年内的健康缺陷引用数量减少了8%。这一效应至少持续五年，并且在跨市场并购以及涉及较小或低缺陷连锁机构的并购中更为显著。估计一个结构模型表明，这种缺陷减少是由并购后服务居民的边际成本降低所驱动的。然而，这种成本效率伴随着直接护理工时的下降。此外，缺陷的减少集中在低严重性类别，这表明并购改善了监管合规性而非临床结果。与此一致，我们发现并购对居民的健康结果没有显著影响。
