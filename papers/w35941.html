The Returns to Temporary Migration: Experimental Evidence from the H-2A Program

临时迁移的回报：来自H-2A项目的实验证据

Melanie Morten, Beatriz Magaloni, Sebastián Cubillos, Alice Yiqian Wang

健康与医疗 墨西哥、美国

Abstract

In 2024, 315,000 H-2A temporary seasonal work visas were issued to agricultural workers in the US. The H-2A program is controversial: earnings are high, but critics argue that workers face poor working and living conditions and that families bear the costs of separation. We randomize H-2A seasonal visas for Mexican workers. During a single US season, migrants earn more than USD 16,000 and send 70% of these earnings to origin households. Living standards in Mexico rise: food security increases, the probability of living in poverty falls by 10 percentage points, and families invest in home improvements, durable assets, and debt reduction, with little investment in businesses or productive assets. Migration has spillovers onto the household: the mental health of household respondents worsens and they avoid higher-risk situations such as walking at night, and intra-household dynamics change. Migrants generally report satisfaction with H-2A jobs and contracts, all report that they would migrate again, and 85% do so the following season.

中文摘要

2024年，美国向农业工人发放了315,000份H-2A临时季节性工作签证。H-2A项目存在争议：收入很高，但批评者认为工人面临恶劣的工作和生活条件，且家庭承担分离的成本。我们为墨西哥工人随机分配H-2A季节性签证。在单个美国季节期间，移民收入超过16,000美元，并将其中70%的收入的汇回原籍家庭。墨西哥的生活水平上升：粮食安全提高，生活在贫困中的概率下降10个百分点，家庭投资于住房改善、耐用资产和债务削减，但对生意或生产性资产的投资很少。迁移对家庭产生溢出效应：家庭受访者的心理健康恶化，他们回避夜间行走等风险更高的情境，家庭内部动态也发生变化。移民普遍报告对H-2A工作和合同感到满意，所有人都报告他们会再次迁移，85%的人在下一个季节确实这样做了。
