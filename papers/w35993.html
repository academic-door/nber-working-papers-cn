Peer Influence in College Applications

大学申请中的同伴影响

Ana M. Gazmuri, Elena Prager

金融与资产定价 未指明区域

Abstract

This paper studies how exposure to peers affects college application behavior. We use neighbors who attend different high schools to mitigate concerns about common shocks. Using granular classroom-level data, we find that exposure to peers who have applied to a program increases application rates. Consistent with an information mechanism, there is no additional effect of peers who are admitted. In counterfactuals, the effect is large enough to close urban-rural gaps and gender gaps in applications to selective college programs. We make the (generally NP-hard) application portfolio choice problem tractable using a setting where admission probabilities are observable and conditionally independent.

中文摘要

本文研究接触同伴如何影响大学申请行为。我们利用就读不同高中的邻居来缓解对共同冲击的担忧。利用细粒度的班级层面数据，我们发现接触曾申请某个项目的同伴会提高申请率。与信息机制一致，被录取的同伴没有额外效应。在反事实分析中，该效应大到足以缩小选择性大学项目申请中的城乡差距和性别差距。我们利用一个录取概率可观测且条件独立的设定，使（通常为NP难的）申请组合选择问题变得可处理。
