CFOs Meet LLMs

CFO与大型语言模型

John R. Graham, Campbell R. Harvey, Manish Jha

AI 与数字经济 未指明区域

Abstract

Business sentiment is closely watched, but measurement is slow and costly: surveys reach a limited number of firms, arrive periodically, and take time to field and compile. We show that large language models hold the potential to address some of these shortcomings. We prompt an LLM to role-play as the CFO of a specific company on a specific date, for every public-company CFO who responded to the Duke–Federal Reserve CFO Survey from 2002- 2025. We find that LLM-generated forecasts reasonably match the cross-section of CFO expectations, with accuracy increasing with the amount of information provided to the LLM. We discuss the potential as well as the hurdles to using digital twins to mimic individual decision-makers.

中文摘要

商业情绪受到密切关注，但测量缓慢且成本高昂：调查仅覆盖有限数量的企业，定期进行，且需要时间实地开展和汇总。我们表明大型语言模型有潜力解决其中一些不足。对于2002—2025年期间回应杜克—美联储CFO调查的每一位上市公司CFO，我们提示大型语言模型在特定日期角色扮演特定公司的CFO。我们发现大型语言模型生成的预测与CFO预期的横截面合理匹配，准确性随着提供给大型语言模型的信息量增加而提高。我们讨论了使用数字孪生模仿个体决策者的潜力以及障碍。
