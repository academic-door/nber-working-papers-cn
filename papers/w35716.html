Expectations-based Capital Inflow Shocks

基于预期的资本流入冲击

Jongrim Ha, Dohan Kim, M. Ayhan Kose, Francis E. Warnock

其他主题 未指明区域

Abstract

Identifying the impact of capital inflows on output is challenging because inflows are forward-looking and respond to expectations about future economic conditions. We develop a new measure of capital inflow shocks using a simple and broadly applicable expectations-based framework that isolates the unexpected component of inflows by purging movements predicted by professional forecasters’ expectations. Estimates using the new measure for 27 emerging market economies indicate that capital inflows have sizable expansionary effects on output, operating through stronger domestic consumption and investment and easier financing conditions. Additional analysis indicates that equity-type inflows generate greater and more persistent effects than debt-type inflows, while large inflow reversals result in more pronounced effects than comparable increases in inflows. These results are robust to a wide range of alternative specifications.

中文摘要

识别资本流入对产出的影响颇具挑战性，因为资本流入具有前瞻性，并会对未来经济状况的预期做出反应。我们利用一个简单且广泛适用的基于预期的框架，开发了一种新的资本流入冲击衡量方法，该方法通过剔除专业预测者预期所预测的变动，来分离出资本流入的意外成分。使用这一新指标对27个新兴市场经济体的估计表明，资本流入对产出具有显著的扩张效应，其作用机制是通过增强国内消费和投资以及改善融资条件来实现的。进一步分析表明，股权类流入比债务类流入产生更大且更持久的影响，而大规模的流入逆转比同等规模的流入增加产生更显著的影响。这些结果对多种替代性设定均保持稳健。
