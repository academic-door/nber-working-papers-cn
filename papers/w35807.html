Network General Equilibrium as a Threat to Identification: The Failure of Time Fixed Effects

网络一般均衡对识别的威胁：时间固定效应的失败

Bernard Herskovic, Gill Segal

其他主题 美国

Abstract

Time fixed effects are not a general-equilibrium control. In network economies, aggregate shocks do not enter firm outcomes uniformly: they propagate through equilibrium prices, costs, and demand, leaving a residual network gradient after time demeaning. When an instrument, treatment, or exposure design even partially aligns with this gradient, standard panel estimators recover the direct effect plus an endogenous network-leakage component. We develop a tractable framework that signs and quantifies this Network-SUTVA bias in closed form, showing how network topology governs its magnitude. Weak instruments magnify the leakage. Difference-in-differences designs are similarly contaminated because controls can be untreated by assignment but treated in equilibrium. Calibrated to the U.S. production network, the distortion can exceed 130% of the true direct effect. We propose observed- and hidden-network diagnostics and remedies, and test them on canonical difference-in-differences designs, showing that standard estimators can confound direct policy responses with systemic network leakage.

中文摘要

时间固定效应不是一般均衡控制。在网络经济中，总体冲击不会均匀地进入企业结果：它们通过均衡价格、成本和需求传播，在时间去均值后留下残余的网络梯度。当工具、处理或暴露设计即使部分地与这一梯度对齐时，标准面板估计量所恢复的是直接效应加上内生的网络泄漏成分。我们开发了一个易于处理的框架，以闭式形式刻画并量化这种Network-SUTVA偏误，并展示网络拓扑如何决定其大小。弱工具会放大泄漏。双重差分设计同样受到污染，因为控制组在分配上可能未受处理，但在均衡中却受到处理。校准到美国生产网络后，这种扭曲可超过真实直接效应的130%。我们提出可观测网络和隐藏网络的诊断与补救方法，并在经典双重差分设计上对其进行检验，表明标准估计量可能将直接政策反应与系统性网络泄漏混为一谈。
