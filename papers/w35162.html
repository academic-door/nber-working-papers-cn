Tipping Points and Australia’s Social Media Ban

临界点与澳大利亚的社交媒体禁令

Leonardo Bursztyn, Angela L. Duckworth, Rafael Jiménez-Durán, Aaron Leonard, Filip Milojević, Christopher Roth, Cass R. Sunstein

其他主题 澳大利亚

Abstract

In December 2025, Australia became the first country to ban under-16s from major social media platforms, a policy under consideration in more than a dozen countries. Because individually complying with the ban when others do not comply is socially costly, an easily circumvented ban may work only if compliance crosses a tipping point. Surveying Australian teenagers four months after implementation, we found that fewer than one in three 14–15-year-olds were in compliance with the ban. However, teenagers reported requiring two out of three peers to comply before complying themselves and, in addition, perceived compliers as less popular. These forces predict erosion, and indeed, two months later, compliance had fallen to about one in twenty. Reducing social media use in adolescents likely requires supplementing bans with individual incentives and social norms interventions.

中文摘要

2025年12月，澳大利亚成为首个禁止16岁以下未成年人使用主要社交媒体平台的国家，目前有十多个国家正在考虑类似政策。由于当他人不遵守禁令时，个人遵守禁令会带来社会成本，因此一项容易被规避的禁令只有在遵守率达到临界点后才可能奏效。在禁令实施四个月后对澳大利亚青少年的调查中，我们发现14至15岁青少年中只有不到三分之一遵守了禁令。然而，青少年报告称，他们需要三分之二同龄人遵守后自己才会遵守，此外，他们认为遵守者更不受欢迎。这些因素预示着遵守率将下降，事实上，两个月后遵守率已降至约二十分之一。减少青少年的社交媒体使用，可能需要在禁令之外辅以个人激励和社会规范干预措施。
