Cross-Section Estimation of Long-Run Relations Using Time-Compressed Data

使用时间压缩数据的长期关系横截面估计

Serena Ng, Nikolay Gospodinov

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

Many empirical investigations of long-run relations are based on cross-section regressions in averaged or long differenced data that effectively have the time dimension of a T × N panel compressed. We analyze a class of time-compressed I(1) data and show that they have magnified variability stemming from the fact that the cross-section variance of a non-stationary panel ‘fans out’ with time. Cross-section regressions in time compressed data can potentially yield estimates that are super-consistent and asymptotically normal, whether the regressors are stationary, non-stationary, or highly persistent. The fastest convergence rate of √NT requires a compression scheme that not only magnifies the non-stationary signal, but also dilutes the regression noise. Omitted fixed effects preclude noise dilution but the estimates remain superconsistent. However, the fanning out effect can be weakened when the data have a strong force for mean-reversion or convergence, a problem that seems relevant for temperature data. We consider three applications and find that the long-run relation between consumption and income, and between growth/inflation and demographic variables are reasonably well determined, but the estimated relation between growth and warming temperature is fragile.

中文摘要

许多关于长期关系的实证研究基于平均化或长期差分数据的横截面回归，这些数据实际上将T × N面板的时间维度压缩了。我们分析了一类时间压缩的I(1)数据，并表明它们具有放大的变异性，这源于非平稳面板的横截面方差随时间“扇形展开”。在时间压缩数据中进行横截面回归，无论回归元是平稳的、非平稳的还是高度持久的，都可能产生超一致且渐近正态的估计。最快的收敛速度√NT需要一种压缩方案，该方案不仅放大非平稳信号，还要稀释回归噪声。遗漏固定效应会妨碍噪声稀释，但估计仍然保持超一致性。然而，当数据具有强烈的均值回归或收敛力量时，扇形展开效应可能会减弱，这个问题似乎与温度数据相关。我们考虑了三个应用，发现消费与收入之间、增长/通胀与人口统计变量之间的长期关系得到了相当好的确定，但增长与升温温度之间的估计关系是脆弱的。
