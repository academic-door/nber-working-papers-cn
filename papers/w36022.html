The Last Arbitrage? Capital Flows, Development, and Infrastructure as an Asset Class

最后的套利？资本流动、发展与作为资产类别的基础设施

Anusha Chari, Monroe Gamble, Peter Blair Henry

金融与资产定价 美国

Abstract

Lucas (1990) asked why capital does not flow from rich to poor countries. When poor countries opened their stock markets to foreign investors in the late 1980s, it did: rich-country capital arbitraged away the excess return on emerging-market equity and created a new asset class. Infrastructure may offer the last such arbitrage. The median social return on paved roads in poor countries was 3.7 times the return on U.S. capital at the time of liberalization, but investors capture only the share of social returns that contracts, user fees, and political risk allow, a constraint we call the private foreign hurdle. Confidential IFC data show that its infrastructure equity portfolio outperformed the S&P 500 by 2 percentage points per year, even though only 41 percent of individual investments in the portfolio did so. This suggests that strong risk-adjusted portfolio returns can mask substantial project-specific uncertainty. Despite high social returns, transportation accounted for just 9 IFC infrastructure project investments versus 157 in power and telecommunications. This raises the question of whether private investors can appropriate enough of the social returns on infrastructure to make these investments. Moreover, investors cannot learn from returns they cannot see. Disclosure of historical equity returns is therefore necessary, but not sufficient, to efficiently mobilize more private capital for infrastructure. Because the benefits of disclosure accrue to the market, not to any one firm, multilateral development banks, working with an independent research partner, are best placed to provide it.

中文摘要

Lucas（1990）追问为何资本不从富国流向穷国。当穷国在1980年代末向外国投资者开放股票市场时，资本确实流入了：富国资本套利掉了新兴市场股票的超额回报，并创造了一个新的资产类别。基础设施可能提供最后这样的套利机会。在自由化时期，穷国铺装道路的社会回报中位数为美国资本回报的3.7倍，但投资者只能获取合同、使用者收费和政治风险所允许的那部分社会回报，我们将这一约束称为私人外国门槛。国际金融公司（IFC）的机密数据显示，其基础设施股权组合每年跑赢标普500指数2个百分点，尽管该组合中仅有41%的单项投资做到了这一点。这表明强劲的风险调整后组合回报可能掩盖了巨大的项目特定不确定性。尽管社会回报很高，交通仅占IFC基础设施项目投资的9项，而电力和电信为157项。这提出了私人投资者能否占有足够多的基础设施社会回报以使这些投资可行的问题。此外，投资者无法从他们看不见的回报中学习。因此，披露历史股权回报对于有效动员更多私人资本进入基础设施是必要的，但并不充分。由于披露的收益归于市场而非任何单一企业，多边开发银行与独立研究伙伴合作最适合提供这一披露。
