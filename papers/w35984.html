Target What? The Foundations of Poverty Targeting

瞄准什么？贫困瞄准的基础

Rema Hanna, Emily Breza, Benjamin A. Olken, Abhijit Banerjee, Arun G. Chandrasekhar, Gideon Moore

其他主题 印度、印度尼西亚

Abstract

Most government transfer programs aim to target the poor. Doing so implicitly assumes that the lowest-consumption households have the highest marginal social valuation, i.e., have the highest social welfare gain from an additional dollar. But this need not be the case. In 600 Indonesian villages, we ask over 5,500 villagers to rank other villagers on how well off they are and how much a modest cash transfer would help them, and compare these rankings to consumption and self-assessed welfare. Targeting consumption misses 77% of households with the highest marginal social valuation. Targeting self-assessed welfare misses 57%. Even targeting those the community ranks worst off in levels, not margins, misses 25%. Divergences between different notions of neediness are statistically predictable. In a randomized controlled trial, proxy means tests best target consumption, self-targeting ordeals capture self-assessed welfare levels, and community targeting comes closest to marginal social valuation. Targeting strategies inescapably embed normative stances.

中文摘要

大多数政府转移支付项目旨在瞄准穷人。这样做隐含地假设最低消费家庭具有最高的边际社会估值，即从额外一美元中获得最高的社会福利增益。但情况未必如此。在600个印度尼西亚村庄中，我们请超过5,500名村民对其他村民的富裕程度以及一笔适度现金转移对他们的帮助程度进行排序，并将这些排序与消费和自评福利进行比较。以消费为瞄准标准遗漏了77%具有最高边际社会估值的家庭。以自评福利为瞄准标准遗漏了57%。即使以社区在水平而非边际上排名最差的家庭为瞄准标准，也遗漏了25%。不同需求概念之间的分歧在统计上是可以预测的。在一项随机对照试验中，代理均值检验最能瞄准消费，自我瞄准的考验捕捉自评福利水平，而社区瞄准最接近边际社会估值。瞄准策略不可避免地嵌入了规范性立场。
