NBER Working Paper w35883所在周报

Producing to Signal: Private Information in Dynamic Quantity Competition

为信号而生产：动态数量竞争中的私人信息

Andrew Sweeting, Xuezhen Tao, Qian Wang

其他主题 全球/多国

Abstract

We model a finite-horizon game of repeated oligopoly competition in which sellers choose quantities of a homogeneous product with linear demand and have private information about their own marginal costs, which are serially correlated over time. Focusing on fully separating equilibria, we provide conditions for existence and uniqueness. Relative to static complete- and incomplete-information benchmarks, sellers' incentives to signal low costs can substantially affect output, productive efficiency, and welfare, even when there are ten or more sellers. We apply the model to the global oil market, and compare predictions to a model where sellers can make quantity commitments.

中文摘要

我们建模了一个有限期界的重复寡头竞争博弈，其中卖方选择线性需求同质产品的数量，并拥有关于自身边际成本的私人信息，这些成本随时间序列相关。聚焦于完全分离均衡，我们提供了存在性和唯一性的条件。相对于静态完全信息和不完全信息基准，卖方发出低成本信号的激励可以显著影响产出、生产效率和社会福利，即使有十个或更多卖方也是如此。我们将该模型应用于全球石油市场，并将预测与卖方可以做出数量承诺的模型进行比较。
