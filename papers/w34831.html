More Hours, More Work: Head Start Expansions Boost Maternal Employment

更多时间，更多工作：启蒙计划的扩展促进了母亲就业

Chloe Gibbs, Esra Kose, Maria Rosales-Rueda

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

Women’s employment remains highly sensitive to childcare constraints, making childcare availability a critical lever for supporting mothers’ labor force attachment. We study the effects of expanded full-day Head Start programming, using the 2016 federal funding initiative that targeted grantees with low full-day enrollment. Linking administrative program data, geo-coded center locations, and household employment data, we use a difference-in-differences design by comparing mothers in treated and untreated areas. The policy increased full-day enrollment by 19% and raised single mothers’ employment (2.5%), full-time employment (3.2%), hours (2.9%), and earnings (7%). Results show that extending program duration meaningfully improves maternal labor outcomes.

中文摘要

女性就业对育儿限制仍然高度敏感，这使得育儿服务的可获得性成为支持母亲劳动力依附的关键杠杆。我们利用2016年针对全日制入学率低的受助者的联邦资助计划，研究了扩展全日制启蒙计划的效果。通过关联行政项目数据、地理编码的中心位置和家庭就业数据，我们使用双重差分设计，比较了处理区和未处理区的母亲。该政策使全日制入学率提高了19%，并提高了单身母亲的就业率（2.5%）、全职就业率（3.2%）、工作时间（2.9%）和收入（7%）。结果表明，延长项目时长能显著改善母亲的劳动成果。
