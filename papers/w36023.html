The Role of Parental Beliefs and Public Policies in Financing Higher Education: An Experimental Study

父母信念与公共政策在高等教育融资中的作用：一项实验研究

Francesco Agostinelli, Guglielmo Briscese, Giovanni M. Topa

教育与劳动 未指明区域

Abstract

Parents who want to financially support their child’s pursuit of higher education must save against assumptions about what college will cost and how it will be funded. This study examines how parental beliefs and future borrowing costs shape college saving decisions. Through a survey experiment with an embedded financial planning tool, we elicit parental beliefs about the future costs of college and their expectations of different funding sources, including financial aid and student loans. We find that nearly all parents want to cover some of the future costs of college, but many may fall short in their savings due to underestimating future expenses and overestimating available financial aid. A corrective information intervention encourages most parents to recalibrate their saving target. However, when a substantial gap between expected and actual costs is combined with a salient high repayment burden for their child, we find a discouragement effect that diminishes both their willingness to save and their aspirations for their child’s educational attainment. We calibrate a model of parental saving with learning about college costs, which reproduces both the absence of an average response to borrowing costs and the shift of children away from college attendance, and use it to price the welfare cost of misperception. Parents with higher financial literacy hold better-calibrated beliefs and respond more to correction. We discuss policy implications for outreach activities to support informed financial planning for college education.

中文摘要

希望为子女追求高等教育提供经济支持的父母，必须基于对大学费用以及资金来源的假设进行储蓄。本研究考察父母信念和未来借贷成本如何塑造大学储蓄决策。通过一项嵌入财务规划工具的调查实验，我们引出父母对大学未来费用的信念以及他们对不同资金来源（包括经济援助和学生贷款）的预期。我们发现，几乎所有父母都希望覆盖部分未来大学费用，但许多人可能因低估未来支出和高估可用经济援助而在储蓄上不足。一项纠正性信息干预促使大多数父母重新校准其储蓄目标。然而，当预期成本与实际成本之间的巨大差距与子女面临显著的高还款负担相结合时，我们发现一种挫败效应，既削弱了他们的储蓄意愿，也削弱了他们对子女教育成就的期望。我们校准了一个包含大学成本学习的父母储蓄模型，该模型再现了对借贷成本平均反应缺失以及子女远离大学入学的转变，并用其估算误知的福利成本。金融素养较高的父母持有校准更好的信念，并对纠正信息反应更强。我们讨论了支持知情大学教育财务规划的 outreach 活动的政策含义。
