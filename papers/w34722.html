Speculative Growth and the AI "Bubble"

投机性增长与AI“泡沫”

Ricardo J. Caballero

宏观与货币财政 未指明区域

Abstract

High AI valuations are usually read as fundamentals or a bubble. This paper develops a third possibility: temporary overvaluation can leave a permanent real legacy. AI investment expands productive capacity and shifts income toward capital owners, whose propensity to save rises with wealth. This feedback supports a rational high-capital steady state. Rational pricing preserves the low-capital path; temporary optimism supplies the valuation needed for transition. It raises valuation and investment demand, pushing up the equilibrium real interest rate as capitalists postpone consumption to fund installation. The outcome depends on size and duration: optimism must open the high-capital path and last until capital crosses the threshold at which the rational high continuation becomes available. An earlier correction returns the economy to the low state; a later one lowers valuation but leaves the installed capital in place. The high-capital destination has higher worker wages and a lower real interest rate.

中文摘要

高企的AI估值通常被解读为基本面或泡沫。本文提出了第三种可能性：暂时的估值过高可以留下永久的实际遗产。AI投资扩大了生产能力，并将收入转向资本所有者，而资本所有者的储蓄倾向随财富增加而上升。这种反馈支持一个理性的高资本稳态。理性定价维持了低资本路径；暂时的乐观情绪提供了转型所需的估值。它提高了估值和投资需求，推动均衡实际利率上升，因为资本家推迟消费以资助资本形成。结果取决于规模和持续时间：乐观情绪必须开启高资本路径，并持续到资本超过理性高持续增长可用的阈值。过早的修正会使经济回到低状态；较晚的修正会降低估值，但已安装的资本会保留下来。高资本目的地具有更高的工人工资和更低的实际利率。
