Access to Capital and the IPO Decision: An Analysis of US Private Firms

资本获取与IPO决策：对美国私营企业的分析

Andres Almazan, Nathan Swem, Sheridan Titman, Gregory Weitzner

金融与资产定价 美国

Abstract

We analyze firms’ IPO decisions using detailed financial data on US private firms. Firms with higher external capital needs are more likely to go public. Following the IPO, firms increase investment and debt issuance, leaving their leverage ratios un-changed from pre-IPO levels. Finally, newly public firms borrow from an expanded pool of lenders on improved terms, and the within-firm dispersion in banks’ private risk assessments declines. Our analysis suggests that firms go public to improve their access to capital, which is facilitated by reduced asymmetric information, and that the benefit extends beyond the IPO itself to new sources of debt.

中文摘要

我们利用美国私营企业的详细财务数据分析企业的IPO决策。外部资本需求更高的企业更可能上市。IPO之后，企业增加投资和债务发行，但其杠杆率与IPO前水平保持不变。最后，新上市企业从更广泛的贷款人群体以更优条件借款，并且银行私人风险评估的企业内离散度下降。我们的分析表明，企业上市是为了改善其资本获取，这得益于信息不对称的降低，而且这种收益不仅限于IPO本身，还延伸到新的债务来源。
