When Food Stamps Became Free: Impacts and Implications of Removing the Purchase Requirement

当食品券变为免费：取消购买要求的影響与启示

Amy Finkelstein, Matthew J. Notowidigdo, Charlie Rafkin

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

We analyze the food stamp program both as a potential investment in children’s health capital and as a redistributive tool by studying the 1979 reform that made food stamps free. We leverage geographic variation in exposure to the reform, which raised the program’s food subsidy from 65% to 100%. This raised enrollment by 1.85 percentage points (standard error = 0.15), or about 25%, with new enrollees substantially poorer than existing participants. From an investment perspective, we do not find evidence of improvement in infant health, and 95% confidence intervals rule out gains in birth weight from enrolling in food stamps exceeding 2%. From a redistribution perspective, the enrollment response implies that the average recipient of free food stamps acts as if they value them at 59 cents per dollar, with nonparametric bounds of 42–77 cents. Despite this substantial dissipation in transfer value relative to a frictionless cash transfer, under standard redistributive preferences the free food stamp program increases social welfare because of its targeting to very low-income households. The move to free stamps from a partially subsidized program improved targeting on social value but worsened targeting on private value.

中文摘要

我们通过研究1979年使食品券变为免费的改革，将食品券计划既作为对儿童健康资本的潜在投资，又作为再分配工具进行分析。我们利用该改革暴露程度的地理差异，该改革将计划的食品补贴从65%提高到100%。这使登记人数增加了1.85个百分点（标准误=0.15），约25%，新登记者的贫困程度远高于现有参与者。从投资角度看，我们没有发现婴儿健康改善的证据，95%置信区间排除了登记食品券带来的出生体重增加超过2%的可能性。从再分配角度看，登记响应意味着免费食品券的平均接受者的行为如同他们每美元估值59美分，非参数界限为42–77美分。尽管相对于无摩擦现金转移，转移价值存在显著耗散，但在标准再分配偏好下，免费食品券计划因其针对极低收入家庭的定向性而增加了社会福利。从部分补贴计划转向免费食品券，在社会价值方面的定向性改善，但在私人价值方面的定向性恶化。
