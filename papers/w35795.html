NBER Working Paper w35795所在周报

Innovation, Business Cycles, and the Climate Transition

创新、商业周期与气候转型

Diego R. Känzig, Maximilian Konradt, Lixing Wang, Donghai Zhang

环境与气候 未指明区域

Abstract

We study how business cycle fluctuations shape the pace and direction of innovation. Non-green innovation is procyclical, while green innovation is countercyclical, both over the cycle and in response to economic shocks. We explain this divergence in a dynamic general equilibrium model with endogenous green and non-green innovation. Green patents derive more value from distant profits, making green innovation less sensitive to short-run fluctuations. Endogenous innovation costs reinforce this mechanism, making green and non-green innovation effective substitutes. Evidence from market-implied patent valuations and scientist earnings supports the mechanism. The model implies larger and greener effects of innovation subsidies during downturns.

中文摘要

我们研究商业周期波动如何塑造创新的速度和方向。非绿色创新是顺周期的，而绿色创新是逆周期的，无论是在周期中还是在对经济冲击的响应中都是如此。我们在一个具有内生绿色和非绿色创新的动态一般均衡模型中解释这种分化。绿色专利从远期利润中获得更多价值，使绿色创新对短期波动不那么敏感。内生创新成本强化了这一机制，使绿色和非绿色创新成为有效替代品。来自市场隐含专利估值和科学家收入的证据支持这一机制。该模型意味着，创新补贴在经济下行期间会产生更大且更绿色的效果。
