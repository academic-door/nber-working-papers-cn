Foreign Tax Advantages after the U.S. Tax Reform of 2017

2017年美国税改后的外国税收优势

Anmol Bhandari, Ellen McGrattan

公共财政与税收 美国

Abstract

We construct a firm-year panel of income-tax rate reconciliations from annual 10-K filings of public companies to study the effects of the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA) on U.S. multinational profit shifting. These reconciliations decompose the gap between company-level effective tax rates and the federal statutory corporate tax rate, allowing us to isolate the contribution of foreign tax advantages before and after the reform. We find that TCJA significantly reduced the tax rate gap and increased effective tax rates for companies most exposed to pre-TCJA international tax benefits. This resulted in an onshoring of profits with foreign profit shares falling 1.3 percentage points per percentage point of tax increase. Among more exposed companies, we find no differential change in investment, employment, or the geographic distribution of sales and installed capital. A neoclassical model of a multinational that chooses where to book its income rationalizes this pattern: the reform raised the average tax on income booked abroad relative to income booked at home, which governs where profits are reported, while the marginal tax on capital, which governs investment, changed by an amount common to all firms. Controlling for deferred-tax windfall gains leaves our main results unchanged.

中文摘要

我们利用上市公司年度10-K申报中的所得税税率调节表构建了一个企业-年度面板，以研究《减税与就业法案》（TCJA）对美国跨国公司利润转移的影响。这些调节表分解了公司层面有效税率与联邦法定公司税率之间的差距，使我们能够分离改革前后外国税收优势的贡献。我们发现，TCJA显著缩小了税率差距，并提高了那些在TCJA前最受国际税收优惠影响公司的有效税率。这导致了利润回流，外国利润份额每对应税率上升1个百分点便下降1.3个百分点。在受影响更大的公司中，我们没有发现投资、就业或销售与已安装资本地理分布存在差异化变化。一个选择其收入记账地的跨国公司新古典模型可以解释这一模式：改革提高了相对于国内记账收入而言的海外记账收入平均税率，而这一税率决定利润报告地；与此同时，决定投资的资本边际税率的变化对所有企业而言是共同的。控制递延税意外收益后，我们的主要结果保持不变。
