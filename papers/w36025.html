The Health Effects of Eating Red Meat: A Natural Experiment in Alpha-Gal Syndrome

吃红肉的健康影响：α-半乳糖综合征中的自然实验

Vishal Patel, Christopher M. Worsham, Michael Liu-Hass, Anupam Jena

健康与医疗 未指明区域

Abstract

Observational associations between red meat consumption and cardiometabolic disease are confounded by diet and other health behaviors. Alpha-gal syndrome, a tick-borne allergy to mammalian meat, leads to an abrupt and involuntary cessation of red meat consumption. Using national electronic health records, we compared changes in weight and cardiometabolic health over two years before and after newly detected alpha-gal syndrome with those in patients with other tick-borne diseases, who were also bitten and sought care but whose diagnoses carry no dietary restriction. Relative to this comparison group, diagnosis of alpha-gal syndrome led to a sustained 0.8 kg (95% CI, 0.6 to 1.0) reduction in body weight that increased with the degree of sensitization, as well as small reductions in triglycerides, glycated hemoglobin, glucose, and low-density lipoprotein cholesterol. Not eating red meat appears to yield modest but measurable cardiometabolic benefits.

中文摘要

红肉消费与心脏代谢疾病之间的观察性关联受到饮食和其他健康行为的混杂。α-半乳糖综合征是一种由蜱传播的哺乳动物肉过敏，导致突然和非自愿地停止红肉消费。我们使用国家电子健康记录，比较了新检测出α-半乳糖综合征的患者在诊断前后两年内体重和心脏代谢健康的变化，与患有其他蜱传播疾病的患者进行比较，后者也被叮咬并寻求护理，但其诊断不带有饮食限制。相对于这一比较组，α-半乳糖综合征的诊断导致体重持续减少0.8 kg（95% CI，0.6至1.0），且随致敏程度增加而增加，同时甘油三酯、糖化血红蛋白、葡萄糖和低密度脂蛋白胆固醇略有降低。不吃红肉似乎产生适度但可测量的心脏代谢益处。
