Correcting Endogeneity via Nonparametric Copula Control Functions

通过非参数Copula控制函数修正内生性

Xixi Hu, Yi Qian, Hui Xie

理论与方法 未指明区域

Abstract

We propose a new framework to address endogenous regressors using a novel conditional copula endogeneity model. Endogenous regressor models are nonparametric and agnostic to the functions that determine the values of endogenous regressors from exogenous regressors and unobservables. To capture the regressor-error dependence unexplained by exogenous regressors, conditional Gaussian copulas are used to link the structural error terms and the nonparametric models for endogenous regressors. Building on the model, we develop a two-stage nonparametric control function approach for endogeneity correction without relying on instrumental variables. Specifically, the approach constructs control functions using nonparametric estimates of the conditional cumulative distribution functions of endogenous regressors given exogenous regressors. The method relaxes the assumption of regressors and error jointly following Gaussian copula dependence structure and eliminates the need to model regressors. It unifies and generalizes existing copula-based endogeneity correction methods, while minimizing assumptions about how endogenous regressors are determined. Unlike existing copula control function methods, it can handle discrete endogenous regressors (e.g., binary or low-count) by leveraging variation in relevant exogenous control regressors. We demonstrate the robustness and broad applicability of the proposed method compared to existing copula-based endogeneity correction methods in simulation studies and empirical applications.

中文摘要

我们提出一个新的框架，利用一种新颖的条件Copula内生性模型来处理内生回归元。内生回归元模型是非参数的，并且对由外生回归元和不可观测因素决定内生回归元值的函数形式不作假设。为了捕捉外生回归元无法解释的回归元-误差项依赖性，我们使用条件高斯Copula来连接结构误差项和内生回归元的非参数模型。基于该模型，我们开发了一种两阶段非参数控制函数方法，用于在不依赖工具变量的情况下进行内生性修正。具体来说，该方法利用内生回归元在给定外生回归元条件下的累积分布函数的非参数估计来构建控制函数。该方法放宽了回归元和误差项联合服从高斯Copula依赖结构的假设，并消除了对回归元建模的需要。它统一并推广了现有的基于Copula的内生性修正方法，同时最小化了对内生回归元如何确定的假设。与现有的Copula控制函数方法不同，它可以通过利用相关外生控制回归元的变动来处理离散的内生回归元（例如，二元或低计数）。我们在模拟研究和实证应用中证明了所提出方法相对于现有基于Copula的内生性修正方法的稳健性和广泛适用性。
