The Labor Market Impact of Temporary High-Skill Immigration Programs

临时高技能移民项目的劳动力市场影响

George J. Borjas

教育与劳动 美国

Abstract

In 2019, about 1.2 million foreign workers were employed through temporary high-skill programs, including H-1B and OPT. This paper identifies likely temporary workers in the American Community Survey and measures their supply by education and field of degree. Natives in the most exposed skill groups experienced slower earnings growth and a relative decline in full-time year-round work. Many temporary workers stay after their status ends, but surveys cannot identify these stayers. A model of temporary inflows and stayers shows that, under specified conditions, the coefficient from an IV regression of native outcomes on the number of current temporary workers embodies a stayer multiplier, capturing the effects of both those workers and earlier stayers. Multiplying the coefficient by the observed increase in temporary workers recovers the programs’ full effect without data on stayers. The estimates imply that natives with science and engineering degrees earn about 3.5 percent less, and those with master’s degrees in computing or electrical and computer engineering about 16 percent less. Production complementarities raise average wages by about 1.4 percent, offsetting only part of those losses. Absent externalities, the programs mainly redistribute earnings.

中文摘要

2019年，约120万外国工人通过临时高技能项目就业，包括H-1B和OPT。本文在美国社区调查中识别可能的临时工人，并按教育程度和学位领域衡量其供给。暴露程度最高的技能组中的本土人经历了更慢的收入增长和全职全年工作的相对下降。许多临时工人在身份结束后留下，但调查无法识别这些留下者。一个关于临时流入和留下者的模型表明，在特定条件下，将本土结果对当前临时工人数量进行IV回归所得的系数体现了一个留下者乘数，捕捉了这些工人和更早留下者的效应。将系数乘以观察到的临时工人增加量，可在没有留下者数据的情况下恢复项目的全部效应。估计结果表明，拥有科学和工程学位的本土人收入约低3.5%，拥有计算或电气与计算机工程硕士学位的本土人收入约低16%。生产互补性使平均工资提高约1.4%，仅抵消了这些损失的一部分。在没有外部性的情况下，这些项目主要重新分配收入。
