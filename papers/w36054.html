Mortality Effects of the 2025 US Aid Freeze: Evidence from Zambia

2025年美国援助冻结的死亡率效应：来自赞比亚的证据

Torsten Figueiredo Walter, Dean Karlan, Niclas Moneke, Ana Radu, Khozya Zyambo

健康与医疗 美国、赞比亚

Abstract

We study the impact of the 2025 sudden freeze then subsequent thaw of US foreign aid on HIV prevention, treatment, infection, and death. In Zambia, HIV-related aid was administered through two agencies. Activities under one were disrupted for several months, and those under the other much less. Comprehensive data from all Zambian health facilities reveal immediate differential reductions in preventive HIV measures that mostly later recovered, followed by more HIV-positive pregnant women, more HIV-exposed births, more opportunistic infections, and more recorded deaths. For example, after one year we observe a 14% [95% CI: 3, 25] increase in HIV-positive pregnant women and a 26% [95% CI: 8, 45] increase in deaths among people living with HIV who were receiving antiretroviral therapy.

中文摘要

我们研究2025年美国对外援助突然冻结及随后解冻对HIV预防、治疗、感染和死亡的影响。在赞比亚，HIV相关援助通过两个机构实施。其中一个机构的活动被中断了数月，而另一个机构的活动受影响小得多。来自赞比亚所有卫生机构的综合数据显示，预防性HIV措施立即出现差异性减少，随后大多恢复，接着出现更多HIV阳性孕妇、更多HIV暴露新生儿、更多机会性感染和更多记录死亡。例如，一年后我们观察到HIV阳性孕妇增加14%[95% CI：3, 25]，接受抗逆转录病毒治疗的HIV感染者死亡增加26%[95% CI：8, 45]。
